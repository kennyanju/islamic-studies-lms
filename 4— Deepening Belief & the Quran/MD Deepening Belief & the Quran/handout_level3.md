## 1. The Living Miracle: The Noble Speech of Allah

The Holy Quran is the literal, uncreated speech of Allah revealed through Archangel Jibril (AS) to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ over a period of twenty-three years (thirteen in Makkah and ten in Madinah). Unlike previous scriptures that were entrusted to human scribes and suffered alterations over centuries, Allah guaranteed the direct preservation of the Quran:  
> **إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا ٱلذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ**  
> *"Indeed, it is We who sent down the Quran and indeed, We will be its guardian."* (Surah Al-Hijr 15:9)

## 2. The Science of Tajweed: Noon Sakinah & Qalqalah

When reciting the Quran, we beautify our recitation by applying the classical rules of **Tajweed** (precise articulation and rhythmic delivery):

### The Four Rules of Noon Sakinah (نْ) and Tanween (ً ٍ ٌ)

| Number & Rule | Arabic Name | Meaning & Articulation | Trigger Letters | Practical Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Al-Izhar al-Halqi** | الإِظْهَارُ الْحَلْقِيُّ | Clear, distinct pronunciation of the Noon without nasal hum (*Ghunnah*). | The 6 throat letters: **Hamzah (ء), Ha (هـ), 'Ayn (ع), Ha (ح), Ghayn (غ), Kha (خ)** | *Man amana*, *Min khawf* |
| **2. Al-Idgham** | الإِدْغَامُ | Merging the Noon into the following letter. Has two sub-types: | | |
| **2a. With Ghunnah** | إدغام بغنة | Merging with a 2-count nasal resonance (*Ghunnah*). | The 4 letters of **Yanmu (ي ن م و)** | *Man yaqul*, *Min ma'* |
| **2b. Without Ghunnah** | إدغام بغير غنة | Complete merging without nasal hum (*Idgham Kamil*). | The 2 letters **Lam (ل) and Ra (ر)** | *Mil-ladunhu*, *Mir-rabbihim* |
| **3. Al-Iqlab** | الإِقْلابُ | Converting the Noon sound into a soft, hidden **Meem (م)** with a 2-count Ghunnah. | The single letter **Ba (ب)** | *Min ba'di* (recited *Mim-ba'di*) |
| **4. Al-Ikhfa' al-Haqiqi** | الإِخْفَاءُ الْحَقِيقِيُّ | Concealing the Noon between Izhar and Idgham with a gentle 2-count nasal tone. | The remaining **15 letters** of the Arabic alphabet | *Min duni*, *Anfusakum* |

### The Rule of Qalqalah (Echoing Vibration)
When any of the five letters of **Qutbu Jaddin (ق ط ب ج د)** carries a Sukun (or you stop upon it), the vocal point rebounds with a crisp echo:
* **Minor Qalqalah (Sughra):** When the letter occurs in the middle of a word (e.g. *Yaq-ta'un*, *Ab-na'ahum*).
* **Major Qalqalah (Kubra):** When stopping upon the letter at the end of an ayah (e.g. *Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad [d]*).

## 3. Thematic Tafsir: Surah An-Naba' (The Great News: Surah 78)

Surah An-Naba' opens the 30th Juz' of the Quran, addressing the skepticism of the Makkan disbelievers regarding the Day of Resurrection:

### The Three Thematic Sections of Surah An-Naba'
1. **Cosmic Signs of Divine Omnipotence (Verses 6-16):**  
   Allah directs human contemplation to His observable creation to demonstrate His power to resurrect humanity:
   * The earth spread out as a stable resting place (*Mihada*).
   * The mountains driven into the crust as stabilizing pegs (*Awtada*).
   * The creation of humanity in pairs, the gift of night for rest, the daytime for seeking livelihood, the seven heavens, the radiant sun (*Siraja Wahhaja*), and rain clouds producing abundant gardens.
2. **The Day of Sorting (*Yawm al-Fasl*: Verses 17-30):**  
   The blowing of the Trumpet, the opening of heavenly gates, the dispersal of mountains, and the severe consequence awaiting persistent arrogance in Jahannam.
3. **The Reward of the Righteous (*Al-Muttaqun*: Verses 31-40):**  
   Enclosed gardens, cooling beverages, and peaceful tranquility for those who guarded their faith. The surah concludes with a solemn reminder: on that Day, the unrepentant will gaze upon their deeds and lament:  
   > **يَٰلَيْتَنِى كُنتُ تُرَٰبًۢا**  
   > *"Oh, I wish that I were dust!"* (78:40)

## 4. Intention, Hadith Sciences, and Following a Madhhab

To deepen our understanding of faith and sacred practice, Level 3 students learn four foundational sciences:

### A. Intention (Niyyah) & Sincerity (Ikhlas)
The first hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari states:  
> *"Actions are judged only by intentions, and every person will have only what they intended."* (Sahih al-Bukhari 1, Sahih Muslim 1907)  
* **Ikhlas (Sincerity):** Doing an act purely for Allah's pleasure alone.
* **Riya' (Ostentation):** Performing worship to impress other people. The Prophet ﷺ warned that Riya' is "minor shirk" because it seeks praise from creation rather than the Creator.
* **Ash'ari Kasb (Acquisition):** Ash'ari theology teaches that Allah creates the act, while the human being chooses and acquires (*Kasb*) responsibility through their intention and free will.

### B. Tafsir of Surah Al-'Asr (Time: Surah 103)
Imam al-Shafi'i observed: *"If Allah had revealed only this surah, it would have sufficed humanity."* In three short verses, Allah swears by Time and establishes the four conditions of true success:
1. True Faith (*Iman*).
2. Righteous deeds (*'Amal Salih*).
3. Advising one another to truth and justice (*Al-Tawasi bil-Haqq*).
4. Advising one another to patient perseverance (*Al-Tawasi bil-Sabr*).

### C. Introduction to Hadith Sciences
A Prophetic Hadith consists of two elements:
* **The Isnad (Chain):** The sequence of verified narrators transmitting the report from one generation to the next.
* **The Matn (Text):** The actual spoken words or described action of the Prophet ﷺ.  
Scholars classify Hadith into three major categories based on narrator integrity and continuous chains: **Sahih** (Sound/Authentic), **Hasan** (Good/Reliable), and **Da'if** (Weak). The most authoritative early hadith texts include *Al-Muwatta'* of Imam Malik and the Sahih compilations of al-Bukhari and Muslim.

### D. Why Muslims Follow a Sunni Madhhab
No single individual can independently interpret thousands of Quranic verses and hadiths without systematic training. The four Sunni schools of jurisprudence (**Hanafi, Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali**) represent four verified legal methodologies developed by master scholars. In the Maliki school, Imam Malik prioritized the continuous inherited practice of the people of Madinah (*'Amal Ahl al-Madinah*), providing an unbroken living transmission of the Prophetic Sunnah.

## 5. Curriculum Anchors in Practice

1. **Purifying Your Daily Intention:** Before beginning study, chores, or sports, make a silent intention (*Niyyah*): *"O Allah, I am doing this to gain strength, knowledge, and discipline to serve You and help my family."*
2. **Applying the Four Rules of Noon Sakinah:** Read the opening page of Juz' 'Amma (Surah An-Naba'). Identify at least one instance of Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab, and Ikhfa', reciting each with correct Tajweed.
3. **Surah Al-'Asr in Action:** When working on group assignments, how can you practice the two community duties of Surah Al-'Asr: advising to truth (*Al-Tawasi bil-Haqq*) and encouraging patience (*Al-Tawasi bil-Sabr*)?
