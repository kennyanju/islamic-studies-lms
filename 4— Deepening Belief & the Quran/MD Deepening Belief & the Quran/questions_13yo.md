### Part 3 Answers
16. **Kasb Explanation:**
    * *[Ash‘arī Creed Flag]* **Kasb** (Acquisition) is the Ash‘arī formulation of free will and predestination. The *Qadariyyah* claimed that human beings create their own actions, which compromises the belief that Allah is the Creator of all things. The *Jabariyyah* claimed that human beings have no free will and are forced to act, which compromises the justice of Allah in holding humans accountable. 
    * The Ash‘arīs argue that Allah alone creates (*Khāliq*) the action and the power to perform it. The human being possesses a limited will (*al-Irādah al-Juz’iyyah*). When the human wills to do something, Allah creates the action. The human "acquires" (*yaksib*) it because their intention matches the creation of the act. Therefore, because we choose the intention, we are legally responsible for our deeds.
17. **Quran Compilation Analysis:**
    * **Abu Bakr’s Compilation:** Prompted by the deaths of memorizers at the Battle of Yamamah. The goal was to collect the scattered written fragments into a single master text (*Ṣuḥuf*). The methodology required two witnesses to swear that each written piece was written in front of the Prophet ﷺ.
    * **Uthman’s Standardization:** Prompted by regional disputes over pronunciation (*Ahruf*) among non-Arab converts. The goal was to standardize the writing system into one authorized script (*Rasm al-‘Uthmānī*). Official copies were made in the Qurayshi dialect and sent to key cities; variant drafts were destroyed.
    * **Zayd ibn Thābit’s Role:** He led both committees. His strictness in demanding multiple sources of verification (written text + memory + physical witnesses) prevented any errors from entering the text, preserving it in its authentic state.
18. **Aṣ-Ṣamad & Tanzīh Analysis:**
    * **Aṣ-Ṣamad:** Linguistically denotes the supreme leader to whom all turn, the Self-Sufficient who has no needs, and that which is solid/without cavity. 
    * **Tanzīh (Transcendence):** Since Allah is *Aṣ-Ṣamad*, He has no biological functions, does not eat, drink, change, or decay. This refutes anthropomorphic comparisons.
    * Combined with the verse *"Nor is there to Him any equivalent,"* it forms the theological basis of Ash‘arī belief: Allah does not resemble creation in His essence, attributes, or actions. He is transcendent above limits, space, time, or parts.
19. **‘Amal Ahl al-Madīnah Analysis:**
    * **‘Amal Ahl al-Madīnah:** *[Mālikī Fiqh Flag]* Refers to the lived practice of the people of Madinah. Imam Malik argued that since the Prophet ﷺ lived and established the law in Madinah in front of thousands of Sahabah, their collective behavior passed down to the next generation is a mass-transmitted (*Mutawātir*) physical Sunnah.
    * **Override Condition:** If a single Hadith narrated by one person (*Khabar al-Wāḥid*) contradicts this city-wide practice, the practice of Madinah is preferred. Malik argued a single narrator could forget, make a mistake, or report an abrogated rule, whereas an entire city of scholars and families cannot collectively fabricate a daily ritual.
    * **Legal Example:** *Sadl* (praying with arms at the side). Although some single Hadiths mention clasping the hands (*Qabḍ*), Imam Malik observed the people of Madinah praying with their arms down. He concluded *Sadl* was the continuous, authentic Sunnah of Madinah.
20. **Applying Surah Al-`Aṣr to Student Life:**
    * **Īmān (Faith):** Continually studying the rational proofs of Islamic creed and reading the Quran with understanding.
    * **‘Amal Ṣāliḥ (Righteous Action):** Setting clean intentions (*Niyyah*) for studies, treating classmates with respect, and keeping up with daily prayers.
    * **Tawāṣī bi’l-Ḥaqq (Advising to Truth):** Studying with friends, sharing helpful resources, standing up against bullying, and speaking truthfully.
    * **Tawāṣī bi’ṣ-Ṣabr (Advising to Patience):** Showing discipline during exam preparation, encouraging peers who are struggling, and remaining steady in moral habits even when peer pressure is strong.
