## Part 3: Scenario & Short Answer (Questions 16-20)

**16. The Carrot Scenario:**  
Aamina eats a bowl of carrots because she likes the crunchy taste. Salma eats a bowl of carrots because she wants to keep her eyes healthy to read the Quran and please Allah. Explain who gets a spiritual reward for eating, and why, using the concept of *Niyyah*.

**17. Why did Caliph Uthman compile and standardize the physical Mushaf?**  

**18. What are the two main parts that make up a Hadith, and why do we need both of them?**  

**19. How did Imam Malik’s love for the Prophet ﷺ show in his daily habits in Madinah?**  

**20. The Madinah Practice:**  
If a single person tells you they saw the Prophet ﷺ do something in a certain way, but you see that thousands of scholars and families in the Prophet’s ﷺ own city of Madinah have been doing it differently for generations, which path would Imam Malik choose? Explain why using the term *‘Amal Ahl al-Madīnah*.

---

## Answer Key & Explanations

### Part 1 Answers
1. **b) *Niyyah***. (Intention).
2. **a) *Riyā’***. (Ostentation / Showing off). This is the "minor shirk" that destroys the rewards of our deeds.
3. **c) Allah creates all actions, and we choose them**. *[Ash‘arī Creed Flag]* This is the concept of *Kasb* (Acquisition)—Allah is the Creator (*Khāliq*) of all actions, but humans are the choosers/acquirers (*Muktasib*).
4. **b) 23 years**. (13 years in Makkah and 10 years in Madinah).
5. **c) The eternal, uncreated Attribute of Speech belonging to Allah**. *[Ash‘arī Creed Flag]* *Al-Kalām al-Nafsī* refers to Allah's uncreated attribute of Speech, distinct from the physical paper, ink, and voice which are created (*al-Kalām al-Lafẓī*).
6. **a) Abu Bakr**. He ordered the compilation after the Battle of Yamamah, on the advice of Umar.
7. **a) *Rasm al-‘Uthmānī***. This is the sacred orthography of the Mushaf.
8. **b) The Self-Sufficient Besought of all**. *Aṣ-Ṣamad* means He is free of all needs, has no cavities, and does not eat or drink, while all creation depends on Him.
9. **c) The chain of narrators showing who passed the report down**. The *Isnād* is the chain, while the *Matn* is the text.
10. **c) *‘Amal Ahl al-Madīnah***. This is the living practice of the people of Madinah.

### Part 2 Answers
11. **Pillar (Rukn)**. *[Mālikī Fiqh Flag]* In the Mālikī school, the *niyyah* is a core pillar (*rukn*), not just a recommendation.
12. **Time**. Swearing by *Al-`Aṣr* (Time) shows its high value.
13. **Matn**. The *Matn* is the text, and the *Isnād* is the chain of narrators.
14. **Tanzīh**. *[Ash‘arī Creed Flag]* *Tanzīh* means absolving Allah from resembling creation or having physical body parts.
15. **Kasb**. *[Ash‘arī Creed Flag]* *Kasb* (Acquisition) is the term for how humans choose their actions.

### Part 3 Answers
16. **Answer:** Salma gets the spiritual reward, while Aamina does not. Even though both are eating carrots, Salma used her **Niyyah** (intention) to turn a normal habit (*‘adah*) into an act of worship (*‘ibadah*), eating to stay healthy to serve Allah. Aamina ate purely out of habit.
17. **Answer:** Caliph Uthman standardized the Quranic spelling and compiled the official *Muṣḥafs* because the Muslim empire had expanded, and regional differences in pronunciation and dialects (*Ahruf*) were causing arguments and division among Muslims.
18. **Answer:** A Hadith is made of the **Isnād** (the chain of narrators) and the **Matn** (the actual text). We need the *Isnād* to prove the report is authentic and really came from the Prophet ﷺ, and we need the *Matn* to understand the message and teachings.
19. **Answer:** Imam Malik showed his love by never riding a horse or camel in Madinah out of respect for the soil where the Prophet ﷺ is buried. He also performed Wudu, put on his best clothes, and wore perfume before sitting down to teach Hadith.
20. **Answer:** Imam Malik would follow the continuous practice of the people of Madinah (**‘Amal Ahl al-Madīnah**). *[Mālikī Fiqh Flag]* He argued that a continuous practice passed down by thousands of people to thousands of people in the Prophet's ﷺ own city is a living Sunnah, which is stronger and less likely to contain errors than a single Hadith narrated by only one person (*Khabar al-Wāḥid*).
