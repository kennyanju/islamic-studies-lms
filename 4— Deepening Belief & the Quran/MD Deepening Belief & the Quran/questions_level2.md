# Level 2 Worksheet: Deepening Belief & the Quran
**Age Group:** Ages 8-10 (Level 2 Track)  
**Focus:** 23 Years of Revelation, Tajweed, Tafkhim, and Surah Al-Ikhlas  

---

## Part A: Multiple Choice

### 1. Over how many years was the Noble Quran revealed gradually to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ?
(A) 23 years (13 in Makkah and 10 in Madinah)
(B) 1 year during Ramadan
(C) 10 years in Madinah only
(D) 40 years from youth

### 2. What was the primary focus of the Makkan surahs revealed during the first thirteen years before the Hijrah?
(A) Complex inheritance calculations and municipal courts
(B) Guidelines for regional trade agreements
(C) Pure Tawhid, the Day of Judgment, moral steadfastness, and building sincere faith
(D) Rules for international postal communications

### 3. What does the divine Name *Al-Samad* signify in Surah Al-Ikhlas?
(A) The Owner of fertile desert pastures
(B) The Guardian of physical strength
(C) The Designer of celestial stars
(D) The Eternal, Self-Sufficient Refuge upon Whom all creation relies, while He needs nothing

### 4. In the science of Tajweed, what is the term for pronouncing a heavy letter with a full, resonant mouth sound?
(A) Tarqiq (Light pronunciation)
(B) Tafkhim (Heavy, full-mouth pronunciation)
(C) Ghunnah (Nasalization)
(D) Waqf (Pausing)

### 5. Why did Allah reveal the Quran gradually in stages (*Tanzil*) over twenty-three years rather than all at once?
(A) To strengthen the heart of the Prophet ﷺ and allow believers to memorize, understand, and apply its guidance step-by-step
(B) Because writing parchment was unavailable in early Makkah
(C) Because early companions requested fewer verses per season
(D) To match the seasonal caravan travel schedules between Syria and Yemen

* Madd Tabee'ee ─── Natural two-count elongation of long vowel sounds

## Part D: Reflection

10. Why is it easier to build lasting good habits when we learn them step-by-step, just as the Quran was revealed gradually over twenty-three years?

### Reflection Rubric
* **Full Understanding:** Student explains that gradual learning prevents feeling overwhelmed, allows time for practice, and leads to deep understanding and lasting character habits.
* **Basic Understanding:** Student notes that learning slowly is easier without connecting it to habit formation or the wisdom of gradual Quranic revelation.

## Teacher Answer Key
1. A - The Quran was revealed over 23 years (13 in Makkah and 10 in Madinah).
2. C - Makkan surahs focused on establishing Tawhid, the Hereafter, and steadfast faith.
3. D - Al-Samad means the Self-Sufficient Master upon Whom all creation depends.
4. B - Tafkhim refers to the heavy, resonant pronunciation produced by the seven letters of Isti'la'.
5. A - Surah Al-Furqan (25:32) explains that gradual revelation strengthened the Prophet's heart and facilitated comprehension.
