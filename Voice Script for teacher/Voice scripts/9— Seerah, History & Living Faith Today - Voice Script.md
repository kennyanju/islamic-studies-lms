## Slide 8: What is the Caliphate? (*Khilāfah*)
* **Slide Summary:** Defining the Caliphate and introducing the first four Rightly Guided Caliphs.
* **Delivery Direction:** `[Speak in a clear, historical tone]`
* **Narrative Script (ElevenLabs v3 Ready):**
  "[thoughtful] How was the Muslim community managed after the Prophet's sallallahu alayhi wa sallam passing? This is where the **Caliphate**—or *Khilāfah*—comes in. 
  
  *Khilāfah* literally means succession or stewardship. [pause] A Caliph does not receive new revelation—revelation ended with the Prophet sallallahu alayhi wa sallam. [pause] Rather, their job is to protect the faith and manage the community according to Islamic values. [pause] The first four Caliphs are called **Al-Khulafā’ al-Rāshidūn**—the Rightly Guided Caliphs—because they followed the Prophet's sallallahu alayhi wa sallam model of leadership with absolute integrity, using mutual consultation (*Shūrā*) to solve new challenges."

* **Check-for-Understanding Question:** "Does a Caliph receive new revelation from Allah? (Answer: No, revelation ended with the Prophet sallallahu alayhi wa sallam; the Caliph manages the community based on existing revelation)."
