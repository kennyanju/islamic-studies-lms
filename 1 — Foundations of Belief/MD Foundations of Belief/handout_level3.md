## 1. Introduction: Building an Analytical Faith

Welcome to Level 3! At ages 10–12, you are beginning to think more logically, categorize ideas, and ask deeper questions about reality. Faith in Islam (*Iman*) is not a blind emotional wish; it is firm, reasoned conviction (*Tawhid*) rooted in a sound intellect (*'Aql*) illuminated by divine revelation (*Wahy*).

In this unit, we study the foundational theological tools used by classical Sunni scholars to define reality, protect our hearts from superstition, and understand who Allah is.

## 2. The Three Judgments of the Mind (Ahkam al-'Aql)

Imam Ibn 'Ashir explains in *Al-Murshid al-Mu'in* that before studying specific theological dogmas, every thinking person must understand how the human mind categorizes statements:

1. **Al-Wajib (الْوَاجِبُ الْعَقْلِيُّ) — The Rationally Necessary:**
   * That which the rational mind cannot conceive of as nonexistent.
   * *Universal example:* The whole of an apple is larger than half of it.
   * *Theological example:* The existence of a supreme, uncreated Creator (*Wujud Allah*). Because the universe has order, laws, and a beginning, it is rationally necessary that an Eternal Cause brought it into being.

2. **Al-Mustahil (الْمُسْتَحِيلُ الْعَقْلِيُّ) — The Rationally Impossible:**
   * That which the rational mind cannot conceive of as existing.
   * *Universal example:* A shape that is simultaneously a perfect circle and a perfect square at the exact same moment.
   * *Theological example:* Allah having a partner, a beginning, a physical body, or a child. These are logical contradictions that cannot exist.

3. **Al-Ja'iz (الْجَائِزُ الْعَقْلِيُّ) — The Rationally Possible:**
   * That which the intellect can conceive of as either existing or not existing without contradiction.
   * *Universal example:* Whether a spaceship lands on Mars tomorrow, or whether it rains in your city on Eid day.
   * *Theological example:* Allah creating humanity, sending Prophets, or forgiving a repentant sinner. Allah was not forced to create us; He created the cosmos out of pure Divine Will (*Iradah*) and Wisdom (*Hikmah*).

## 3. Divine Transcendence: Tanzih vs. Anthropomorphism

One of the great glories of Sunni Islam is **Tanzih** (تَنْزِيه), which means keeping Allah completely free and exalted above any created limitation, flaw, or bodily form.

### The Principle of Mukhalafah lil-Hawadith (مُخَالَفَتُهُ لِلْحَوَادِثِ)
This attribute states: **Allah is entirely different from created things.**
* Creation occupies space; Allah created space and is not inside it.
* Creation is bounded by time (past, present, future); Allah created time and does not change across seconds or centuries.
* Creation has physical dimensions (height, weight, color, limbs); Allah has no physical shape, mass, or body (*Jism*).

Imam Zu al-Nun al-Misri (and Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal) famously stated:
> *"Whatever pops into your imagination, Allah is not like that."* (*Kullu ma khatara bi-balika fa-Allahu bi-khilafi dhalik*).

## 4. Classical Matn: Lines from Ibn 'Ashir (*Al-Murshid al-Mu'in*)

Imam 'Abd al-Wahid Ibn 'Ashir (d. 1040 AH) composed his famous didactic poem to summarize creed and Maliki fiqh:

```
فَصْلٌ: يَجِبُ لِلَّهِ الْوُجُودُ وَالْقِدَمْ *** كَذَا الْبَقَاءُ وَالْغِنَى الْمُطْلَقُ عَمْ
وَخُلْفُهُ لِخَلْقِهِ بِلاَ مِثَالْ *** وَوَحْدَةُ الذَّاتِ وَوَصْفٍ وَالْفِعَالْ
```
*Translation:*  
*"It is necessary for Allah to have Existence and Beginninglessness,*  
*Likewise Endlessness and Absolute Self-Sufficiency encompassing all.*  
*His Absolute Difference from His creation without any resemblance,*  
*And the Oneness of His Essence, His Attributes, and His Actions."*

## 5. The Six Pillars of Eemaan Deepened

At Level 3, we connect the Six Pillars of Eemaan to daily moral responsibility:
1. **Belief in Allah:** Recognizing His sole sovereignty and worshipping Him with awe (*Khawf*) and hope (*Raja'*).
2. **Belief in the Angels:** Recognizing their unceasing obedience; being conscious of the Kiraman Katibin recording our deeds.
3. **Belief in the Divine Books:** Acknowledging the Torah, Gospel, and Psalms in their original revelations, and following the final, uncorrupted Noble Quran.
4. **Belief in the Messengers:** Loving all 25 named Prophets, with Prophet Muhammad ﷺ as the Seal of Prophethood (*Khatam an-Nabiyyin*).
5. **Belief in the Last Day:** Remembering accountability, the Scale (*Mizan*), the Bridge (*Sirat*), Paradise (*Jannah*), and Hellfire (*Jahannam*).
6. **Belief in Divine Decree (Al-Qadar):** Trusting that Allah's knowledge precedes all events, while striving wholeheartedly in doing righteous deeds.

## 6. Real-Life Dilemma: "The Fiqh of Belief Clinic"

> **Case Scenario:**  
> Your classmate Yusuf says: *"If scientists find that the universe has billions of galaxies, doesn't that make humans too small for God to care about?"*  
> **How you answer using Level 3 Knowledge:**  
> 1. Remind him of **Al-Khaliq** and **Al-'Alim**: A human builder gets overwhelmed by large numbers, but Allah's knowledge is not divided or limited. He knows the fall of every single leaf on every planet.  
> 2. The vastness of the universe does not diminish human significance; rather, it highlights Allah's immense generosity in honoring humanity (*Bani Adam*) with intellect, revelation, and moral choice!
