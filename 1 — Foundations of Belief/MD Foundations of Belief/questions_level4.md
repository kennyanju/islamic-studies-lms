# Level 4 Quiz: Foundations of Belief

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. At what developmental milestone does a Muslim youth transition into full religious accountability (Taklif)?
   (A) When graduating from secondary high school.
   (B) At the onset of physical maturity and puberty (Bulugh).
   (C) When completing the memorization of the entire Quran.
   (D) Upon opening an independent financial bank account.

2. Which 9th-century scholar authored the classical theological primer *Umm al-Barahin*?
   (A) Imam Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Sanusi.
   (B) Imam Ibn Khaldun al-Hadrami.
   (C) Imam Abu Hanifah al-Nu'man.
   (D) Imam Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti.

3. Into how many distinct categories did Imam al-Sanusi organize the 20 necessary divine attributes?
   (A) Two categories: Outward and Inward.
   (B) Four categories: Nafsiyyah, Salbiyyah, Ma'ani, and Ma'nawiyyah.
   (C) Seven categories matching the days of the week.
   (D) Ten categories corresponding to the ten blessed companions.

4. What is the precise function of the five "Negative/Privative" Attributes (Al-Sifat al-Salbiyyah)?
   (A) They negate any concept of deficiency, beginning, end, or partnership from Allah.
   (B) They prohibit humans from asking philosophical questions about science.
   (C) They describe the punishment awaiting sinners in the Hereafter.
   (D) They list the foods that are ritually unlawful to consume.

5. What does the attribute "Al-Qiyam bin-Nafs" signify?
   (A) Allah rests on a physical seat of gold in the highest heaven.
   (B) Allah is completely Self-Subsisting, needing no creator, space, or supporter.
   (C) Allah requires the daily prayers of angels to maintain the cosmic order.
   (D) Allah changes His essence depending on human historical progress.

6. Which of the following is classified under the Seven "Sifat al-Ma'ani"?
   (A) Al-Qidam (Beginninglessness).
   (B) Al-Wujud (Existence).
   (C) Al-Qudrah (Omnipotent Power) and Al-Iradah (Divine Will).
   (D) Al-Wahdaniyyah (Absolute Oneness).

7. How does classical Sunni theology define Divine Speech (Kalam Allah)?
   (A) Created sound waves recorded on prehistoric stone tablets.
   (B) An eternal, uncreated attribute of Allah that does not consist of physical sounds, breaths, or letters.
   (C) Human poetry composed by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ during his retreats.
   (D) Angelic thoughts whispered by Jibril into the ears of Arabian poets.

8. Which four moral and intellectual qualities are rationally necessary for all Prophets of Allah?
   (A) Wealth, high political rank, military invincibility, and poetic eloquence.
   (B) Truthfulness (Sidq), Trustworthiness ('Ismah), Conveyance (Tabligh), and Wisdom (Fatanah).
   (C) Physical immunity from illness, complete silence, and hermit living.
   (D) Advanced knowledge of architecture, astronomy, and maritime navigation.

9. What does the doctrine of Prophetic 'Ismah (Infallibility) protect Prophets from?
   (A) Catching a physical cold, headache, or fever.
   (B) Eating normal human food or getting fatigued after walking.
   (C) Committing major sins (Kaba'ir) or shameful minor sins before and after Prophethood.
   (D) Forgetting where they placed their walking staff or clothing.

10. What unites the Ash'ari, Maturidi, and mainstream Athari traditions within Sunni Islam?
    (A) They all affirm complete Tanzih (transcendence) and reject that Allah has physical body parts or places.
    (B) They all agree that human reason should completely override authentic revelation.
    (C) They all hold that the universe has existed from eternity alongside Allah.
    (D) They all mandate that Muslims must avoid modern science and medicine.

2. The divine attribute that negates any beginning to Allah's existence is called ______.
3. The specific divine attribute that determines which possibilities occur, at what time, and in what manner is ______.
4. Power, Will, Knowledge, Life, Hearing, Sight, and Speech are collectively categorized as ______.
5. The moral protection guaranteeing that Prophets never commit major sins or acts of betrayal is termed ______.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Al-Wujud ─── The single Nafsiyyah attribute denoting necessary existence
* Al-Salbiyyah ─── The 5 attributes that negate flaws (Qidam, Baqa', etc.)
* Sifat al-Ma'ani ─── The 7 qualitative existential attributes including Qudrah and 'Ilm
* Al-Fatanah ─── The intellectual acuity and wisdom necessary for all Messengers
* Al-Tabligh ─── The complete and faithful delivery of divine revelation without fear

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. Explain why knowing Allah through rational reflection (Nazar) is considered the first duty of the mature believer (Mukallaf).
2. Distinguish between Al-Qudrah (Power) and Al-Iradah (Will) using a practical example of a created event.
3. How does the concept of Prophetic 'Ismah safeguard the credibility and reliability of the Holy Quran and Hadith?
4. A friend remarks: "The differences between the Ash'ari and Athari scholars show that Muslims cannot agree on basic beliefs." How would you answer this based on the "Other Scholars Say" matrix?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - Bulugh marks the biological and spiritual transition into moral and legal accountability (Taklif).
2. A - Imam Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Sanusi wrote *Umm al-Barahin* (also called *Al-'Aqidah al-Sughra*).
3. B - Al-Sanusi classified the 20 attributes into Nafsiyyah, Salbiyyah, Ma'ani, and Ma'nawiyyah.
4. A - Salbiyyah attributes negate non-existence, end, beginning, multiplicity, and resemblance to creation.
5. B - Al-Qiyam bin-Nafs affirms that Allah is Self-Sufficient and independent of all creation.
6. C - Qudrah, Iradah, 'Ilm, Hayat, Sam', Basar, and Kalam are the seven Sifat al-Ma'ani.
7. B - Ash'ari and Sunni creed affirms Kalam as an eternal divine attribute transcendent of created sounds and letters.
8. B - Sidq, Amanah/'Ismah, Tabligh, and Fatanah are the four essential attributes of all Messengers.
9. C - Infallibility ensures prophets are protected from sins that would compromise moral authority.
10. A - All branches of Ahl al-Sunnah agree on Tanzih, denying bodily containment or physical form to the Creator.
