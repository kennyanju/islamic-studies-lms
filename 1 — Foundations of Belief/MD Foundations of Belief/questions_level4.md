# Module 1: Foundations of Belief (Level 4 Assessment)

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
*Select the single best answer for each question.*

**1. At what developmental milestone does a Muslim youth transition into full personal religious accountability (Taklif)?**
(A) When opening an independent personal bank account
(B) When graduating from secondary education
(C) At the onset of physical maturity and puberty (Bulugh)
(D) After memorizing the complete text of the Qur'an

**2. Which 9th-century North African Maliki scholar authored the classical theological primer *Umm al-Barahin*?**
(A) Imam Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Sanusi
(B) Imam Ibn Khaldun al-Hadrami
(C) Imam Abu Hanifah al-Nu'man
(D) Imam Jalal al-Din al-Suyuti

**3. Into which four distinct categories did Imam al-Sanusi organize the 20 necessary divine attributes?**
(A) Outward, Inward, Legal, and Moral
(B) Universal, Particular, Historical, and Prophetic
(C) Primary, Secondary, Intermediate, and Final
(D) Nafsiyyah, Salbiyyah, Ma'ani, and Ma'nawiyyah

**4. What is the theological function of the five "Negative/Privative" Attributes (Al-Sifat al-Salbiyyah)?**
(A) They describe the physical punishment awaiting oppressors in the Hereafter
(B) They negate any concept of deficiency, beginning, end, physical composition, or partnership from Allah
(C) They prohibit believers from investigating natural sciences
(D) They list the dietary items that are ritually prohibited to consume

**5. What does the divine attribute "Al-Qiyam bin-Nafs" (Self-Subsistence) signify in Sunni Creed?**
(A) Allah demands that humans stand for hours in nightly voluntary prayers
(B) Allah relies upon the continuous praise of angels to sustain planetary orbits
(C) Allah is completely independent, needing neither a creator, a physical place, nor an external supporter
(D) Allah changes His Divine Essence according to historical eras

**6. Which of the following divine attributes is classified under the Seven "Sifat al-Ma'ani"?**
(A) Al-Qudrah (Power), Al-Iradah (Will), and Al-'Ilm (Knowledge)
(B) Al-Wujud (Existence)
(C) Al-Qidam (Beginninglessness)
(D) Al-Wahdaniyyah (Absolute Oneness)

**7. How does classical Sunni theology define Divine Speech (Kalam Allah)?**
(A) Sound waves that began to exist inside the atmosphere at a specific historical point
(B) Textual inscriptions carved onto ancient stone monuments
(C) Poetic reflections composed by the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ during solitary retreats
(D) An eternal, uncreated attribute of Allah that is without physical sound, vocal letters, or pauses

**8. Which four moral and intellectual qualities are rationally necessary for all Prophets of Allah?**
(A) High political rank, personal wealth, military power, and secular status
(B) Truthfulness (Sidq), Trustworthiness and Infallibility (Amanah and 'Ismah), Full Conveyance (Tabligh), and Intellectual Acuity (Fatanah)
(C) Total immunity from physical illness, monastic seclusion, and perpetual silence
(D) Natural mastery of architecture, metallurgy, and maritime navigation

**9. What does the doctrine of Prophetic Infallibility ('Ismah) protect all Messengers from?**
(A) Experiencing normal human fatigue, hunger, or thirst
(B) Catching a natural illness such as a fever or headache
(C) Committing major sins (Kaba'ir) or shameful minor sins before and after Prophethood
(D) Expressing personal grief or sadness when facing hardship

**10. What unites the Ash'ari, Maturidi, and mainstream Athari traditions within Sunni Islam?**
(A) They all claim that rational philosophy supersedes clear revelation
(B) They reject the authority of mass-transmitted Prophetic Sunnah
(C) They forbid the study of the classical Arabic language
(D) They all affirm complete Divine Transcendence (Tanzih) and reject physical limbs, bodily shape, and spatial containment for Allah

# Answer Key & Marking Guide

### Part A: Multiple Choice Answers
1. C - Bulugh (puberty) marks the arrival of Taklif and adult religious responsibility (Handout Section 1).
2. A - Imam al-Sanusi authored Umm al-Barahin, standardizing the 20-attribute framework (Handout Section 2).
3. D - The 20 attributes comprise Nafsiyyah (1), Salbiyyah (5), Ma'ani (7), and Ma'nawiyyah (7) (Handout Section 2).
4. B - Salbiyyah attributes strip away nonexistence, beginning, end, resemblance, and plurality (Handout Section 2).
5. C - Al-Qiyam bin-Nafs establishes that Allah is independent of cause, space, or supporter (Handout Section 2).
6. A - Qudrah, Iradah, and 'Ilm are among the seven qualitative existential attributes (Handout Section 2).
7. D - Kalam Allah is eternal, subsisting with the Divine Essence without physical sounds or letters (Handout Section 2).
8. B - Prophets must possess Sidq, Amanah, Tabligh, and Fatanah to fulfill their mission (Handout Section 3).
9. C - 'Ismah preserves the moral integrity of prophets from all major and shameful sins (Handout Section 3).
10. D - All three schools of Ahl al-Sunnah agree on Tanzih and reject anthropomorphic bodily forms (Handout Section 4).
