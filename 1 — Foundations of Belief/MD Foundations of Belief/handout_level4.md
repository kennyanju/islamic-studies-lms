## 1. The Threshold of Moral Accountability (Bulugh)

As you enter the years of **Bulugh** (puberty and moral responsibility), Islamic law recognizes you as an adult believer (*Mukallaf*). Your religious duties are no longer mere childhood imitation; they are deliberate, rational acts of worship recorded directly in your book of deeds.

Sunni theology teaches that the very first obligation upon the responsible human being is to know Allah through rational reflection (*Ma'rifat Allah bi-Nazar*). This does not mean complex philosophical jargon; it means using the intellect Allah gifted you to recognize His existence, majesty, and unshared perfection.

## 2. The Sanusi Framework: 20 Necessary Attributes (*Al-Sifat al-Wajibah*)

Imam Muhammad ibn Yusuf al-Sanusi (d. 895 AH) organized the necessary divine attributes into four clear categories in his masterpiece *Umm al-Barahin*:

### A. Al-Sifah al-Nafsiyyah (The Essential Attribute — 1)
1. **Al-Wujud (Existence):** Allah exists necessarily of Himself. His existence has no beginning, no end, and does not depend on an external cause.

### B. Al-Sifat al-Salbiyyah (The Negative / Privative Attributes — 5)
These attributes "negate" or strip away any concept of deficiency from Allah:
2. **Al-Qidam (Beginninglessness / Pre-Eternity):** Allah is the First (*Al-Awwal*); there was never a moment when He did not exist.
3. **Al-Baqa' (Endlessness / Immortality):** Allah is the Last (*Al-Akhir*); He cannot cease to exist.
4. **Mukhalafah lil-Hawadith (Absolute Incomparability):** Allah does not resemble created things in any way—He is not a body, has no physical size, and is not inside space.
5. **Al-Qiyam bin-Nafs (Absolute Self-Subsistence):** Allah needs no creator, no location, and no supporter; all of creation depends utterly upon Him.
6. **Al-Wahdaniyyah (Absolute Oneness):** He is One without partner in His Essence (*Dhat*), His Attributes (*Sifat*), and His Actions (*Af'al*). No other entity creates anything.

### C. Sifat al-Ma'ani (The Qualitative Existential Attributes — 7)
These are eternal, active attributes established with the Divine Essence:
7. **Al-Qudrah (Omnipotence):** Power to bring any possible thing into existence or nonexistence.
8. **Al-Iradah (Divine Will):** Specifying possible things with specific qualities, times, and places.
9. **Al-'Ilm (Omniscience):** Comprehensive knowledge encompassing everything necessary, possible, and impossible without learning or forgetting.
10. **Al-Hayah (Eternal Life):** Life that is uncreated, needing no soul, food, breath, or heart.
11. **Al-Sam' (Hearing):** Hearing all sounds and existences without physical ears or eardrums.
12. **Al-Basar (Sight):** Beholding all existences without physical eyes, pupils, or light rays.
13. **Al-Kalam (Speech):** Eternal divine speech that is without created sound, letters, or vocal chords.

### D. Sifat al-Ma'nawiyyah (The Relational States — 7)
14–20. Being All-Powerful (*Kaunuhu Qadiran*), Willing (*Muridan*), Knowing (*'Aliman*), Living (*Hayyan*), Hearing (*Sami'an*), Seeing (*Basiran*), and Speaking (*Mutakalliman*).

## 3. What is Impossible (*Mustahil*) and Permissible (*Ja'iz*) for Allah?

* **The 20 Impossible Attributes:** The direct opposites of the 20 necessary attributes (e.g. Nonexistence, having a beginning, dying, resembling creation, being powerless, ignorance).
* **The Permissible (Al-Ja'iz):** Creating any possible thing or leaving it uncreated; sending Messengers; rewarding believers by His grace and punishing oppressors by His justice.

---

## 4. Prophetic Infallibility (*'Ismah*) & Prophetology

Messengers are the human conduits of Divine Revelation. Because they represent Allah's moral guidance, they possess four necessary qualities:
1. **Al-Sidq (Truthfulness):** It is impossible for a Prophet to lie in conveying revelation or in daily life.
2. **Al-Amanah (Trustworthiness / 'Ismah):** Complete moral protection from committing major sins (*Kaba'ir*) or disgraceful minor sins (*Sagha'ir*), both before and after receiving prophethood.
3. **Al-Tabligh (Conveyance):** They delivered every single word and instruction commanded by Allah without concealing anything out of fear or favor.
4. **Al-Fatanah (Intellectual Acuity):** Sharp intellect and wisdom capable of presenting proofs and defending the truth against opponents.
