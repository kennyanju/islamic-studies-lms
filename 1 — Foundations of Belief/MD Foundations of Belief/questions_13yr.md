## Teacher Answer Key

1. **C** - Intellect logically proves the Creator, and revelation tells us who He is.
2. **B** - Allah is self-sufficient and doesn't need a creator or space to exist.
3. **C** - Tafwid (affirming the text but delegating the exact meaning to Allah).
4. **C** - An eternal, uncreated attribute of Allah that exists without language/letters/sounds.
5. **A** - If they sinned, Allah would be commanding us to follow sin, which is logically impossible.
6. **C** - Allah creates the action, but the human chooses/intends it and acquires responsibility.
7. **B** - The legal practice of the children of the Sahabah in Madinah.
8. **C** - It is omitted/silent before Al-Fatihah; we start directly with Al-Hamdu lillahi.
9. **Matching Theology:** 1-B, 2-C, 3-D, 4-A
10. **Matching Prophetic Traits:** 1-D, 2-C, 3-A, 4-B
11. **Matching Maliki Fiqh:** 1-C, 2-A, 3-B, 4-D
12. *The student is incorrect because Sadl (praying with hands at the sides) is the primary Sunnah in fard prayers for the Maliki school. Imam Malik based this on the practice of the people of Madinah, which represents the living Sunnah of the Prophet's city. Both Sadl and folding (Qabd) are valid Sunni practices, and we must respect this diversity.*
13. *Kalam Nafsi is the eternal, language-less attribute of Speech in the Divine Essence. Kalam Lafzi is the physical representation (created letters, sounds, paper, ink) that points to the Kalam Nafsi. This distinction is crucial because it resolves the debate of whether the Quran is created or uncreated, upholding that Allah's attribute of Speech is uncreated, while physical representations are created.*
14. *If Prophets were not infallible ('Ismah), we could not trust their guidance, message, or laws, as they could be lying or acting out of self-interest. This would render revelation unreliable.*
15. *No. Writing in Qadar is based on Allah's eternal knowledge of what we WOULD choose. It is a record of our choices, not a force. For example, if a teacher knows a smart student will get an A, and writes it down beforehand, the teacher didn't force the student to study; they just knew the outcome.*
16. *Accept any correct analogy. E.g., The engine of a car provides the power to move (created by Allah), but the driver decides where to steer (Kasb). The driver gets the ticket if they crash, because they chose the direction.*
17. *Imam Malik argued that the public, mass-transmitted practices of the city of Madinah were passed down from thousands of Sahabah to their children. A single written report (Ahad Hadith) has a higher risk of being isolated, misunderstood, or abrogated, whereas the living practice of an entire city cannot be easily fabricated.*
18. *Zainab's error is assuming that Qadar acts without means (Asbab). Allah decrees both the outcome (passing/failing) and the means to get there (studying/slacking). By choosing not to study (Kasb), she acquired the failure. We must take action and use our choices responsibly.*
19. *Space and time are created things. If the Creator lived inside space or time, He would be dependent on His own creation to exist, which violates His attribute of Qiyamuhu bi-Nafsihi (Self-sufficiency). Thus, He is beyond directions and limits.*
20. *It gives peace because it removes self-blame for things out of our control (we know it was decreed and contains hidden wisdom). However, we do not become lazy because we know we are still commanded to act and make good choices (Kasb) in response to the trial.*
