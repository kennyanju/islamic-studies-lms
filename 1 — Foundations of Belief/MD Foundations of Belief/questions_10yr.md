# Islamic Studies Worksheet: 20 Questions
**Age Group:** 10 Years Old (Level 1)
**Focus:** Beliefs (Aqidah) & Practices (Fiqh) - Ash'ari / Maliki Perspective

---

## Part A: Multiple Choice (Circle the correct answer)

### 1. What is the Arabic word for the absolute Oneness and Uniqueness of Allah?
* A) Fiqh
* B) Tawhid (تَوْحِيد)
* C) Iman
* D) Shari'ah

### 2. The Ash'ari creed teaches the concept of *Mukhalafah lil-Hawadith*. What does this mean?
* A) Allah can do anything.
* B) Allah knows everything.
* C) Allah is completely different from created things.
* D) Allah is the Provider.

### 3. Which of the following is a Pillar of **Iman** (Faith), and NOT a physical Pillar of **Islam**?
* A) Giving Zakat (Charity)
* B) Believing in the Angels (Mala'ikah)
* C) Fasting in Ramadan
* D) Performing Hajj

### 4. Which Archangel is responsible for rain and distributing sustenance (*Rizq*) by Allah's command?
* A) Jibril (جِبْرِيل)
* B) Israfil (إِسْرَافِيل)
* C) Mika'il (مِيكَائِيل)
* D) Malik al-Mawt (مَلَكُ الْمَوْت)

### 5. Which Holy Book was revealed to Prophet Dawud (David)?
* A) Tawrah (تَوْرَاة)
* B) Zabur (زَبُور)
* C) Injil (إِنْجِيل)
* D) Quran (قُرْآن)

### 6. What is the preferred position of the arms during fard (obligatory) prayers in the Maliki school of law?
* A) Folded on the chest (*Qabd*)
* B) Resting naturally at the sides (*Sadl*)
* C) Placed on the stomach
* D) Folded behind the back

### 7. If you decide to share your lunch with a classmate, who creates the physical action and who chooses it?
* A) You create the action, and you choose it.
* B) Allah creates the action, and you choose/intend it (*Kasb*).
* C) Nobody chooses it; it is just random.
* D) The angel on your shoulder creates it.

### 8. What does the term *'Ismah* (عِصْمَة) mean when describing the Prophets?
* A) They were very wealthy.
* B) They had the ability to fly.
* C) They were protected by Allah from committing sins (Infallibility).
* D) They only spoke to angels.

4. **C** - Mika'il (Michael)
5. **B** - Zabur (Psalms)
6. **B** - Resting naturally at the sides (*Sadl*)

8. **C** - Protected from committing sins (Infallibility)
9. **Iman**
10. **Quran**

17. *Accept any answer indicating that Allah is the Creator and we are creation; He does not have a physical body, shape, or occupy space; and whatever we imagine, He is not like that.*
18. *The left angel waits for a few hours before writing a sin, giving us time to repent. (Accept answers expressing gratitude or relief).*
19. *Qadar is not an excuse for laziness. Allah knows what we will do, but we still make the choice to study or play. We only know Qadar after it happens.*
20. *It helps me realize that both folding hands and praying with arms at the side are valid Sunnahs from different scholars (like Imam Malik in Madinah) and we must respect diversity.*
