2. List and explain the Six Articles of Faith (Arkan al-Iman).

6. Understand why Imam Malik taught that good character (Adab) precedes academic study.

## Section 1: The Architecture of Faith: The Hadith of Jibril
In a famous Hadith recorded in Sahih Muslim, Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (RA) relates that while the companions were seated with the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, a traveler entered their gathering. He was wearing pure white garments with deep black hair. No signs of travel were visible upon him, yet none of the companions recognized him.

He knelt before the Prophet ﷺ and asked: *"O Muhammad, tell me about Iman (Faith)!"*

The Prophet ﷺ replied that **Iman has Six Pillars**:
1. **Belief in Allah**: His Oneness, absolute perfection, and freedom from all deficiencies.
2. **Belief in the Angels**: Noble beings created from light who carry out divine commands.
3. **Belief in the Revealed Scriptures**: The original books given to the messengers.
4. **Belief in the Messengers**: From Adam to the final messenger, Muhammad ﷺ. Before receiving revelation, the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was known across Makkah by the title **Al-Amin** (The Trustworthy) because he never lied or betrayed a trust.
5. **Belief in the Last Day**: The Resurrection, the divine scale of deeds, and eternal life in Paradise.
6. **Belief in Divine Destiny (Al-Qadar)**: That everything in the universe unfolds by Allah's eternal knowledge, will, and wisdom.

### Adab Before Knowledge: A Golden Lesson from Imam Malik
Imam Malik ibn Anas (may Allah have mercy on him), the great scholar of Madinah, remembered that when he was young, his wise mother dressed him in student robes and said: *"Go to your teacher Rabi'ah, and learn his good manners (Adab) before learning his knowledge."* In Islam, beautiful character, humility, and respect for teachers and parents are the vessels that hold true knowledge.

## Section 2: Divine Transcendence (Tanzih) in Sunni Creed
In the Sunni theological tradition formulated by Imam Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari, we describe Allah with the attributes of majesty that He revealed, while upholding **Tanzih** (exalting Allah above created limits, physical shapes, or human deficiencies).

| Attribute | Arabic Term | Meaning in Sunni Creed |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Beginningless Existence** | *Al-Qidam* | Allah has always existed; there was never a point when He did not exist. |
| **Endless Permanence** | *Al-Baqa'* | Allah is eternal and will never cease to exist. |
| **Absolute Dissimilarity** | *Mukhalafah lil-Hawadith* | Allah does not resemble created things, possesses no physical body parts, and does not occupy space or change over time (Surah Ash-Shura 42:11). |
| **Self-Subsistence** | *Al-Qiyam bin-Nafs* | Allah needs neither location, physical direction, nor a creator. He is completely independent, while creation depends on Him. |

### The Consensus of the Four Sunni Imams on Transcendence
All four foundational Imams of Sunni Islam agreed upon the principle of Tanzih:
* **Imam Abu Hanifah** (d. 150 AH) wrote in *Al-Fiqh al-Absat*: Allah existed eternally before any place existed, and He is now as He ever was, without place or change.
* **Imam Malik ibn Anas** (d. 179 AH) and the scholars of creed taught that Allah created both space and time, and that Allah does not resemble anything in His creation (Surah Ash-Shura 42:11). As the scholars of creed summarized: *"Whatever comes to your mind, Allah is different from that."*
* **Imam al-Shafi'i** (d. 204 AH) and **Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal** (d. 241 AH) affirmed that Allah is exalted above limits, bodily form, and physical boundaries.

## Section 3: The Heavenly Scriptures & The Final Miracle
Allah sent divine revelations through Angel Jibril to guide humanity to truth, justice, and worship:
* **The Suhuf (Scrolls)**: Granted to Prophet Ibrahim (Abraham).
* **The Tawrah (Torah)**: The original scripture revealed to Prophet Musa (Moses).
* **The Zabur (Psalms)**: Revealed to Prophet Dawud (David).
* **The Injil (Gospel)**: The original scripture given to Prophet 'Isa (Jesus).
* **The Glorious Qur'an**: The final, unchangeable, perfectly preserved Speech of Allah revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

While earlier communities were tasked with guarding their own scriptures, Allah took upon Himself the direct preservation of the Noble Qur'an:
> "Indeed, it is We who sent down the Qur'an, and indeed, We will surely guard it." (Surah Al-Hijr 15:9)

## Section 4: The Concept of Ihsan: Living with God-Consciousness
When Angel Jibril asked the Prophet ﷺ: *"Tell me about Ihsan!"*, the Prophet ﷺ answered:
> "It is to worship Allah as though you see Him; and if you do not see Him, know that He surely sees you." (Sahih Muslim 8, Sahih; 40 Hadith Nawawi, Hadith 2)

* **Muraqabah (Inner Awareness)**: When you are alone in your room, you choose what is right and speak the truth because you know Allah is watching over you with complete awareness.
* **Excellence in Action**: Ihsan means performing every task with care, sincerity, and thoroughness, whether studying, helping around the house, or performing your daily prayers.

## Section 5: Curriculum Anchors in Practice

### 5.1 Qur'an and Tafsir Anchor: Surah Al-A'la (87:1-5)
> **سَبِّحِ اسْمَ رَبِّكَ الْأَعْلَى ۝ الَّذِي خَلَقَ فَسَوَّىٰ ۝ وَالَّذِي قَدَّرَ فَهَدَىٰ**  
> "Glorify the name of your Lord, the Most High, Who created and proportioned, and Who determined and guided." (Surah Al-A'la 87:1-3)

**Tafsir Insights**: In this Surah, Allah commands us to glorify His Name above all imperfections. The name *Al-A'la* (The Most High) refers to Allah's supreme status, majesty, and power, not to a physical location in space.

### 5.2 Tajwid Rule in Action: Ghunnah and Heavy Letters
* **Ghunnah**: A nasal sound held for two counts when pronouncing a Nun or Mim with a Shaddah (`نّ`, `مّ`).
* **Heavy Letters (Huruf al-Isti'la')**: Seven Arabic letters are pronounced with a full, elevated tongue: `خ, ص, ض, غ, ط, ق, ظ`. Notice the full sound of the letter *Qaf* (`ق`) in *Al-Khaliq*.

### 5.3 Classical Text Anchor: Matn al-'Ashmawiyyah
> "The first obligation upon a morally responsible person is to know Allah, His messengers, and what has reached us of the religion." (*Matn al-'Ashmawiyyah*, Introduction).

**Explanation**: Classical Maliki manuals place understanding of faith at the very beginning of a student's education, because correct faith guides every action that follows.

### 5.4 Prophetic Hadith Anchor: Hadith 2 of An-Nawawi (Iman & Ihsan)
> "He said: 'Then tell me about Ihsan.' He replied: 'It is to worship Allah as though you see Him; and if you do not see Him, know that He sees you.'" (Sahih Muslim 8, Sahih; 40 Hadith Nawawi, Hadith 2)

**Living the Hadith**: Before starting any good action today, pause for three seconds and remind yourself that Allah sees your effort and rewards your good intention.

## Summary and Key Takeaways
* Iman has Six Pillars: belief in Allah, His Angels, His Books, His Messengers, the Last Day, and Divine Destiny (Qadar).
* The Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was known as Al-Amin (The Trustworthy) before receiving divine revelation.
* Tanzih means recognizing that Allah does not resemble creation, has no physical body, and needs no location.
* Imam Malik emphasized that students must cultivate good manners (Adab) before seeking advanced knowledge.
* Ihsan means worshiping Allah with the deep awareness that He sees every action and intention.
