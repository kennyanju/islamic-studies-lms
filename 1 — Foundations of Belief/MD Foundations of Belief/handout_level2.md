## 🌟 1. The Architecture of Faith: The Hadith of Jibril
In a famous Hadith recorded in *Sahih Muslim*, Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab relates that one day, while the companions were sitting with the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, a man appeared wearing extraordinarily white clothes and with jet-black hair. No signs of travel were on him, yet none of the companions recognized him!

He knelt before the Prophet ﷺ and asked: *"O Muhammad, tell me about Iman (Faith)!"*  
The Prophet ﷺ replied that **Iman has Six Pillars**:

## 🛡️ 2. Divine Transcendence (Tanzih) in the Ash'ari Creed
In the Sunni theological tradition formulated by Imam Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari, we learn to describe Allah with all the attributes of majesty that He revealed, while upholding **Tanzih** (keeping Allah transcendent above human or physical limitations).

### The Four Foundational Negative Attributes (Al-Sifat al-Salbiyyah):
1. **Al-Qidam (Beginningless Existence):** Allah has always existed; there was never a time when He was not.
2. **Al-Baqa' (Endless Permanence):** Allah will never perish or pass away; He is eternal.
3. **Mukhalafah lil-Hawadith (Absolute Dissimilarity to Creation):**  
   Allah is not a physical object, does not have physical body parts, does not occupy space, and does not change over time. As Allah says in the Qur'an:  
   > **لَيْسَ كَمِثْلِهِۦ شَىْءٌۭ ۖ وَهُوَ ٱلسَّمِيعُ ٱلْبَصِيرُ**  
   > *"There is nothing whatever like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearing, the All-Seeing!"* (Surah Ash-Shura 42:11).
4. **Al-Qiyam bin-Nafs (Self-Sufficiency):** Allah needs neither place, space, food, nor a creator. He is independent, while all of creation is entirely dependent on Him.

## 📖 3. The Heavenly Scriptures & The Final Miracle
Allah sent divine scriptures through Angel Jibril to guide humanity to truth and justice:
* **The Suhuf (Scrolls):** Given to Prophet Ibrahim (Abraham).
* **The Tawrah (Torah):** The original revelation given to Prophet Musa (Moses).
* **The Zabur (Psalms):** Revealed to Prophet Dawud (David).
* **The Injeel (Gospel):** The original revelation given to Prophet 'Isa (Jesus).
* **The Glorious Qur'an:** The final, unchangeable, perfectly preserved Speech of Allah revealed to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

While earlier scriptures were entrusted to human keepers and altered over time, Allah made a direct divine promise regarding the Holy Qur'an:  
> **إِنَّا نَحْنُ نَزَّلْنَا ٱلذِّكْرَ وَإِنَّا لَهُۥ لَحَٰفِظُونَ**  
> *"Indeed, it is We who sent down the Qur'an and indeed, We will surely guard it!"* (Surah Al-Hijr 15:9).

## 🌿 4. The Concept of Ihsan: Living with God-Consciousness
When Angel Jibril asked: *"Tell me about Ihsan!"*, the Prophet ﷺ gave this golden definition:  
> **أَنْ تَعْبُدَ ٱللَّٰهَ كَأَنَّكَ تَرَاهُ فَإِنْ لَمْ تَكُنْ تَرَاهُ فَإِنَّهُ يَرَاكَ**  
> *"It is to worship Allah as though you see Him; and if you do not see Him, know that He surely sees you!"* (Hadith 2 of An-Nawawi).

* **Muraqabah (Inner Awareness):** When you are in your room and nobody is watching, you still speak the truth and avoid doing wrong because your heart knows Allah is watching over you with love and care.
* **Excellence in Action:** Ihsan means that whether you are doing your homework, helping your parents, or performing your prayer, you do it with beauty, care, and your absolute best effort!

## 💡 Key Takeaways for Level 2:
1. **Iman has 6 Pillars:** Allah, Angels, Books, Messengers, Day of Judgment, and Divine Decree (Qadar).
2. **Tanzih:** Allah is unlike anything created; He does not live in a location, have a physical body, or need anything.
3. **The 4 Sunni Imams:** Abu Hanifah, Malik, Al-Shafi'i, and Ahmad all agreed that Allah is transcendent and unique.
4. **Ihsan:** Worshiping and living with the loving awareness that Allah sees and knows all that we do.
