# Module 1: Foundations of Belief (Level 5 Assessment)

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
*Select the single best answer for each question.*

**1. Which of the following accurately defines the three legitimate sources of knowledge (Asbab al-'Ilm) in classical Sunni epistemology?**
(A) Secular consensus, personal emotion, and philosophical intuition
(B) Unrestricted skepticism, ancestral mythology, and private dreams
(C) Laboratory measurement, speculative fiction, and political legislation
(D) Sound Senses (Al-Hiss al-Salim), Pure Reason (Al-'Aql al-Sarih), and Veracious Report (Al-Khabar al-Sadiq)

**2. Why is the philosophical ideology of "Scientism" logically self-defeating?**
(A) Because experimental equipment is expensive and subject to mechanical error
(B) Because the foundational claim "only empirical science provides truth" cannot itself be empirically verified or measured
(C) Because scientific papers are published across many languages
(D) Because scientific theories change and evolve across generations

**3. In the Kalam Cosmological Argument (Dalil al-Huduth), why is an "actual infinite regress of past temporal events" impossible?**
(A) Because if an infinite series of prior temporal moments had to elapse first, time could never arrive at the present moment
(B) Because stars deplete their nuclear fuel over billions of years
(C) Because astronomical calendars have a finite number of digits
(D) Because human memory cannot recall ancient history

**4. What crucial deduction is made about the Creator from the fact that spacetime, matter, and energy had an absolute beginning?**
(A) The Creator must dwell within the physical boundaries of the cosmos
(B) The Creator must be composed of uncreated physical light particles
(C) The Creator must necessarily be transcendent of space, timeless, immaterial, and supremely powerful
(D) The Creator must be subject to the physical laws of entropy

**5. How does the Ash'ari doctrine of Kasb (Acquisition) differ fundamentally from the Jabriyyah position?**
(A) Kasb denies that Allah has pre-eternal knowledge of human choices
(B) Kasb claims that human beings independently create the physical laws of nature
(C) The Jabriyyah affirmed human free will, whereas Kasb rejects moral responsibility
(D) The Jabriyyah claimed humans have zero choice (fatalism), whereas Kasb affirms intentional moral choice (Qasd) as the ground of accountability

**6. What major theological error did the Mu'tazilah commit regarding human actions?**
(A) They asserted that humans independently create their actions from nothing, introducing multiple independent creators alongside Allah
(B) They claimed that prayer was only obligatory once a month
(C) They denied that the prophets were historical human beings
(D) They rejected the use of logic in understanding divine revelation

**7. In Sunni theodicy, how is the existence of worldly suffering reconciled with Divine Mercy and Wisdom?**
(A) By claiming that Allah is unaware of suffering until humans call upon Him
(B) By understanding that Earth is a transient testing realm (Dar al-Imtihan), where suffering tests virtues, purifies souls, and is infinitely redressed in the Hereafter
(C) By asserting that evil is an independent divine force opposing the Creator
(D) By arguing that physical suffering is merely an optical illusion that has no reality

**8. Which Qur'anic narrative in Surah Al-Kahf serves as the primary theological example for understanding hidden divine wisdom behind apparent tragedy?**
(A) The account of Prophet Yunus inside the whale
(B) The dialogue between Prophet Yusuf and his fellow prisoners
(C) The journey of Prophet Musa with the righteous servant Al-Khidr
(D) The account of Prophet Sulayman commanding the wind

**9. Why does Moral Relativism fail to provide a binding foundation for universal human dignity and moral duties?**
(A) Because ancient legal treaties were inscribed upon stone tablets
(B) Because linguistic vocabulary differs across international borders
(C) Because international diplomacy requires majority consensus
(D) Because without an objective, transcendent Divine Lawgiver, moral values reduce to subjective preferences or majority power

**10. What did Imam al-Ghazali demonstrate in *Tahafut al-Falasifah* regarding natural causality?**
(A) That observed physical correlation (such as fire touching cotton) does not prove that the physical fire is the ultimate independent creator of burning
(B) That empirical medicine is invalid and believers should refuse medical treatment
(C) That the natural physical universe has no discernible order or predictability
(D) That human senses can never be trusted in observing natural phenomena

4. C - The Cause of physical spacetime and matter must necessarily be immaterial, timeless, and supremely powerful (Handout Section 2).
5. D - Kasb preserves human moral agency without compromising Allah's exclusive power of origination (Handout Section 3).
6. A - The Mu'tazilah compromised absolute Tawheed by positing humans as independent creators of their acts (Handout Section 3).
