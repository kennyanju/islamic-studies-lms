## 🌟 Welcome, Little Believer!
Have you ever looked up at the deep blue sky, watched a shiny butterfly flutter from flower to flower, or listened to the gentle pitter-patter of rain on your window? 

When you see a beautiful drawing, you know an artist painted it. When you see a delicious warm honey muffin, you know a baker baked it. When we look at this big, wonderful world, our hearts know that **Allah is its Maker!**

Allah is **Al-Khaliq** (The Creator). He made the sun to give us warm light, the tall green trees to give us sweet fruit, and He gave us our beating hearts, bright eyes, and loving families!


## 🤲 Page 1: The Golden Promise of Faith — Tawheed & Shahaadah
The most precious treasure in our hearts is **Tawheed** (تَوْحِيد). Tawheed means knowing that **Allah is One and Unique!**

* Allah has no partners, no mom, no dad, and no children. 
* He never gets tired, He never sleeps, and He never needs to eat.
* He created all of us because He is full of loving kindness.

Every day, we say our golden promise:
> **أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ**  
> *"Ash-hadu an la ilaha illa Allah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan Rasulullah"*  
> (I bear witness that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah!)

### The Golden Rule of Our Creed:
Imam Malik and the great scholars taught us this rule:
> *"Whatever pops into your head or your imagination, Allah is not like that!"*  
Allah is far greater, more majestic, and more pure than anything a person can imagine or draw!

## 🏛️ Page 3: The Five Beautiful Pillars of Islam
Our religion of Islam is like a strong, shining palace held up by **Five Pillars**:
1. **The Shahaadah**: Believing with all our heart that Allah is One and Muhammad is His Messenger ﷺ.
2. **Salah (Prayer)**: Talking to Allah five times every day with clean clothes and a peaceful heart.
3. **Zakah (Giving)**: Sharing some of our wealth with poor and needy families so everyone is happy.
4. **Sawm (Fasting)**: Practicing self-control during the blessed month of Ramadan from dawn until sunset.
5. **Hajj (Pilgrimage)**: Visiting the holy Ka'bah in Makkah once in our lifetime if we are able.

## 🎨 Page 5: My Daily Sunnah Challenge:
1. **Look out the window:** Spot three beautiful things Allah made (like a cloud, a bird, or a tree) and say: *"SubhanAllah!"* (Glory be to Allah).
2. **Spread peace:** Look at your mother, father, brother, or sister, smile warmly, and say: *"As-Salamu 'Alaykum!"* (Peace be upon you).
3. **Say thank you:** Eat a delicious fruit today and say: *"Alhamdulillah!"* (All praise belongs to Allah).
