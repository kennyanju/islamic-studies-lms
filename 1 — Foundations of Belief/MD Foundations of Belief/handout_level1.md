## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:
1. Explain that Allah is Al-Khaliq (The Creator) Who made everything in the heavens and the earth.
2. State the golden promise of faith: the Shahaadah (testimony of faith).
3. Name the three judgements of character and list the Five Pillars of Islam.
4. Name the angels of pure light (Jibril, Mika'il, and the guardian angels).
5. Recite Surah Al-Ikhlas with understanding and apply daily Sunnah manners.

## Section 1: Welcome to the Wonder of Creation
Have you ever looked up at the deep blue sky, watched a butterfly flutter between flowers, or listened to the gentle rain tapping on your window?

When you see a colorful painting, you know an artist painted it. When you taste fresh, warm bread, you know a baker prepared it. When we look at this vast, orderly world, our hearts know that Allah is its Maker and Sustainer.

Allah is Al-Khaliq (The Creator). He made the sun to give us warm light, the tall trees to produce sweet fruit, and the ocean for creatures of all shapes. He gave each of us a beating heart, bright eyes, two ears, and a caring family. Whenever we see beauty in nature, our hearts remember Allah and we say: **SubhanAllah** (Glory be to Allah).

---

## Section 2: The Golden Promise of Faith (Tawheed & Shahaadah)
The most precious treasure in our hearts is **Tawheed** (تَوْحِيد). Tawheed means knowing and declaring that Allah is One, Unique, and without partner.

* Allah has no partners, no parents, and no children.
* He never grows tired, He never sleeps, and He never needs food or drink.
* He created everything out of His infinite Wisdom and Mercy.

Every day, Muslims declare their faith through the golden words of the Shahaadah:

> **أَشْهَدُ أَنْ لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا ٱللَّٰهُ وَأَشْهَدُ أَنَّ مُحَمَّدًا رَسُولُ ٱللَّٰهِ**  
> *"Ash-hadu an la ilaha illa Allah, wa ash-hadu anna Muhammadan Rasulullah"*  
> (I bear witness that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah, and I bear witness that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah!)

### The Golden Rule of Our Creed:
The great Sunni scholars of creed taught us a golden guiding rule:
> *"Whatever comes to your mind or imagination, Allah is completely different from that!"* (Attributed to Dhu al-Nun al-Misri and Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal; cited by al-Qushayri).

Allah is greater, more majestic, and more pure than anything a human being can imagine or draw. We never draw pictures or imagine shapes of Allah, because nothing in creation is like Him (Surah Ash-Shura 42:11).

## Section 3: Allah's Helpers: The Angels of Pure Light
Allah created the noble **Angels** (*Mala'ikah*) from pure light (*Nur*). They love Allah, carry out His commands perfectly, and never disobey.

| Angel | Noble Duty |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Angel Jibril (Gabriel)** | The leader of the angels who brought the Qur'an and revelation to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and all the prophets. |
| **Angel Mika'il (Michael)** | Entrusted by Allah with the rainfall that causes plants and crops to grow. |
| **Guardian Angels (Al-Kiram al-Katibin)** | Stay near us, protect us by Allah's leave, and write down our good deeds. |

When you say *"Bismillah"*, smile at your parents, or share your toys with your sibling, the angels record your good deeds in your book of blessings.

---

## Section 4: The Five Pillars of Islam
Our religion of Islam is like a strong building supported by **Five Pillars**:

1. **The Shahaadah**: Believing with full conviction that Allah is One and Muhammad is His Messenger ﷺ.
2. **Salah (Prayer)**: Speaking directly to Allah in prayer five times every day with clean clothes and a focused heart.
3. **Zakah (Giving)**: Sharing a portion of savings with families in need once every year.
4. **Sawm (Fasting)**: Abstaining from food and drink during daylight hours in the blessed month of Ramadan.
5. **Hajj (Pilgrimage)**: Journeying to the sacred Ka'bah in Makkah once in a lifetime for those who are able.

## Section 5: Curriculum Anchors in Practice

### 5.1 Qur'an and Tafsir Anchor: Surah Al-Ikhlas (112:1-4)
> **بِسْمِ اللَّهِ الرَّحْمَٰنِ الرَّحِيمِ**  
> **قُلْ هُوَ اللَّهُ أَحَدٌ ۝ اللَّهُ الصَّمَدُ ۝ لَمْ يَلِدْ وَلَمْ يُولَدْ ۝ وَلَمْ يَكُن لَّهُ كُفُوًا أَحَدٌ**  
> *Qul Huwa Allahu Ahad. Allahus-Samad. Lam yalid wa lam yulad. Wa lam yakun lahu kufuwan ahad.*  
> "Say: He is Allah, the One! Allah, the Eternal Refuge! He neither begets nor is born, nor is there to Him any equivalent." (Surah Al-Ikhlas 112:1-4)

**Tafsir Insights**: This short chapter teaches us pure Tawheed. The word *Al-Ahad* means Allah is perfectly One without parts or partners. *Al-Samad* means every created thing depends on Allah, while Allah needs nothing from anyone.

### 5.2 Tajwid Rule in Action: Clear Pronunciation of Basmalah
When reading the Qur'an, we begin with:
* **Al-Isti'adhah**: *A'udhu billahi min ash-shaytan ir-rajim* (seeking refuge with Allah from the expelled devil).
* **Al-Basmalah**: *Bismillah ir-Rahman ir-Rahim* (in the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful).
* **Pronunciation Tip**: Pronounce each Arabic letter gently and clearly from its correct exit point (*Makhraj*) without rushing.

### 5.3 Classical Text Anchor: 'Aqidat al-'Awamm
> "We begin with the name of Allah, the Entirely Merciful, the Especially Merciful, and all praise belongs to Allah, the Eternal Who has no beginning." ('Aqidat al-'Awamm, Lines 1-2).

**Explanation**: Classical teachers across generations have started their lessons with these lines to remind young students that all blessings come from Allah.

### 5.4 Prophetic Hadith Anchor: Hadith Jibril
> "Islam is that you bear witness that there is no god worthy of worship except Allah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah, that you establish prayer, give Zakah, fast Ramadan, and make the pilgrimage to the House if you are able." (Sahih Muslim 8, Sahih; 40 Hadith Nawawi, Hadith 2)

**Living the Hadith**: Practice your daily prayers on time and make it a habit to say *Bismillah* before eating and *Alhamdulillah* after finishing.

## Summary and Key Takeaways
* Allah is Al-Khaliq, the Creator of the heavens, the earth, and everything within them.
* Tawheed means knowing that Allah is One, unique, and does not resemble anything in creation.
* Islam is built upon Five Pillars: Shahaadah, Salah, Zakah, Sawm, and Hajj.
* The angels are created from light and always obey Allah's commands.
