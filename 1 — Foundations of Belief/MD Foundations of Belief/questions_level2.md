## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
*Select the single best answer for each question.*

**1. How many Pillars of Iman (Faith) did the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ define in the Hadith of Jibril?**
(A) 5 Pillars
(B) 6 Pillars
(C) 7 Pillars
(D) 4 Pillars

**2. Which Arabic theological concept expresses that Allah is completely different from all created things and has no physical body or limits?**
(A) Wudu
(B) Qiblah
(C) Istinja'
(D) Mukhalafah lil-Hawadith

**3. Which divine scripture was revealed to Prophet Dawud (David)?**
(A) The Zabur (Psalms)
(B) The Injil (Gospel)
(C) The Tawrah (Torah)
(D) The Suhuf (Scrolls)

**4. Which noble angel is entrusted by Allah with blowing the Trumpet on the Day of Resurrection?**
(A) Angel Jibril
(B) Angel Mika'il
(C) Angel Israfil
(D) Angel Ridwan

**5. What makes the Noble Qur'an unique compared to all previous divine scriptures?**
(A) It was revealed in a single day
(B) It is the final, perfectly preserved Speech of Allah protected by divine promise from all alteration
(C) It was written down only after centuries of oral transmission
(D) It was addressed exclusively to one specific desert tribe

**6. What did Imam Malik's mother advise him when sending him to study sacred knowledge?**
(A) To argue and debate with other students
(B) To rush through books without understanding them
(C) To ignore manners and focus entirely on memorizing facts
(D) To learn good manners (Adab) from his teacher Rabi'ah before learning his knowledge
