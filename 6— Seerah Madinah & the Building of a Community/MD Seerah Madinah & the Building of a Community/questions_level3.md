# Level 3 Quiz: Seerah: Madinah & Building a Community

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. Why did the early companions choose the Hijrah (Migration) as the starting year of the Islamic calendar?
   (A) Because it marked the Prophet's birthday.
   (B) Because it marked the historic transition of Islam from persecution to a sovereign, thriving community.
   (C) Because it was the hottest summer recorded in Arabia.
   (D) Because the Roman empire surrendered that year.

2. Who courageously volunteered to sleep in the Prophet's bed on the night of the planned assassination in Makkah?
   (A) Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq.
   (B) Sayyidina 'Ali ibn Abi Talib.
   (C) Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab.
   (D) Sayyidina 'Uthman ibn 'Affan.

3. What serene reassurance did the Prophet ﷺ give to Abu Bakr when trackers stood outside Cave Thawr?
   (A) "Run out into the desert as fast as you can!"
   (B) "What do you think of two with whom Allah is their third?"
   (C) "Draw your sword and attack them immediately!"
   (D) "Offer them fifty bags of gold to go away!"

4. What astonishing prophecy did the Prophet ﷺ make to the bounty hunter Suraqah ibn Malik?
   (A) That Suraqah would become the king of Rome.
   (B) That Suraqah would one day wear the golden bracelets of the Emperor of Persia (Chosroes).
   (C) That Suraqah would discover an emerald mine in Yemen.
   (D) That Suraqah would build thirty ships in the Mediterranean.

5. What was the name of the first mosque founded by the Prophet ﷺ on the outskirts of Madinah?
   (A) The Blue Mosque.
   (B) Masjid Quba'.
   (C) The Umayyad Mosque.
   (D) Masjid al-Aqsa.

6. How did the Prophet ﷺ impartially resolve which clan would host his residence in Madinah?
   (A) By holding a public archery competition.
   (B) By allowing his camel Al-Qaswa' to choose her resting place freely under divine direction.
   (C) By selecting the wealthiest palace in the city.
   (D) By casting lots with stones.

7. Who was the blessed companion whose home hosted the Prophet ﷺ during the construction of the Prophet's Mosque?
   (A) Abu Jahl.
   (B) Abu Ayyub al-Ansari.
   (C) Abu Sufyan.
   (D) Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr.

8. In the Fraternal Pact (Al-Mu'akhah), what noble response did 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf give when offered half of his Ansar brother's wealth?
   (A) He demanded double the amount in gold coins.
   (B) "May Allah bless your wealth; simply show me the way to the marketplace!"
   (C) He accepted and retired from working forever.
   (D) He purchased thirty racing horses.

9. Which companion was shown the exact words of the Adhan in a truthful dream?
   (A) Sayyidina 'Abdullah ibn Zayd.
   (B) Sayyidina Khalid ibn al-Walid.
   (C) Sayyidina Salman al-Farsi.
   (D) Sayyidina Abu Hurairah.

10. Why did the Prophet ﷺ instruct 'Abdullah ibn Zayd to teach the Adhan to Sayyidina Bilal?
    (A) Because Bilal was the oldest man in Madinah.
    (B) Because Bilal possessed a deeper, more resonant, and beautiful voice.
    (C) Because Bilal was a skilled horseman.
    (D) Because Bilal had memorized the Torah.

3. The divinely guided camel of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was named ______.
4. The sacred institutional pact of brotherhood between Muhajirun and Ansar was ______.
5. The first Mu'adhin of Islam renowned for his powerful and soulful voice was Sayyidina ______.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Cave Thawr ─── The 3-day sanctuary where Abu Bakr and the Prophet ﷺ hid
* Masjid Quba' ─── The first mosque in Islam built upon the foundation of Taqwa
* Al-Qaswa' ─── The Prophet's camel whose kneeling selected the mosque location
* Al-Mu'akhah ─── The fraternal brotherhood established between Muhajirun and Ansar
* Sayyidina Bilal ─── The noble Ethiopian companion appointed as first Mu'adhin

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. How does the Prophet's plan during the Hijrah (using a guide, taking a southern detour, hiding in a cave) teach Muslims the balance between reliance on Allah (*Tawakkul*) and meticulous practical preparation?
2. Analyze the economic ethic displayed by 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf when he asked: *"Show me the way to the marketplace!"*
3. What civic lessons can contemporary communities learn from the Ansar's radical hospitality and selflessness toward refugees?
4. Why did the Prophet ﷺ choose the human voice (the Adhan) to announce prayer rather than mechanical horns or bells?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - The Hijrah marked the birth of an independent Islamic society, making it the calendar baseline.
2. B - Sayyidina 'Ali slept in the bed to return trusts (*Amanat*) and mislead the assassins.
3. B - "Ma zannuka ya Aba Bakrin bi-ithnayn Allahu thalithuhuma" demonstrates ultimate Tawakkul.
4. B - Prophecy of Chosroes' bracelets came to pass when Persia fell under 'Umar's caliphate.
5. B - Masjid Quba' was the first mosque founded upon arrival in the Madinan territory.
6. B - The camel Al-Qaswa' was divinely guided, avoiding tribal jealousies among the clans.
7. B - Abu Ayyub al-Ansari had the honor of hosting the Prophet on his ground floor.
8. B - 'Abd al-Rahman exemplified self-reliance, enterprise, and dignified work ethics.
9. A - 'Abdullah ibn Zayd's dream was confirmed by the Prophet as divine inspiration.
10. B - The Prophet recognized Bilal's vocal beauty and projection for public assembly.
