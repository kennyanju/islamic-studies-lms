# Level 3 Quiz: Seerah: Madinah & Building a Community

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. Why did the companions choose the Hijrah as the starting year of the Islamic calendar?
   (A) Because it was the hottest summer recorded in Arabia.
   (B) Because it coincided with the Prophet's birth in Makkah.
   (C) Because it marked the conquest of Damascus.
   (D) Because it marked the historic transition from persecution to a sovereign, thriving Muslim community.

2. Who courageously volunteered to sleep in the Prophet's bed on the night of the planned assassination in Makkah to mislead Quraysh and return people's trusts (*Amanat*)?
   (A) Sayyidina 'Ali ibn Abi Talib.
   (B) Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq.
   (C) Sayyidina 'Uthman ibn 'Affan.
   (D) Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab.

3. What serene reassurance did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ give to Abu Bakr when enemy trackers stood right outside the entrance of Cave Thawr?
   (A) "Run out into the desert as fast as you can!"
   (B) "What do you think of two people with whom Allah is the Third?"
   (C) "Offer them all our gold coins to leave us alone."
   (D) "Draw your sword and charge against them immediately."

4. What prophecy did the Prophet ﷺ make to the pursuing bounty hunter Suraqah ibn Malik?
   (A) That Suraqah would become governor of Makkah.
   (B) That Suraqah would build thirty ships on the Red Sea.
   (C) That Suraqah would one day wear the golden arm bracelets of Chosroes (Emperor of Persia).
   (D) That Suraqah would discover gold mines in Oman.

5. What was the name of the first mosque founded by the Prophet ﷺ on the outskirts of Madinah upon his arrival?
   (A) Masjid Quba'.
   (B) Masjid al-Aqsa.
   (C) The Umayyad Mosque.
   (D) Masjid al-Haram.

6. How was the location for Al-Masjid an-Nabawi determined impartially without sparking clan rivalries?
   (A) Through an archery contest among the youths.
   (B) By choosing the home of the wealthiest merchant.
   (C) By rolling dice made of carved date stones.
   (D) By letting the Prophet's camel, Al-Qaswa', move freely until she knelt by divine command.

7. Who was the blessed companion who had the honor of hosting the Prophet ﷺ in his home while the Prophet's Mosque was being built?
   (A) Abu Jahl.
   (B) Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr.
   (C) Abu Ayyub al-Ansari.
   (D) Sa'd ibn Mu'adh.

8. In the Fraternal Pact (Al-Mu'akhah), what dignified reply did 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf give when offered half of his Ansari brother's wealth?
   (A) He demanded all the orchards in Madinah.
   (B) "May Allah bless your family and wealth; simply show me the way to the marketplace!"
   (C) He accepted the money and retired permanently from commerce.
   (D) He asked for a large share of gold dirhams.

9. Which companion saw the complete wording of the Adhan in a truthful dream confirmed by the Prophet ﷺ?
   (A) Sayyidina Khalid ibn al-Walid.
   (B) Sayyidina Salman al-Farsi.
   (C) Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah.
   (D) Sayyidina 'Abdullah ibn Zayd.

10. During prayer in Rajab 2 AH at Masjid al-Qiblatayn, to which direction was the Qiblah permanently shifted by divine command (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:144)?
    (A) From Bayt al-Maqdis (Jerusalem) toward the Ka'bah (Al-Masjid al-Haram in Makkah).
    (B) From Makkah toward Yemen.
    (C) From Madinah toward Mount Sinai.
    (D) From Damascus toward Alexandria.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Cave Thawr ─── The 3-day sanctuary where Abu Bakr and the Prophet ﷺ hid
* Masjid Quba' ─── The first mosque in Islam built upon the foundation of Taqwa
* Al-Qaswa' ─── The Prophet's camel whose kneeling selected the mosque location
* Al-Mu'akhah ─── The fraternal brotherhood established between Muhajirun and Ansar
* Sayyidina Bilal ─── The noble companion appointed as the first Mu'adhin

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. How does the Prophet's plan during the Hijrah (using a guide, taking a southern detour, hiding in a cave) teach Muslims the balance between reliance on Allah (*Tawakkul*) and meticulous practical preparation (*Al-Akhdh bil-Asbab*)?
2. Analyze the economic ethic displayed by 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf when he asked: *"Show me the way to the marketplace!"*
3. What civic lessons can contemporary communities learn from the Ansar's radical hospitality and selflessness toward refugees?
4. Why did the Prophet ﷺ choose the human voice (the Adhan) to announce prayer rather than mechanical horns or bells?

## Answer Key
1. D - The Hijrah marked the birth of an independent Islamic society, making it the calendar baseline.
2. A - Sayyidina 'Ali slept in the bed to return trusts (*Amanat*) and mislead the assassins.
3. B - "Ma zannuka ya Aba Bakrin bi-ithnayn Allahu thalithuhuma" demonstrates ultimate Tawakkul.
4. C - Prophecy of Chosroes' bracelets came to pass when Persia fell under 'Umar's caliphate.
5. A - Masjid Quba' was the first mosque founded upon arrival in the Madinan territory.
6. D - The camel Al-Qaswa' was divinely guided, avoiding tribal jealousies among the clans.
7. C - Abu Ayyub al-Ansari had the honor of hosting the Prophet on his ground floor.
8. B - 'Abd al-Rahman exemplified self-reliance, enterprise, and dignified work ethics.
9. D - 'Abdullah ibn Zayd's dream was confirmed by the Prophet as divine inspiration.
10. A - The Qiblah was redirected from Jerusalem toward the Ka'bah in Surah Al-Baqarah 2:144.
