# Level 3 Student Handout: Seerah: Madinah & Building a Community
**Age Group:** Ages 10–12 (Level 3 Track: Years 5 & 6)  
**Topics:** The Miraculous Hijrah, Building the Prophet's Mosque, The Fraternal Pact (Al-Mu'akhah), The Call to Prayer, and The Change of the Qiblah  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah* of Ibn Hisham & *Zad al-Ma'ad* of Ibn al-Qayyim  
**Riwayah & Tajweed:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 30: Surah Al-Mutaffifin to At-Tariq; Tajweed Drills)

---

## 1. The Great Migration: From Persecution to Sanctuary
The **Hijrah** from Makkah to Madinah (622 CE) was not a flight of fear; it was a masterclass in divine reliance (*Tawakkul*) combined with meticulous planning. The Hijrah was so momentous that the Sahabah later chose it as the starting milestone of the Islamic calendar!

| Event | Strategic Action | Divine Blessing & Protection |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Night Siege** | Quraysh surrounded the Prophet's home with assassins. Sayyidina 'Ali slept in his bed to return trusts (*Amanat*). | The Prophet ﷺ walked through them reciting Surah Yasin (36:9) unnoticed. |
| **Cave Thawr** | The Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr traveled south and hid for three days. | Trackers reached the mouth, but Allah protected them with calm trust (*Sakinah*) and unseen aid (Surah At-Tawbah 9:40). |
| **Suraqah's Pursuit** | A bounty hunter pursued them for a 100-camel reward. | The horse's hooves sank into the sand; the Prophet ﷺ prophesied Suraqah would wear the bracelets of Chosroes (Sahih al-Bukhari 3906). |

### Key Milestones of the Journey:
1. **The Escape:** With assassins surrounding his house, the Prophet ﷺ emerged reciting: *"And We have put before them a barrier and behind them a barrier and covered them, so they do not see"* (Surah Yasin 36:9). He scattered dust over their heads and departed safely.
2. **Cave Thawr:** When Quraysh trackers stood inches from the cave mouth, Abu Bakr whispered: *"O Messenger of Allah! If one of them looks down at his feet, he will see us!"* The Prophet ﷺ calmly replied:  
   *"O Abu Bakr! What do you think of two with whom Allah is their third?"* (Sahih al-Bukhari 3653, Sahih Muslim 2381; Surah At-Tawbah 9:40).
3. **Suraqah ibn Malik:** Seeking the reward, Suraqah charged on his stallion, but every time he neared, the horse's legs sank into the firm earth. Suraqah asked for amnesty, and the Prophet ﷺ prophesied: *"How will you feel, O Suraqah, when you wear the golden bracelets of Chosroes (Emperor of Persia)?"* This prophecy was fulfilled two decades later during the caliphate of Sayyidina 'Umar!

## 2. Arrival in Madinah: Joy & The Camel Qaswa'
Upon reaching Quba' on the outskirts of Madinah, the Prophet ﷺ laid the foundations of **Masjid Quba'**, the first mosque founded on Taqwa (Surah At-Tawbah 9:108).

When he entered Madinah, women, children, and elders climbed onto rooftops singing:  
> **طَلَعَ الْبَدْرُ عَلَيْنَا • مِنْ ثَنِيَّاتِ الْوَدَاعْ**  
> **وَجَبَ الشُّكْرُ عَلَيْنَا • مَا دَعَا لِلَّهِ دَاعْ**  
> *"The full moon has risen upon us, from the valleys of Wada'!*  
> *Gratitude is incumbent upon us, as long as a caller calls to Allah!"*

Every clan begged the Prophet ﷺ to stay in their home. The Prophet ﷺ gently said:  
*"Let my camel go freely, for she is divinely commanded!"*  
His camel, **Al-Qaswa'**, walked through the streets and knelt in an open drying ground belonging to two orphan boys, Sahl and Suhayl. The Prophet ﷺ purchased the land to build his mosque and home, staying temporarily with the noble neighbor **Abu Ayyub al-Ansari**.

## 3. The Fraternal Pact: *Al-Mu'akhah* (The Brotherhood)
The refugees from Makkah (*Al-Muhajirun*) had left behind all their homes, businesses, and savings. To unite the divided society, the Prophet ﷺ enacted the **Pact of Brotherhood (Al-Mu'akhah)**:
* He paired each Muhajir with an Ansari brother in faith.
* Sayyidina Sa'd ibn al-Rabi' (Ansar) told his brother 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf (Muhajir): *"I am the wealthiest in Madinah. Half my wealth is yours, and choose which of my two orchards you prefer!"*
* 'Abd al-Rahman ibn 'Awf declined charity with profound dignity: *"May Allah bless your family and wealth! Just show me the way to the marketplace!"* Within months, 'Abd al-Rahman was an independent, prosperous merchant.

## 4. The Call to Prayer & The Change of the Qiblah
### The Institution of the Adhan (1 AH):
When Muslims gathered in Madinah, they needed a dignified way to assemble for prayer without imitating the horns or bells of other traditions. The companion **'Abdullah ibn Zayd** experienced a truthful dream in which he was taught the words of the **Adhan**. The Prophet ﷺ confirmed it:  
*"Indeed, it is a true dream! Stand with Bilal and teach it to him, for he has a more beautiful and resonant voice than you!"* (Sunan Abi Dawud 499, Sahih). Sayyidina Bilal climbed to call the first Adhan in Madinah.

### The Change of the Qiblah (Rajab 2 AH):
For sixteen to seventeen months after the Hijrah, Muslims prayed facing **Bayt al-Maqdis** (Jerusalem). The Prophet ﷺ cherished praying toward the Ka'bah, the sacred house built by Ibrahim. While the Prophet ﷺ was leading prayer at the mosque of Banu Salamah (later known as **Masjid al-Qiblatayn**, the Mosque of the Two Qiblahs), Allah revealed:  
> *"We have certainly seen the turning of your face toward the heaven, and We will surely turn you to a qiblah with which you will be pleased. So turn your face toward al-Masjid al-Haram."* (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:144).  
The congregation turned mid-prayer from facing north toward Jerusalem to facing south toward Makkah!
