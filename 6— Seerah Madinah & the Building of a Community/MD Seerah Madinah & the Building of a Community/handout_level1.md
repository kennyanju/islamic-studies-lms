## 🐪 1. The Wonderful Journey of the Hijrah
When the wicked leaders of Quraysh tried to harm the Muslims and stop Islam in Makkah, Allah gave Prophet Muhammad ﷺ permission to migrate to a welcoming green city in the north called **Yathrib** (which became known as **Madinat al-Nabi**—The City of the Prophet, or **Madinah**!).

The Prophet ﷺ chose his most faithful, loving friend, **Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq** (RA), to travel with him on this great migration called the **Hijrah**.

When Quraysh chased them into the desert, the Prophet ﷺ and Abu Bakr hid in **Cave Thawr**. When the enemy came right up to the cave entrance, Abu Bakr whispered with worry: *"O Messenger of Allah! If one of them looks down at his feet, he will see us!"*  
The Prophet ﷺ smiled with complete peace and said:  
> **مَا ظَنُّكَ يَا أَبَا بَكْرٍ بِاثْنَيْنِ اللهُ ثَالِثُهُمَا؟**  
> *"What do you think, O Abu Bakr, of two people with whom Allah is the Third?"* (Sahih al-Bukhari).  
Allah sent a tiny spider to spin a web across the cave opening and a dove to lay eggs in a nest. Seeing the unbroken web, Quraysh thought nobody could possibly be inside and walked away!

## 🎶 2. "Tala'a al-Badru 'Alayna!" — Welcome to Madinah
After traveling across the scorching desert sands for days, the Prophet's camel arrived in the oasis of Madinah! 

The girls, boys, and families of Madinah dressed in their finest clothes, climbed onto their rooftops, and beat their drums with joy, singing the most famous welcome song in Islamic history:
> **طَلَعَ الْبَدْرُ عَلَيْنَا • مِنْ ثَنِيَّاتِ الْوَدَاعِ**  
> **وَجَبَ الشُّكْرُ عَلَيْنَا • مَا دَعَا لِلَّهِ دَاعِ**  
> *"The full moon has risen upon us, from the valleys of Wada'!*  
> *Gratitude is forever due upon us, as long as any caller calls unto Allah!"*

Every family wanted the Prophet ﷺ to stay at their house, but his camel knelt down in front of the house of **Abu Ayyub al-Ansari**, who welcomed the Prophet ﷺ with tears of happiness!

## 🧱 3. Building the Prophet's Mosque (Al-Masjid an-Nabawi)
The very first thing the Prophet ﷺ did was purchase a piece of land to build a house of worship where all believers could pray together: **Al-Masjid an-Nabawi**.

* **Working Together:** The Prophet ﷺ rolled up his sleeves and carried heavy unbaked clay bricks alongside his companions! When the companions saw the Messenger of Allah working in the dust, they sang happily and worked twice as hard.
* **The Roof of Date Palms:** The walls were made of mud bricks, the pillars were trunks of date palm trees, and the roof was thatched with dry palm branches.
* **Bilal's Voice:** Sayyidina Bilal, with his deep, melodious voice, climbed to the top to call the first Adhan in Madinah!

---

## 🤝 4. The Blessed Brotherhood (Al-Mu'akhah)
In Madinah, there were two groups of believers:
1. **The Muhajirun (The Emigrants):** Those who left their homes, clothes, and businesses in Makkah for the sake of Allah.
2. **The Ansar (The Helpers):** The kind, generous Muslim families of Madinah who owned date farms and houses.

The Prophet ﷺ paired each Muhajir with an Ansari brother in a sacred pact called **Al-Mu'akhah**. The Ansar shared their homes, split their date harvests in half, and shared their wealth with their new brothers with open hearts! Allah praised them in the Qur'an for loving others more than themselves.
