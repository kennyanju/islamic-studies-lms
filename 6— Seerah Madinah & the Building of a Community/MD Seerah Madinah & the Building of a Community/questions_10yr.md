### Part B: Scenario-Based Questions (5 Questions)
*Read the scenario and choose or write the correct response based on what you have learned.*

11. **Imran wants to get a good grade on his Quran test. He says, "I have Tawakkul (trust) in Allah, so I don't need to study. Allah will give me an A." How should you correct Imran using the Ash'ari creed?**
    *   *Answer:* You should tell Imran that true trust (*Tawakkul*) requires using the physical means (*Asbab*) that Allah created. Ignoring the means (not studying) is foolishness (*Sufah*). He must study hard with his body, but trust in Allah with his heart for the result.

12. **Zainab is writing a contract for a school project. She remembers the Constitution of Madinah and wants to include her non-Muslim class partners fairly. What lesson from the Constitution can she apply?**
    *   *Answer:* Zainab can apply the lesson of **coexistence and freedom of faith**. The Constitution of Madinah showed that people of different beliefs can form a single community (*Ummah*) for mutual help and joint goals, treating each other with equal justice.

13. **During a friendly soccer match, Amina's team is winning. The referee makes a mistake, and the other team gets a penalty kick. Amina gets angry and says, "Let's cheat on the next play to make it fair." How does this violate the Prophetic lessons of Uhud?**
    *   *Answer:* In Uhud, when the archers disobeyed the Prophet's instructions because they wanted the spoils of war (*Ghanimah*), it led to a major defeat. Disobeying rules for worldly gain or cheating always ruins the community. A Muslim must be patient (*Sabr*) and follow rules honestly.

14. **Yousef finds out that some people are cutting down olive trees in a park during a protest. He remembers the military rules codified by Imam Malik. Why would Yousef argue that this action is against Islamic ethics?**
    *   *Answer:* In Maliki fiqh, the ethics of engagement are so strict that even during a defensive war, the Prophet (pbuh) and Caliph Abu Bakr banned the cutting down of fruitful trees or the destruction of crops. If destroying nature is banned during war, it is certainly banned during times of peace.

15. **A non-Muslim student travels to a Muslim country as a tourist. According to the Maliki ruling on Aman (safety), what is the duty of the citizens toward this tourist?**
    *   *Answer:* The citizens have a duty to protect and respect the tourist. In Maliki fiqh, if a visitor is granted *Aman* (safety) by a covenant, it is a sacred obligation (*'Ahd*) that cannot be broken. Doing them harm would be treachery (*Ghadr*), which is a major sin.

### Part C: Short Answer & Matching (5 Questions)
*Answer the questions briefly or match the terms.*

16. **Match the Arabic term to its correct English meaning:**
    *   1. *Ansar* -> ______ (Answer: The Helpers of Madinah)
    *   2. *Muhajirun* -> ______ (Answer: The Emigrants from Makkah)
    *   3. *Niyyah* -> ______ (Answer: The inner intention of the heart)
    *   4. *Riba* -> ______ (Answer: Usury or high interest, which is forbidden)

17. **What did the Prophet (pbuh) say about racism in his Farewell Sermon?**
    *   *Answer:* He declared that no race is superior to another (Arab or non-Arab, white or black), and that the only thing that makes one person better than another in the sight of Allah is their piety (*Taqwa*).

18. **Why did the Prophet (pbuh) insist on buying the land for Al-Masjid al-Nabawi from the two orphans instead of taking it as a gift?**
    *   *Answer:* To establish the legal rule that the rights and property of orphans (*Aytam*) must be protected with absolute justice and never taken advantage of.

19. **What is the Golden Chain (Silsilat al-Dhahab) of knowledge that links the Prophet (pbuh) to Imam Malik?**
    *   *Answer:* It is the direct chain of transmission: Prophet Muhammad (pbuh) -> Abdullah ibn Umar -> Nafi' (the student of Ibn Umar) -> Imam Malik.

20. **Why did Imam Malik believe that the Practice of the People of Madinah ('Amal Ahl al-Madinah) is a strong source of law?**
    *   *Answer:* Because the children of the Companions in Madinah learned their daily actions directly from their parents, who learned from the Prophet (pbuh). This continuous living practice represents the Sunnah in action.
