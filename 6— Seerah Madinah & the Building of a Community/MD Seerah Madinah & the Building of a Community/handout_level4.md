## 1. Defensive Sanctity: The Ethics of Warfare in Islam

Islam did not spread by the sword. For thirteen years in Makkah, Muslims endured torture, murder, and exile without striking a single blow in retaliation. Permission to engage in military defense was granted only after the migration to Madinah, when the infant state faced existential extermination by Quraysh:  
*“Permission [to fight] has been given to those who are being fought, because they were wronged. And indeed, Allah is competent to give them victory.”* (Surah Al-Hajj 22:39).

## 2. The Battle of Badr (2 AH — The Day of Criterion / *Yawm al-Furqan*)

* **The Context:** Quraysh had confiscated all property left behind by the Muhajirun in Makkah and sold it. When the Muslims intercepted a Quraysh trade caravan to recover stolen assets, Abu Jahl mobilized an army of **1,000 armored warriors** to crush the Muslims forever.
* **The Reality:** The Prophet ﷺ marched with only **313 men**, two horses, and 70 camels.
* **The Night of Tears:** The Prophet ﷺ spent the entire night weeping with hands raised in his shelter:  
  *“O Allah! If this small band of believers is destroyed today, You will never be worshipped upon the earth!”*
* **The Divine Aid:** Allah sent **1,000 angels** under Jibril (Surah Al-Anfal 8:9). The battle resulted in a stunning victory for truth: Abu Jahl and 70 top persecutors were slain, 70 were captured, and only 14 Muslims achieved martyrdom.

Seeking revenge for Badr, Quraysh marched with **3,000 troops** toward Mount Uhud outside Madinah:

* **The Tragedy:** In the rear attack, 70 great companions were martyred, including the Beloved's uncle **Sayyidina Hamzah (Sayyid ash-Shuhada')**. The Prophet's helmet was pierced into his cheek, his tooth chipped, and a rumor spread that he had been killed.
* **The Spiritual Lesson:** Allah taught the believers an eternal law: **Victory is not guaranteed by numbers or military pride; it depends entirely on absolute spiritual obedience to divine commands.**

## 4. The Battle of the Trench (*Al-Khandaq* / 5 AH)

In the 5th year, Quraysh formed a coalition of **10,000 confederates (Al-Ahzab)**, uniting pagan tribes and hostile groups to wipe Madinah off the map:
* **The Persian Innovation:** Sayyidina **Salman al-Farsi** proposed a military tactic never seen in Arabia: digging a massive deep trench across the vulnerable northern border of Madinah.
* **The Siege:** For a month in freezing winter, 3,000 Muslims endured near-starvation, guarding the trench day and night.
* **Divine Wind:** When all human options were exhausted, Allah sent a ferocious, freezing gale wind (*Rih*) that flipped the enemy's tents, extinguished their fires, and terrified their horses, causing the 10,000 to flee in utter panic!

---

## 5. The Treaty of Hudaybiyyah (6 AH): The Manifest Victory

In 6 AH, the Prophet ﷺ set out with 1,400 unarmed companions to perform 'Umrah in Makkah. Blocked at **Al-Hudaybiyyah**, he negotiated a 10-year peace treaty with Quraysh.
* **Apparent Concessions:** Muslims agreed to return to Madinah without 'Umrah that year, and to return any Makkan runaway who converted to Islam. Some companions, including Sayyidina 'Umar, struggled with these conditions.
* **The Divine Verdict:** Allah revealed:  
  *“Indeed, We have granted you a clear victory (Fathan Mubina).”* (Surah Al-Fath 48:1).
* **The Result:** With war paused, peaceful dialogue flourished. In the two years following Hudaybiyyah, **more people accepted Islam than in the previous eighteen years combined**, including military geniuses like Khalid ibn al-Walid and 'Amr ibn al-'As!
