### Group 2: Purpose-Gate (Priority: MEDIUM)

6. **R-08 (Button Arrows: Purpose Required)**
   - **Location**: `server.js:844` and `functions/api/[[route]].js:1178`
   - **Reason**: Navigation link contains a purely decorative trailing arrow (`Return to Islamic Studies LMS →`) without functional purpose.

7. **R-08 (Button Arrows: Purpose Required)**
   - **Location**: `server.js:1190` and `functions/api/[[route]].js:1602`
   - **Reason**: Quiz call-to-action button appends a decorative arrow glyph (`Take Quiz →`) without functional directionality.

8. **R-10 (Glassmorphism: Dose Cap Violation)**
   - **Location**: `public/style.css:571, 710, 856, 4291`
   - **Reason**: Blur/backdrop-filter is applied simultaneously across the header navbar (`.top-header`), buttons (`.hero-action-btn-sm`), cards (`.stat-card`), and auth modals (`.landing-auth-card`), exceeding the maximum dose cap of 1-2 accent elements.

9. **R-31 (Every Decision Must Have a Reason / Comment Hygiene: Decorative Separators)**
   - **Location**: `server.js:131, 397, 436, 491, 854, 879, 1261, 1457, 1678, 1754, 1779`
   - **Reason**: Route files employ multi-line boxed separator comments (`/* ===================== ... ===================== */`) that signal AI-generated styling without adding semantic information.

10. **R-31 (Every Decision Must Have a Reason / Comment Hygiene: Decorative Separators)**
    - **Location**: `functions/api/[[route]].js:12, 74, 91, 150, 272, 297, 417, 551`
    - **Reason**: Edge worker contains 8 redundant 76-character dashed divider comments (`// --------------------------------------------------------------------------`) wrapping utility headings.

11. **R-31 (Every Decision Must Have a Reason / Comment Hygiene: Decorative Separators)**
    - **Location**: `public/app.js:1, 210, 309, 1072, 1226, 1301, 1993, 2073, 2179, 2235, 2278, 2547, 2804, 2888, 2940, 3035, 4876, 5535, 5661`

12. **R-31 (Every Decision Must Have a Reason / Comment Hygiene: Workflow Narration)**
