# 02: Comparative Framework: "Other Scholars Say"

> **Purpose.** The Maliki madhhab and Ash'ari creed are taught as *our practice*. Children learn from Year 1 that the other Sunni schools are also valid, rightly guided, and worthy of love and respect. This document sets the policy, the box template, the age progression, and a checked list of difference topics that the Year documents draw on.

---

## 1. Guiding Policy

1. **Practise one, respect all.** The child performs worship according to the Maliki school. Other schools are presented as *different correct ways of following the Prophet ﷺ*, never as "mistakes".
2. **The four Imams loved each other.** Every comparison is introduced with the *adab* of the Imams before the ruling itself.
3. **Ijma' first, ikhtilaf second.** Before teaching a difference, teach what all schools **agree** on (e.g. all agree salah is five times daily and wudu is a condition). Differences are shown as *details within agreement*.
4. **Sunni boundaries.** The four madhhabs (Maliki, Hanafi, Shafi'i, Hanbali) in fiqh, and the Ash'ari, Maturidi and Athari approaches within Ahl al-Sunnah in creed, are presented as valid. Positions outside Ahl al-Sunnah (Mu'tazila, Khawarij, and others) are introduced in later years as *history to understand*, not as "other valid opinions".
5. **Evidence is always available.** From Year 4, every school's view is given with its main proof, so children see that each Imam had a reason.
6. **No talfiq or madhhab-hopping is taught to young children.** Guidance on following a madhhab, taqlid and talfiq waits for Year 5 Core and the Extension layer.
7. **The teacher's tone matters.** Use "Imam al-Shafi'i taught…", never "the Shafi'is wrongly…".

## 2. Age Progression

| Year (Age) | Stage | Fiqh: what the child learns | Creed: what the child learns | Box frequency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1 (6–7)** | Awareness | "People at the masjid may pray a little differently. They are following the Prophet ﷺ too." Stories of the Imams' kindness | We are *Ahl al-Sunnah wa'l-Jama'ah* | 1 per term (story form) |
| **2 (7–8)** | Recognition | Names of the 4 Imams, where they lived, one famous story each; spotting visible differences (hand position, Amin) | Allah is unlike creation (*tanzih*); all Sunni Muslims agree | 1 per unit (where relevant) |
| **3 (8–9)** | Identification | Simple 4-column tables (Maliki / Hanafi / Shafi'i / Hanbali) | Imam al-Ash'ari and Imam al-Maturidi as the two great Sunni schools of creed | In every fiqh unit |
| **4 (9–10)** | Reasons | *Why* scholars differ: a hadith reached one scholar and not another; the meaning of words; *'amal ahl al-Madinah*; qiyas | Basic Ash'ari–Maturidi differences; the Athari approach to the attributes and the *tafwid* shared ground | Every fiqh unit + creed units |
| **5 (10–11)** | Evidence | Each school's main Qur'an/hadith proof for selected issues; why we follow a madhhab | Mu'tazila and Khawarij as historical groups; overview of Shi'ism | Every unit, with evidence column |
| **Ext. (13+)** | Analysis | *Bidayat al-Mujtahid*-style case studies; taqlid, talfiq, fatwa bodies; fiqh of minorities | Comparative theology; responding to atheism and scientism | Case-study format |

## 3. Box Templates

### 3a. Years 1–2 (story box)
```markdown
> 🌍 **Other Scholars Say: Many Ways, One Sunnah**
> *Story:* [short story of an Imam showing respect]
> *What we do:* [Maliki practice in one sentence]
> *What some friends do:* [other practice in one sentence]
> *Remember:* Both are following the Prophet ﷺ. We love all the Imams.
```

### 3b. Years 3–4 (table box)
```markdown
> 🌍 **Other Scholars Say: [Topic]**
> **All agree:** [point of ijma']
>
> | | Maliki (our practice) | Hanafi | Shafi'i | Hanbali |
> |---|---|---|---|---|
> | Ruling | … | … | … | … |
>
> **Why the difference?** (Year 4) [one-sentence reason]
> **Adab:** [one line]
```

### 3c. Year 5 / Extension (evidence box)
```markdown
> 🌍 **Other Scholars Say: [Topic]**
> **All agree:** …
> | School | Position | Main evidence | Reason for difference |
> |---|---|---|---|
> **Reflection:** How would you pray politely beside someone who does this differently?
> **Source:** Ibn Rushd, *Bidayat al-Mujtahid*, Kitab al-[…]
```

### 3d. Creed box (Years 3+)
```markdown
> 🧭 **Ahl al-Sunnah Map: [Topic]**
> **All Sunni schools agree:** …
> **Ash'ari (our school):** … | **Maturidi:** … | **Athari:** …
> **Why this is not a split:** …
```

## 4. Adab Stories Bank (Years 1–3)

| # | Story | Lesson | Use in |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The Caliph Harun al-Rashid wanted to make everyone follow the *Muwatta'*. **Imam Malik refused**, because the Companions had spread to many lands with different knowledge | Knowledge is wide; do not force one view on everyone | Y1 T1, Y3 history |
| 2 | **Imam al-Shafi'i studied with Imam Malik** as a young man and memorised the *Muwatta'*. He later learned from Imam Muhammad al-Shaybani, the student of Abu Hanifa | The Imams were teachers and students of each other | Y2 |
| 3 | Imam al-Shafi'i said: "All people in fiqh depend on Abu Hanifa." | Praising other scholars | Y2 |
| 4 | **Imam Ahmad was a student of Imam al-Shafi'i**, and al-Shafi'i made du'a for him | Love between the Imams | Y2 |
| 5 | Imam Ahmad was asked whether one could pray behind someone who doesn't renew wudu after bleeding. He replied: "Would I not pray behind Malik and Sa'id ibn al-Musayyib?" | Praying behind those who follow another school | Y3, Y4 |
| 6 | Imam al-Shafi'i is reported to have left the Fajr qunut when praying near the grave of Abu Hanifa, out of respect | Respect in practice | Y3 |
| 7 | Imam Malik: "Every person's word may be taken or left, except the occupant of this grave ﷺ." | Humility of the scholars | Y4 |

*(Stories 3 and 6 are widely reported in the tabaqat literature. The teacher's notes should present them as "it is reported".)*

## 5. Fiqh Difference Topics (checked list)

Each item gives the **Maliki (mashhur) position** and the other schools, plus the year where it is first introduced. *Year documents must take positions from here, so every unit is consistent.*

### 5.1 Purification (Taharah)
| Topic | Maliki | Hanafi | Shafi'i | Hanbali | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fara'id of wudu | **7**: niyyah, face, arms to elbows, wipe the **whole** head, feet to ankles, **dalk** (rubbing), **muwalat** (continuity) | **4**: face, arms, wipe ¼ of head, feet (niyyah sunnah) | **6**: niyyah, face, arms, wipe part of head, feet, **tartib** | 6–7: face (incl. mouth & nose rinsing), arms, wipe whole head (incl. ears), feet, tartib, muwalat; niyyah a condition | Y3 (table); Y1–2 Maliki steps only |
| Touching the opposite gender | Breaks wudu **if with pleasure** intended or felt | Does not break | Breaks with **any skin contact** (non-mahram adult) | Breaks if with desire | Y4 |
| Bleeding | Does not break | Flowing blood **breaks** | Does not break | A large amount breaks | Y3 |
| Eating camel meat | Does not break | Does not break | Does not break | **Breaks** | Y4 |
| Status of a living dog | Body and saliva **pure**; washing a vessel 7× is an act of worship (*ta'abbudi*) | Saliva impure, body not | Impure | Impure | Y4 |
| Wiping over khuffs | Valid; **no fixed time limit** (recommended to remove weekly) | 1 day / 3 days travelling | 1 day / 3 days | 1 day / 3 days | Y4 |
| Tayammum strikes | 1 strike obligatory (face & hands to wrists), 2nd strike to elbows sunnah | 2 strikes, to elbows | 2 strikes, to elbows | 1 strike, face and hands | Y4 |

### 5.2 Prayer (Salah)
| Topic | Maliki | Hanafi | Shafi'i | Hanbali | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hands in qiyam | **Sadl** (arms at sides) is the mashhur position in fard; qabd allowed in nafl (and adopted by many Malikis) | Qabd below the navel | Qabd below the chest, above the navel | Qabd below the navel (main narration) | **Y1** (awareness) |
| Raising the hands | At the opening takbir only (mashhur) | Opening takbir only | Opening, before ruku', rising from ruku', rising from 1st tashahhud | Opening, before ruku', rising from ruku' | Y2 |
| Basmalah in al-Fatihah | **Not recited** in fard prayers | Recited silently | A verse of al-Fatihah; recited aloud in loud prayers | Recited silently | Y3 |
| Saying Amin | Follower says it silently; imam says it in silent prayers | Silently | Aloud in loud prayers | Aloud in loud prayers | Y2 |
| Fatihah behind the imam | Recommended in silent prayers; disliked in loud prayers | Not recited | **Obligatory** in every prayer | Recommended in silent prayers | Y4 |
| Qunut | **Fajr**, before ruku', silently (recommended) | In witr only, before ruku' | Fajr, after ruku' (sunnah) | In witr, after ruku' | Y3 |
| Witr | Sunnah mu'akkadah; 1 rak'ah after a separate *shaf'* (2) | **Wajib**; 3 rak'ahs with one salam | Sunnah; 1–11 rak'ahs | Sunnah; 1–11 rak'ahs | Y3 |
| Sujud al-sahw | **Before** salam for omission, **after** salam for addition | After salam | Before salam | Mostly before salam, with details | Y3 (Maliki); Y4 (table) |
| Shortening when travelling | Sunnah mu'akkadah | **Wajib** | Permitted (rukhsah) | Permitted (preferred) | Y4 |
| Combining when travelling | Permitted | **Not permitted** (except 'Arafah/Muzdalifah) | Permitted | Permitted | Y4 |
| End of traveller status | Intention to stay 4 days | 15 days | 4 days | More than 20 prayers | Y5 |
| Minimum number for Jumu'ah | 12 besides the imam | 3 besides the imam | 40 | 40 | Y5 |
| Eid prayer | Sunnah mu'akkadah | Wajib | Sunnah mu'akkadah | Fard kifayah | Y3 |
| Tarawih | Imam Malik: 36 + 3 witr (*'amal* of Madinah); 20 widely practised | 20 | 20 | 20 | Y3 |

### 5.3 Fasting, Zakah & Hajj
| Topic | Maliki | Hanafi | Shafi'i | Hanbali | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Intention for Ramadan | **One intention** at the start covers the month (if unbroken) | Each day; may be made before midday | Each night | Each night | Y3 |
| Moon sighting | A reliable sighting binds other lands (*ittihad al-matali'*), except very distant ones | Sighting binds all | Each region by its own horizon (*ikhtilaf al-matali'*) | Sighting binds all | Y5 |
| Zakah on worn gold jewellery | **Not due** on permissible worn jewellery | **Due** | Not due | Not due | Y5 |
| Zakat al-Fitr in cash | Staple food (cash not accepted in the mashhur) | **Cash value permitted** | Staple food | Staple food | Y4 |
| Types of Hajj preferred | **Ifrad** | Qiran | Ifrad (with conditions) | Tamattu' | Y5 |

| Topic | Maliki | Hanafi | Shafi'i | Hanbali |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sea creatures | **All** halal | Fish only | Almost all | All except frog, crocodile, snake |
| Tasmiyah at slaughter | Obligatory if remembered | Obligatory if remembered | Sunnah | Obligatory if remembered |

## 6. Creed Map Topics (Ahl al-Sunnah)

| Topic | All Sunnis agree | Ash'ari (our school) | Maturidi | Athari | Year |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Allah's attributes such as Hand, Face, *istiwa'* | Allah is unlike creation (*laysa ka-mithlihi shay'*); no body, no limbs | **Tafwid** (consign the meaning to Allah) or **ta'wil** (fitting interpretation) | Same as Ash'ari | Affirm the words as revealed, without asking "how" (*bila kayf*); consign the "how" | Y4 |
| *Takwin* (bringing into being) | Allah alone creates | A relation of the attribute of Power (*qudrah*) | A separate eternal attribute | Affirms Allah's actions | Y4 |
| Knowing Allah before revelation | Revelation is the source of the law | Obligation comes only through revelation | Reason alone obliges knowing Allah | Through revelation and fitrah | Y5 |
| Saying "I am a believer, in sha' Allah" | Iman is belief in the heart | Permitted (about one's final state) | Not said if it implies doubt | Permitted | Y4 |
| Increase/decrease of iman | Righteous deeds are part of a complete faith | Iman increases and decreases | Iman's core does not increase or decrease; its fruits do | Increases and decreases | Y3 |
| Good and evil | Allah's law decides rulings | Rulings are known only through revelation | Reason can recognise some good and evil | Rulings by revelation | Ext. |

Groups outside Ahl al-Sunnah, taught as history (Year 5 / Ext.): **Mu'tazila** (createdness of the Qur'an, the *mihnah* and Imam Ahmad's steadfastness), **Khawarij** (takfir of sinners; why the Sunni position rejects this), **Jabriyya/Qadariyya** (and how *kasb* answers both), and an **overview of Shi'ism** (history, main differences, respectful coexistence).
