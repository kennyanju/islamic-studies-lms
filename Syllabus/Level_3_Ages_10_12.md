# Level 3 Syllabus: Systematic Practice & Understanding (Ages 10–12)

> **App Track**: **Level 3** (`level3`)  
> **Target Learner**: Ages 10–12 (Pre-Adolescent / Years 5 & 6)  
> **ICO Benchmarks Absorbed**: Grade 5 and Grade 6  
> **Weekly Schedule**: 4 sessions × 40 minutes = 160 min/week (96 h/year)  
> **Classical Text Spine**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (*Al-Murshid al-Mu'in* — Creed & Taharah), *Muqaddimat al-Risalah*, and Nawawi's *40 Hadith* (Hadiths 11–20)  
> **Riwayah**: Hafs 'an 'Asim (Surahs 85 to 78, completing the entire 30th Juz' / Juz' 'Amma!)

---

## Pedagogical Vision for Level 3 (Ages 10–12)

At ages 10–12, children develop analytical thinking, logical categorization, and moral self-awareness. Level 3 introduces **systematic legal texts (*Mutun*) and comparative matrices**:
* **Creed**: The 3 Judgments of the Mind (*Wajib*, *Mustahil*, *Ja'iz*); Divine Transcendence (*Tanzih*); understanding that Allah does not occupy space or time.
* **Worship**: The 7 Obligatory Acts (*Fara'id*) of Wudu in *Ibn 'Ashir*; Ghusl; Tayammum on natural earth (*Sa'id*); the logic of Sujud as-Sahw (*Qabli* for subtraction, *Ba'di* for addition).
* **Seerah**: The open call at Safa, the boycott of Shi'b Abi Talib, migration to Abyssinia, the Year of Sorrow, and the forgiving prayer of Ta'if.
* **Comparative Foundation**: *"Other Scholars Say"* advances to **Identification**: 4-school comparative tables (Maliki, Hanafi, Shafi'i, Hanbali) for wudu fara'id, the Basmalah in prayer, and Sujud as-Sahw.

## Core Level Units (Ages 10–12)

1. **Unit 3.1: The Three Judgments of the Mind & Divine Transcendence**
   * Classifying statements into Rationally Necessary, Impossible, and Possible; *Tanzih* (Allah is free from body, space, and limits).
2. **Unit 3.2: The Call to Prayer & The Sacred Assembly**
   * The Madinan Adhan with Tarji'; rules of congregational prayer (*Salah al-Jama'ah*); obligations and adab of Friday Jumu'ah.
3. **Unit 3.3: The Early Call: Mount Safa, Persecution, & Boycott**
   * Public preaching; steadfastness of Bilal, Yasir, and Sumayyah; surviving the 3-year famine in Shi'b Abi Talib.
4. **Unit 3.4: The 7 Fara'id of Wudu according to Ibn 'Ashir**
   * Memorising and demonstrating: Niyyah, Face, Arms, whole Head wipe, Feet, *Dalk* (rubbing), and *Muwalat* (continuity).
5. **Unit 3.5: Ghusl & Tayammum: Purification with Sa'id**
   * When ritual bathing is required; dry ablution when water is absent or harmful; defining *Sa'id* as pure natural earth/rock.
6. **Unit 3.6: Abyssinia, Negus, & The Valley of Ta'if**
   * The Christian king who oppressed no one; Ja'far's speech and Surah Maryam; the Year of Sorrow and the du'a of Ta'if.
7. **Unit 3.7: Sujud as-Sahw: Repairing Prayer Mistakes**
   * The Maliki rule: *Qabli* (before salam) for omissions; *Ba'di* (after salam) for additions; handling doubts (*Shakk*).
8. **Unit 3.8: The Fiqh of Fasting (Sawm) & The Joy of Ramadan**
   * Pillars of fasting (intention and abstaining); the Maliki ruling of a single monthly intention; Taraweeh traditions in Madinah.
9. **Unit 3.9: Sincerity (Ikhlas), Guarding the Tongue, & The Final News**
   * Sincerity vs Ostentation (*Riya'*); avoiding backbiting (*Ghibah*); completing the study of Surah An-Naba'.

## Hifz & Classical Matn Targets (Level 3)
* **Qur'an (8 Long Surahs — Completing Juz' 30!)**: Al-Burooj, Al-Inshiqaq, Al-Mutaffifin, Al-Infitar, At-Takwir, 'Abasa, An-Nazi'at, An-Naba'.
* **Tajweed**: Comprehensive rules of **Noon Sakinah & Tanween** (Izhar, Idgham with/without ghunnah, Iqlab, Ikhfa') and **Qalqalah** on the letters of *Qutbu Jaddin*.
* **Classical Matn**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (Lines 11 to 35: Creed and the 7 Obligations of Wudu in Arabic).
* **Hadith**: Imam an-Nawawi's *40 Hadith* (Hadiths 11 to 20).
* **Daily Adhkar**: Morning and evening fortress adhkar, post-prayer Tasbih Fatimi (33/33/34), and the Adhan response du'a.
