# Year 3 Syllabus: Systematic Practice & Understanding

> **Target Learner**: Level 3 Track: Upper Elementary (Ages 10-12)  
> **Weekly Schedule**: 4 sessions × 40 minutes = 160 min/week  
> **Annual Instructional Volume**: 36 Weeks (3 Terms of 12 Weeks) = 96 hours  
> **ICO Benchmarks Absorbed**: Grade 5 and Grade 6 (systematic taharah, Jumu'ah, Sawm, Hijrah, Caliphate)  
> **Classical Text Spine**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (*Al-Murshid al-Mu'in*: Creed & Taharah sections), *Muqaddimah* of Ibn Abi Zayd's *Risalah*, and Nawawi's *40 Hadith* (Hadiths 11–20)  
> **Riwayah**: Hafs 'an 'Asim & Warsh 'an Nafi' (Completion of Juz' 'Amma: Surah Al-Inshiqaq to Surah An-Naba', introduction to Noon Sakinah rules)

---

## Term 1: The Ash'ari Creed of Ibn 'Ashir & The Call to Prayer (Weeks 1–12)

### Unit 3.1: The Three Judgments of the Mind & Divine Transcendence (Weeks 1–4)
* **Strand**: 'Aqidah (Ash'ari)
* **Session Allocation**: 16 sessions × 40 min = 10.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (Lines 11–25: *Hukm al-'Aql* and Necessary Attributes).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 8–9)**:
  1. The child classifies statements into the three mental judgments (*Ahkam 'Aqliyyah*):
     * **Wajib 'Aqli** (Rationally Necessary): Something the mind cannot imagine not existing (e.g. Allah exists, is One, and has no beginning).
     * **Mustahil 'Aqli** (Rationally Impossible): Something the mind cannot imagine existing (e.g. Allah having a partner, child, or physical shape).
     * **Ja'iz 'Aqli** (Rationally Possible): Something that can either exist or not exist (e.g. creating the universe, rain falling, living or dying).
  2. The child explains Divine Transcendence (**Tanzih**): Whatever pops into your imagination, Allah is completely unlike it (*Kullu ma khatara bi-balika fa-Allahu bi-khilafi dhalik*).
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Epistemological role of the intellect (*'Aql*) in Sunni theology: The mind recognizes the logical necessity of the Creator, while divine revelation (*Sama'*) establishes legal obligations and afterlife details.
  2. The Sanusi taxonomy of attributes: Distinction between the Personal Attribute (*Al-Wujud*), the Five Negating Attributes (*Salbiyyah*), and the Existential Attributes (*Ma'ani*).
* **Ash'ari Creed Flag**: Rejection of *Tajsim* (attributing a physical body to Allah) and *Makan* (attributing a spatial location or physical direction to Allah). Allah existed before space and time, and He is now as He has always been.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say: Identification Box**:
  > *Topic: Who are the Sunni Schools of Creed?*
  > **All Sunnis agree**: Allah is One, eternal, all-powerful, without body or parts (*Laysa ka-mithlihi shay'*).
  > *Imam Abu al-Hasan al-Ash'ari* (Iraq) and *Imam Abu Mansur al-Maturidi* (Samarkand) systematized Sunni theology using logic to defend the Qur'an and Sunnah against deviants.
  > *The Athari / Hanbali scholars* affirmed the words of the Qur'an while consigning the ultimate modality to Allah (*Bila Kayf*). All three form the broad, united fortress of *Ahl al-Sunnah wa'l-Jama'ah*.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Ash-Shura (42:11: *"There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the Hearing, the Seeing."*).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "The Mind Judgment Sorting Game": Flashcards featuring scenarios (e.g. "A circle having four corners" -> Impossible; "A human flying in a plane" -> Possible; "The Creator having no beginning" -> Necessary).
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: *Ibn 'Ashir* Lines 11–15 (Arabic matn memorisation). Surah Al-Inshiqaq.

### Unit 3.2: The Call to Prayer (Adhan/Iqamah) & The Sacred Assembly (Weeks 5–8)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki)
* **Session Allocation**: 16 sessions × 40 min = 10.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari* (Kitab al-Salah) / *Ibn 'Ashir* (Salat al-Jama'ah).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 8–9)**:
  1. The child recites the Adhan and Iqamah according to the Madinan transmission (the Tarji' in the Adhan: repeating the two testimonies silently before saying them aloud).
  2. The child identifies the rules of praying in congregation (*Salah al-Jama'ah*): Standing shoulder-to-shoulder, following the Imam without preceding him (*Musabaqah*) or delaying excessively.
  3. The child explains the spiritual requirements of Friday Prayer (*Jumu'ah*): Performing ghusl, wearing clean white clothes, listening attentively to the two Khutbahs in total silence.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The legal minimum quorum for Jumu'ah in the Maliki Madhhab: 12 resident adult Muslim males besides the Imam (contrasted with 40 in Shafi'i/Hanbali, and 3 in Hanafi).
  2. The legal ruling on speaking during the Khutbah: Strictly prohibited (*Haram*), invalidating the spiritual reward of Jumu'ah.
* **Maliki Fiqh Flag**:
  * **Tarji' in Adhan**: The Mu'adhin recites the Shahadah twice quietly to himself, then proclaims it aloud twice with full voice, following the practice of Abu Mahdhura in Makkah and the people of Madinah.
  * **Basmalah in Prayer**: The Basmalah is omitted in fard prayers before Al-Fatihah and the following surah.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say: Identification Box**:
  > *Topic: Reciting the Basmalah in Al-Fatihah*
  > **All agree**: Al-Fatihah is the greatest chapter of the Qur'an and must be recited in prayer.
  >
  > | School | Position on Basmalah in Fard Salah |
  > |---|---|
  > | **Maliki (our practice)** | Disliked (*Makruh*) in fard; start directly with *"Alhamdulillahi Rabbil-'Alamin"*. |
  > | **Hanafi** | Recited silently (*Sirran*) before Al-Fatihah in every rak'ah. |
  > | **Shafi'i** | Obligatory verse of Al-Fatihah; recited **aloud** in loud prayers! |
  > | **Hanbali** | Recited silently as part of the opening sunnah. |
  > *Remember*: When you hear a Shafi'i Imam recite "Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim" out loud, you know he is following his school with complete sincerity!
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Jumu'ah (62:9); Hadith: *"Whoever says to his companion 'Be quiet' while the Imam is giving the Khutbah on Friday has engaged in idle talk."* (Bukhari).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Setting up a full congregational simulation in class: A student appointed as Mu'adhin, another as Imam, others aligning straight rows (*Taswiyat al-Sufuf*).
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Mutaffifin. Nawawi Hadith 11 (*"Leave that which makes you doubt for that which does not make you doubt"*).

### Unit 3.3: The Early Call: Mount Safa, Boycott, & Steadfastness (Weeks 9–12)
* **Strand**: Seerah & Islamic History
* **Session Allocation**: 16 sessions × 40 min = 10.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Ibn Hisham's Seerah* / *Ar-Rahiq al-Makhtum* (The Sealed Nectar).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 8–9)**:
  1. The child narrates the Prophet's ﷺ public proclamation on Mount Safa: *"If I were to tell you that horsemen were in the valley behind this mountain ready to attack, would you believe me?"*
  2. The child recounts the torture of early vulnerable believers: Bilal ibn Rabah under the burning rocks whispering *"Ahad, Ahad!"*, and the family of Yasir and Sumayyah (the first martyr in Islam).
  3. The child describes the 3-year social and economic boycott of Banu Hashim in Shi'b Abi Talib, where Muslims survived on tree leaves, ending when termites ate the parchment except Allah's name.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Socio-political analysis of Qurayshi resistance: How monotheism threatened the economic monopoly of the idol trade and tribal hierarchy.
  2. The ethics of steadfastness (*Sabr*): Differentiating between passive suffering and active spiritual resistance against tyranny.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Ash-Shu'ara (26:214: *"And warn your closest kin"*); Surah Al-Masad (111:1–5).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Creative writing journal: Writing a personal diary entry from the perspective of a child living inside the three-year boycott of Shi'b Abi Talib.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Infitar. Nawawi Hadith 12 (*"From the perfection of a person's Islam is leaving that which does not concern him"*).

## Term 2: In-Depth Taharah & The Journey of Faith (Weeks 13–24)

### Unit 3.4: The 7 Fara'id of Wudu according to Ibn 'Ashir (Weeks 13–16)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki)
* **Session Allocation**: 16 sessions × 40 min = 10.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (Lines 26–35: Fara'id al-Wudu).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 8–9)**:
  1. The child lists and demonstrates the **7 Fara'id (Obligations)** of Wudu without which wudu is legally invalid:
     1. **Al-Niyyah** (Intention at the first wash of the face).
     2. **Ghasl al-Wajh** (Washing face from hairline to chin, ear to ear).
     3. **Ghasl al-Yadayn** (Washing arms including elbows).
     4. **Mash ar-Ra's** (Wiping the whole head from forehead to nape of neck).
     5. **Ghasl ar-Rijlayn** (Washing feet including ankles).
     6. **Al-Dalk** (Rubbing each limb with hand while washing).
     7. **Al-Muwalat / Al-Fawr** (Continuity: performing the steps without long interruptions).
  2. The child distinguishes these 7 pillars from the Sunnan (washing hands first, rinsing mouth, sniffing water, wiping ears, and sequence).
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Textual analysis of the Arabic poem: *"Fara'idu al-wudū'i sab'atun tu'ad / Niyyatun bi-bad'ihi wa ghaslu wajhin qad..."*
  2. The legal theory behind *Dalk* (rubbing): Why Imam Malik held that mere immersion in water without friction is not called "washing" (*Ghasl*) in classical Arabic linguistic custom.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say: Identification Box**:
  > *Topic: The Obligatory Pillars of Wudu Across the Four Schools*
  > **All agree**: The Face, Arms, Head, and Feet must be purified (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6).
  >
  > | School | Number of Fara'id | Unique Features |
  > |---|---|---|
  > | **Maliki** | **7** | Requires **Dalk** (rubbing) and **Muwalat** (continuity). Entire head wiped. |
  > | **Hanafi** | **4** | Only the 4 Quranic limbs; Niyyah and Tartib are sunnahs! Wipes ¼ head. |
  > | **Shafi'i** | **6** | Requires **Niyyah** and **Tartib** (sequence). Wipes part of head. |
  > | **Hanbali** | **6–7** | Includes mouth and nose rinsing with face, whole head with ears, and tartib. |
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:6); Hadith of 'Amr ibn 'Abasah on the sins washed away with wudu drops (Muslim).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Reciting the 7 Fara'id rhyming chant while acting out each step; oral examination of peers in pairs.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: *Ibn 'Ashir* Lines 26–30 (Arabic lines on wudu fara'id). Surah At-Takwir.

### Unit 3.5: Ghusl & Tayammum: Purification with Sa'id (Weeks 17–20)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki)
* **Session Allocation**: 16 sessions × 40 min = 10.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (Fara'id al-Ghusl and Shurut al-Tayammum).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 8–9)**:
  1. The child explains when the full ritual bath (**Ghusl**) is required: Major ritual impurity, post-puberty changes, and for Friday Jumu'ah (sunnah).
  2. The child lists the obligations of Ghusl: Intention, continuity, rubbing the body (*Dalk*), and ensuring water reaches every single hair and fold of skin.
  3. The child demonstrates **Tayammum** (dry purification) when clean water is completely unavailable or medically harmful: Striking clean natural earth (*Sa'id*), wiping the face, and wiping the arms to the wrists.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The Maliki definition of *Sa'id*: Any natural surface of the earth (pure dirt, sand, unhewn natural rock, clay, or stone), refuting the narrow restriction to dust alone.
  2. The legal steps of Tayammum: The first strike is obligatory (for face and hands to wrists); the second strike and wiping from wrists to elbows is an emphasized Sunnah.
* **Maliki Fiqh Flag**: In the Maliki school, Tayammum is connected directly to one specific fard prayer and must be performed after the prayer time has entered.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah An-Nisa (4:43: *"Then take for yourselves clean earth and wipe over your faces and your hands."*).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Practical dry workshop with clean natural desert stones: Demonstrating the gentle touch and wiping of Tayammum.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah 'Abasa. Nawawi Hadith 13 (*"None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself"*).

### Unit 3.6: Abyssinia, Negus, & The Valley of Ta'if (Weeks 21–24)
* **Strand**: Seerah & Islamic History
* **Session Allocation**: 16 sessions × 40 min = 10.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Ibn Hisham's Seerah* / *Al-Fusul fi Seerat ar-Rasul*.
* **Core Outcomes (Age 8–9)**:
  1. The child narrates the First and Second Migrations to Abyssinia (*Habashah*): Seeking refuge with a just Christian king (the Negus / *Najashi*) who oppressed no one.
  2. The child retells the speech of Ja'far ibn Abi Talib (RA) before the royal court contrasting pre-Islamic ignorance with the beauty of Islam, followed by the recitation of Surah Maryam that brought tears to the Negus' eyes.
  3. The child recounts the Year of Sorrow (*'Am al-Huzn*): The loss of Khadijah and Abu Talib, followed by the stoning at Ta'if and the Prophet's sublime forgiving prayer: *"O Allah, guide my people, for they do not know."*
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Diplomacy in the early community: Strategic alliance-building, the ethics of asylum, and interfaith engagement founded upon theological honesty.
  2. Prophetic emotional resilience: The du'a of Ta'if (*"To You, O Lord, I complain of my weakness..."*) as the supreme manifesto of *'Ubudiyyah* (complete servitude to Allah).
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Maryam (verses read by Ja'far); The Du'a of Ta'if.
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Courtroom drama reenactment: The Quraysh envoys ('Amr ibn al-'As before Islam) vs Ja'far ibn Abi Talib before the throne of the Negus.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Maryam (Verses 16–22: The story of Maryam). Nawawi Hadith 14 & 15 (*"Whoever believes in Allah and the Last Day, let him speak good or remain silent"*).

## Term 3: Congregational Spirit, Fasting Basics, & Sincerity (Weeks 25–36)

### Unit 3.7: Sujud as-Sahw: Repairing Prayer Mistakes (Weeks 25–28)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki)
* **Session Allocation**: 16 sessions × 40 min = 10.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari* (Bab Sujud al-Sahw) / *Matn al-'Ashmawiyyah*.
* **Core Outcomes (Age 8–9)**:
  1. The child explains that human forgetfulness in prayer can be repaired through two extra prostrations called **Sujud as-Sahw**.
  2. The child masters the elegant Maliki rule:
     * **Al-Qabli (Before the Salam)**: Performed if you **omitted** a confirmed sunnah (e.g. forgot the first Tashahhud or forgot silent recitation).
     * **Al-Ba'di (After the Salam)**: Performed if you **added** something extra by mistake (e.g. accidentally prayed a 5th rak'ah).
  3. The child understands that missing an obligatory Pillar (*Fard*) cannot be fixed by sujud alone; the pillar must be completed.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The combined situation: If a person both omitted a sunnah and added an extra action in the same prayer, the **Qabli** (subtraction) takes precedence over the Ba'di.
  2. The Maliki ruling on doubts (*Shakk*): Build upon what is certain (the minimum number of rak'ahs prayed), complete the prayer, and perform a **Ba'di** prostration after salam.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say: Identification Box**:
  > *Topic: When to perform Sujud as-Sahw?*
  >
  > | School | Rule for Sujud as-Sahw |
  > |---|---|
  > | **Maliki** | **Qabli** (before salam) for omissions; **Ba'di** (after salam) for additions. |
  > | **Hanafi** | Always performed **after the salam** (with one turn of the head). |
  > | **Shafi'i** | Always performed **before the salam** in all situations. |
  > | **Hanbali** | Mostly before salam, but after salam in two specific textual cases. |
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Hadith of Dhu al-Yadayn (Bukhari & Muslim); Hadith of Ibn Buhaynah on forgetting the middle sitting.
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "The Salah Emergency Clinic": The teacher gives a prayer mistake scenario card, and the child must prescribe whether the patient needs a *Qabli*, a *Ba'di*, or a full replacement of a rak'ah.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Nawawi Hadith 16 (*"Do not become angry"*). Surah An-Nazi'at (first 25 verses).

### Unit 3.8: The Fiqh of Fasting (Sawm) & The Joy of Ramadan (Weeks 29–32)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki)
* **Session Allocation**: 16 sessions × 40 min = 10.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (Kitab al-Siyam) / *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari*.
* **Core Outcomes (Age 8–9)**:
  1. The child defines fasting (*Sawm*): Refraining from food, drink, and invalidating desires from the break of dawn (*Fajr*) until sunset (*Maghrib*) with the intention of worship.
  2. The child identifies the two pillars of fasting: The **Niyyah** (Intention) and **Al-Imsak** (Abstaining).
  3. The child practices "half-day" or full-day weekend fasts to build physical stamina and joy for Ramadan.
  4. The child celebrates the Sunnahs of fasting: Taking the pre-dawn meal (*Suhoor*), rushing to break the fast at sunset (*Iftar*) with dates and water, and praying Taraweeh.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The Maliki position on the Ramadan intention: **One single intention** at the beginning of the month suffices for the entire unbroken month of Ramadan (unlike the Shafi'i school which requires a fresh intention every single night).
  2. Rulings on exemptions: Sickness, travel, pregnancy, and elderly age; distinguishing between making up missed days (*Qada'*) and paying food compensation (*Fidyah*).
* **Maliki Fiqh Flag**:
  * **One intention for Ramadan**: A person intends to fast the entire month of Ramadan on the first night; this covers every day unless interrupted by illness or travel.
  * **Taraweeh**: Imam Malik recorded that the people of Madinah prayed 36 rak'ahs plus 3 witr, while 20 rak'ahs was the standard established by Sayyidina 'Umar in congregation.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:183: *"O you who believe, fasting is prescribed for you as it was prescribed for those before you, that you may attain Taqwa."*); Hadith: *"Whoever fasts Ramadan with faith and seeking reward, his past sins will be forgiven."* (Bukhari).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Crafting a family "Ramadan Good Deed Calendar"; assembling Iftar gift bags with dates and water bottles to deliver to local mosque attendees.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Fast-breaking du'a: *"Dhahaba al-dhama'u wabtallat al-'uruqu wa thabata al-ajru in sha' Allah"*. Surah An-Nazi'at (completion).

### Unit 3.9: Sincerity (Ikhlas), Guarding the Tongue, & The Final News (Weeks 33–36)
* **Strand**: Akhlaq, Tazkiyah & Qur'an
* **Session Allocation**: 16 sessions × 40 min = 10.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (Tazkiyah section: Purifying the Heart from Riya') / *Bidayat al-Hidayah* (Imam al-Ghazali).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 8–9)**:
  1. The child defines **Ikhlas** (Pure Sincerity): Doing every good action exclusively for the pleasure of Allah, not to be praised or noticed by people.
  2. The child identifies the poison of **Riya'** (Ostentation/Showing off): The minor shirk that burns the reward of good deeds like fire burns dry wood.
  3. The child learns the danger of the tongue: Staying away from backbiting (*Ghibah*, speaking ill of someone behind their back even if true), slander (*Buhtan*), and lying.
  4. The child completes the memorisation and reflection of **Surah An-Naba'**, marking the joyful completion of the entire 30th Juz' (Juz' 'Amma).
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Tazkiyah anatomy: The spiritual organs of the heart (*Qalb*), the lower self (*Nafs*), and spiritual vigilance (*Muraqabah*).
  2. Tafsir analysis of Surah An-Naba': The Great Event (*Al-Naba' al-'Azim*), cosmic proofs of resurrection, and the reality of the Day of Sorting (*Yawm al-Fasl*).
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Bayyinah (98:5: *"And they were not commanded except to worship Allah, sincere to Him in religion"*); Hadith: *"Whoever guarantees for me what is between his jaws and what is between his legs, I guarantee for him Paradise."* (Bukhari).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: The "Secret Good Deed Challenge": The child must perform 3 good deeds that no human being on earth discovers (only Allah knows); celebratory Juz' 'Amma completion assembly.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah An-Naba' (complete). Nawawi Hadith 17, 18, 19, and 20 (*"If you feel no shame, then do whatever you wish"*).

## Year 3 Assessment & Review Framework
* **Weekly Oral Examinations**: Testing 4 lines of *Ibn 'Ashir* or 1 Hadith of An-Nawawi every Friday.
* **Term Practicums**:
  * *Term 1*: "Mind Judgments" oral quiz + Adhan and Iqamah performance with Tarji'.
  * *Term 2*: Complete recitation of the 7 Fara'id of Wudu in Arabic and English from memory.
  * *Term 3*: "The Salah Clinic": executing Sujud as-Sahw (Qabli vs Ba'di) across 5 blind scenarios.
* **Annual Milestone**: Awarding the prestigious **"Juz' 'Amma & Ibn 'Ashir Foundation Certificate"**, celebrating the complete memorisation of the 30th Juz', the core foundations of Ash'ari creed, and Maliki worship literacy.
