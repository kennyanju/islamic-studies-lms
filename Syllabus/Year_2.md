# Year 2 Syllabus: Habits of Devotion & Character

> **Target Learner**: Level 2 Track: Middle Childhood (Ages 8-10)  
> **Weekly Schedule**: 5 sessions × 30 minutes = 150 min/week  
> **Annual Instructional Volume**: 36 Weeks (3 Terms of 12 Weeks) = 90 hours  
> **ICO Benchmarks Absorbed**: Grade 3 and Grade 4 (worship conditions, pillars of iman, early revelation)  
> **Classical Text Spine**: *Matn al-'Ashmawiyyah* (Fiqh of Prayer) & Imam an-Nawawi's *40 Hadith* (Hadiths 1–10)  
> **Riwayah**: Hafs 'an 'Asim & Warsh 'an Nafi' (Juz' 'Amma mid-section, rules of Madd basics)

---

## Term 1: Pillars of Faith & The Sanctuary of Prayer (Weeks 1–12)

### Unit 2.1: The Six Pillars of Eemaan (Weeks 1–4)
* **Strand**: 'Aqidah (Ash'ari) & Hadith
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 30 min = 10 hours
* **Classical Reference**: Hadith Jibril (Hadith 2 of An-Nawawi's 40) / *'Aqidat al-'Awamm* (Lines 11–16).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 7–8)**:
  1. The child lists from memory the Six Articles of Faith: Belief in (1) Allah, (2) His Angels, (3) His Books, (4) His Messengers, (5) The Last Day, and (6) Divine Decree (*Al-Qadar*).
  2. The child distinguishes between *Islam* (outward physical actions of worship) and *Iman* (inner certitude and conviction of the heart).
  3. The child explains that true faith shows itself through cheerful obedience, honesty, and kindness to creation.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Ash'ari Epistemological breakdown: The definition of *Tasdiq* (assent of the heart) as the root of Iman.
  2. Does Iman increase and decrease? Comparing the Ash'ari position (the core assent is indivisible, but its illumination and righteous fruits fluctuate) with other theological frameworks.
* **Ash'ari Creed Flag**: Iman resides in the heart's submission; a sinner who commits a major sin does not exit Islam, refuting the extremist Kharijite doctrine of takfir.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say: Recognition Box**:
  > *Who were the Four Great Imams?*
  > 1. **Imam Abu Hanifa** (Kufa, Iraq): Known for his deep wisdom and generous character.
  > 2. **Imam Malik** (Madinah, Arabia): The Imam of Dar al-Hijrah, who preserved the lived tradition of the Prophet's city.
  > 3. **Imam al-Shafi'i** (Gaza / Makkah / Egypt): The master of the Arabic language and jurisprudence.
  > 4. **Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal** (Baghdad, Iraq): The patient champion of the Sunnah.
  > *Remember*: All four Imams agreed completely on the Six Pillars of Faith!
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:285); Hadith Jibril (Muslim).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Constructing a "Pillars of Faith Hexagon Mobile" to hang in the room; role-playing the arrival of Angel Jibril in pure white garments.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Nawawi Hadith 2 (opening dialogue on Iman). Surah Ash-Sharh and Surah Ad-Duha with clear vowel elongation (*Madd Tabee'ee*).

### Unit 2.2: Conditions (Shurut) of Salah & Sacred Boundaries (Weeks 5–8)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki)
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 30 min = 10 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Matn al-'Ashmawiyyah* (Fasl fi Shurut al-Salah).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 7–8)**:
  1. The child identifies the 5 necessary conditions (*Shurut*) that must be verified *before* stepping onto the prayer mat:
     * Purity from major and minor impurities (*Taharat al-Hadath*).
     * Purity of body, clothing, and place from physical filth (*Taharat al-Khabath*).
     * Covering the 'Awrah (navel to knee for boys; entire body except face and hands for girls).
     * Facing the Qiblah (the Ka'bah in Makkah).
     * Verification that the prayer time has entered (*Dukhul al-Waqt*).
  2. The child independently checks their clothing and prayer area before beginning prayer.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Differentiating between Conditions of Obligation (*Shurut al-Wujub*) and Conditions of Validity (*Shurut al-Sihhah*) in Maliki jurisprudence.
  2. The Maliki ruling on praying with a forgotten impurity (*Najasah*): Validity vs recommended repetition within the prayer time (*al-Waqt al-Ikhtiyari*).
* **Maliki Fiqh Flag**: In the Maliki school, if a person realizes after finishing prayer that there was an impurity on their clothes which they did not know about or forgot, their prayer remains valid, though repeating it within the time is recommended (*Mustahabb*), illustrating the school's avoidance of excessive hardship.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:144: *"Turn your face toward the Sacred Mosque"*); Hadith: *"Allah does not accept the prayer of any of you if he is in a state of impurity until he performs ablution."* (Bukhari).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "The Qiblah Compass Challenge": Learning how to find North/South/East/West using the sun and a compass app; checking mock prayer spaces for cleanliness.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Balad and Surah Ash-Shams. Memorising the prayer time du'a after hearing the Adhan.

### Unit 2.3: Rebuilding the Ka'bah & The Arbiter of Peace ﷺ (Weeks 9–12)
* **Strand**: Seerah & Islamic History
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 30 min = 10 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Ibn Hisham's Seerah* / *Zad al-Ma'ad* (Ibn al-Qayyim).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 7–8)**:
  1. The child narrates the flooding of the Ka'bah when the Prophet ﷺ was 35 years old and the Quraysh's decision to rebuild it using only pure, lawfully earned wealth (*Halal Tayyib*).
  2. The child describes the fierce dispute between the tribal leaders over who would have the honor of placing the Black Stone (*Al-Hajar al-Aswad*), almost causing a bloody war.
  3. The child explains the brilliant conflict resolution of the Prophet ﷺ: placing the Stone in the middle of a cloak and having every tribal chieftain lift a corner together.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Analyzing Prophetic leadership and conflict de-escalation: shared dignity, inclusive decision-making, and emotional intelligence.
  2. The historical boundaries of the Ka'bah: why the Quraysh ran out of pure wealth, leaving the semi-circular *Hijr Isma'il* outside the physical stone walls.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Anbiya' (21:107: *"And We have not sent you except as a mercy to the worlds."*).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Physical classroom reenactment: A group of children holding the edges of a large blanket to carry a symbolic "Black Stone" into place peacefully.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Layl and Surah Al-Fajr. Du'a for entering the mosque: *"Allahumma-ftah li abwaba rahmatik"*.

## Term 2: The Words of Salah & The Light of Revelation (Weeks 13–24)

### Unit 2.4: Praying 2, 3, and 4 Rak'ahs with Precision (Weeks 13–16)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki) & Adab al-Ikhtilaf
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 30 min = 10 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Matn al-'Ashmawiyyah* (Sifat al-Salah).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 7–8)**:
  1. The child performs a 2-rak'ah prayer (Fajr), a 3-rak'ah prayer (Maghrib), and a 4-rak'ah prayer (Dhuhr/'Asr/'Isha) with full vocalization and transitions.
  2. The child knows where to recite aloud (*Jahr*: Fajr, and first 2 rak'ahs of Maghrib/'Isha) and where to recite silently (*Sirr*: Dhuhr, 'Asr, and third/fourth rak'ahs).
  3. The child memorises the middle Tashahhud and the final Tashahhud with the Salawat Ibrahimiyyah.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Distinguishing between *Arkan* (Pillars that cannot be compensated by prostration) and *Sunan Mu'akkadah* (confirmed sunnahs compensated by *Sujud al-Sahw*) in prayer.
  2. The Maliki ruling on reciting the Basmalah before Surah Al-Fatihah: Disliked (*Makruh*) in obligatory prayers because it is not considered an ayah of Al-Fatihah in the relied-upon Madinan tradition.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say: Recognition Box**:
  > *Topic: Raising the Hands in Prayer (Takbir)*
  > *Maliki practice*: We raise our hands to shoulder level at the very beginning of prayer (*Takbirat al-Ihram*). During the rest of the prayer, we move into bowing and standing peacefully without raising our hands again (as recorded in the primary Maliki transmissions).
  > *Other Scholars Say*: Imam al-Shafi'i and Imam Ahmad taught raising hands before ruku' and when rising from ruku'.
  > *Remember*: Both ways were reported from the companions of the Prophet ﷺ. We never judge someone who raises their hands or leaves them peaceful.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Hadith: *"Pray as you have seen me pray."* (Bukhari).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "The Salah Simulator": The child takes turns being the "Prayer Leader" (Imam) for family members, practicing proper posture cues and quiet transitions.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Complete Tashahhud and Salawat Ibrahimiyyah (*"Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad wa 'ala ali Muhammad..."*). Surah Al-A'la.

### Unit 2.5: The Angels of Light & The Heavenly Scriptures (Weeks 17–20)
* **Strand**: 'Aqidah (Ash'ari)
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 30 min = 10 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *'Aqidat al-'Awamm* (Lines 17–25: Angels and Revealed Books).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 7–8)**:
  1. The child describes angels as noble beings created by Allah from light who never disobey Allah and tirelessly carry out His divine commands.
  2. The child identifies the major archangels and their responsibilities:
     * **Jibril** (Gabriel): Conveying Divine Revelation (*Wahy*).
     * **Mika'il** (Michael): Rain, clouds, and sustenance.
     * **Israfil** (Raphael): Blowing the Trumpet on the Day of Judgment.
     * **Malik**: Keeper of the Hellfire; **Ridwan**: Keeper of Paradise.
     * **Munkar & Nakir**: Questioning the soul in the grave.
     * **Kiram Katibin**: The noble recording angels on our shoulders.
  3. The child matches the Heavenly Books with their Prophets: The *Suhuf* (Scrolls) to Ibrahim, the *Tawrah* (Torah) to Musa, the *Zabur* (Psalms) to Dawud, the *Injeel* (Gospel) to 'Isa, and the *Qur'an* to Muhammad ﷺ.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The metaphysical nature of Angels: Free from gender, appetite, sleep, physical exhaustion, and free will in the human sense.
  2. The Speech of Allah (*Kalamullah*): Ash'ari distinction between the eternal uncreated attribute (*Kalam Nafsi*) and the revealed Arabic expression (*Kalam Lafzi*) preserved in codices.
* **Ash'ari Creed Flag**: The Qur'an in its divine meaning is the uncreated speech of Allah; books sent to previous nations were altered over time (*Tahrif*), but Allah guaranteed the preservation of the Final Qur'an.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:97–98); Surah Al-Hijr (15:9: *"Indeed, it is We who sent down the Qur'an and indeed, We will be its guardian."*).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Designing an "Angel Responsibility Chart" and a "Timeline of the Heavenly Books"; creative writing: "A day in the life of my recording angels".
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Ghashiyah and Surah At-Tariq. Bedtime du'a: *"Bismika Rabbi wada'tu janbi wa bika arfa'uh"*.

---

### Unit 2.6: The Cave of Hira & The First "Iqra'!" (Weeks 21–24)
* **Strand**: Seerah & Qur'an
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 30 min = 10 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Sahih al-Bukhari* (Kitab Bad' al-Wahy, Hadith 3).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 7–8)**:
  1. The child describes the Prophet's ﷺ habit of solitary reflection (*Tahannuth*) in the Cave of Hira atop Jabal an-Nur overlooking Makkah.
  2. The child narrates the dramatic encounter on the 27th night of Ramadan: The descent of Archangel Jibril, the three embraces, and the first revealed command: **"Iqra'!" (Read!)**.
  3. The child explains how Sayyidah Khadijah (RA) comforted the shivering Prophet ﷺ (*"Zammiluni, zammiluni!"*) and affirmed his moral integrity: *"Never! By Allah, Allah will never disgrace you, for you maintain family ties, help the poor, and honor the guest."*
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The epistemology of revelation: Distinguishing prophetic inspiration (*Wahy*) from poetic delusion, clairvoyance, or psychological projection.
  2. Sayyidah Khadijah's theological intuition: Drawing upon empirical moral character to discern divine favor; the consultation with the Christian scholar Waraqah ibn Nawfal.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-'Alaq (96:1–5); Hadith of 'A'ishah on the beginnings of revelation (Bukhari).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Diorama crafting of Mount Hira and the cave entrance; practicing expressive dramatic recitation of the first 5 verses of Surah Al-'Alaq.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-'Alaq (first 8 verses). Nawawi Hadith 1 (*"Innama al-a'malu bin-niyyat"*, Actions are judged by intentions).

## Term 3: Community, Family Rights, & The Living Sunnah (Weeks 25–36)

### Unit 2.7: Family Devotion & The Rights of Parents (Weeks 25–28)
* **Strand**: Akhlaq, Adab & Tazkiyah
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 30 min = 10 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Al-Adab al-Mufrad* (Chapters on Birr al-Walidayn).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 7–8)**:
  1. The child identifies that loving and obeying parents (*Birr al-Walidayn*) is the greatest deed after prayer in its proper time.
  2. The child identifies the special status of mothers: The hadith *"Your mother, then your mother, then your mother, then your father."*
  3. The child practices daily acts of domestic service: Cleaning their room without being asked, clearing dishes, and speaking with a soft voice.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The legal limits of obedience to parents: *"There is no obedience to creation in disobedience to the Creator"* combined with maintaining companionship in kindness (*Sahibhuma fi al-dunya ma'rufa*).
  2. The rights of extended family (*Silat al-Rahim*) and how severing ties shrinks spiritual blessings (*Barakah*).
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Luqman (31:14: *"Be grateful to Me and to your parents; to Me is the final destination."*); Hadith: *"Paradise lies at the feet of mothers."* (Ahmad/Nasa'i).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "The Parent Secret Kindness Mission": 7 consecutive days of secret service chores completed for parents without seeking immediate praise.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Burooj (first 10 verses). Du'a for parents from Surah Al-Isra' (17:24).

### Unit 2.8: Invalidators of Wudu (Nawaqid) & Salah Errors (Weeks 29–32)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki)
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 30 min = 10 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Matn al-'Ashmawiyyah* (Fasl Nawaqid al-Wudu).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 7–8)**:
  1. The child clearly identifies what breaks wudu:
     * Anything exiting the two private passages (urine, stool, wind).
     * Deep sleep where one is unaware of surroundings.
     * Loss of consciousness (fainting, sickness).
  2. The child learns that eating, laughing, or shedding a little blood does **not** break wudu in the Maliki school.
  3. The child knows what to do if they make a mistake in prayer: Stay calm, do not panic, and complete the prayer.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Precise Maliki categorization of *Nawaqid*:
     * *Ahdath* (direct bodily exits).
     * *Asbab al-Ahdath* (causes of exits like deep sleep, intoxication, insanity, and touching with pleasure).
     * *Al-Riddah* (apostasy) and intense doubt in purity (*Shakk*).
  2. The principle of certainty: In the Maliki school, if one is certain of wudu but doubts whether it broke, re-performing wudu is required for prayer to remove doubt completely.
* **Maliki Fiqh Flag**:
  * **Touching with pleasure**: Touching the opposite gender only breaks wudu in the Maliki school if accompanied by sexual pleasure or if pleasure was intended (unlike Shafi'is who break on any skin contact, and Hanafis who do not break except with intimacy).
  * **Bleeding**: Blood flowing from a cut does **not** break wudu in the Maliki school.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say: Recognition Box**:
  > *Topic: Bleeding and Wudu*
  > *Maliki practice*: If your nose bleeds or you get a paper cut, your wudu is still perfectly valid. You simply wash the blood off your skin or clothing.
  > *Hanafi view*: If blood flows past the wound, wudu is broken and must be repeated.
  > *Remember*: When you see a friend wash their wudu after a nosebleed, you understand they are following Imam Abu Hanifa!
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Hadith of the man who wondered if he passed wind during prayer: *"Do not leave unless you hear a sound or detect an odor."* (Bukhari).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "Fiqh Detective": Case-study scenario cards ("Zayd had a short 2-minute sitting nap," "Maryam got a small finger cut," "Bilal drank orange juice"), the child votes "Wudu Valid" or "Wudu Broken".
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Burooj (completion). Nawawi Hadith 3 (The Five Pillars of Islam).

### Unit 2.9: Merciful Stewardship: Animals, Water, and Environment (Weeks 33–36)
* **Strand**: Akhlaq & Islamic History
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 30 min = 10 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Riyad al-Salihin* (Chapters on Mercy to Animals and Avoiding Waste).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 7–8)**:
  1. The child narrates the hadith of the woman punished for locking up a cat, and the sinner forgiven by Allah for giving water to a thirsty dog using a shoe.
  2. The child practices water conservation during wudu: The Prophet ﷺ used just one *Mudd* (about two cupped hands of water) for his entire wudu!
  3. The child demonstrates kindness to plants, birds, and school grounds by picking up litter and planting seeds.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The Islamic concept of *Khilafah* (Vicegerency): Earth as a cosmic trust (*Amanah*) rather than a resource for unrestrained exploitation.
  2. The legal status of living animals in Maliki Fiqh: The bodily purity of all living creatures, including dogs, birds of prey, and domestic pets.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-An'am (6:38: *"And there is no creature on earth or bird that flies with its wings except that they are communities like you."*); Hadith: *"Do not waste water, even if you are on the banks of a flowing river."* (Ibn Majah).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "The Prophetic One-Mudd Wudu Challenge": Providing each child with a bowl of 600ml of water to complete a perfect Sunnah wudu without spilling or running out; planting a household herb garden.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Nawawi Hadith 4 (Creation in the Womb and Destiny). Surah At-Teen and Surah Al-Qadr.

## Year 2 Assessment & Review Framework
* **Weekly Hadith & Surah Recitation**: 5-minute individual oral testing every Friday.
* **Term Practicums**:
  * *Term 1*: Recite Six Articles of Faith + identify all 5 Conditions of Salah.
  * *Term 2*: Complete performance of 3-rak'ah Maghrib prayer with silent/loud rules and Tashahhud.
  * *Term 3*: "Fiqh Detective" scenario test: Correctly solving 10 wudu and cleanliness dilemmas.
* **Annual Milestone**: Awarding the "Habits of Devotion (Year 2) Certificate" confirming mastery of 10 Surahs of Juz' 'Amma, the first 10 Hadiths of Imam an-Nawawi, and practical prayer fluency.
