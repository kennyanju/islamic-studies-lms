# Level 4 Syllabus: Evidence, Law, & Prophetic Mission (Ages 12–14)

> **App Track**: **Level 4** (`level4`)  
> **Target Learner**: Ages 12–14 (Early Adolescent / Puberty / Years 7 & 8)  
> **ICO Benchmarks Absorbed**: Grade 7 and Grade 8  
> **Weekly Schedule**: **3 extended sessions × 70 minutes** = 210 min/week (126 h/year)  
> **Classical Text Spine**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (Salah, Fasting, Zakah, Hajj), Sanusi's *Umm al-Barahin*, *Al-Risalah* of Ibn Abi Zayd, and Qadi 'Iyad's *Al-Shifa*  
> **Riwayah**: Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 29: Surahs Al-Mulk, Al-Qalam, Al-Haqqah, Al-Ma'arij, Nuh, Al-Jinn, Al-Muzzammil; Meem Sakinah & Madd rules)

---

## Pedagogical Vision for Level 4 (Ages 12–14)

At ages 12–14, youth reach religious accountability (**Bulugh**). Level 4 addresses the transition into moral adulthood, providing **theological proofs, legal mechanics, and deep love for the Prophet ﷺ**:
* **Creed**: The 20 Necessary Attributes of Allah organized into the Sanusi framework (*Nafsiyyah*, *Salbiyyah*, *Ma'ani*, *Ma'nawiyyah*); the nature of Prophetic Infallibility (*'Ismah*).
* **Worship**: The complete fard and sunnah rulings of Zakah (calculating Nisab and 2.5%), detailed fasting expiations (*Kaffarah* vs *Fidyah*), travel concessions (*Qasr* and *Jam'*), and the complete rites of Hajj and 'Umrah.
* **Seerah & History**: The Madinan era: The defensive battles of Badr, Uhud, and the Trench; the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah; the peaceful Conquest of Makkah; the physical and moral portrait of the Prophet ﷺ from *Al-Shama'il*; the biographies and leadership models of the Four Rightly Guided Caliphs.
* **Comparative Foundation**: *"Other Scholars Say"* advances to **Reasons**: understanding *why* scholars differed (hadith reach, word definitions, *'Amal Ahl al-Madinah*, qiyas).

## Core Level Units (Ages 12–14)

1. **Unit 4.1: The 20 Necessary Attributes of Allah (The Sanusi Framework)**
   * Personal (*Wujud*), Negative (*Qidam*, *Baqa'*, *Mukhalafah*, *Qiyam bi-Nafs*, *Wahdaniyyah*), Qualitative (*Qudrah*, *Iradah*, *'Ilm*, *Hayat*, *Sam'*, *Basar*, *Kalam*), and State attributes; what is impossible and permissible for Allah.
2. **Unit 4.2: Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj & The Great Hijrah to Madinah**
   * Night journey to Jerusalem, ascension through the heavens, gifting of the 5 prayers; Cave Thawr with Abu Bakr; arriving in Madinah.
3. **Unit 4.3: The Fiqh of Zakah: Wealth, Nisab, & Social Justice**
   * The Nisab threshold (85g gold / 595g silver); 2.5% calculation on savings/cash; the 8 Qur'anic categories of recipients; Maliki exemption for worn jewelry.
4. **Unit 4.4: In-Depth Fasting: Kaffarah, Fidyah, & Medical Exemptions**
   * Making up days (*Qada'*) vs Expiation (*Kaffarah*); the Maliki preference for feeding 60 poor people; medical rulings (inhalers, injections, chronic illness).
5. **Unit 4.5: Travel Concessions: Shortening, Combining, & Khuffs**
   * Shortening 4-rak'ah prayers to 2 (*Qasr*) for journeys over 48 miles; combining prayers (*Jam'*); wiping over leather socks (*Khuffayn*) and medical casts (*Jaba'ir*).
6. **Unit 4.6: Defending the Sanctuary: Badr, Uhud, & The Trench**
   * Defensive combat ethics; Badr's divine aid; the archers' lesson at Uhud; Salman al-Farsi's trench; Hudaybiyyah and the Conquest of Makkah.
7. **Unit 4.7: Shama'il: The Sublime Person & Character of the Prophet ﷺ**
   * Physical description and noble temperament from Qadi 'Iyad's *Al-Shifa* and Tirmidhi's *Shama'il*; loving the Prophet ﷺ above oneself.
8. **Unit 4.8: The Fiqh of Hajj & 'Umrah (The Journey of a Lifetime)**
   * The 4 Pillars (*Arkan*): Ihram, Tawaf al-Ifadah, Sa'i, Standing at 'Arafah; the Maliki preference for *Ifrad*.
9. **Unit 4.9: The Rightly Guided Caliphs: Leadership & Legacy**
   * Abu Bakr (unity), 'Umar (justice & institutions), 'Uthman (Qur'an standardization), 'Ali (legal wisdom); revering all Companions.

## Hifz & Classical Matn Targets (Level 4)
* **Qur'an (Juz' 29 Selections)**: Surah Al-Mulk, Surah Al-Qalam, Surah Al-Haqqah, Surah Al-Ma'arij, Surah Nuh, Surah Al-Jinn, Surah Al-Muzzammil.
* **Tajweed**: Rules of **Meem Sakinah** (Ikhfa' Shafawi, Idgham Shafawi, Izhar Shafawi) and all rules of **Madd** (Wajib Muttasil, Ja'iz Munfasil, 'Arid lis-Sukun, Lazim).
* **Classical Matn**: *Ibn 'Ashir* (Lines 36 to 70: Salah, Fasting, Zakah, and Hajj) and opening 15 lines of *Qasidat al-Burdah*.
* **Hadith**: Imam an-Nawawi's *40 Hadith* (Hadiths 21 to 30).
* **Daily Adhkar**: Complete authentic set of Morning and Evening Fortress Adhkar (*Adhkar al-Sabah wal-Masa'*), travel supplications, and market du'a.
