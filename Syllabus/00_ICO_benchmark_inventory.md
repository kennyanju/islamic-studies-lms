# 00 — ICO Benchmark Inventory: Grades 1–12

> **Document Purpose**: Establishes the full reference benchmark from the International Curricula Organization (ICO, Riyadh) 12-Grade *Islamic Studies* curriculum. Every unit and lesson topic across all 12 grades is catalogued, tagged by strand, and assigned a confidence rating based on publisher samples, retailer listings, and curriculum documentation. This inventory serves as the baseline against which our 5-Year Ash'ari-Maliki Syllabus guarantees **100% coverage at equal or greater depth**.

---

## 1. Methodology & Confidence Legend

Because the International Curricula Organization does not distribute open unencumbered digital PDFs of all Teacher's Manuals, this benchmark has been synthesized from direct publisher previews (iconetwork.com), distributor listings (Noorart, Madinah Media, Jarir Books, Islamic Bookstore Australia), academic schemes of work, and established ICO grade syllabi.

| Confidence Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **✅ Verified** | Observed directly in published ICO tables of contents, publisher unit previews, or distributor sample pages. |
| **🟡 Inferred** | Inferred from documented ICO grade progression, spiral curricula milestones, or adjacent level syllabi. |
| **🛡️ Safety Net** | Standard Islamic studies topics mandated by international benchmarks at this age to prevent any coverage gap. |

### The 5 ICO Core Strands:
1. **'Aqeedah** (Creed & Articles of Faith)
2. **The Glorious Qur'an & Its Sciences** (Recitation, Memorisation, Tafsir, Tajweed, 'Ulum al-Qur'an)
3. **Seerah & Islamic History** (Prophetic Biography, Caliphate, Civilisation)
4. **'Ibaadaat & Mu'amalat** (Worship, Transactions, Family Law, Legal Rulings)
5. **Morals & Manners** (Akhlaaq, Adab, Adhkar, Social Responsibility, Contemporary Issues)
