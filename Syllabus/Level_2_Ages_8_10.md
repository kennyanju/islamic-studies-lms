# Level 2 Syllabus: Habits of Devotion & Character (Ages 8–10)

> **App Track**: **Level 2** (`level2`)  
> **Target Learner**: Ages 8–10 (Upper Primary / Years 3 & 4)  
> **ICO Benchmarks Absorbed**: Grade 3 and Grade 4  
> **Weekly Schedule**: 5 sessions × 30 minutes = 150 min/week (90 h/year)  
> **Classical Text Spine**: *Matn al-'Ashmawiyyah* & Imam an-Nawawi's *40 Hadith* (Hadiths 1–10)  
> **Riwayah**: Hafs 'an 'Asim (Surahs 101 to 86, natural madd, heavy/light letters)

---

## Pedagogical Vision for Level 2 (Ages 8–10)

At ages 8–10, learners transition from purely experiential learning into **structured habits, independent reading, and systematic worship**:
* **Creed**: The 6 Pillars of Iman deepened; recognizing the wisdom of sending Messengers; divine transcendence (*Tanzih*).
* **Worship**: Complete mastery of the physical mechanics of Salah; understanding conditions (*Shurut*) vs pillars (*Arkan*); Adhan and Iqamah; joining congregational prayer (*Jama'ah*).
* **Seerah**: The early Makkan period, persecution of early believers, steadfastness of Bilal and Yasir, the migration to Abyssinia, and the forgiving prayer of Ta'if.
* **Comparative Foundation**: *"Other Scholars Say"* advances to **Recognition**: learning the names and cities of the 4 Imams (Abu Hanifa, Malik, Al-Shafi'i, Ahmad), raising hands in prayer, and saying Amin.

## Core Level Units (Ages 8–10)

1. **Unit 2.1: The Architecture of Faith (The 6 Pillars of Iman)**
   * Hadith Jibril dialogue; distinguishing Islam (outward practice) from Iman (inner certitude); the angels and their duties.
2. **Unit 2.2: Conditions (Shurut) of Salah & Sacred Boundaries**
   * Purity from hadath and khabath; covering the 'awrah; facing the Qiblah using compass/sun; verifying prayer times.
3. **Unit 2.3: Rebuilding the Ka'bah & The Arbiter of Peace ﷺ**
   * The flood of Makkah; placing the Black Stone using the cloak; pre-prophetic moral leadership.
4. **Unit 2.4: Praying 2, 3, and 4 Rak'ahs with Precision**
   * Vocalizing Fajr, Dhuhr, 'Asr, Maghrib, 'Isha; loud (*Jahr*) vs silent (*Sirr*) recitations; Tashahhud and Salawat.
5. **Unit 2.5: The Angels of Light & The Heavenly Scriptures**
   * Jibril, Mika'il, Israfil, Malik; the Tawrah, Zabur, Injeel, and Qur'an; divine preservation of the Qur'an.
6. **Unit 2.6: Cave Hira, Revelation, & The Support of Khadijah (RA)**
   * Solitary contemplation; the descent of Jibril; "Iqra'!"; Khadijah's affirmation of the Prophet's ﷺ moral integrity.
7. **Unit 2.7: Family Devotion & The Rights of Parents**
   * Filial piety (*Birr al-Walidayn*); rights of mothers and fathers; rights of neighbors, teachers, and classroom adab.
8. **Unit 2.8: Invalidators of Wudu (Nawaqid) & Salah Errors**
   * Categorizing what breaks wudu; why bleeding and eating do not break wudu in Maliki fiqh; staying calm during prayer mistakes.
9. **Unit 2.9: Merciful Stewardship: Animals, Water, and Environment**
   * The woman and the cat; the forgiveness of the thirsty dog; using only one *Mudd* of water; caring for plants and streets.

## Hifz & Classical Matn Targets (Level 2)
* **Qur'an (16 Surahs)**: Al-Qari'ah, Al-'Adiyat, Az-Zalzalah, Al-Bayyinah, Al-Qadr, Al-'Alaq, At-Teen, Ash-Sharh, Ad-Duha, Al-Layl, Ash-Shams, Al-Balad, Al-Fajr, Al-Ghashiyah, Al-A'la, At-Tariq.
* **Tajweed**: Natural prolongation (*Madd Tabee'ee* - 2 counts), identifying the 7 permanently heavy letters (*Khussa Daghtin Qiz*), and basic rules of the letter Ra'.
* **Classical Matn**: *'Aqidat al-'Awamm* (Lines 11–25: The Prophets, Angels, and Heavenly Books).
* **Hadith**: Imam an-Nawawi's *40 Hadith* (Hadiths 1 to 10).
* **Daily Adhkar**: Complete Tashahhud (Sayyidina 'Umar formulation), Salawat Ibrahimiyyah, entering/leaving the mosque, and Ayat al-Kursi after prayer.
