# Level 1 Syllabus: Foundations of Wonder & Practice (Ages 6–8)

> **App Track**: **Level 1** (`level1`)  
> **Target Learner**: Ages 6–8 (Early Primary / Years 1 & 2)  
> **ICO Benchmarks Absorbed**: Grade 1 and Grade 2  
> **Weekly Schedule**: 5 sessions × 20–25 minutes = 100–125 min/week (60–75 h/year)  
> **Classical Text Spine**: Oral introduction to *'Aqidat al-'Awamm* & foundational rules of *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari*  
> **Riwayah**: Hafs 'an 'Asim (Oral recitation of Surahs 114 to 102, Makharij basics)

---

## Pedagogical Vision for Level 1 (Ages 6–8)

At ages 6–8, faith is cultivated through **wonder, sensory engagement, loving narrative, and daily habituation**. Young children learn through doing, seeing, role-playing, and gentle repetition:
* **Creed**: Wonder at the beauty and order of Allah's creation (*Al-Khaliq*, *Al-Wadud*); understanding that Allah has no partners or physical shape.
* **Worship**: Tactile wudu practicum (feeling the water, washing each limb thoroughly, rubbing with care); joyous participation in prayer postures (standing in *Sadl*, bowing, prostrating).
* **Character**: Learning love for Prophet Muhammad ﷺ through stories of his truthfulness (*Al-Amin*) and childhood; learning the greeting of peace (*Salam*), kindness to parents, and caring for animals.
* **Comparative Foundation**: *"Other Scholars Say"* introduced as awareness that Muslims pray in slightly different ways and that all follow the Prophet ﷺ with love.

## Year-by-Year Unit Structure (Years 1 & 2)

### Year 1 (Age 6–7)
1. **Unit 1.1: Allah Our Loving Creator & The Marvels of Creation** (The universe tells of Allah, *Laysa ka-mithlihi shay'*, gratitude through *Alhamdulillah*).
2. **Unit 1.2: The Beloved Messenger ﷺ: Childhood and Purity** (Year of the Elephant, Aminah & Halimah, the blessed orphan child).
3. **Unit 1.3: Clean Habits & Taharah: The Foundation of Prayer** (Clean vs unclean, bathroom etiquette, tooth care & siwak).
4. **Unit 1.4: The Shahaadah: Our Golden Promise of Faith** (The meaning of *La ilaha illa Allah, Muhammadur Rasulullah*).
5. **Unit 1.5: Hands-On Wudu Practicum (Maliki Method)** (Washing face, arms, wiping whole head, washing feet, gentle rubbing / *Dalk*, and continuity / *Muwalat*).
6. **Unit 1.6: The Trustworthy Youth ﷺ & Hilf al-Fudul** (Why Makkah called him *Al-Amin*; standing up for justice; truthfulness challenge).
7. **Unit 1.7: The Five Daily Prayers: Names, Times, and Rak'ahs** (Fajr, Dhuhr, 'Asr, Maghrib, 'Isha; building the habit).
8. **Unit 1.8: The Physical Postures of Salah (Sadl & Respect)** (Takbir, standing with arms at sides / *Sadl*, ruku', sujud; awareness of folded arms / *Qabd*).
9. **Unit 1.9: Manners of the Home, Gatherings, and Elders** (Spreading salam, right-hand eating, honoring parents).

### Year 2 (Age 7–8)
1. **Unit 2.1: The Six Pillars of Eemaan** (Belief in Allah, Angels, Books, Messengers, Last Day, Qadar; the story of Hadith Jibril).
2. **Unit 2.2: Conditions (Shurut) of Salah & Sacred Boundaries** (Clean body, clean clothes, clean space, Qiblah direction, covering 'awrah).
3. **Unit 2.3: Rebuilding the Ka'bah & The Arbiter of Peace ﷺ** (Placing the Black Stone on the cloak; resolving conflict peacefully).
4. **Unit 2.4: Praying 2, 3, and 4 Rak'ahs with Precision** (Silent vs loud recitation, Tashahhud, Salawat Ibrahimiyyah).
5. **Unit 2.5: The Angels of Light & The Heavenly Scriptures** (Jibril, Mika'il, Israfil; the Tawrah, Zabur, Injeel, and Qur'an).
6. **Unit 2.6: The Cave of Hira & The First "Iqra'!"** (Solitude, the first revelation, Sayyidah Khadijah's comforting words).
7. **Unit 2.7: Family Devotion & The Rights of Parents** (Dutifulness / *Birr al-Walidayn*, kindness to siblings and neighbors).
8. **Unit 2.8: Invalidators of Wudu (Nawaqid) & Salah Errors** (What breaks wudu; why eating or minor bleeding does not break wudu in Maliki fiqh).
9. **Unit 2.9: Merciful Stewardship: Animals, Water, and Environment** (The thirsty dog story, using only one *Mudd* of water for wudu).

## Hifz & Classical Matn Targets (Level 1)
* **Qur'an (14 Surahs)**: Al-Fatihah, An-Nas, Al-Falaq, Al-Ikhlas, Al-Masad, An-Nasr, Al-Kafirun, Al-Kawthar, Al-Ma'un, Quraysh, Al-Fil, Al-Humazah, Al-'Asr, At-Takathur.
* **Classical Matn**: *'Aqidat al-'Awamm* (Lines 1–10).
* **Hadith**: 3 Short Prophetic Sayings on Cleanliness, Steadfastness, and Good Words.
* **Daily Adhkar**: Morning/evening protection surahs, eating/drinking du'as, sleeping and waking du'as, Tashahhud.
