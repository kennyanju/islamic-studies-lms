# Year 1 Syllabus: Foundations of Wonder & Practice

> **Target Learner**: Age 6–7  
> **Weekly Schedule**: 5 sessions × 20 minutes = 100 min/week  
> **Annual Instructional Volume**: 36 Weeks (3 Terms of 12 Weeks) = 60 hours  
> **ICO Benchmarks Absorbed**: Grade 1 and Grade 2 (foundational stages)  
> **Classical Text Spine**: Oral introduction to *'Aqidat al-'Awamm* & foundational rules of *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari*  
> **Riwayah**: Hafs 'an 'Asim (oral recitation and sensory letter familiarity)

---

## Term 1: The Creator, The Prophet ﷺ, and Cleanliness (Weeks 1–12)

### Unit 1.1: Allah Our Loving Creator & The Marvels of Creation (Weeks 1–4)
* **Strand**: 'Aqidah (Ash'ari) & Akhlaq
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 20 min = 6.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *'Aqidat al-'Awamm* (Lines 1–4: Bismillah, praise of Allah, Affirming Divine Existence).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 6–7)**:
  1. The child articulates that Allah created the skies, trees, animals, and their own self out of Divine Love and Wisdom (*Al-Khaliq*, *Al-Wadud*).
  2. The child identifies that Allah cannot be seen with physical eyes in this life, has no parents, no children, and has no likeness (*Laysa ka-mithlihi shay'*).
  3. The child expresses gratitude to Allah by saying *"Alhamdulillah"* upon experiencing good things.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Systematic definition of the personal attribute *Al-Wujud* (Necessary Existence) and negative attribute *Al-Wahdaniyyah* (Oneness in Essence, Attributes, and Actions).
  2. Refutation of anthropomorphism (*Tashbih*) and cosmic accidentalism via teleological observation (*Dalil al-Kawn*).
* **Ash'ari Creed Flag**: Allah is not a physical body, does not occupy space or time, and needs nothing from creation (*Al-Ghaniyy*).
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say — Awareness Box**:
  > *Story*: When Imam Malik was asked how we know Allah exists without seeing Him, he pointed to the sour milk, the sweet honey, and the bitter olive coming from the very same soil and rain. All Sunni scholars agree that looking at nature with curiosity is a form of worship.
  > *What we do*: We love Allah and reflect on His gifts with humility.
  > *Remember*: Muslims of every school and every land share this love for Allah.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Ikhlas (112:1–4); Hadith: *"Allah is Beautiful and loves beauty."* (Muslim).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "Creation Scavenger Hunt" in the garden or park; creating a "Blessings Tree" where the child sticks leaves naming things Allah gave them.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Fatihah and Surah An-Nas with perfect oral makharij. Daily du'a: *"Rabbi zidni 'ilma"* (O Lord, increase me in knowledge).

---

### Unit 1.2: The Beloved Messenger ﷺ: Childhood and Purity (Weeks 5–8)
* **Strand**: Seerah, Shama'il & History
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 20 min = 6.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Al-Fusul fi Seerat ar-Rasul* (Ibn Kathir) / *Qadi 'Iyad's Al-Shifa* (Ch. on early childhood).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 6–7)**:
  1. The child narrates the key events of the Year of the Elephant (*'Am al-Fil*) and the miraculous protection of the Ka'bah.
  2. The child identifies the Prophet's ﷺ parents (Abdullah and Aminah) and his loving foster mother Halimah al-Sa'diyyah in the desert.
  3. The child learns that the Prophet ﷺ was an orphan who grew up under the care of his grandfather 'Abd al-Muttalib and uncle Abu Talib, yet remained pure and kind.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Theological analysis of prophetic preparation (*Irhast*): the splitting of the chest (*Shaqq al-Sadr*) as divine purification.
  2. Socio-economic conditions of Pre-Islamic Arabia and divine providence in preserving the prophetic lineage.
* **Ash'ari Creed Flag**: Infallibility (*'Ismah*) of the prophets even prior to formal commission from major sins and degrading character traits.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Fil (105:1–5) and Surah Ad-Duha (93:6: *"Did He not find you an orphan and give you refuge?"*).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Building a 3D clay model of the desert hills of Bani Sa'd; puppet/shadow storytelling of the Year of the Elephant and the birds (*Ababil*).
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Fil and Surah Al-Falaq. Salawat upon the Prophet: *"Allahumma salli 'ala Sayyidina Muhammad"*.

### Unit 1.3: Clean Habits & Taharah: The Foundation of Prayer (Weeks 9–12)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki) & Akhlaq
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 20 min = 6.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari* (Kitab al-Taharah, Sections on clean water and hygiene).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 6–7)**:
  1. The child distinguishes between clean (*Tahir*) and unclean/impure (*Najis*) substances.
  2. The child masters proper bathroom manners: entering with the left foot, saying the entering du'a, cleaning thoroughly with water (*Istinja*), and leaving with the right foot saying *"Ghufranak"*.
  3. The child practices daily hand-washing, trimming fingernails, and using the siwak or toothbrush before prayer.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Legal classification of waters in Maliki fiqh: *Mutlaq/Tahur* (purifying), *Tahir* (pure but unpurifying), and *Najis* (impure).
  2. Legal parameters of removal of physical impurities (*Izalat al-Najasah*) from body, clothing, and prayer area.
* **Maliki Fiqh Flag**: Water remains purifying as long as its color, taste, or odor has not been altered by an impurity, reflecting the school's practical preference for ease and non-rigidity.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say — Awareness Box**:
  > *Story*: All four Imams agreed that cleanliness is half of faith (*Shatr al-Iman*). Some scholars say you can clean yourself with stones or toilet paper alone (*Istijmar*), while all agree that finishing with clean water is the best and most complete practice.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:222: *"Indeed, Allah loves those who turn to Him and loves those who purify themselves."*); Hadith: *"Cleanliness is half of faith."* (Muslim).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Color-sorting water bottles (clear pure water vs water changed with food coloring vs soapy water) to demonstrate the concept of *Tahur* water.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Bathroom entering and exiting du'as. Surah Al-Ikhlas recitation with clear *Qalqalah*.

## Term 2: The Two Testimonies & The Steps of Wudu (Weeks 13–24)

### Unit 1.4: The Shahaadah: Our Golden Promise of Faith (Weeks 13–16)
* **Strand**: 'Aqidah (Ash'ari) & Qur'an
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 20 min = 6.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *'Aqidat al-'Awamm* (Lines 5–10: The Apostles and Books).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 6–7)**:
  1. The child recites the Shahaadah in Arabic with accurate pronunciation and understands its English meaning: *"There is none worthy of worship except Allah, and Muhammad is the Messenger of Allah."*
  2. The child identifies that saying the Shahaadah from the heart enters a person into the worldwide family of Muslims.
  3. The child explains that obeying Allah and following the Prophet's ﷺ sunnah is how we keep our golden promise.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Epistemology of the Shahaadah: The necessity of knowledge (*'Ilm*), certainty (*Yaqin*), and submission (*Inqiyad*) according to Ash'ari theologians.
  2. Understanding the two components: Negation (*Nafy*: *La ilaha*) followed by Affirmation (*Ithbat*: *illa Allah*).
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Muhammad (47:19: *"Know that there is no deity except Allah"*); Hadith Jibril (First pillar of Islam).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Crafting an illuminated Islamic calligraphy art piece of the Shahaadah to hang in the child's bedroom; group choral recitation.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Masad and Surah An-Nasr. Adhkar: *"Raditu billahi Rabba, wa bil-Islami dina, wa bi-Muhammadin Nabiyya"* (I am pleased with Allah as my Lord, Islam as my religion, and Muhammad as my Prophet).

### Unit 1.5: Hands-On Wudu Practicum (Maliki Method) (Weeks 17–20)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki)
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 20 min = 6.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari* / *Al-Murshid al-Mu'in* (Fara'id al-Wudu).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 6–7)**:
  1. The child independently executes the complete physical routine of wudu without omitting any limb.
  2. The child distinguishes the major actions: washing the face, arms including elbows, wiping the **entire** head from front to back, and washing feet including ankles.
  3. The child practices rubbing the limbs gently (*Dalk*) and maintaining continuity (*Muwalat*) without turning the water tap on full blast.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The 7 Fara'id (Obligations) of Wudu in the Maliki School: *Niyyah*, Washing Face, Washing Arms to Elbows, Wiping the Entire Head, Washing Feet to Ankles, *Dalk* (rubbing), and *Muwalat* (continuity).
  2. The Sunnan of Wudu: Washing hands to wrists 3 times at start, rinsing mouth (*Madmadah*), sniffing water into nose (*Istinshaq*), blowing it out (*Istinthar*), wiping ears, and adhering to the prescribed order (*Tartib*).
* **Maliki Fiqh Flag**:
  * **Wiping the whole head**: Malikis require wiping the complete head from hairline to nape of neck, while Shafi'is allow a few hairs and Hanafis require a quarter.
  * **Dalk (Rubbing)**: Rubbing the limb with the hand while pouring water is an obligatory pillar (*Fard*) in the Maliki school, ensuring complete contact.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say — Awareness Box**:
  > *Story*: When you visit mosques in Turkey, Egypt, or Saudi Arabia, you will see people wiping just the front of their hair, or leaving the tap running. Imam Malik taught that gently wiping the whole head and rubbing the skin with your wet hand honors the exact way the Sahabah saw the Prophet ﷺ perform wudu in Madinah.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:6); Hadith of 'Uthman (RA) demonstrating the Prophet's wudu (Bukhari & Muslim).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "Wudu Bathroom Master Certification": The child performs wudu under the teacher/parent's gentle observation and receives a physical "Junior Mutatahhir" badge.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Wudu completion du'a: *"Ashhadu alla ilaha illallah wahdahu la sharika lah, wa ashhadu anna Muhammadan 'abduhu wa rasuluh"*. Surah Al-Kafirun.

### Unit 1.6: The Trustworthy Youth ﷺ & Hilf al-Fudul (Weeks 21–24)
* **Strand**: Seerah & Akhlaq
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 20 min = 6.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah* (Ibn Hisham).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 6–7)**:
  1. The child describes how the people of Makkah nicknamed Prophet Muhammad ﷺ **"Al-Amin"** (The Trustworthy) and **"As-Sadiq"** (The Truthful) because he never lied or broke a promise.
  2. The child narrates the story of the League of the Virtuous (*Hilf al-Fudul*), where young Muhammad ﷺ joined an alliance to protect strangers and oppressed people from injustice.
  3. The child commits to always telling the truth, returning borrowed toys, and defending classmates who are teased.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The ethical validity of universal pre-Islamic moral pacts: analyzing the Prophet's ﷺ post-prophetic praise of Hilf al-Fudul ("Even in Islam, I would answer it").
  2. Trade ethics in Makkah: the commercial enterprise of Sayyidah Khadijah and the test of integrity in business transactions.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah At-Tawbah (9:119: *"O you who believe, fear Allah and be with those who are truthful."*); Hadith: *"Truthfulness leads to righteousness, and righteousness leads to Paradise."* (Bukhari).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Role-play drama: The merchant from Yemen seeking his stolen goods and the youth of Makkah standing up for him; "Truth Challenge Week" sticker chart.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Kawthar and Surah Al-Ma'un. Daily du'a before eating: *"Bismillah wa 'ala barakatillah"*.

## Term 3: Standing Before Allah: The 5 Prayers & Good Manners (Weeks 25–36)

### Unit 1.7: The Five Daily Prayers: Names, Times, and Rak'ahs (Weeks 25–28)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki)
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 20 min = 6.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari* (The Timings and Rak'ahs of Salah).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 6–7)**:
  1. The child names the 5 daily prayers in order: Fajr (Dawn - 2 rak'ahs), Dhuhr (Midday - 4 rak'ahs), 'Asr (Afternoon - 4 rak'ahs), Maghrib (Sunset - 3 rak'ahs), and 'Isha (Night - 4 rak'ahs).
  2. The child identifies that Salah is the Second Pillar of Islam and the direct conversation between a servant and Allah.
  3. The child begins the habit of joining family prayers for at least 1 or 2 prayers each day.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The theological reality of *Mawaqit* (Cosmic prayer windows) as signs of Divine Order (*Nizam al-Kawn*).
  2. Distinction between preferred time (*Waqt Ikhtiyari*) and necessary time (*Waqt Daruri*) in Maliki jurisprudence.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah An-Nisa (4:103: *"Indeed, prayer has been decreed upon the believers a decree of specified times."*); Hadith: *"The first matter about which the slave will be held accountable on the Day of Judgment is prayer."* (Tirmidhi).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Creating a colorful "Sun & Sky Clock" wheel showing the position of the sun during each prayer time; daily Salah sticker chart.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Quraysh and Surah At-Takathur. Practice reciting the Adhan words orally.

---

### Unit 1.8: The Physical Postures of Salah (Sadl & Respect) (Weeks 29–32)
* **Strand**: Fiqh (Maliki) & Adab al-Ikhtilaf
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 20 min = 6.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari* (Sifat al-Salah) / *Ibn 'Ashir* (Pillars of Salah).
* **Core Outcomes (Age 6–7)**:
  1. The child demonstrates the postures with stillness and calmness (*Tumani'nah*): Takbirat al-Ihram, Qiyam (standing), Ruku' (hands on knees, back straight), I'tidal (rising), Sujud (7 points touching the ground), and Jalsah.
  2. The child learns the Maliki practice of standing with arms naturally at the sides (**Sadl**) with dignity and reverence.
  3. The child learns that other Muslims fold their arms across their chest (**Qabd**) and that both are beautiful ways of worshiping Allah.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. Detailed analysis of *Sadl* (praying with hands at sides) in the Maliki Madhhab: The evidence from *'Amal Ahl al-Madinah* (the living practice of thousands of descendants of the Sahabah in Madinah) recorded in *Al-Mudawwanah*.
  2. How Imam Malik viewed *Qabd* as permissible in voluntary prayers (*Nawafil*) or as a secondary narration.
* **🌍 Other Scholars Say — Awareness Box**:
  > *Story*: When Imam Malik taught in the Prophet's Mosque in Madinah, he prayed with his arms resting straight at his sides (*Sadl*), because that is how he saw the scholars of Madinah pray. Imam Abu Hanifa, Imam al-Shafi'i, and Imam Ahmad placed their right hand over their left (*Qabd*). When we see someone pray with hands folded or at their sides, we smile, because both follow authentic narrations from the Prophet ﷺ!
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Baqarah (2:238: *"And stand before Allah in devout obedience."*); Hadith of the man who prayed improperly (*Al-Musi'u Salatahu*).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: "Salah Movement Mirror": The child mirrors the teacher's deliberate, peaceful prayer movements; gentle posture adjustments for back straightness in ruku'.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: The Tashahhud (Sayyidina Umar's formulation relied upon in the Maliki school: *"Al-Tahiyyatu lillah, az-zakiyatu lillah, at-tayyibatu as-salawatu lillah..."*). Surah Al-'Asr.

---

### Unit 1.9: Manners of the Home, Gatherings, and Elders (Weeks 33–36)
* **Strand**: Akhlaq, Adab & Tazkiyah
* **Session Allocation**: 20 sessions × 20 min = 6.6 hours
* **Classical Reference**: *Al-Adab al-Mufrad* (Imam al-Bukhari) / Selections from *Tanbih al-Ghafilin*.
* **Core Outcomes (Age 6–7)**:
  1. The child consistently initiates the greeting of peace: *"As-Salamu 'Alaykum wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh"* and answers with equal warmth.
  2. The child eats and drinks sitting down, with the right hand, saying Bismillah before and Alhamdulillah after.
  3. The child practices listening without interrupting when parents and elders speak, kissing parents' hands in respect, and sharing toys with siblings.
* **Extension Outcomes (13+)**:
  1. The metaphysical impact of Adab: Imam Malik's advice to Ibn al-Qasim: *"Learn good manners before you learn sacred knowledge."*
  2. The social cohesion of the Ummah built upon filial piety (*Birr al-Walidayn*) and the eradication of selfishness.
* **Qur'an & Hadith Evidence**: Surah Al-Isra' (17:23: *"And your Lord has decreed that you not worship except Him, and to parents, good treatment."*); Hadith: *"He is not one of us who does not show mercy to our young and respect to our elders."* (Tirmidhi).
* **Pedagogical Activities**: Hosting an "Adab Tea Party": The child serves water or tea to their parents with both hands, offers greetings, and practices table etiquette.
* **Hifz & Memorisation**: Surah Al-Humazah and Surah Al-Bayyinah (oral familiarity). Du'a for parents: *"Rabbi-rhamhuma kama rabbayani saghira"*.

## Year 1 Assessment & Review Framework
* **Weekly Oral Check-ins**: 2-minute recitation check on Friday during circle time.
* **Term Practicum**:
  * *Term 1*: Wudu demonstration (order, complete washing, not wasting water).
  * *Term 2*: Shahaadah oral meaning explanation and Salah movements demonstration.
  * *Term 3*: Complete 2-rak'ah prayer recited aloud with correct postures and Tashahhud.
* **Annual Milestone**: Awarding the "Foundations of Faith (Year 1) Certificate" celebrating completion of 14 Surahs of Juz' 'Amma, foundational wudu/salah fluency, and living prophetic manners at home.
