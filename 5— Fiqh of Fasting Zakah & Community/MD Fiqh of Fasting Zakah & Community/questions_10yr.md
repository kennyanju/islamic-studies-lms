### Part B: Scenario-Based Questions (5 Questions)
*Read the scenario and choose or write the correct response based on Maliki rulings.*

11. **Amina makes her intention to fast on the first night of Ramadan. On the tenth day, she becomes very sick and cannot fast for three days. When she recovers and is ready to fast again on the thirteenth day, what must she do regarding her intention?**
    *   *Answer:* Amina must make a new intention (*Niyyah*) the night before she resumes fasting. Because her continuous fasting was interrupted by illness, the single intention she made at the start of Ramadan is no longer covering her fasts.

12. **Bilal wants to pay his Zakat al-Fitr. He buys some dates and wants to know when is the absolute best time (Mustahabb) to give it to the poor family down the street. What should you tell him?**
    *   *Answer:* The best time to pay Zakat al-Fitr is on Eid morning, **after the dawn (Fajr) prayer but before going out to perform the Eid prayer**.

13. **Maryam is a wealthy 9-year-old girl who has savings from an inheritance that have been sitting in her bank account for a full year. Her savings are above the Nisab threshold. Does she owe Zakah in the Maliki school?**
    *   *Answer:* Yes. In the Maliki school, Zakah is obligatory on the wealth of children. Her parents or guardian must calculate and pay Zakah (2.5%) from her savings on her behalf. (Hanafis, on the other hand, say children do not owe Zakah).

14. **Yusuf's father wants to pay Zakat al-Fitr. He decides to give a bag of flour that weighs 2.4 kg to a poor family. Is this amount correct?**
    *   *Answer:* Yes. One *Sa'* of flour or wheat is approximately 2.1 to 2.4 kg, which satisfies the requirement of one *Sa'* per person.

15. **A poor traveler arrives in a Muslim town on Eid night. He has no food or money. The townspeople are busy celebrating and do not feed him, and he goes hungry. Who is held responsible for this in Islamic law?**
    *   *Answer:* The entire community is responsible. Feeding the poor and travelers is a **Fard Kifayah** (communal obligation). Since the townspeople neglected this duty, the whole community shares in the sin.

### Part C: Short Answer & Matching (5 Questions)
*Answer the questions briefly or match the terms.*

16. **Match the Arabic term to its correct definition:**
    *   1. *Imsak* -> ______ (Answer: Abstaining from food and drink)
    *   2. *Hilal* -> ______ (Answer: The new crescent moon)
    *   3. *Nisab* -> ______ (Answer: The minimum threshold for Zakah)
    *   4. *Mudd* -> ______ (Answer: A double-handful scoop)

17. **What are the two levels of fasting that a Muslim should try to achieve?**
    *   *Answer:* 
        1. Fasting of the stomach and private parts (refraining from food, drink, etc.).
        2. Fasting of the limbs and heart (refraining from lying, arguing, jealousy, and bad deeds).

18. **Why does the Maliki school prefer feeding 60 poor people over fasting two months for Kaffarah?**
    *   *Answer:* Because feeding 60 poor people has a direct, social benefit (*mutata'addi*) for the needy members of the community, whereas fasting only benefits the individual person.

19. **How many extra Takbeers do we recite in the Eid prayer according to the Maliki school?**
    *   *Answer:* There are **6 extra Takbeers** in the first Rak'ah (after the opening Takbeer) and **5 extra Takbeers** in the second Rak'ah (before reading Quran).

20. **Why is voluntary charity called "Sadaqah"? What word does it come from?**
    *   *Answer:* It comes from the word *Sidq*, which means "truthfulness." Giving charity proves that a person's faith in Allah is true and sincere.
