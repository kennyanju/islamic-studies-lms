# Level 4 Quiz: Fiqh of Fasting, Zakah & Community

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. What percentage of qualifying surplus savings and business inventory must be distributed annually as obligatory Zakah?
   (A) Exactly 1.0% (one hundredth).
   (B) Exactly 5.0% (one twentieth).
   (C) Exactly 2.5% (one fortieth).
   (D) Exactly 10.0% (one tenth).

2. What is the classic Nisab threshold for gold in Islamic jurisprudence?
   (A) 10 grams of pure gold.
   (B) 500 grams of pure gold.
   (C) 1 kilogram of pure gold.
   (D) 20 gold dinars, equivalent to approximately 85 grams of pure gold.

3. What does the juristic prerequisite of "Al-Hawl" signify in Zakah calculations?
   (A) The wealth must be earned through international export commerce.
   (B) One full continuous lunar year (approximately 354 days) must pass while maintaining qualifying wealth at or above the Nisab.
   (C) The wealth must be deposited in an official state treasury.
   (D) The Zakah must be paid during the first ten days of Ramadan.

4. What is the relied-upon position of the Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali schools regarding Zakah on women's personal gold jewelry?
   (A) Lawful jewelry kept for customary personal adornment is exempt from annual Zakah, as it is classified as personal use property.
   (B) All personal jewelry is taxed at an elevated rate of 5.0%.
   (C) Jewelry must be melted down into coins before Zakah can be accepted.
   (D) Zakah is mandatory on jewelry only if it was inherited from grandparents.

5. How many legitimate categories of Zakah recipients are explicitly defined in Surah At-Tawbah (9:60)?
   (A) Four categories.
   (B) Six categories.
   (C) Eight categories.
   (D) Ten categories.

6. What is the fundamental legal difference between "Al-Qada'" and "Al-Kaffarah" in the fiqh of fasting?
   (A) Qada' is paid as cash, whereas Kaffarah is a formal congregational prayer.
   (B) Qada' makes up missed days one-for-one due to legitimate excuses or mistake, whereas Kaffarah is a major expiation for deliberate, defiant violation of Ramadan.
   (C) Qada' applies only to minors, whereas Kaffarah is required exclusively for the elderly.
   (D) Qada' can only be performed in Makkah during the pilgrimage season.

7. In the Maliki school, what is the preferred and primary option when performing Kaffarah for an intentional violation of Ramadan?
   (A) Fasting six days in Shawwal.
   (B) Sponsoring a student's travel expenses.
   (C) Sacrificing three camels in Mina.
   (D) Feeding sixty poor persons (one Mudd of staple food each), or fasting two consecutive lunar months.

8. How many essential pillars (Arkan) of Hajj are codified in classical Maliki manuals such as Matn Ibn 'Ashir?
   (A) Four pillars: Al-Ihram, Tawaf al-Ifadah, Al-Sa'i, and Wuquf bi-'Arafah.
   (B) Two pillars: Ihram and slaughtering an animal.
   (C) Six pillars: Tawaf, Sa'i, Jamarat, Mina, Muzdalifah, and Madinah.
   (D) Seven pillars including drinking Zamzam water.

9. Why did the Prophet ﷺ declare in a sahih narration that "Hajj is 'Arafah"?
   (A) Because 'Arafah is the historic site where the Ka'bah was first constructed.
   (B) Because pilgrims spend all five days of Hajj stationed at 'Arafah.
   (C) Because standing on the plain of 'Arafah during the prescribed time on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah is the indispensable core pillar of Hajj.
   (D) Because the sacrificial animals of Hajj are distributed exclusively at 'Arafah.

10. Which mode of Hajj is classified as the most virtuous and preferred in the Maliki legal tradition?
    (A) Hajj al-Tamattu' (performing 'Umrah first, exiting Ihram, then entering Hajj).
    (B) Hajj al-Ifrad (performing Hajj alone, followed by 'Umrah at a later time).
    (C) Hajj al-Qiran (combining both rites under a single Ihram).
    (D) Hajj al-Badal (performing pilgrimage on behalf of another person).

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Nisab ─── The minimum threshold of surplus wealth before Zakah becomes due
* Al-Hawl ─── The passage of one full lunar year over the qualifying Nisab
* Al-Fuqara' ─── The poor who earn less than half of their basic survival needs
* Kaffarah ─── Major expiation (feeding 60 poor or fasting 2 consecutive months)
* Wuquf at 'Arafah ─── The indispensable pillar of Hajj performed on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. A person holds $8,000 in qualifying personal savings. Assuming an illustrative gold Nisab of $5,500, calculate their exact Zakah liability after one full lunar year.
2. Explain the socio-economic philosophy of Zakah: How does circulating 2.5% of surplus liquid assets prevent capital stagnation and build community solidarity?
3. Contrast the Maliki and Hanafi rulings on women's personal gold jewelry: What is Imam Malik's legal reasoning for treating personal jewelry as exempt personal property?
4. Contemplate the spiritual experience of the Day of 'Arafah: Why do scholars describe it as an earthly reflection of the Day of Standing before Allah?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. C - 2.5% is the fixed Qur'anic and prophetic rate for liquid assets and trade inventory.
2. D - 20 dinars corresponds to 85 grams of pure gold, representing the classical gold Nisab benchmark.
3. B - Al-Hawl requires the passage of a full lunar cycle (approx. 354 days) while holding qualifying wealth at or above Nisab.
4. A - Malikis, Shafi'is, and Hanbalis exempt jewelry intended for customary personal adornment.
5. C - Surah At-Tawbah 9:60 enumerates exactly eight specific recipient categories.
6. B - Qada' replaces days one-for-one; Kaffarah penalizes deliberate rebellious desecration of the sacred fast.
7. D - The Maliki school prioritizes feeding sixty poor persons as the preferred community expiation.
8. A - In Matn Ibn 'Ashir, the four pillars of Hajj are Ihram, Tawaf al-Ifadah, Sa'i, and Wuquf at 'Arafah.
9. C - Missing the standing at 'Arafah between noon and dawn invalidates the entire Hajj pilgrimage.
10. B - Hajj al-Ifrad is the preferred Maliki methodology based on Madinan practice and the narration of Sayyidah 'A'ishah.
