# Level 4 Quiz: Fiqh of Fasting, Zakah & Community

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. What percentage of qualifying surplus wealth must be distributed annually as Zakah?
   (A) 1.0%
   (B) 2.5% (or 1/40th)
   (C) 5.0%
   (D) 10.0%

2. What is the standard Nisab threshold calculated using pure gold?
   (A) 10 grams of gold.
   (B) 85 grams of pure gold.
   (C) 500 grams of gold.
   (D) 1 kilogram of gold.

3. What does the condition of "Al-Hawl" mean in Zakah calculations?
   (A) The wealth must be earned through international maritime trade.
   (B) A full lunar year (approx. 354 days) must pass while holding wealth above the Nisab.
   (C) The wealth must be deposited in a government treasury.
   (D) The Zakah must be paid during the daylight hours of Eid day.

4. What is the position of the Maliki, Shafi'i, and Hanbali schools regarding Zakah on women's personal gold jewelry?
   (A) It is subject to a 10% penalty tax.
   (B) Personal jewelry kept for customary, lawful adornment is exempt from Zakah.
   (C) It must be melted down and given to the poor.
   (D) It must be weighed every Friday.

5. How many distinct recipient categories for Zakah are explicitly defined in Surah At-Tawbah (9:60)?
   (A) Four categories.
   (B) Eight categories.
   (C) Twelve categories.
   (D) Unlimited categories at the Imam's discretion.

6. What distinguishes *Qada'* from *Kaffarah* in the jurisprudence of fasting?
   (A) Qada' is a cash payment, while Kaffarah is a prayer.
   (B) Qada' makes up missed days one-for-one, while Kaffarah is a major expiation for deliberate, rebellious violation of Ramadan.
   (C) Qada' applies only to children, while Kaffarah is for the elderly.
   (D) Kaffarah can only be performed in Makkah.

7. What is the preferred order of performing *Kaffarah* in the Maliki Madhhab?
   (A) Donating twenty horses to the army.
   (B) Feeding sixty poor persons (or fasting two consecutive months).
   (C) Building a road between Makkah and Madinah.
   (D) Standing on one foot for twenty-four hours.

8. How many essential pillars (Arkan) of Hajj are codified in classical Maliki manuals like *Matn Ibn 'Ashir*?
   (A) Two pillars.
   (B) Four pillars: Ihram, Tawaf al-Ifadah, Sa'i, and Wuquf at 'Arafah.
   (C) Seven pillars.
   (D) Twelve pillars.

9. Why did the Prophet ﷺ declare that "Hajj is 'Arafah"?
   (A) Because 'Arafah is where all pilgrim luggage is stored.
   (B) Because standing on the plains of 'Arafah on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah is the indispensable core pillar of Hajj.
   (C) Because 'Arafah is the highest mountain in Arabia.
   (D) Because the pilgrims buy their sacrificial animals at 'Arafah.

10. Which mode of Hajj is classified as most virtuous in the Maliki school?
    (A) Hajj al-Tamattu'.
    (B) Hajj al-Qiran.
    (C) Hajj al-Ifrad (performing Hajj alone, followed by 'Umrah).
    (D) Performing Hajj without wearing Ihram garments.

2. The requirement that wealth be maintained for a complete lunar year is termed ______.
3. Debtors who are overwhelmed by lawful obligations they cannot pay are called ______.
4. The severe expiation imposed for intentionally violating a Ramadan fast is ______.
5. The crowning pillar of Hajj celebrated on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah is the standing at ______.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Nisab ─── The minimum threshold of surplus wealth before Zakah becomes due
* Al-Hawl ─── The passage of one full lunar year over the Nisab
* Al-Fuqara' ─── The poor who lack basic essentials of survival
* Kaffarah ─── Major expiation (feeding 60 poor or fasting 2 consecutive months)
* Wuquf at 'Arafah ─── The indispensable pillar of Hajj performed on the 9th of Dhul-Hijjah

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. A young professional saves $8,000 in a bank account. Assuming the gold Nisab is $5,500, calculate their exact Zakah liability after one full lunar year.
2. Explain the socio-economic philosophy of Zakah: How does circulating 2.5% of surplus wealth prevent economic oligarchy and class resentment?
3. Contrast the Maliki and Hanafi views on women's personal jewelry. What is Imam Malik's reasoning for exempting personal jewelry?
4. Describe the spiritual atmosphere of the Day of 'Arafah: Why is it considered a preview of the Day of Resurrection?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - 2.5% is the fixed Qur'anic and prophetic rate for liquid assets and trade goods.
2. B - 85 grams of 24k gold (or 20 Mithqals) represents the classical Nisab benchmark.
3. B - Al-Hawl requires the passage of a full lunar cycle (354 days) of wealth retention.
4. B - Malikis, Shafi'is, and Hanbalis exempt jewelry intended for personal lawful adornment.
5. B - Surah At-Tawbah 9:60 enumerates exactly 8 specific recipient categories.
6. B - Qada' replaces days 1-for-1; Kaffarah penalizes deliberate rebellious desecration of the fast.
7. B - The Maliki school prioritizes feeding 60 poor people as the most beneficial community expiation.
8. B - The four arkan are Ihram, Tawaf al-Ifadah, Sa'i, and Wuquf at 'Arafah.
9. B - Missing the standing at 'Arafah between noon and dawn invalidates the entire Hajj pilgrimage.
10. C - Hajj al-Ifrad is the preferred Maliki methodology based on Madinan practice.
