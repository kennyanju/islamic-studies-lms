# Level 3 Quiz: Fiqh of Fasting, Zakah & Community

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. What is the primary spiritual objective of fasting established in Surah Al-Baqarah (2:183)?
   (A) To attain Taqwa (God-consciousness and moral self-restraint).
   (B) To experience physical exhaustion.
   (C) To alter sleep patterns permanently.
   (D) To eliminate the obligation of daily prayers.

2. What are the two universal pillars (Arkan) of fasting in Islamic jurisprudence?
   (A) Reciting twenty pages of history and attending community lectures.
   (B) Performing Tayammum and wearing unstitched clothing.
   (C) Donating one third of all personal assets to the mosque.
   (D) The Intention (Al-Niyyah) in the heart and Abstention (Al-Imsak) from true dawn to sunset.

3. In the Maliki school, what is the ruling regarding the intention (Niyyah) for Ramadan?
   (A) An intention must be written on parchment every Friday afternoon.
   (B) It is only valid if renewed verbally after every prayer.
   (C) A single comprehensive intention formed on the first night of Ramadan suffices for the entire uninterrupted month.
   (D) A separate intention before every dawn is strictly mandatory, or the fast is void.

4. In the relied-upon Maliki position (Matn Ibn 'Ashir, Al-Risalah), if a person eats or drinks out of pure forgetfulness (Nisyan) during an obligatory Ramadan fast, what must they do?
   (A) They may eat and drink for the remainder of that day, then fast two consecutive months after Ramadan as Kaffarah.
   (B) Stop immediately, complete the rest of that day's fast, and make up (Qada') that single day after Ramadan without any Kaffarah penalty.
   (C) Their fast remains completely unaffected and no make-up day (Qada') is required in either obligatory or voluntary fasts.
   (D) They must stop eating immediately, complete the day's fast, and feed sixty poor people one Mudd of staple food each.

5. Which of the following medical treatments does NOT invalidate a person's fast?
   (A) Swallowing oral prescription tablets with water.
   (B) Drinking liquid medicine for a cough.
   (C) Ingesting nutritional glucose supplements through the mouth.
   (D) A non-nutritive medical injection (such as insulin or a vaccine) into the muscle or vein.

6. Under what condition is a traveler legally granted the concession (Rukhsah) to break their fast in Ramadan?
   (A) When undertaking a lawful journey exceeding the shortening distance (approximately 48 miles / 80 km).
   (B) On any daily commute that involves riding public transport.
   (C) Only when crossing an ocean on a sailing vessel.
   (D) Only if they are walking on foot without supplies.

7. What is the legal compensation (Fidyah) required for an elderly or chronically ill person who is permanently incapable of fasting?
   (A) Sponsoring the construction of a city library.
   (B) Fasting sixty days during the winter months.
   (C) Feeding one poor person for each missed day of Ramadan.
   (D) Planting fruit trees in their neighborhood.

8. What are the Four Indispensable Pillars (Arkan) of Hajj according to the Maliki school?
   (A) Suhur, Iftar, Taraweeh, and Itikaf.
   (B) Al-Ihram, Tawaf al-Ifadah, Al-Sa'i, and Al-Wuquf bi-'Arafah.
   (C) Reciting Surah Al-Kahf, donating gold, fasting three days, and drinking Zamzam.
   (D) Visiting Madinah, wearing green robes, slaughtering three sheep, and shaving the head.

9. What is the recommended prophetic Sunnah regarding the pre-dawn meal (Suhur)?
   (A) Delaying Suhur until shortly before the true dawn (Fajr) to maintain strength and receive barakah.
   (B) Eating Suhur before going to sleep at midnight.
   (C) Skipping Suhur completely to show greater asceticism.
   (D) Consuming only liquids without solid food.

10. What is the Nisab threshold for gold in calculating annual Zakah?
    (A) 200 gold dinars, which equals approximately 595 grams of pure gold.
    (B) 5 gold dinars, which equals approximately 21.25 grams of pure gold.
    (C) 40 gold dinars, which equals approximately 170 grams of pure gold.
    (D) 20 gold dinars, which equals approximately 85 grams of pure gold.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Al-Imsak ─── Refraining from food, drink, and nullifiers from true dawn to sunset
* Maliki Monthly Niyyah ─── A single intention on Night 1 covering the entire consecutive month
* Suhur ─── The blessed pre-dawn meal delayed until shortly before Fajr
* Fidyah ─── Feeding a poor person for each day missed due to chronic illness or old age
* Wuquf bi-'Arafah ─── Standing on the plain of 'Arafah, the decisive pillar of Hajj

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. Explain the wisdom behind the Maliki ruling allowing a single monthly intention for Ramadan: How does viewing Ramadan as a unified act of worship grant ease to families?
2. Detail the Maliki ruling on forgetfulness during an obligatory fast: Why is the person required to make up (Qada') the day while remaining free of any sin or Kaffarah?
3. What spiritual lessons can adolescents draw from standing on the plain of 'Arafah during Hajj, where all social distinctions disappear?
4. How does the economic mechanism of Zakah and the Nisab threshold protect society from the accumulation of wealth in few hands?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. A - Surah Al-Baqarah 2:183 establishes that the ultimate purpose of fasting is achieving Taqwa.
2. D - Niyyah and Imsak are the two universal pillars of a valid fast in Islamic jurisprudence.
3. C - In Maliki fiqh, a single comprehensive intention on night 1 covers the entire consecutive 30-day month.
4. B - In Maliki fiqh (Matn Ibn 'Ashir line 158), forgetful eating carries no sin and no Kaffarah, but requires completing the day and performing Qada' after Ramadan.
5. D - Non-nutritive injections that do not nourish via the digestive tract do not break the fast.
6. A - A lawful journey exceeding 48 miles (80 km) qualifies for the concession of breaking the fast.
7. C - Fidyah provides daily nutritional compensation to a needy person when physical fasting is permanently impossible.
8. B - In Maliki law, the four pillars of Hajj are Ihram, Tawaf al-Ifadah, Sa'i, and Wuquf bi-'Arafah.
9. A - The Prophet ﷺ recommended delaying Suhur as close to Fajr as possible to maintain physical vitality.
10. D - The classic gold Nisab is 20 dinars, which corresponds to approximately 85 grams of pure gold.
