## 🌙 1. The Fiqh Pillars of Fasting (Sawm)
In Islamic law, **Sawm** is an obligatory act of worship resting upon **Two Essential Pillars (Rukn)**:
1. **The Intention (Al-Niyyah):** Purely dedicating the fast to Allah in your heart before true dawn (*Fajr*).
2. **Abstaining (Al-Imsak):** Completely refraining from eating, drinking, and any invalidators from the break of dawn until the full setting of the sun (*Maghrib*).

## 🌾 2. Who is Exempt from Fasting?
Allah is full of mercy and does not burden any soul beyond its capacity. The Qur'an specifies exemptions:
* **The Sick:** Anyone with an acute illness that would worsen with fasting may break their fast and make up missed days later (*Qada'*).
* **The Traveler:** Anyone on a long journey (over 48 miles) has the concession (*Rukhsah*) to eat and make up the days.
* **The Elderly & Chronically Ill:** Those permanently unable to fast feed one poor person for each missed day (*Fidyah*).
* **Young Children:** Fasting is not legally obligatory until the age of puberty (*Bulugh*), though training at ages 8–10 is highly encouraged!

## 🕌 3. Taraweeh: The Night Vigil of the Community
During the evenings of Ramadan, Muslims gather after 'Isha prayer for special night prayers called **Taraweeh**:
* **The Name:** *Taraweeh* comes from *Raha* (rest), because the worshippers rested every four rak'ahs while glorifying Allah.
* **The Sunnah in Madinah:** In the city of Madinah, during the time of the early righteous scholars and Imam Malik, the community prayed **thirty-six rak'ahs** of Taraweeh plus three rak'ahs of Witr, filling the night with Qur'an!
* **The Reward:** The Prophet ﷺ promised:  
  > *"Whoever stands in prayer during Ramadan with faith and expecting Allah's reward, all their past sins will be forgiven!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari).

## ⚖️ 4. Zakah vs. Sadaqah: Purifying Wealth
In Islam, economic justice is built on circulating wealth so that money does not remain only with the rich:

| Dimension | Obligatory Zakah | Voluntary Sadaqah |
|---|---|---|
| **Status** | 3rd Pillar of Islam (Mandatory) | Highly recommended Sunnah |
| **Who Must Pay** | Muslims whose wealth exceeds the **Nisab** for 1 full lunar year | Anyone, rich or poor, at any time |
| **Amount** | Exactly **2.5%** (1/40th) of qualifying surplus savings | Any amount, large or small |
| **Recipients** | 8 specific Quranic categories (Fuqara', Masakin, etc.) | Any good cause, human, or animal! |

## 🍞 5. Zakat al-Fitr: The Charity of Fast-Breaking
Before the Eid al-Fitr prayer, every Muslim household head must pay **Zakat al-Fitr** on behalf of themselves and all dependents (including children!):
* **What is Given:** One full **Sa'** (approximately four double-handfuls / 2.5 kg) of staple food of the country (such as rice, wheat, dates, or barley).
* **The Purpose:** To purify the fasting person from minor shortcomings and ensure that no poor person goes hungry on the day of Eid!
