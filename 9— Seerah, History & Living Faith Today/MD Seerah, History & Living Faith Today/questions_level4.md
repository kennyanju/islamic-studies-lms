# Level 4 Quiz: Seerah, History & Living Faith Today

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ instruct the Ummah regarding the Sunnah of the Rightly Guided Caliphs?
   (A) To follow their administrative decisions only within the borders of the Arabian peninsula.
   (B) To set aside their legal precedents once formal schools of jurisprudence formed.
   (C) To adhere firmly to their Sunnah along with his Sunnah and bite onto it with molar teeth.
   (D) To consult their historical actions as optional cultural suggestions without legal weight.

2. What historic crisis did Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq decisively resolve during his two-year caliphate?
   (A) Settling maritime border disputes with the Byzantine empire.
   (B) Resolving competing currency standards across provincial markets.
   (C) Negotiating agricultural trade treaties with Persian regional governors.
   (D) The Apostasy Wars (Hurub al-Riddah) and false claimants to prophethood, preserving communal unity and the Quran.

3. Which major state institutions and civil systems were established during the caliphate of Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab?
   (A) A hereditary royal succession council, minted gold dinars bearing caliphal portraits, and provincial tax-farming monopolies.
   (B) The Islamic Hijri calendar, the public state treasury (Bayt al-Mal), social welfare stipends, and independent judicial courts.
   (C) The translation bureau of Bayt al-Hikmah in Baghdad, paper mills in Samarkand, and standardized madrasa endowments.
   (D) A standing palace guard in Damascus, mandatory military conscription for non-Muslim citizens, and maritime customs ports.

4. How did Sayyidina 'Umar enter the city of Jerusalem in 16 AH upon concluding its peaceful surrender?
   (A) Walking on foot leading his mount, upon which his servant took turns riding, wearing humble patched garments.
   (B) Marching at the front of a heavily armored cavalry division in full ceremonial regalia.
   (C) Remaining stationed outside the gates until the civic leadership delivered gold tribute.
   (D) Entering in a royal carriage escorted by Roman imperial envoys.

5. Why did Sayyidina 'Umar decline the invitation of Patriarch Sophronius to perform his prayer inside the Church of the Holy Sepulchre?
   (A) Because he considered praying inside any structure built of stone impermissible.
   (B) Because he was waiting for specific guidance from the consultative council in Madinah.
   (C) Because the legal time window for the prayer had already expired before entering.
   (D) Because he feared future Muslims might use his prayer as a pretext to convert the church into a mosque, thereby protecting Christian worship.

6. What crucial service did Sayyidina 'Uthman ibn 'Affan render to safeguard the transmission of the Holy Quran?
   (A) He commissioned a single personal scroll that remained privately locked in his family library.
   (B) He ordered the recitation to be restricted to scholars residing only within Makkah.
   (C) He authorized the standardization and distribution of the Uthmanic Master Codex (Al-Mushaf al-Imam) to unite all regions.
   (D) He instructed regional governors to translate the text into regional vernaculars.

7. How did Sayyidina 'Uthman respond when armed agitators besieged his residence in Madinah?
   (A) He refused to permit Muslim blood to be shed in his defense, choosing patient endurance until he met martyrdom while reciting the Quran.
   (B) He commanded the city garrison to execute all protesters without trial.
   (C) He abdicated immediately and relocated his administration to Damascus.
   (D) He dissolved the council of senior companions and called for international mediation.

8. Which authentic hadith describes the lofty station of Sayyidina 'Ali ibn Abi Talib before the Prophet ﷺ?
   (A) "The leadership after me shall belong exclusively to 'Ali and his descendants."
   (B) "You are to me in the position that Harun was to Musa, except that there is no prophet after me."
   (C) "All legal rulings of the companions are void unless ratified by 'Ali."
   (D) "'Ali is the sole compiler and interpreter of the revelation."

9. What is the core Sunni doctrine concerning the moral uprightness of the Companions ('Adalat al-Sahabah)?
   (A) That individual companions never made personal administrative mistakes in worldly matters.
   (B) That only the companions who migrated to Abyssinia are considered reliable in religion.
   (C) That all companions are morally upright and trustworthy in transmitting religion, praised by Allah in the Quran, and deserving of reverence.
   (D) That later generations have authority to evaluate their intentions and declare specific companions insincere.

10. What warning did the Prophet ﷺ deliver regarding disparaging or insulting his companions?
    (A) That violators must fast three consecutive months as legal penance.
    (B) That insults would result in disqualification from civil appointments in Madinah.
    (C) That verbal abuse of elders requires paying an annual fine to the public treasury.
    (D) That if someone were to spend Mount Uhud in gold, it would not equal a handful of their deeds nor even half of it.

## Part C: Matching Caliphs & Legacies
* Abu Bakr al-Siddiq ─── Preserved Islamic unity during the Riddah crisis and compiled the first Quranic sheets
* 'Umar al-Faruq ─── Established the Hijri calendar, Bayt al-Mal, and entered Jerusalem with exemplary justice
* 'Uthman Dhun-Nurayn ─── Standardized the Uthmanic Master Codex and financed community wells and expeditions
* 'Ali ibn Abi Talib ─── Renowned for decisive legal judgments, supreme chivalry, and noble steadfastness
* 'Adalat al-Sahabah ─── The theological doctrine affirming the universal moral uprightness of all Companions

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. Analyze Sayyidina 'Umar's conduct upon entering Jerusalem: How does the Covenant of 'Umar provide an enduring historical framework for minority rights and religious freedom?
2. Why did Sayyidina 'Uthman choose patient martyrdom rather than deploy armed force against fellow Muslims? What does this demonstrate regarding the sanctity of Muslim life?
3. What leadership qualities made Sayyidina Abu Bakr the indispensable pillar of the community during the acute grief of the Prophet's death?
4. How should contemporary Muslim adolescents respond when encountering online sectarian content that insults the Companions or the Mothers of the Believers?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. C - Adhering firmly to the Sunnah of the Khulafa' al-Rashidun is explicitly commanded in authentic Hadith (Sunan Abi Dawud 4607).
2. D - Abu Bakr's steadfast resolve overcame the Riddah crisis, preserving communal unity and safeguarding the Quran.
3. B - 'Umar was the architect of foundational civil systems: the Hijri calendar, Bayt al-Mal, welfare stipends, and independent judiciary.
4. A - 'Umar walked into Jerusalem on foot sharing turns on a single camel with his servant, wearing patched garments.
5. D - 'Umar preserved Christian shrines by praying outside, establishing that Muslims could not claim the church as a mosque.
6. C - 'Uthman standardized master codices (Al-Masahif) to preserve textual consistency across expanding regions.
7. A - 'Uthman chose martyrdom over shedding Muslim blood in civil conflict, demonstrating prophetic forbearance.
8. B - In Sahih al-Bukhari 3701 and Sahih Muslim 2404, the Prophet ﷺ compared 'Ali to Harun's relationship with Musa.
9. C - Ahl al-Sunnah affirm that all companions are upright ('Udool), praised by Allah in Surah At-Tawbah 9:100.
10. D - The Prophet ﷺ prohibited disparaging his companions, noting that spending Mount Uhud in gold could not match their deeds (Sahih al-Bukhari 3673).
