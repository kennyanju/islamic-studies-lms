# Level 4 Quiz: Seerah, History & Living Faith Today

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ instruct the Ummah regarding the Sunnah of the Four Rightly Guided Caliphs?
   (A) To ignore their rulings once thirty years had passed.
   (B) To adhere firmly to their Sunnah and bite onto it with molar teeth.
   (C) To translate their speeches into ancient Greek.
   (D) To write poems about them only on Fridays.

2. What historic crisis did Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq decisively resolve during his two-year caliphate?
   (A) A severe winter storm that destroyed the date palms.
   (B) The Apostasy Wars (Hurub al-Riddah) and false prophets, preserving the unity of the Ummah and the Quran.
   (C) An invasion by the navy of ancient Carthage.
   (D) A monetary dispute over copper coinage.

3. Which major state institution was established by Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab?
   (A) The Islamic Hijri calendar, the state treasury (Bayt al-Mal), and public welfare stipends.
   (B) The Olympic sports games in Makkah.
   (C) The construction of stone pyramids in Egypt.
   (D) The printing press in Damascus.

4. How did Sayyidina 'Umar enter the liberated city of Jerusalem in 16 AH?
   (A) Riding on a golden stallion surrounded by 500 bodyguards.
   (B) Walking on foot leading his camel, upon which his servant was riding, wearing patched robes.
   (C) Sailing on a war galley up the Jordan river.
   (D) Flying banners of silk while throwing coins to the crowd.

5. Why did Sayyidina 'Umar refuse the invitation of the Christian Patriarch Sophronius to pray inside the Church of the Holy Sepulchre?
   (A) Because he was angry with the Patriarch.
   (B) Because he feared that future Muslims would convert the historic church into a mosque, thereby protecting Christian heritage.
   (C) Because the church floor was too dusty.
   (D) Because the prayer time had already expired.

6. What great service did Sayyidina 'Uthman ibn 'Affan render to the preservation of the Holy Quran?
   (A) He commissioned the standardization of master codices (Al-Masahif al-Uthmaniyyah) and distributed them to imperial capitals.
   (B) He translated the Quran into Persian.
   (C) He prohibited anyone from reciting Juz' 'Amma.
   (D) He wrote the first dictionary of Arabic synonyms.

7. How did Sayyidina 'Uthman react when armed rebels surrounded his home demanding his resignation?
   (A) He ordered his royal guard to execute all the protesters immediately.
   (B) He refused to allow Muslim blood to be shed in his defense, meeting martyrdom while reciting the Quran.
   (C) He escaped secretly on a trade ship to Rome.
   (D) He surrendered the city of Madinah to Persian forces.

8. What honorific title did the Prophet ﷺ bestow upon Sayyidina 'Ali regarding legal and spiritual wisdom?
   (A) "The King of Yemen."
   (B) "I am the city of knowledge, and 'Ali is its gate!"
   (C) "The architect of the desert."
   (D) "The treasurer of gold coins."

9. What is the fundamental Sunni creedal position regarding the Companions of the Prophet (*Sahabah*)?
   (A) They were ordinary politicians whose actions should be condemned on social media.
   (B) They are all upright (*'Udool*), praised by Allah in the Quran, and believers must love and revere them all without insult.
   (C) Only five companions are recognized as true believers.
   (D) The companions have no relevance to modern Islamic law.

10. What severe warning did the Prophet ﷺ give regarding anyone who insults or disparages his companions?
    (A) They will be fined ten silver dirhams.
    (B) Even if an ordinary person spent Mount Uhud in gold, it would not equal a handful of the companions' deeds.
    (C) They must walk on foot from Damascus to Makkah.
    (D) They will be denied entry into public markets.

## Part C: Matching Caliphs & Legacies
* Abu Bakr al-Siddiq ─── Preserved Islamic unity during the Riddah crisis and compiled the first Quran
* 'Umar al-Faruq ─── Established the Hijri calendar, Bayt al-Mal, and liberated Jerusalem with justice
* 'Uthman Dhun-Nurayn ─── Standardized the Uthmanic Master Codex and financed the Tabuk expedition
* 'Ali ibn Abi Talib ─── The gate of knowledge renowned for profound legal wisdom and chivalry
* 'Adalat al-Sahabah ─── The theological doctrine of the universal moral uprightness of all Companions

3. What leadership qualities made Sayyidina Abu Bakr the indispensable rock of the Ummah during the catastrophic shock of the Prophet's death?
4. How should contemporary Muslim students respond when encountering online sectarian content that insults the Companions or the Mothers of the Believers?
