## 🏰 1. The Conquest of Makkah (Fath Makkah / 8 AH)
In the eighth year of the Hijrah, when the pagan Quraysh violated their peace treaty, the Prophet ﷺ marched toward Makkah with **10,000 companions**.

* **The Entry of Supreme Humility:** He did not enter like a proud conqueror seeking blood or revenge. Instead, he entered riding his camel with his head bowed so low in gratitude to Allah that his beard almost touched his saddle!
* **The Cleansing of the Ka'bah:** He approached the 360 stone idols surrounding the Ka'bah, pointing his staff and reciting:  
  > **جَآءَ ٱلْحَقُّ وَزَهَقَ ٱلْبَٰطِلُ ۚ إِنَّ ٱلْبَٰطِلَ كَانَ زَهُوقًا**  
  > *"The truth has arrived, and falsehood has perished! Truly, falsehood is bound to perish!"* (Surah Al-Isra' 17:81).
* **The General Pardon:** He looked upon the trembling leaders of Quraysh who had tortured his companions and boycotted his family, and said:  
  > *"Go, for you are completely free!"* (`اِذْهَبُوا فَأَنْتُمُ الطُّلَقَاءُ`).

## 👑 2. The Four Rightly Guided Caliphs (Al-Khulafa' al-Rashidun)
The Prophet ﷺ commanded: *"Adhere to my Sunnah and the sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs after me!"*

| Caliph | Title & Reign | Defining Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| **Abu Bakr al-Siddiq** | *Al-Siddiq* (11–13 AH) | Preserved the unity of Islam during the Riddah crisis and commissioned the first compilation of the Qur'an. |
| **'Umar ibn al-Khattab** | *Al-Faruq* (13–23 AH) | Established the Islamic Hijri calendar, the public state treasury (*Bayt al-Mal*), and liberated Jerusalem with supreme justice. |
| **'Uthman ibn 'Affan** | *Dhun-Nurayn* (23–35 AH) | Standardized the authoritative Uthmanic Master Codex (*Al-Mushaf al-Imam*), uniting the world upon one script. |
| **'Ali ibn Abi Talib** | *Bab al-'Ilm* (35–40 AH) | The fountainhead of legal wisdom, chivalry (*Futuwwah*), and transcendent moral steadfastness. |

## 🕌 3. Our Scholarly Heritage: Imam Malik of Madinah
Who was **Imam Malik ibn Anas** (93–179 AH), whose teachings form the foundation of our Maliki fiqh?
* **Born in the City of the Prophet:** He grew up in Madinah, studying directly under the children and students of the Sahabah!
* **Al-Muwatta:** He authored the world's first major compiled book of Hadith and Islamic law: *Al-Muwatta* (The Well-Trodden Path).
* **Deep Reverence for the Prophet ﷺ:** Whenever someone asked him a Hadith of the Prophet ﷺ, Imam Malik would make full wudu, put on his finest clothes and perfume, sit up straight with tears in his eyes, and refuse to move until he had finished reciting! He never rode a horse in Madinah out of respect for the soil where the Prophet's ﷺ body rests.
* **The Four Great Sunni Schools:**
  1. **Hanafi School:** Founded by Imam Abu Hanifah in Kufa, Iraq.
  2. **Maliki School:** Founded by Imam Malik in the blessed city of Madinah.
  3. **Shafi'i School:** Founded by Imam Muhammad ibn Idris al-Shafi'i in Cairo, Egypt.
  4. **Hanbali School:** Founded by Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal in Baghdad, Iraq.

All four Imams were righteous masters of truth who loved and respected each other. As students of knowledge, we study the Maliki school with discipline while honoring and loving all four Sunni traditions!
