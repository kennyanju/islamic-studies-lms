## 1. The Conquest of Makkah (Fath Makkah, 8 AH)
In the eighth year of the Hijrah, when the Quraysh broke the terms of the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah, the Prophet ﷺ marched peacefully toward Makkah with ten thousand companions (Sahih al-Bukhari 4287).

* **The Entry of Supreme Humility:** The Prophet ﷺ did not enter like a proud ruler seeking vengeance. Instead, he entered riding his camel with his head lowered so humbly in gratitude to Allah that his beard touched his saddle.
* **The Cleansing of the Ka'bah:** Approaching the 360 stone idols surrounding the Ka'bah, he struck them with his staff and recited:  
  > **جَآءَ ٱلْحَقُّ وَزَهَقَ ٱلْبَٰطِلُ ۚ إِنَّ ٱلْبَٰطِلَ كَانَ زَهُوقًا**  
  > *"The truth has arrived, and falsehood has perished! Truly, falsehood is bound to perish!"* (Surah Al-Isra' 17:81, Sahih al-Bukhari 4720).
* **The General Pardon:** Addressing the assembled people of Makkah who had persecuted believers for two decades, he granted a general amnesty:  
  > *"Go, for you are completely free!"* (Recorded in Ibn Hisham's *Sirah* and Sunan al-Bayhaqi).

## 2. The Four Rightly Guided Caliphs (*Al-Khulafa' al-Rashidun*)
The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Adhere firmly to my Sunnah and the sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs after me"* (Sunan Abi Dawud 4607, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 2676).

| Caliph | Title & Reign | Defining Contribution |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Abu Bakr al-Siddiq** | *Al-Siddiq* (11-13 AH) | Preserved the unity of the Ummah during the crisis of apostasy (*Riddah*) and initiated the first gathering of Quranic parchments. |
| **'Umar ibn al-Khattab** | *Al-Faruq* (13-23 AH) | Established the Hijri calendar, organized the public treasury (*Bayt al-Mal*), and entered Jerusalem in exemplary justice. |
| **'Uthman ibn 'Affan** | *Dhun-Nurayn* (23-35 AH) | Prepared and distributed the standardized Uthmanic Master Codex (*Al-Mushaf al-Imam*), preserving Quranic recitation globally. |
| **'Ali ibn Abi Talib** | *Abu Turab* (35-40 AH) | Renowned for judicial excellence, chivalry (*Futuwwah*), and moral steadfastness (Sahih al-Bukhari 3701, Sahih Muslim 2404). |

## 3. Our Scholarly Heritage: Imam Malik of Madinah
Who was **Imam Malik ibn Anas** (93-179 AH), whose legal methodology forms the foundation of our Maliki fiqh?
* **Born in the City of the Prophet:** He spent his life in Madinah, studying directly under the scholars and descendants of the Companions.
* **Al-Muwatta:** He compiled *Al-Muwatta* (The Well-Trodden Path), uniting authentic Hadith with the living legal consensus of Madinah (*'Amal Ahl al-Madinah*).
* **Deep Reverence for the Prophet ﷺ:** Whenever teaching Hadith, Imam Malik performed wudu, wore clean garments and perfume, and sat with complete stillness and respect.
* **Where Did the Maliki School Spread?** Students traveled to Madinah from across the Islamic world. The Maliki Madhhab became the relied-upon school throughout **North Africa** (Morocco, Algeria, Tunisia, Libya, Egypt), **West Africa** (Mauritania, Senegal, Mali, Nigeria), the Arabian Gulf, and historically **Muslim Spain (Al-Andalus)**.
* **The Four Great Sunni Schools:**
  1. **Hanafi School:** Founded by Imam Abu Hanifah (d. 150 AH) in Kufa, Iraq.
  2. **Maliki School:** Founded by Imam Malik (d. 179 AH) in Madinah al-Munawwarah.
  3. **Shafi'i School:** Founded by Imam Muhammad ibn Idris al-Shafi'i (d. 204 AH) in Egypt.
  4. **Hanbali School:** Founded by Imam Ahmad ibn Hanbal (d. 241 AH) in Baghdad, Iraq.

All four Imams shared deep mutual respect and were united upon orthodox Sunni creed. As students, we study the Maliki school while honoring and respecting all four schools.
