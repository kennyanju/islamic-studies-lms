# Level 3 Quiz: Seerah, History & Living Faith Today

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. In what year of the Hijri calendar did the peaceful Conquest of Makkah (Fath Makkah) take place?
   (A) 2 AH.
   (B) 6 AH.
   (C) 8 AH.
   (D) 11 AH.

2. How did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ physically enter the city of Makkah during its conquest?
   (A) Standing proudly on a golden chariot with trumpets blaring.
   (B) Riding his camel with his head bowed so low in gratitude that his beard almost touched his saddle.
   (C) Refusing to enter until all citizens brought him gold tribute.
   (D) Carried on a royal palanquin by Roman soldiers.

3. What historic words did the Prophet ﷺ proclaim to the trembling chieftains of Quraysh after cleansing the city?
   (A) "You will all be imprisoned for twenty years!"
   (B) "No reproach upon you today! Go, for you are completely free!"
   (C) "All your homes are now confiscated by the state."
   (D) "You must pay an annual penalty tax."

4. What verse of the Quran did the Prophet ﷺ recite as he pointed his staff at the 360 idols inside the Ka'bah?
   (A) "Al-Hamdu lillahi Rabbil-'Alamin."
   (B) "The truth has come, and falsehood has perished. Indeed, falsehood is bound to perish!" (Surah Al-Isra' 17:81).
   (C) "Say: He is Allah, the One."
   (D) "Tabarak alladhi bi-yadihi al-Mulk."

5. Whom did the Prophet ﷺ honor by commanding him to climb atop the roof of the Ka'bah and call the Adhan?
   (A) Sayyidina Khalid ibn al-Walid.
   (B) Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah.
   (C) Abu Sufyan.
   (D) Sayyidina 'Ali ibn Abi Talib.

6. Approximately how many companions attended the Farewell Pilgrimage (*Hajjat al-Wada'*) alongside the Prophet ﷺ?
   (A) 313 companions.
   (B) 1,400 companions.
   (C) Over 100,000 companions.
   (D) 5,000 companions.

7. What universal principle of racial equality did the Prophet ﷺ proclaim in his Farewell Sermon at Mount 'Arafah?
   (A) That wealth determines human excellence.
   (B) That an Arab has no superiority over a non-Arab, nor white over black, except by piety (*Taqwa*).
   (C) That only members of the Quraysh tribe may hold leadership.
   (D) That language determines a person's status in Paradise.

8. Which momentous verse was revealed on the Day of 'Arafah during the Farewell Pilgrimage?
   (A) "Read in the name of your Lord who created."
   (B) "This day I have perfected for you your religion, completed My favor upon you, and have chosen for you Islam as your religion." (5:3).
   (C) "Say: O disbelievers, I do not worship what you worship."
   (D) "When the victory of Allah has come and the conquest."

9. What were the profound final words whispered by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ before his passing?
   (A) "Protect my personal treasures."
   (B) "Nay, rather, the Highest Companion in Paradise! (Bal al-Rafiq al-A'la)."
   (C) "Conquer the western oceans."
   (D) "Build me a monumental tomb."

10. What iconic sermon did Sayyidina Abu Bakr deliver to calm the grief-stricken companions in Madinah?
    (A) "Let us elect twenty new kings to govern the provinces."
    (B) "Whoever worshipped Muhammad, then Muhammad has died; but whoever worships Allah, Allah is Ever-Living and never dies!"
    (C) "We must abandon Madinah and return to Makkah."
    (D) "No more prayers will be offered in the mosque."

2. The event known in Arabic as the Great Opening is ______.
3. The formerly enslaved companion who proclaimed the Adhan from the Ka'bah roof was ______.
4. The Prophet's Farewell Sermon stated that superiority between human races is determined solely by ______.
5. The final words uttered by the Beloved Prophet ﷺ were ______.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Fath Makkah ─── The peaceful conquest of Makkah in 8 AH cleansing the Ka'bah of idols
* Khutbat al-Wada' ─── The historic Farewell Sermon proclaiming universal human equality
* Sayyidina Bilal ─── The hero of Tawhid who called the Adhan atop the roof of the Ka'bah
* Al-Rafiq al-A'la ─── "The Highest Companion", the final whisper of the Prophet ﷺ
* Sayyidina Abu Bakr ─── The leader who anchored the Ummah upon Tawhid after the Prophet's passing

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. Contrast the Prophet's magnanimous general pardon of Quraysh at *Fath Makkah* with typical military conquests in ancient and modern world history.
2. Why was having Sayyidina Bilal climb the Ka'bah roof to call the Adhan one of the most powerful anti-racist statements in human history?
3. Analyze the Farewell Sermon's proclamation of racial equality: How does this 7th-century speech dismantle modern white supremacy and ethnic tribalism?
4. How did Sayyidina Abu Bakr's words upon the Prophet's death protect Islamic creed from falling into the trap of deifying its Messenger?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. C - The Conquest of Makkah occurred in Ramadan of 8 AH following Quraysh's treaty breach.
2. B - The Prophet ﷺ displayed supreme humility, bowing his head in awe and gratitude to Allah.
3. B - Modeling Prophet Yusuf's forgiveness, he pardoned all persecutors unconditionally.
4. B - Surah Al-Isra' 17:81 was recited as the 360 idols were dismantled around the Ka'bah.
5. B - Bilal was appointed to call the Adhan from the Ka'bah, symbolizing racial equality and justice.
6. C - Over 100,000 pilgrims gathered on the plains of 'Arafah for the Farewell Hajj.
7. B - The Farewell Sermon permanently abolished racial and ethnic superiority, establishing Taqwa as the sole measure.
8. B - Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:3 confirmed the completion and perfection of the divine religion.
9. B - "Bal al-Rafiq al-A'la" confirmed his yearning for the divine presence above worldly life.
10. B - Abu Bakr's declaration anchored the Ummah in absolute Tawhid: Allah alone is eternal.
