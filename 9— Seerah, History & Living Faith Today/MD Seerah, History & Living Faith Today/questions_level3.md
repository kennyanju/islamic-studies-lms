# Level 3 Quiz: Seerah, History & Living Faith Today

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. In what year of the Hijri calendar did the peaceful Conquest of Makkah (Fath Makkah) take place?
   (A) 8 AH (Ramadan).
   (B) 2 AH (Ramadan).
   (C) 6 AH (Dhu al-Qi'dah).
   (D) 10 AH (Dhu al-Hijjah).

2. How did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ enter the city of Makkah on the day of its conquest?
   (A) Walking in front of the army carrying an unsheathed sword.
   (B) Riding his camel with his head bowed low in humility and gratitude to Allah.
   (C) Riding an armored horse accompanied by a royal military guard.
   (D) Remaining outside the city walls until all clan elders surrendered unconditionally.

3. What verse of the Quran did the Prophet ﷺ recite as the 360 idols around the Ka'bah were dismantled?
   (A) Surah Al-Ikhlas (112:1-4).
   (B) The opening verses of Surah Al-Fath (48:1-3).
   (C) "The truth has come, and falsehood has perished. Indeed, falsehood is bound to perish!" (Surah Al-Isra' 17:81).
   (D) The Ayat al-Kursi (Surah Al-Baqarah 2:255).

4. Whom did the Prophet ﷺ honor by commanding him to ascend the roof of the Ka'bah and call the Adhan?
   (A) Sayyidina 'Ali ibn Abi Talib.
   (B) Sayyidina Sa'd ibn Abi Waqqas.
   (C) Sayyidina Abu Mahdhurah.
   (D) Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah.

5. What historic words did the Prophet ﷺ pronounce to the gathered people of Quraysh after cleansing the sanctuary?
   (A) He recalled the words of Prophet Yusuf: "No blame will there be upon you today," granting them general amnesty.
   (B) He ordered them to pay reparations for the property seized from the Muhajirun in Makkah.
   (C) He banished the clan chieftains who had organized persecution against the early Muslims.
   (D) He required all citizens to swear an oath of political fealty within three days.

6. Approximately how many companions attended the Farewell Pilgrimage (Hajjat al-Wada') alongside the Prophet ﷺ?
   (A) About 1,400 companions.
   (B) Over 100,000 companions.
   (C) Exactly 313 companions.
   (D) Around 12,000 companions.

7. What universal principle of racial equality did the Prophet ﷺ proclaim in his Farewell Sermon at Mount 'Arafah?
   (A) That tribal lineage and ancestral nobility determine spiritual rank before Allah.
   (B) That material wealth and generosity are the primary measures of human excellence.
   (C) That native fluency in Arabic grants inherent superiority over other linguistic groups.
   (D) That an Arab has no superiority over a non-Arab, nor white over black, except by piety (Taqwa).

8. Which momentous verse was revealed on the Day of 'Arafah during the Farewell Pilgrimage?
   (A) Surah Al-Baqarah (2:286).
   (B) Surah An-Nasr (110:1-3).
   (C) "This day I have perfected for you your religion, completed My favor upon you, and have chosen for you Islam as your religion." (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:3).
   (D) Surah At-Tawbah (9:128).

9. What were the profound final words spoken by Prophet Muhammad ﷺ before his passing?
   (A) "Nay, rather, the Highest Companion in Paradise!" (Bal al-Rafiq al-A'la).
   (B) "Preserve the borders of the Muslim territories."
   (C) "Appoint my successor before the sunset."
   (D) "Record my final wills concerning worldly property."

10. What address did Sayyidina Abu Bakr deliver to anchor the grief-stricken companions in Madinah upon the Prophet's passing?
    (A) He called for an immediate military mobilization to secure the trade routes of the Hijaz.
    (B) He remained silent until the elders of the Ansar had selected a civil administrator.
    (C) He declared that leadership must alternate annually between the Muhajirun and the Ansar.
    (D) "Whoever worshipped Muhammad, then Muhammad has died; but whoever worships Allah, Allah is Ever-Living and never dies!"

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Fath Makkah ─── The peaceful entry into Makkah in 8 AH that cleared the Ka'bah of idols
* Khutbat al-Wada' ─── The Farewell Sermon at 'Arafah in 10 AH affirming human equality and sacred rights
* Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah ─── The caller to prayer who ascended the Ka'bah roof to proclaim Tawhid
* Al-Rafiq al-A'la ─── The Highest Companion, signifying the final choice of the Prophet ﷺ for the divine presence
* Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq ─── The companion who anchored the Ummah in Tawhid upon the Prophet's passing

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. Contrast the Prophet's peaceful entry and general pardon at Fath Makkah with typical ancient military conquests that involved plunder and revenge.
2. Why was having Sayyidina Bilal proclaim the Adhan from atop the Ka'bah such a profound demonstration of the Islamic eradication of racial caste systems?
3. How does the Farewell Sermon's teaching on universal human equality provide an enduring foundation for resisting modern forms of racism and ethnic prejudice?
4. Explain how Sayyidina Abu Bakr's sermon after the Prophet's death protected the Muslim community from compromising pure monotheism (Tawhid).

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. A - The Conquest of Makkah occurred in Ramadan of 8 AH following Quraysh's breach of the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah.
2. B - The Prophet ﷺ entered Makkah bowing his head so low in humility to Allah that his beard touched his saddle.
3. C - Reciting Surah Al-Isra' 17:81, the Prophet ﷺ declared that truth has arrived and falsehood perished as idols were dismantled.
4. D - Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah was commanded to climb the Ka'bah roof and proclaim the call to prayer.
5. A - The Prophet ﷺ pardoned the people of Makkah, echoing Prophet Yusuf's words: "No blame will there be upon you today."
6. B - Over 100,000 companions joined the Prophet ﷺ on the plains of 'Arafah during the Farewell Hajj in 10 AH.
7. D - The Farewell Sermon permanently abolished racial and ethnic superiority, establishing Taqwa as the sole measure.
8. C - Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:3 was revealed at 'Arafah, confirming the completion and perfection of the divine religion.
9. A - "Bal al-Rafiq al-A'la" confirmed the Prophet's yearning for the divine presence in Paradise above worldly life (Sahih al-Bukhari 4437).
10. D - Abu Bakr recited Surah Ali 'Imran 3:144 and reminded believers that Muhammad was a mortal messenger, while Allah is Ever-Living.
