## 1. The Peaceful Return: The Conquest of Makkah (*Fath Makkah*, 8 AH)

In the 8th year of the Hijrah, Quraysh violated the Treaty of Hudaybiyyah by aiding an allied tribe in attacking a Muslim clan. In response, the Prophet ﷺ mobilized an army of **ten thousand believers** and marched toward Makkah in complete secrecy (Sahih al-Bukhari 4287).

| Dimension of Fath Makkah | Prophetic Conduct | Historical & Spiritual Significance |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Entry of Humility** | Head bowed low upon his saddle in gratitude | Rejected imperial pride, bloodlust, and triumphalism. |
| **No General Plunder** | Property and lives were protected | Set the Islamic standard for peaceful city liberation. |
| **Cleansing the Sanctuary** | Demolished 360 idols around the Ka'bah | Restored monotheism (Surah Al-Isra' 17:81, Sahih al-Bukhari 4720). |
| **The General Pardon** | Declared all former enemies free | Echoed the mercy of Prophet Yusuf to his brothers (Sunan al-Bayhaqi 16661). |

### The Sublime General Pardon:
Standing before the Ka'bah, the Prophet ﷺ looked out over the trembling chieftains of Quraysh (the very men who had persecuted believers, boycotted Banu Hashim, and waged wars against Islam).

He asked them:  
> *“O Quraysh! What do you think I am about to do with you?”*  
> They answered with lowered eyes: *“You are a noble brother, the son of a noble brother!”*  
> The Prophet of Mercy ﷺ proclaimed:  
> *“I say to you what Prophet Yusuf said to his brothers: 'No reproach upon you today! May Allah forgive you, and He is the Most Merciful of those who show mercy!' Go, for you are completely free!”* (`اِذْهَبُوا فَأَنْتُمُ الطُّلَقَاءُ`).

## 2. The Cleansing of the Ka'bah: Restoring Ibrahim's House

Surrounded by 360 stone and wooden idols, the Prophet ﷺ approached each idol with his staff, pointing at them and reciting:  
*“The truth has arrived, and falsehood has perished. Indeed, falsehood is bound to perish!”* (Surah Al-Isra' 17:81).

One by one, the idols toppled and shattered on the ground. The Ka'bah was washed with pure Zamzam water, restored to its pristine monotheistic purpose originally consecrated by Prophet Ibrahim and Isma'il.

Then, the Prophet ﷺ commanded Sayyidina Bilal (who had once been tortured on the burning sands of Makkah for professing Tawhid) to **climb atop the roof of the Ka'bah** and proclaim the Adhan! The voice of Tawhid soared over the entire valley.

## 3. The Farewell Pilgrimage (*Hajjat al-Wada'*, 10 AH)

In the 10th year of the Hijrah, the Prophet ﷺ performed his first and final pilgrimage alongside **over one hundred thousand companions**. On the plains of Mount 'Arafah, he delivered the historic **Farewell Sermon (Khutbat al-Wada')**, establishing the universal charter of human equality:

1. **Universal Human Equality:**  
   *“All mankind is from Adam and Eve. An Arab has no superiority over a non-Arab, nor has a non-Arab any superiority over an Arab; also a white has no superiority over a black, nor has a black any superiority over a white, except by piety and good action!”* (Musnad Ahmad 23489).
2. **Sacred Sanctity of Life & Wealth:**  
   *“Your blood and your property are sacred and inviolable until you meet your Lord, just as this day of yours, in this month of yours, in this city of yours is sacred!”* (Sahih al-Bukhari 1741, Sahih Muslim 1218).
3. **The Rights of Women:**  
   *“Treat women well and be kind to them, for they are your partners and committed helpers!”* (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1163).

At the conclusion of the sermon, Allah revealed the final triumphant declaration:  
*“This day I have perfected for you your religion, completed My favor upon you, and have chosen for you Islam as your religion.”* (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:3).

## 4. The Passing of the Beloved ﷺ (*Al-Rafiq al-A'la*)

In Rabi' al-Awwal of the 11th year, the Prophet ﷺ fell ill with a severe fever. In his final days, he rested in the chamber of Sayyidah 'Aishah (RA), instructing Sayyidina Abu Bakr to lead the congregational prayers.

On a Monday morning, he raised his gaze toward the heavens and whispered his final words on earth:  
**“Nay, rather, the Highest Companion in Paradise!”** (`بَلِ الرَّفِيقَ الْأَعْلَى`) (Sahih al-Bukhari 4437, Sahih Muslim 2444).

The companions were overwhelmed with grief. Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq stood before the weeping community and delivered the eternal foundation of Tawhid:  
> *“O people! Whoever among you worshipped Muhammad, then know that Muhammad has passed away! But whoever among you worships Allah, then know that Allah is Ever-Living and never dies!”* (Sahih al-Bukhari 1241, 1242).
