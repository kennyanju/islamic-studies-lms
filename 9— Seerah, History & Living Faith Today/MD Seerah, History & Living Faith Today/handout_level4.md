## 1. The Rightly Guided Caliphate (*Al-Khilafah al-Rashidah*)

Following the passing of the Prophet ﷺ, the Muslim Ummah was guided by four exceptional leaders known as the **Four Rightly Guided Caliphs (Al-Khulafa' al-Rashidun)**.

The Prophet ﷺ commanded:  
*“Adhere firmly to my Sunnah and the sunnah of the rightly guided caliphs after me; bite onto it with your molar teeth!”* (Sunan Abi Dawud).

## 2. The Four Caliphs & Their Enduring Legacies

### 1. Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (11–13 AH / 2 Years)
* **The Challenge:** Following the Prophet's death, several tribes rebelled and false prophets (like Musaylimah) emerged in the Apostasy Wars (*Hurub al-Riddah*).
* **The Achievement:** With unshakeable courage, Abu Bakr preserved the unity of Arabia, declared that not a single thread of Zakah would be compromised, and commissioned the **first compilation of the Holy Quran** under Zayd ibn Thabit.

### 2. Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (13–23 AH / 10 Years)
* **The Embodying of Justice (*Al-Faruq*):** Renowned for peerless justice, walking the streets of Madinah disguised at night carrying sacks of flour on his own back to feed hungry orphan children!
* **State Institutions:** Established the Islamic Hijri calendar, the public state treasury (*Bayt al-Mal*), public welfare stipends for newborns and the elderly, independent judicial courts, and town planning.
* **The Liberation of Jerusalem (16 AH):** Entered Jerusalem on foot, sharing a single camel with his servant, wearing patched robes. He refused to pray inside the Church of the Holy Sepulchre so Muslims would not later convert it into a mosque, signing the **Covenant of 'Umar** guaranteeing Christian religious freedom and property rights!

### 3. Sayyidina 'Uthman ibn 'Affan (23–35 AH / 12 Years)
* **Modesty and Generosity (*Dhun-Nurayn*):** Renowned for intense modesty; purchased the Well of Rumah for free public drinking water in Madinah, and single-handedly financed the entire expedition of Tabuk.
* **The Standardized Quranic Codex:** As the borders expanded from North Africa to Central Asia, he standardized the **Uthmanic Master Codex (*Al-Mushaf al-Imam*)**, uniting the entire Muslim world upon a single authoritative script.
* **Martyrdom of Patience:** Besieged in his home by rebels, he refused to shed a single drop of Muslim blood in his defense, martyred while reciting the Quran.

### 4. Sayyidina 'Ali ibn Abi Talib (35–40 AH / 5 Years)
* **The Gate of Knowledge (*Bab al-'Ilm*):** The Prophet ﷺ said: *"I am the city of knowledge, and 'Ali is its gate!"* Renowned for peerless legal judgments, unmatched chivalry (*Futuwwah*), and transcendent Arabic eloquence.
* **Principled Steadfastness:** Navigated painful civil strife with supreme moral integrity, refusing to compromise divine justice for political expediency.

## 3. Sunni Creed: Revering the Companions (*Adab ma'a al-Sahabah*)

A non-negotiable pillar of Sunni orthodoxy is **loving, honoring, and defending the integrity of all the Companions (*'Adalat al-Sahabah*)**:
* Allah praised them in the Quran: *"Allah is well-pleased with them, and they are well-pleased with Him"* (Surah At-Tawbah 9:100).
* The Prophet ﷺ warned: *"Do not insult my companions! For by Him in whose Hand is my soul, if one of you were to spend the equivalent of Mount Uhud in gold, it would not reach a handful of their deeds, nor half of it!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari).
* We refrain from passing arrogant judgment on the historical disputes that arose between them, acknowledging that all exercised sincere *Ijtihad*.
