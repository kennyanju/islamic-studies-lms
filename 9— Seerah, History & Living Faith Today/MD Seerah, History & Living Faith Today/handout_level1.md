# Level 1 Student Handout: Seerah, History & Living Faith Today
**Age Group:** Ages 6-8 (Level 1 Track: Primary Stage 1)  
**Core Strands:** Universal Prophetic Mercy | Universal Equality | The Four Great Caliphs | Living as a Faithful Muslim  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah* of Ibn Hisham & *Al-Shifa* of Qadi 'Iyad  
**Riwayah & Surah Target:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 30: Surah An-Nasr & Surah Al-Kawthar)

---

## 1. The Prophet's Message of Universal Mercy
Allah sent Prophet Muhammad ﷺ with the most loving mission in the entire universe:  
> **وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةًۭ لِّلْعَٰلَمِينَ**  
> *"And We have not sent you except as a mercy to all the worlds!"* (Surah Al-Anbiya' 21:107).

He was a mercy to crying babies, a mercy to struggling mothers, a mercy to tired animals, and a mercy to trees and the earth. He taught us that a true believer is someone from whose tongue and hands all people feel safe (Sahih al-Bukhari 10).

In the 8th year of Hijrah, the Prophet ﷺ entered his beloved hometown of Makkah peacefully. He cleared the Ka'bah of idols and showed boundless mercy by forgiving those who had previously opposed him.

---

## 2. Universal Human Equality: Like the Teeth of a Comb
During his historic Farewell Pilgrimage (*Hajjat al-Wada'*), standing before thousands of believers in the plains of 'Arafat, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ proclaimed:
* **All of us are from Adam and Eve.**
* An Arab is not superior to a non-Arab, a white person is not superior to a Black person, and a Black person is not superior to a white person (Musnad Ahmad 23489).
* The only thing that raises a person in the sight of Allah is **Taqwa** (having a clean, God-fearing heart and doing righteous deeds).

Islam established universal human equality over 1,400 years ago, uniting people of every color, language, and heritage into one family called the **Ummah**! Today, over two billion Muslims around the globe pray facing the Ka'bah in Makkah every single day.

---

## 3. The Four Rightly Guided Caliphs (*Al-Khulafa' al-Rashidun*)
After the Prophet ﷺ passed away, four of his closest companions guided the Muslim community:

1. **Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (RA):** The Truthful Friend! The Prophet's closest companion who supported the faith with his whole heart and wealth, and became the first Caliph.
2. **Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (Al-Faruq - RA):** The Leader of Justice! Famous for his fairness, establishing public services, and checking on the needy at night.
3. **Sayyidina 'Uthman ibn 'Affan (Dhun-Nurayn - RA):** The Generous and Modest! He purchased wells to provide free drinking water and organized the standardized copies of the Holy Quran.
4. **Sayyidina 'Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA):** The Brave and Wise! Renowned for his courage, deep knowledge, and chivalry. The Prophet ﷺ said to him: *"You are to me in the position of Harun to Musa, except that there is no prophet after me"* (Sahih al-Bukhari 3701, Sahih Muslim 2404).

---

## 4. Living as a Faithful Muslim Today
Every day, a young Muslim can represent the noble teachings of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ:
* **At Home:** Help parents tidy the breakfast table, speak kindly to brothers and sisters, and say *"Alhamdulillah"*.
* **At School:** Be an honest and friendly classmate, share school supplies, raise your hand politely, and invite lonely students to join games.
* **On the Playground:** Never push, play fairly, follow rules, and smile.
* **In the Community:** Be proud of your faith, pray your Salah with devotion, and let your good character shine as a pure source of goodness!
