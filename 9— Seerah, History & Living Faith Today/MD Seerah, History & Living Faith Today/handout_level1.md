# Level 1 Student Handout: Seerah, History & Living Faith Today
**Age Group:** Ages 6–8 (Level 1 Track — Primary Stage 1)  
**ICO Curriculum Benchmark:** Aligned with ICO Grades 1 & 2 (History & Living Faith Strand)  
**Core Strands:** Universal Prophetic Mercy | Universal Equality | The Four Great Heroes | Being a Shining Star Today  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah* of Ibn Hisham & *Al-Shifa* of Qadi 'Iyad  
**Riwayah & Surah Target:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 30: Surah An-Nasr & Surah Al-Kawthar)

---

## 🕊️ 1. The Prophet's Beautiful Message of Universal Mercy
Allah sent Prophet Muhammad ﷺ with the most loving mission in the entire cosmos:  
> **وَمَآ أَرْسَلْنَٰكَ إِلَّا رَحْمَةًۭ لِّلْعَٰلَمِينَ**  
> *"And We have not sent you except as a mercy to all the worlds!"* (Surah Al-Anbiya' 21:107).

He was a mercy to crying babies, a mercy to struggling mothers, a mercy to tired animals, and a mercy to trees and the earth. He taught us that a true believer is someone from whose tongue and hands all people feel safe!

---

## 🌍 2. Universal Human Equality: Like the Teeth of a Comb
During his historic Farewell Pilgrimage (*Hajjat al-Wada'*), standing before thousands of believers under the sunny sky of 'Arafah, Prophet Muhammad ﷺ proclaimed:
* **All of us are from Adam and Eve.**
* An Arab is no better than a non-Arab, a white person is no better than a Black person, and a Black person is no better than a white person!
* The only thing that makes a person special in the sight of Allah is **Taqwa** (having a clean, God-fearing heart and doing good deeds).

Islam demolished racism 1,400 years ago, uniting people of every color, language, and nation into one beautiful global family called the **Ummah**!

---

## 👑 3. Introduction to the Four Rightly Guided Caliphs
After the Prophet ﷺ passed away, four of his closest, most courageous companions guided the Ummah:

1. **Sayyidina Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (RA):** The Truthful Champion! The Prophet's best friend who gave all his wealth for Islam and united the believers.
2. **Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab (Al-Faruq - RA):** The Champion of Justice! Known for helping the poor at night and protecting freedom of worship.
3. **Sayyidina 'Uthman ibn 'Affan (Dhun-Nurayn - RA):** The Generous & Modest! He bought water wells for the people and unified the Holy Qur'an into master copies.
4. **Sayyidina 'Ali ibn Abi Talib (RA):** The Brave Lion & Gate of Wisdom! Renowned for immense courage, unmatched Arabic eloquence, and chivalry.

---

## ⭐ 4. How to Be a Shining Little Muslim Today!
Every day when you wake up, you represent Prophet Muhammad ﷺ in your world:
* **At Home:** Help Mum tidy the breakfast table, speak kindly to your siblings, and say *"Alhamdulillah"*.
* **At School:** Be the friendliest student, share your colored pencils, raise your hand politely, and invite the lonely child to sit with you.
* **On the Playground:** Never push, never cheat, play fairly, and smile!
* **Everywhere:** Be proud of your Muslim identity, pray your Salah with love, and let your good character shine like a beacon of light!
