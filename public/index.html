Comprehensive 9-Module Curriculum

Designed for progressive, age-appropriate learning from foundational articles of faith to applied contemporary ethics.

Module 1: Foundations of Belief

Level 1: ~10y Level 2: 13y+

Core tenets of Ash'ari Aqidah, the Six Articles of Faith, Tawhid, Prophethood, the Unseen, and Divine Decree.

Module 2: Purification & Prayer

Level 1: ~10y Level 2: 13y+

Maliki Fiqh of Taharah, Wudu, Ghusl, conditions and integrals of Salah, Congregational Prayer, and Khushu'.

Module 3: Seerah: Early Life of Prophet ﷺ

Level 1: ~10y Level 2: 13y+

Makkan period, noble character of the Prophet ﷺ, First Revelation at Cave Hira, persecution, and the Night Journey (Isra & Mi'raj).

Module 4: Deepening Belief & the Quran

Level 1: ~10y Level 2: 13y+

Quranic revelation, preservation, miracles, angels, divine scriptures, and analytical proofs of Prophethood.

Module 5: Fiqh of Fasting & Zakah

Level 1: ~10y Level 2: 13y+

Spiritual and legal dimensions of Ramadan, Sawm, Zakat al-Fitr, wealth purification (Zakah), and voluntary charity (Sadaqah).

Module 6: Seerah: Madinah Community

Level 1: ~10y Level 2: 13y+

The Hijrah, building the Prophetic Mosque, the Constitution of Madinah, Mu'akhah (Brotherhood), and the Conquest of Makkah.

Module 7: Applied Fiqh & Everyday Life

Level 1: ~10y Level 2: 13y+

Halal and Haram in diet, financial ethics, honest trade, digital etiquette, and Islamic legal maxims for modern living.

Module 8: Character, Society & Family

Level 1: ~10y Level 2: 13y+

Islamic Akhlaq, honoring parents (Birr al-Walidayn), kinship ties (Silat ar-Rahim), neighbor rights, and social justice.

Module 9: Living Faith Today

Level 1: ~10y Level 2: 13y+

Preserving Islamic identity, navigating modern cultural pressures, science & revelation, civic duty, and the Farewell Sermon.
