## 🌊 1. Taharah: The Gateway to the Divine Presence
In Islamic jurisprudence (*Fiqh*), **Taharah** (Purification) is the first and most fundamental prerequisite of worship. Purity is two-fold:
1. **Purity from Hadath (Ritual Impurity):** Lifted by performing Wudu (minor impurity) or Ghusl (major ritual bath).
2. **Purity from Khabath (Physical Filth):** Removing physical impurities (like urine, blood, or stool) from your body, your clothing, and your prayer rug.

## 🚫 3. What Breaks Wudu (*Nawaqid al-Wudu*)
Knowing what breaks wudu prevents unnecessary doubts and waswas:
* **Things that definitely break wudu:**
  1. Any natural bodily discharge (urine, stool, passing gas).
  2. Heavy, deep sleep where you lose conscious awareness of your surroundings.
  3. Losing consciousness (fainting, dizziness, medication).
* **Things that DO NOT break wudu in Maliki fiqh:**
  * ❌ Eating food (including camel meat, contrary to the Hanbali school).
  * ❌ Minor nosebleeds, scrapes, or cuts.
  * ❌ Light drowsiness where you still hear people speaking around you.
  * ❌ Touching non-filthy animals or items.

## 🕌 4. The 5 Conditions (Shurut) for a Valid Prayer
Before you raise your hands for the opening Takbir, **five sacred conditions** must be fulfilled:
1. **Taharah:** Cleanliness of body, clothes, and place from both Hadath and Khabath.
2. **Covering the 'Awrah:** Boys must cover from navel to knee with loose clothing; girls cover their whole body except the face and hands.
3. **Facing the Qiblah:** Aligning your body toward the Holy Ka'bah in Makkah.
4. **Entry of the Prayer Time:** A prayer performed even two minutes before its time starts is not valid!
5. **The Intention (Niyyah):** Knowing in your heart specifically which prayer you are praying (e.g. Dhuhr today for the pleasure of Allah).

## 📢 5. The Adhan, Iqamah & Congregational Prayer (Jama'ah)
* **The Call to Glory:** The Adhan is the public announcement that prayer time has entered. We listen quietly, repeat the words after the Mu'adhdhin, and make the Sunnah du'a for Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
* **The Reward of Jama'ah:** The Prophet ﷺ said:  
  > *"Prayer in congregation is twenty-seven times more virtuous than praying alone!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari).
* **Masjid Etiquette:** Step into the masjid with your right foot, say the entry du'a, pray two quiet rak'ahs of *Tahiyyat al-Masjid* (greeting the mosque), walk calmly without running, and maintain peaceful respect.
