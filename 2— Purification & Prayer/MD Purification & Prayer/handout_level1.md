## Section 1: Cleanliness is Half of Faith

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught us a golden rule for our daily life:

> **الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ**  
> *"Cleanliness is half of faith."* (Sahih Muslim 223, Sahih).

Allah loves children who are clean, neat, and fresh. A Muslim takes care of their body, brushes their teeth every morning and evening, clips their nails, washes their hands before eating, and wears clean clothes.

When we prepare to stand before Allah in prayer, we perform a special wash called **Wudu** (ablution). Wudu washes away physical dust and brings light, joy, and peace into our hearts.

## Section 2: Bathroom Manners (Istinja and Istijmar)

Islam teaches us polite, healthy manners for every part of our day, including using the washroom:

1. **Enter with your left foot** and say the protection du'a:  
   *“Bismillah, Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-khubthi wal-khaba'ith”*  
   (In the name of Allah; O Allah, I seek refuge in You from all foul and harmful things). (Sahih al-Bukhari 142, Sahih Muslim 375, Sahih).
2. **Keep clean:** Take care that no drops or dirt splash onto your clothes or body.
3. **Wash with pure water:** Clean yourself thoroughly using clean water (*Istinja*) with your left hand. If water is not immediately available, clean dry paper or stones may be used to remove impurities (*Istijmar*).
4. **Exit with your right foot** and say:  
   *“Ghufranak”*  
   (O Allah, I ask for Your forgiveness and thank You for relief). (Sunan Abi Dawud 30, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 7, Sahih).
5. **Wash your hands with soap and water** so your hands are clean and fresh.

## Section 3: The Seven Essential Steps of Wudu (Fara'id al-Wudu)

In the Maliki school, following the practice of the Prophet ﷺ and the people of Madinah, there are **seven essential steps** (*Fara'id*). If any of these seven steps is missed, the wudu is not complete and must be corrected:

| Step Number | Obligatory Action (*Fard*) | How to Perform It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Intention (*Al-Niyyah*)** | Make the intention in your heart to perform wudu for the pleasure of Allah. |
| **2** | **Washing the Face (*Ghasl al-Wajh*)** | Wash your entire face, from the normal hairline down to the chin, and from earlobe to earlobe. |
| **3** | **Washing the Arms (*Ghasl al-Yadayn*)** | Wash both hands and arms thoroughly, starting from the fingertips up to and including the elbows. |
| **4** | **Wiping the Head (*Mash al-Ra's*)** | Wet your hands and wipe your entire head from the forehead back to the nape of the neck. |
| **5** | **Washing the Feet (*Ghasl al-Rijlayn*)** | Wash both feet thoroughly up to and including the ankle-bones, making sure water passes between the toes. |
| **6** | **Gentle Rubbing (*Al-Dalk*)** | Pass your wet hand gently over each limb while washing with water so the water reaches all the skin. |
| **7** | **Continuity (*Al-Muwalat*)** | Wash each limb right after the other without taking long breaks in between. |

## Section 4: Sunnahs and Meritorious Acts (Sunan wa Fada'il)

In addition to the seven obligations, our Prophet ﷺ taught us beautiful actions that bring extra reward and make our wudu complete:

* **Saying Bismillah (Meritorious Act / *Fadilah*):** We begin our wudu by saying *Bismillahir-Rahmanir-Rahim* (In the Name of Allah, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful).
* **Washing hands to the wrists (Sunnah):** Wash your hands three times before dipping them into the water basin.
* **Rinsing the mouth (*Madmadah* - Sunnah):** Take fresh water into your mouth, swirl it around, and spit it out gently three times.
* **Sniffing and blowing out water (*Istinshaq* and *Istinthar* - Sunnah):** Sniff clean water into your nostrils and blow it out gently three times.
* **Wiping the ears (Sunnah):** Wipe the inside and outside of both ears using fresh, moist fingers.
* **Returning the head wipe (*Radd al-Mash* - Sunnah):** Bring your wet hands forward from the nape of the neck back to the forehead.
* **Washing three times (*Al-Tathlith* - Meritorious Act / *Fadilah*):** Washing each limb three times is beloved and meritorious, while washing once thoroughly with rubbing is the obligatory minimum.

## Section 5: The Five Daily Prayers: Our Golden Meetings with Allah

Every day, Muslims around the world hear the call to prayer (*Adhan*) and stand facing the Holy Ka'bah in Makkah to perform the **Five Daily Prayers**:

| Prayer Name | When We Pray | Number of Rak'ahs | How We Recite |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fajr** | Before sunrise at dawn | **2 Rak'ahs** | Recited aloud |
| **Dhuhr** | Midday, after the sun passes its peak | **4 Rak'ahs** | Recited quietly |
| **'Asr** | Late afternoon before sunset | **4 Rak'ahs** | Recited quietly |
| **Maghrib** | Right after sunset | **3 Rak'ahs** | First 2 rak'ahs aloud, 3rd rak'ah quiet |
| **'Isha** | Nighttime when darkness settles | **4 Rak'ahs** | First 2 rak'ahs aloud, last 2 rak'ahs quiet |

### The Movements of Salah: Standing in Respect
When we stand before Allah, our whole body expresses reverence and humility:
* **The Opening Takbir (*Takbirat al-Ihram*):** We raise our hands to shoulder or ear level and say: *"Allahu Akbar"* (Allah is Greater than all things).
* **Standing (*Qiyam*):** In the Maliki school, we stand with our arms resting naturally and gently at our sides (*Sadl*), keeping our body relaxed, still, and respectful.
* **Bowing (*Ruku'*):** We bow with a straight back, place our hands on our knees, and say three times: *"Subhana Rabbiy al-'Azim"* (Glory be to my Lord, the Magnificent).
* **Prostration (*Sujud*):** We place our forehead, nose, palms, knees, and toes on the clean floor and say three times: *"Subhana Rabbiy al-A'la"* (Glory be to my Lord, the Most High). This is the closest a servant comes to Allah.
* **Sitting (*Jalsah*):** We sit peacefully and recite the *Tashahhud* and blessings upon the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.

## Section 6: Curriculum Anchors in Practice

### 1. Qur'an and Tafsir Anchor: Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:6)
Allah commands us in the Holy Qur'an:

> **يَا أَيُّهَا الَّذِينَ آمَنُوا إِذَا قُمْتُمْ إِلَى الصَّلَاةِ فَاغْسِلُوا وُجُوهَكُمْ وَأَيْدِيَكُمْ إِلَى الْمَرَافِقِ وَامْسَحُوا بِرُءُوسِكُمْ وَأَرْجُلَكُمْ إِلَى الْكَعْبَيْنِ**  
> *Ya ayyuha alladhina amanu idha qumtum ila al-salati faghsilu wujuhakum wa aydiyakum ila al-marafiqi wamsahu bi-ru'usikum wa arjulakum ila al-ka'bayn.*  
> *"O you who believe! When you rise up for prayer, wash your faces, and your hands up to the elbows, and wipe your heads, and wash your feet up to the ankles."* (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6).

**Classical Tafsir (*Tafsir al-Qurtubi*):** Imam al-Qurtubi explains that this noble verse is the divine foundation of wudu. It lists the essential bodily limbs that must be washed before prayer, showing that inner purity of the heart begins with outer purification of the body.

### 2. Tajweed Rule in Action: Natural Prolongation (Madd Tabi'i)
*Madd Tabi'i* (natural stretch) occurs when a vowel letter (Alif after Fathah, Waw after Dammah, or Ya after Kasrah) appears without a Hamzah or Sukun after it. It is held for exactly two vowel counts (beats).  
* Examples from Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6:
  * In the word **آمَنُوا** (*amanu*), the Alif and Waw are stretched naturally for two counts.
  * In the word **أَقَامُوا** (*aqamu*), the Alif is held for two counts without rushing or over-stretching.

### 3. Classical Maliki Text Anchor: *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari*
Imam Abu Zayd 'Abd al-Rahman al-Akhdari (d. 983 AH) writes in his foundational manual of Maliki fiqh:
> *"The obligations (Fara'id) of Wudu are seven: intention (Niyyah), washing the face, washing the arms up to the elbows, wiping the entire head, washing the feet up to the ankles, gentle rubbing (Dalk), and continuity (Muwalat)."*

Imam al-Akhdari emphasizes that learning these seven pillars ensures our prayers are built on sound, valid foundations from childhood.

### 4. Prophetic Hadith Anchor: Hadith 10 of Nawawi's 40
The Messenger of Allah ﷺ said:

> **إِنَّ اللَّهَ تَعَالَى طَيِّبٌ لَا يَقْبَلُ إِلَّا طَيِّبًا**  
> *"Indeed, Allah the Exalted is Pure, and He accepts only that which is pure."* (Sahih Muslim 1015, Sahih).

**Family Application:** When we wash for prayer, eat wholesome lawful food, and speak truthful words, we offer Allah what is pure and beloved to Him. Cleanliness in our room, our clothes, and our speech brings peace and blessings into our family home.
