## 💧 1. Cleanliness is Half of Our Faith!
Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught us:  
> **الطُّهُورُ شَطْرُ الإِيمَانِ**  
> *"Cleanliness is half of faith!"* (Sahih Muslim).

Allah loves boys and girls who are clean, neat, and fresh! A Muslim takes care of their body, brushes their teeth with a toothbrush or clean siwak, clips their nails, washes their hands before eating, and wears clean clothes.

When we prepare to stand before Allah in prayer, we perform a special, sparkling wash called **Wudu** (Ablution).

## 🧼 2. Bathroom Manners (Istinja & Istijmar)
Islam teaches us the most polite, respectful manners for every part of our day—including using the bathroom:
1. **Enter with your left foot** and say the protection du'a:  
   *“Bismillah, Allahumma inni a'udhu bika minal-khubthi wal-khaba'ith”*  
   (O Allah, I seek refuge in You from all foul and wicked things).
2. **Keep clean:** Never let dirt or drops splash onto your clothes.
3. **Wash with pure water:** Clean yourself thoroughly using water (*Istinja*) with your left hand.
4. **Exit with your right foot** and say:  
   *“Ghufranak!”* (O Allah, I ask for Your forgiveness and thank You for relief).
5. **Wash your hands** with soap and water so you are completely clean!

## 🌊 3. The 7 Obligatory Steps of Wudu (Fara'id al-Wudu)
In the Maliki school, following the great Imam Malik of Madinah, there are **7 Essential Steps (Fara'id)** that make your wudu complete and valid:

### The Beautiful Maliki Sunnahs:
Before the obligatory steps, we say *"Bismillah"*, wash our hands three times, rinse our mouth with water three times (*Madmadah*), sniff water into our nostrils and blow it out gently three times (*Istinshaq*), and wipe the inside and outside of our ears!

## 🕌 4. The Five Daily Prayers: Our Golden Meetings with Allah
Every day, Muslims all over the world hear the Adhan and stand facing the holy Ka'bah in Makkah to pray **Five Daily Prayers**:

| Prayer Name | Time of Day | Number of Rak'ahs | How We Recite |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fajr** | Before sunrise at dawn | **2 Rak'ahs** | Recited aloud |
| **Dhuhr** | Midday, after sun passes noon | **4 Rak'ahs** | Recited quietly |
| **'Asr** | Late afternoon | **4 Rak'ahs** | Recited quietly |
| **Maghrib** | Right after the sun sets | **3 Rak'ahs** | First 2 aloud, 3rd quiet |
| **'Isha** | Night time when dark arrives | **4 Rak'ahs** | First 2 aloud, last 2 quiet |

## 🤲 5. The Movements of Salah: Standing in Respect
When we pray, our whole body speaks respect to our Creator:
* **The Takbir:** We raise our hands to our shoulders or ears and say: *"Allahu Akbar!"* (Allah is Greater than everything!).
* **The Standing (Qiyam):** In the Maliki school, we stand with our arms resting naturally at our sides (*Sadl*), keeping our body relaxed, humble, and still.
* **Ruku' (Bowing):** We place our hands on our knees, keep our backs straight, and whisper 3 times: *"Subhana Rabbiy al-'Azim"* (Glory be to my Lord, the Magnificent!).
* **Sujud (Prostration):** We place our forehead, nose, palms, knees, and toes on the clean floor and whisper 3 times: *"Subhana Rabbiy al-A'la"* (Glory be to my Lord, the Most High!). This is the closest a servant is to Allah!
* **Jalsah (Sitting):** We sit peacefully and recite the Tashahhud and prayers for Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
