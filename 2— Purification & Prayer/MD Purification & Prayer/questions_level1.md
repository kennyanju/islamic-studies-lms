# Level 1 Activity Sheet: Purification & Prayer

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions

1. What is the special wash we perform with clean water before standing in prayer?
   (A) Tayammum
   (B) Wudu
   (C) Suhoor
   (D) Zakah

2. Which direction do Muslims all over the world face when standing in prayer?
   (A) Toward the rising sun in the east
   (B) Toward the nearest mountain range
   (C) Toward the local community center
   (D) Toward the Holy Ka'bah in Makkah (the Qiblah)

3. In the Maliki school, how do we wipe our head during Wudu?
   (A) Wiping gently over the whole head from the front hairline to the nape of the neck
   (B) Touching only three hairs at the front with one fingertip
   (C) Washing the head with shampoo and warm water
   (D) Wiping only the back of the neck and ears

4. What is the special Maliki obligation in Wudu where we gently pass our wet hand over each limb?
   (A) Al-Muwalat (continuity)
   (B) Al-Niyyah (intention)
   (C) Al-Dalk (gentle rubbing)
   (D) Al-Tartib (following order)

5. What do we whisper with reverence when we place our forehead on the floor in Sujud?
   (A) Subhana Rabbiy al-'Azim
   (B) Subhana Rabbiy al-A'la
   (C) Al-Hamdu lillahi Rabbil-'Alamin
   (D) Astaghfirullah wa Atubu Ilayh

## Part C: Matching Terms

* Al-Dalk ─── Passing the wet hand gently over each limb during wudu
* The Qiblah ─── The direction toward the Holy Ka'bah in Makkah
* Sujud ─── Touching the forehead and nose to the ground in worship
* Sadl ─── Standing with arms resting naturally at the sides in prayer
* Fajr ─── The two-rak'ah dawn prayer recited aloud

## Answer Key & Marking Guide

1. B - Wudu is the special ritual wash performed with clean water before standing in prayer.
2. D - All Muslims face the Holy Ka'bah in Makkah, which is known as the Qiblah.
3. A - In the Maliki school, wiping the entire head from the front hairline to the nape of the neck is an obligatory pillar (Fard).
4. C - Al-Dalk is the gentle rubbing of each limb with the wet hand during wudu.
5. B - In Sujud (prostration), we say Subhana Rabbiy al-A'la (Glory be to my Lord, the Most High) three times.
