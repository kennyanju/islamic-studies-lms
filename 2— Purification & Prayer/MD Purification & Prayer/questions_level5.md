# Level 5 Quiz: Purification & Prayer

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. In classical Maliki Usul, how is water that has been altered by a pure substance (such as tea or floral soap) categorized?
   (A) It becomes impure (Najis) and must be discarded immediately.
   (B) It remains physically clean (Tahir), but loses its ability to remove ritual impurity (Ghayr Mutahhir).
   (C) It remains fully purifying for both wudu and ghusl without restriction.
   (D) It can only be used by children under seven years of age.

2. On what linguistic analysis of the Quranic command *"Faghsilu"* (Surah Al-Ma'idah 5:6) did Imam Malik base the obligation of *Al-Dalk* (rubbing)?
   (A) That classical Arabic distinguishes between *Ghasl* (washing with friction) and *Insibab* (mere pouring).
   (B) That the root letters of the word imply the use of heated desert rocks.
   (C) That the Prophet ﷺ instructed Abu Bakr to scrub with pumice stones.
   (D) That Greek medical treatises required friction for blood circulation.

3. Why did Imam Malik prioritize the practice of *Sadl* (draping arms) in obligatory prayers despite recording the hadith of folding hands in *Al-Muwatta*?
   (A) He forgot that he had included the hadith in his own book.
   (B) He prioritized the continuous, mass-transmitted living practice of the community of Madinah (*'Amal*) over a solitary narration (*Ahad*).
   (C) He wanted to differentiate Muslim prayer from the prayer of surrounding empires.
   (D) He issued the ruling under political coercion from the Abbasid caliph.

4. How does the Maliki school evaluate the invalidation of Wudu through physical contact with the opposite gender?
   (A) Any contact invalidates wudu, even brushing sleeves in a crowded market.
   (B) Physical touch invalidates wudu ONLY if accompanied by sexual pleasure or intent for pleasure.
   (C) Touch never invalidates wudu under any circumstances, even marital relations.
   (D) It invalidates wudu only on Fridays and during the month of Ramadan.

5. What is the status of reciting the *Basmalah* aloud at the beginning of Surah Al-Fatihah during loud prayers in the Maliki Madhhab?
   (A) It is an obligatory pillar of the prayer without which the prayer is invalid.
   (B) It is disliked (*Makruh*) to recite it aloud in obligatory prayers, based on Madinan practice.
   (C) It must be recited twice before every single rak'ah.
   (D) It is an innovation that expels the worshipper from the mosque.

6. In modern medical fiqh, how does undergoing hemodialysis affect a patient's state of Wudu in the Maliki school?
   (A) It automatically invalidates wudu because blood is drawn outside the body.
   (B) It does NOT invalidate wudu, because bleeding from external surgical sites does not break wudu in Maliki jurisprudence.
   (C) It requires the patient to perform three consecutive Ghusls.
   (D) It permanently exempts the patient from performing daily prayers.

7. How does a patient with an ostomy bag discharge their obligation of prayer?
   (A) They must wait until they are fully cured before praying any missed prayers.
   (B) They empty the appliance, make wudu after the prayer time begins, and pray without worrying about involuntary flow.
   (C) They must perform Tayammum on hospital floor tiles exclusively.
   (D) They are prohibited from facing the Qiblah until surgical drains are removed.

8. According to *Bidayat al-Mujtahid*, what is the underlying legal reason (*Sabab al-Ikhtilaf*) for scholarly differences on wiping over footwear?
   (A) Differences in the availability of leather animals in Makkah versus Kufah.
   (B) Reconciling unconditional hadiths of wiping with Quranic verses commanding the washing of feet.
   (C) Political disagreements between the Umayyad rulers and the judges of Basra.
   (D) Disagreements regarding the currency exchange rate for buying shoes.

9. What legal maxim governs wiping over medical casts and surgical splints (*Jaba'ir*)?
   (A) "Necessity renders the unlawful permissible" and "Harm must be removed."
   (B) "Custom dictates grammatical interpretation."
   (C) "All medical decisions must be ratified by an international council."
   (D) "The origin of all medical treatments is prohibition."

10. What does Qadi Ibn Rushd conclude regarding the diverse positions of the four Sunni Imams on prayer mechanics?
    (A) That only one school possesses authentic truth while the other three are corrupted.
    (B) That each Imam exercised brilliant, valid analogical reasoning based on sound linguistic and historical methodologies.
    (C) That Muslims should discard all madhhabs and invent new prayer postures.
    (D) That differences in prayer will be resolved by political decree.

## Part C: Matching Jurisprudential Terms
* Ma' Mutlaq ─── Pure, unadulterated water capable of removing ritual hadath
* Ma' Mukhalat ─── Water altered by pure substances, retaining cleanliness but not ritual purification
* 'Amal Ahl al-Madinah ─── Continuous living consensus of Madinah prioritized over Ahad hadiths
* Jaba'ir ─── Medical splints or casts wiped over during temporary physical injury
* Salas al-Bawl ─── Chronic incontinence governed by the jurisprudence of concession

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - Water altered by pure substances remains Tahir (clean), but cannot be used for ritual purification (Ghayr Mutahhir).
2. A - Malikis prove through Arabic lexicography that Ghasl requires manual friction, unlike mere Insibab.
3. B - Continuous communal tradition ('Amal) is epistemologically definitive (Qat'i), superseding solitary reports.
4. B - Malikis interpret Quranic touch verses as referring to touch with lust/pleasure, avoiding extreme literalism.
5. B - The Madinan tradition was to begin recitation directly with "Al-Hamdu lillahi Rabbil-'Alamin" without loud Basmalah.
6. B - External blood loss from incisions or lines does not break wudu in the Maliki madhhab.
7. B - Governed by the rules of chronic conditions (Salas), requiring a single wudu per prayer window.
8. B - Harmonizing Quranic commands to wash feet with Sunnah permissions to wipe over footwear.
