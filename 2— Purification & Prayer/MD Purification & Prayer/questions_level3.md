# Level 3 Quiz: Purification & Prayer

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How many obligatory acts (Fara'id) of Wudu are codified in the Maliki text *Matn Ibn 'Ashir*?
   (A) Four obligations.
   (B) Seven obligations.
   (C) Ten obligations.
   (D) Twelve obligations.

2. Which two obligations of Wudu are distinctive pillars in the Maliki school, whereas other schools consider them recommended sunnahs?
   (A) Washing the face and washing the feet.
   (B) Rubbing the limbs (Al-Dalk) and continuity without delay (Al-Muwalat).
   (C) Using hot scented water and drying with a silk cloth.
   (D) Reciting poetry between each wash.

3. What is the boundary of the face (*Ghasl al-Wajh*) that must be washed during Wudu?
   (A) From the eyebrows to the lips only.
   (B) From the normal hairline to the bottom of the chin, and from earlobe to earlobe.
   (C) Only the forehead and the tip of the nose.
   (D) The entire head including the back of the neck.

4. When performing Tayammum in the absence of water, what substance is legally classified as *Sa'id*?
   (A) Processed soap bars and synthetic facial foam.
   (B) Pure, natural earth or uncarved rock and stone.
   (C) Modern plastic prayer mats.
   (D) Clean wool carpets or blankets.

5. What is the unique characteristic of *Tarji'* in the Madinan Adhan according to the Maliki school?
   (A) Calling the Adhan in complete darkness without raising the voice.
   (B) Repeating the two Shahadahs aloud after first reciting them softly to oneself.
   (C) Singing the phrases in four different classical poetic meters.
   (D) Calling the Adhan from horseback outside the city walls.

6. In Maliki jurisprudence, when is the Prostration of Forgetfulness performed **BEFORE the final Salam** (*Sujud al-Qabli*)?
   (A) When an additional rak'ah was added by mistake.
   (B) When two or more confirmed sunnahs were omitted (subtracted) from the prayer.
   (C) When a person forgets to wear their shoes into the mosque.
   (D) When the prayer is completed five minutes ahead of schedule.

7. If a praying person forgets the middle Tashahhud and stands up directly for the third rak'ah, what should they do?
   (A) Terminate the prayer immediately and repeat it from the beginning.
   (B) Continue the prayer without sitting back down, and perform Sujud al-Qabli before the final Salam.
   (C) Turn around, sit down, and pay a monetary fine to the poor.
   (D) Perform ten prostrations after the final Salam.

8. When is Sujud al-Ba'di performed?
   (A) When an addition (*Ziyadah*) occurred in the prayer, performed after the final Salam.
   (B) Before standing up from the first rak'ah.
   (C) During the Friday Khutbah while listening to the Imam.
   (D) Whenever the weather is excessively cold.

9. How does a worshipper resolve doubt (*Shakk*) regarding whether they have completed 3 or 4 rak'ahs?
   (A) Guess based on whatever feeling comes first into their heart.
   (B) Build upon absolute certainty (*Al-Yaqin* = 3 rak'ahs), pray the remaining rak'ah, and perform Sujud al-Ba'di.
   (C) Walk out of prayer and ask someone in the congregation.
   (D) Perform the prayer again twelve times to be completely sure.

10. What is the extent of wiping the head (*Mash ar-Ra's*) required in the Maliki school?
    (A) Touching three hairs at the front of the head with a damp finger.
    (B) Wiping the entire head from the forehead to the nape of the neck and back.
    (C) Wiping only the right ear and temple.
    (D) Pouring a full pitcher of warm water over the hair.

## Part B: Fill in the Blanks

3. Pure natural mineral earth, clay, or stone used for dry purification is termed ______.
4. The prostration performed before the final salam to repair an omission of two sunnahs is Sujud ______.
5. When a worshipper accidentally adds an extra bowing or prostration, they repair it with Sujud ______.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Al-Dalk ─── Rubbing the limb with the wet hand during wudu
* Al-Muwalat ─── Continuity of washing limbs without excessive pause
* Sujud al-Qabli ─── Two prostrations before salam for omissions of sunnahs
* Sujud al-Ba'di ─── Two prostrations after salam for additions to prayer
* Al-Tarji' ─── Proclaiming the Shahadah aloud after quietly uttering it in Adhan

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. Explain the wisdom behind requiring *Al-Dalk* (rubbing) in wudu rather than merely pouring water passively over the skin.
2. A student is praying Dhuhr and suddenly doubts whether they are in their 2nd or 3rd rak'ah. Walk through the exact Maliki steps they must take to complete and repair their prayer.
3. Compare the Maliki view on wiping the head with the views of the Shafi'i and Hanafi schools. Why are all three considered valid Sunni paths?
4. When is a traveler permitted to use *Sa'id* for Tayammum instead of searching for water?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - Matn Ibn 'Ashir outlines exactly 7 fara'id: Niyyah, Face, Arms, Head, Feet, Dalk, and Muwalat.
2. B - Dalk (rubbing) and Muwalat (continuity) are obligatory pillars in the Maliki school.
3. B - The legal boundary of the face spans hairline to chin, and earlobe to earlobe.
4. B - Sa'id is pure unadulterated natural earth, rock, stone, or sand.
5. B - Tarji' is the Madinan practice of uttering the Shahadah softly before repeating it aloud.
6. B - Sujud al-Qabli is performed before salam for omissions of two or more confirmed sunnahs.
7. B - The worshipper continues without returning to the sitting position, rectifying the omitted sunnah with Sujud al-Qabli before salam.
8. A - Sujud al-Ba'di is performed after the final salam to repair additions (Ziyadah).
9. B - Legal maxim: Certainty is not dispelled by doubt; build on the minimum (3), complete the 4th, say salam, and perform Sujud al-Ba'di.
10. B - The Maliki school requires wiping the entire head, not merely a fraction of it.
