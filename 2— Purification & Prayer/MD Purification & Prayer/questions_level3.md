# Level 3 Quiz: Purification & Prayer

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions

1. How many obligatory acts (Fara'id) of Wudu are codified in the classical Maliki text *Matn Ibn 'Ashir*?
   (A) Four obligations
   (B) Five obligations
   (C) Six obligations
   (D) Seven obligations

2. Which two acts are among the seven obligations (Fara'id) of Wudu in the Maliki school, whereas the Shafi'i and Hanafi schools classify both as Sunnahs?
   (A) Washing the face and washing the arms to elbows
   (B) Rubbing the limbs (Al-Dalk) and continuity without delay (Al-Muwalat)
   (C) Wiping the ears and rinsing the mouth
   (D) Washing the hands to the wrists and using a siwak

3. What is the exact facial boundary (*Ghasl al-Wajh*) that must be washed during Wudu?
   (A) From the normal top hairline to the bottom of the chin, and from earlobe to earlobe
   (B) From the eyebrows to the bottom of the lower lip only
   (C) From the top of the forehead to the bridge of the nose
   (D) The entire head including the ears and the nape of the neck

4. When performing Tayammum in the absence of water, what substance is legally classified as *Sa'id* in the Maliki school?
   (A) Any dry cloth, wool carpet, or clean blanket
   (B) Processed clay tiles treated with chemical glaze
   (C) Pure natural earth, uncarved stone, rock, or clean sand
   (D) Purified ash or charcoal from wood fires

5. What is the distinct characteristic of *Al-Tarji'* in the Madinan Adhan according to the Maliki school?
   (A) Pronouncing the opening Takbir four times at high volume
   (B) Calling the Adhan in complete silence without moving the lips
   (C) Reciting the Iqamah in a single continuous breath without stopping
   (D) Uttering the two testimonies of faith softly to oneself before repeating them aloud at full volume

6. In relied-upon Maliki fiqh (*Mukhtasar al-Akhdari*, *Matn Ibn 'Ashir*), what is the exact method for performing *Sujud al-Qabli* (before Salam)?
   (A) Complete the final Tashahhud, say Salam, prostrate twice, recite a second Tashahhud, and say Salam again
   (B) Complete the final Tashahhud, perform two prostrations before the Salam, rise and immediately say Salam without reciting a second Tashahhud
   (C) Stand up immediately after the final rak'ah and pray a complete extra rak'ah
   (D) Prostrate four times before the Tashahhud and give charity

7. If a praying person accidentally omits the middle Tashahhud and stands up directly for the third rak'ah, how should they rectify the prayer in Maliki fiqh?
   (A) Sit back down immediately even after standing straight, and repeat the Tashahhud
   (B) Break the prayer immediately and start again from the opening Takbir
   (C) Continue standing, complete the remaining units of prayer, and perform Sujud al-Qabli before the final Salam
   (D) Finish the prayer normally and perform Sujud al-Ba'di after the final Salam

8. When is *Sujud al-Ba'di* (after the final Salam) performed in the Maliki school?
   (A) When an accidental addition (*Ziyadah*, such as an extra bowing or prostration) occurred during the prayer
   (B) When two or more confirmed Sunnahs were omitted from the prayer
   (C) Before reciting the Fatihah in the first rak'ah
   (D) Whenever the worshipper feels tired during voluntary prayers

9. How does a worshipper resolve doubt (*Shakk*) regarding whether they have completed 3 or 4 rak'ahs in a four-rak'ah prayer?
   (A) Guess based on whatever suspicion feels strongest in the heart
   (B) Terminate the prayer immediately, make a new wudu, and restart
   (C) Treat it as 4 rak'ahs, make Salam, and perform Sujud al-Qabli
   (D) Build upon certainty by treating it as 3 rak'ahs, complete the 4th rak'ah, say Salam, and perform Sujud al-Ba'di

10. What is the extent of wiping the head (*Mash al-Ra's*) required as an obligatory pillar in Maliki fiqh?
    (A) Touching three hairs on the crown of the head with a damp finger
    (B) Wiping the entire head from the front hairline back to the nape of the neck
    (C) Wiping only the right side of the head and the ear
    (D) Pouring a cup of water over the head without touching it

---

## Part C: Matching Terms

* Al-Dalk ─── Rubbing each limb with the wet hand during wudu
* Al-Muwalat ─── Washing limbs consecutively without excessive delay
* Sujud al-Qabli ─── Two prostrations before Salam to repair omissions of Sunnahs
* Sujud al-Ba'di ─── Two prostrations after Salam to repair additions to prayer
* Al-Tarji' ─── Reciting the Shahadah softly before repeating it aloud in Adhan

## Part D: Reflection & Application

16. Explain the wisdom behind requiring *Al-Dalk* (rubbing) in wudu rather than allowing water to simply slide over the skin.
17. A worshipper praying Dhuhr forgets whether they have completed 2 or 3 rak'ahs. Outline the exact steps according to Maliki fiqh to resolve their doubt and conclude the prayer lawfully.
18. Compare the Maliki ruling on *Sujud al-Qabli* with *Sujud al-Ba'di*. Why does the omission of a Sunnah take precedence over an addition when both happen in the same prayer?

---

## Answer Key & Marking Guide

1. D - Matn Ibn 'Ashir (lines 48-50) outlines seven obligations: intention, washing face, washing arms, wiping entire head, washing feet, rubbing (Dalk), and continuity (Muwalat).
2. B - Al-Dalk (rubbing) and Al-Muwalat (continuity) are obligatory pillars in the Maliki school, while Shafi'i and Hanafi classify both as Sunnahs.
3. A - The legal boundary of the face spans hairline to chin vertically, and earlobe to earlobe horizontally.
4. C - Sa'id refers to natural unadulterated earth, including stone, rock, sand, and clean soil.
5. D - Al-Tarji' in the Madinan Adhan is the practice of uttering the Shahadah softly to oneself before proclaiming it aloud.
6. B - In relied-upon Maliki fiqh (*Mukhtasar al-Akhdari*, *Matn Ibn 'Ashir*), Sujud al-Qabli is performed before Salam, and the worshipper immediately gives Salam without reciting a second Tashahhud.
7. C - When the middle Tashahhud is omitted, the worshipper continues standing, completes the remaining units, and performs Sujud al-Qabli before the final Salam.
8. A - Sujud al-Ba'di is performed after the final Salam to rectify accidental additions (Ziyadah).
9. D - Legal maxim: Certainty is not dispelled by doubt; build upon the lower number (3), pray the 4th, say Salam, and perform Sujud al-Ba'di.
10. B - The Maliki obligation is to wipe the entire head from the hairline to the nape of the neck (returning the hands to the front is an emphasized Sunnah).

* Al-Muwalat connects with Washing limbs consecutively without excessive delay.

### Reflection Marking Rubric
- Question 16: Awards full points for explaining that rubbing ensures water thoroughly contacts every pore of the skin, removing oils or barriers and embodying conscious, attentive purification rather than rushed, passive wetting.
- Question 17: Awards full points for stating: build on certainty (treat as 2 rak'ahs), pray the 3rd and 4th rak'ahs, say the final Salam, and perform two prostrations Ba'di followed by Tashahhud and a second Salam.
- Question 18: Awards full points for explaining that Qabli repairs a deficiency before the prayer is sealed with Salam, whereas Ba'di repairs an excess after the prayer has completed its core obligations; when combined, repairing the defect before concluding the prayer takes priority.
