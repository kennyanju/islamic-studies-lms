### Part B: Scenario-Based Questions (5 Questions)
*Read the scenario and choose or write the correct response based on Maliki rulings.*

11. **Yusuf is doing Wudu. He pours water over his right arm, but does not rub it with his hand. According to the Maliki school, is his Wudu valid?**
    *   *Answer:* No. Yusuf missed the obligation of **Dalk** (rubbing the limb with the hand). Simply letting water flow over the skin is not enough in the Maliki school.

12. **Amina is performing Wudu. Just as she finishes washing her face, her phone rings. She answers it and talks for 15 minutes. Her face is completely dry. She then washes her arms. Is her Wudu valid?**
    *   *Answer:* No. Amina broke the obligation of **Muwalat** (continuity). Because she paused for so long that her previous limb dried, she must start her Wudu over from the beginning.

13. **Zayd is traveling and cannot find any water to make Wudu. He decides to perform Tayammum. He finds a clean, solid limestone rock. Can he use this rock for Tayammum?**
    *   *Answer:* Yes. In the Maliki school, natural stone is considered **Sa'id** (natural surface of the earth) and is perfectly valid for Tayammum.

14. **Maryam is making Wudu. By mistake, she wipes her head before washing her arms. She remembers her mistake while washing her feet. What should she do according to the Maliki school?**
    *   *Answer:* Her Wudu is still valid because **Tarteeb** (order) is a Sunnah, not a Fard, in the Maliki school. However, to follow the Sunnah, she should wash her arms, then wipe her head, and then wash her feet in the correct order.

15. **Bilal stands up to pray Zuhr. He lets his arms hang naturally at his sides. His friend tells him, "Your prayer is invalid because you didn't cross your arms." How should Bilal respond?**
    *   *Answer:* Bilal should explain that letting the arms rest at the sides is called **Sadl**. It is the standard Sunnah practice of the Maliki school, based on the practice of the people of Madinah (*'Amal Ahl al-Madinah*). Both crossing the arms (Qabd) and letting them rest (Sadl) are valid sunnahs.

### Part C: Short Answer & Matching (5 Questions)
*Answer the questions briefly or match the terms.*

16. **Match the Arabic term to its correct definition:**
    *   1. *'Imad al-Din* -> ______ (Answer: Pillar of the Religion)
    *   2. *Najasah* -> ______ (Answer: Physical filth)
    *   3. *Hadath* -> ______ (Answer: Ritual state of impurity)
    *   4. *Shukr* -> ______ (Answer: Gratitude)

17. **Why is the prayer compared to the center pole of a tent?**
    *   *Answer:* Because if you pull out the center pole (*'imad*), the whole tent collapses. Similarly, prayer holds up our faith, and without it, our connection to Allah collapses.

18. **Name the five obligatory prayers in order, starting from the morning.**
    *   *Answer:* Subh (Fajr), Zuhr, 'Asr, Maghrib, 'Isha.

19. **What are the three things that make Ghusl obligatory?**
    *   *Answer:* Major impurity (Janabah), completion of menstruation (Hayd), and completion of post-natal bleeding (Nifas).

20. **Why did Imam Malik prefer Sadl over crossing the arms in prayer?**
    *   *Answer:* He observed that the scholars and the descendants of the Companions in the city of Madinah prayed with their arms at their sides (*'Amal Ahl al-Madinah*).
