## 1. The Fiqh of Purity: Approaching the Divine Presence

At ages 10–12, your daily prayer (*Salah*) transitions into a structured, mature covenant with Allah. Purity (*Taharah*) is not merely washing off physical mud; it is a sacred prerequisite that cleanses the heart, elevates the soul, and unlocks the gate of prayer.

Imam Malik taught that the beauty of worship lies in following the precise sunnah of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ as practiced by the community of Madinah.

## 2. The 7 Fara'id of Wudu in Matn Ibn 'Ashir

In *Al-Murshid al-Mu'in*, Imam Ibn 'Ashir states:
```
فَرَائِضُ الْوُضُوءِ سَبْعَةٌ وَهِيْ *** نِيَّةٌ بَدْءٍ غَسْلُ وَجْهٍ يَا أُخَيْ
وَغَسْلُ يَدَيْهِ إِلَى الْمِرْفَقَيْنِ *** وَمَسْحُ رَأْسٍ كُلِّهِ كَالْأُذُنَيْنِ
وَغَسْلُ رِجْلَيْهِ إِلَى الْكَعْبَيْنِ *** وَالدَّلْكُ وَالْفَوْرُ عَلَى ذِي الْعَيْنِ
```

1. **Al-Niyyah (النِّيَّة) — Intention:** Formed in the heart at the very first wash (either to remove ritual impurity / *Raf' al-Hadath*, or to fulfill the obligation of wudu).
2. **Ghasl al-Wajh (غَسْلُ الْوَجْهِ) — Washing the Face:** From the normal hairline to the bottom of the chin, and from earlobe to earlobe.
3. **Ghasl al-Yadayn (غَسْلُ الْيَدَيْنِ) — Washing the Arms:** From the fingertips all the way to and including the elbows (*Al-Mirfaqayn*).
4. **Mash ar-Ra's (مَسْحُ الرَّأْسِ) — Wiping the Entire Head:** Wiping the whole scalp from the forehead to the nape of the neck, and returning to the front.
5. **Ghasl al-Rijlayn (غَسْلُ الرِّجْلَيْنِ) — Washing the Feet:** Washing both feet thoroughly up to and including the ankles (*Al-Ka'bayn*).
6. **Al-Dalk (الدَّلْكُ) — Rubbing:** Passing the wet hand over the limb while or immediately after pouring water, ensuring the water penetrates every pore.
7. **Al-Muwalat / Al-Fawr (الْمُوَالاةُ / الْفَوْرُ) — Continuity:** Washing each consecutive limb before the previous limb naturally dries under moderate weather.

## 3. Ghusl and Tayammum: Purification with Sa'id

* **Ghusl (Major Ritual Bath):** Required after major ritual states (e.g. dreams, post-menstruation). Its fara'id are Niyyah, washing the whole body with water, Dalk (rubbing), and Muwalat.
* **Tayammum (Dry Ablution):** When water is genuinely unavailable or medically harmful (e.g. severe burns or illness).
  * Performed using pure natural earth (*Sa'id*), such as uncarved rock, clean sand, or natural stone.
  * *Method:* Strike pure rock once with hands, shake off dust, wipe the face, then wipe both forearms to the elbows.

## 4. The Call to Prayer: The Madinan Adhan with Tarji'

In the Maliki school, the Adhan reflects the historical Madinan practice of Sayyidina Bilal:
* **Takbir:** Announced twice at the beginning (*Allahu Akbar, Allahu Akbar*).
* **Al-Tarji' (التَّرْجِيع):** The caller whispers the two Shahadahs softly to himself, and then proclaims them aloud with full vocal strength, symbolizing faith entering the heart secretly before being announced publicly!

## 5. Sujud as-Sahw Clinic: Repairing Prayer Mistakes

Mistakes happen during prayer. Allah's mercy provides a precise legal mechanism to repair errors without invalidating the entire prayer:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                   SUJUD AS-SAHW IN MALIKI FIQH                         │
│                                                                        │
│ 1. AL-QABLI (الْقَبْلِيُّ) — BEFORE the Final Salam:                   │
│    Performed when you SUBTRACT (omit) two or more confirmed sunnahs    │
│    (such as forgetting the middle Tashahhud and standing up).          │
│    Method: After completing the final Tashahhud, perform two           │
│    prostrations, recite another Tashahhud, and say Salam.              │
│                                                                        │
│ 2. AL-BA'DI (الْبَعْدِيُّ) — AFTER the Final Salam:                    │
│    Performed when you ADD something extra to the prayer (such as an    │
│    extra bowing, prostration, or fifth rak'ah by accident).            │
│    Method: Say Salam to end prayer, perform two prostrations, recite   │
│    Tashahhud, and say Salam again.                                     │
│                                                                        │
│ 3. HANDLING DOUBT (SHAKK):                                             │
│    Always build on CERTAINTY (Al-Yaqin = the lower number of rak'ahs). │
│    If you doubt whether you prayed 3 or 4 rak'ahs, treat it as 3,      │
│    pray the 4th, say Salam, and perform two prostrations BA'DI.        │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
