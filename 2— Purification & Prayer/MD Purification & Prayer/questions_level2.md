   (B) Water with soap and perfume added for fragrance

   (A) Seven obligations (Fara'id)

3. Which of the following is an obligatory act (Fard) of Wudu according to the Maliki school?
   (A) Washing the hands to the wrists three times before starting
   (B) Rinsing the mouth with water three times (Madmadah)
   (C) Saying Bismillah before touching the water
   (D) Gentle rubbing of the limb with the wet hand (Al-Dalk)

4. How many total rak'ahs are prayed for Maghrib prayer?
   (A) Two rak'ahs
   (B) Three rak'ahs
   (C) Four rak'ahs
   (D) Five rak'ahs

5. What is the classical Maliki term for standing in prayer with the arms resting naturally and gently at the sides?
   (A) Qabd
   (B) Ruku'
   (C) Sadl
   (D) Sujud

## Part C: Matching Terms

* Al-Dalk ─── Rubbing each limb with the wet hand during washing
* Al-Muwalat ─── Washing limbs consecutively without delay
* Mash al-Ra's ─── Wiping the entire head from hairline to nape
* Sadl ─── Standing with arms resting naturally at the sides in prayer
* Ma' Tahur ─── Natural water that is pure and purifying for Wudu

11. Why is understanding the difference between obligatory acts (Fara'id) and confirmed Sunnahs helpful when water is limited or when we are traveling?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide

1. C - Al-Ma' al-Tahur (pure and purifying water) is the only category of water legally valid for lifting ritual impurity through Wudu and Ghusl.
2. A - In Maliki jurisprudence (*Matn Ibn 'Ashir* and *al-Akhdari*), there are exactly seven obligatory acts (Fara'id) of Wudu.
3. D - Al-Dalk (rubbing the limb with the wet hand) is an obligatory pillar of Wudu in the Maliki school, while washing hands to wrists, mouth rinsing, and Bismillah are sunnahs or fada'il.
4. B - Maghrib prayer consists of three rak'ahs: the first two recited aloud and the third recited quietly.
5. C - Sadl is the relied-upon Maliki position of letting the arms rest naturally at the sides during the standing posture of obligatory prayer.

8. Muwalat - Continuity without excessive delay.
9. Qiblah - The direction toward the Ka'bah.
10. Tahur - Pure and purifying water.
