## 1. Modesty (*Haya'*): The Crown of Muslim Adulthood

As you mature through puberty (*Bulugh*), society constantly pressures youth to seek validation through physical appearance and superficial trends. Islam liberates you from the tyranny of vanity through **Haya'** (sacred modesty, dignity, and self-respect).

The Prophet ﷺ said:  
*“Modesty (Haya') does not bring anything except good.”* (Sahih al-Bukhari).

### The Three Golden Rules of Islamic Attire:
1. **Loose and Opaque (*Satir*):** Clothes must conceal the shape of the body and must not be see-through or clinging.
2. **Avoiding Arrogance (*Kibr*):** Dressing to show off wealth or look down upon others is strictly forbidden.
3. **Preserving Gender Identity:** The Prophet ﷺ prohibited men from imitating the distinct clothing or mannerisms of women, and women from imitating men.

## 2. Financial Honesty and Commercial Integrity

Adolescence is when young people begin buying, selling, trading sneakers, gaming accounts, or working part-time jobs. Islam demands absolute honesty in trade:

* **The Prohibition of Cheating (*Al-Ghash*):**  
  The Prophet ﷺ once passed by a merchant selling a pile of grain. He inserted his blessed hand deep into the pile and found that the grain underneath was damp. He asked: *"What is this, O merchant?"* The man replied: *"Rain fell on it, O Messenger of Allah!"* The Prophet ﷺ said:  
  *“Why did you not place the damp grain on top so people could see it? Whoever cheats us is not one of us!”* (Sahih Muslim).
* **Full Disclosure of Defects:** If you sell a bike, computer, or shoes that have a flaw or scratch, it is an **obligation (*Fard*)** to disclose that defect to the buyer before accepting payment. Concealing a defect makes your earned profit unlawful (*Suht*)!
