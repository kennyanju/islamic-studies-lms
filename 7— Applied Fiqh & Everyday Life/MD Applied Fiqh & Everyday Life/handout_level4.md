# Level 4 Student Handout: Applied Fiqh & Everyday Life
**Age Group:** Ages 12–14 (Level 4 Track: Adolescent & Puberty / Years 7 & 8)  
**Topics:** Modesty (Haya'), Categories of 'Awrah in Maliki Fiqh, Financial Honesty in Commerce, and Digital Ethics  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari*, *Matn Ibn 'Ashir*, & *Al-Risalah* of Ibn Abi Zayd  
**Riwayah & Tajweed:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 29: Surah Al-Insan and Al-Mursalat)

## 1. Modesty (*Haya'*): The Crown of Muslim Adulthood
As you mature through puberty (*Bulugh*), society constantly pressures youth to seek validation through physical appearance and superficial trends. Islam liberates you from the tyranny of vanity through **Haya'** (sacred modesty, dignity, and self-respect).

The Prophet ﷺ said:  
> *“Modesty (Haya') does not bring anything except good.”* (Sahih al-Bukhari 6117, Sahih Muslim 37).

### Maliki Rulings on 'Awrah in Prayer and Public:
In the Maliki school (*Mukhtasar al-Akhdari*, *Matn Ibn 'Ashir*), scholars divide the areas of modesty into **'Awrah Mughallazah** (severe/heavy nakedness) and **'Awrah Mukhaffafah** (light/secondary nakedness):

| Category | For Young Men | For Young Women |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **'Awrah Mughallazah (Severe)** | The private parts (the front and back passages). | The torso, chest, back, and hair in prayer. |
| **'Awrah Mukhaffafah (Light)** | The thighs and buttocks from below the navel to above the knees. | The limbs (arms, shins, feet). In public, her entire body is covered except face and hands. |
| **Special Male Prohibitions** | Pure silk garments and gold jewelry are strictly forbidden for males. | Permitted for women as personal adornment. |

### The Three Golden Rules of Islamic Attire:
1. **Loose and Opaque (*Satir*):** Clothes must conceal the shape of the body and must not be see-through or clinging.
2. **Avoiding Arrogance (*Kibr*):** Dressing to show off wealth or look down upon others is strictly forbidden.
3. **Preserving Gender Identity:** The Prophet ﷺ prohibited men from imitating the distinct clothing or mannerisms of women, and women from imitating men.

## 2. Financial Honesty and Commercial Integrity
Adolescence is when young people begin buying, selling, trading sneakers, gaming accounts, or working part-time jobs. Islam demands absolute honesty in trade:

* **The Prohibition of Cheating (*Al-Ghash*):**  
  The Prophet ﷺ once passed by a merchant selling a pile of grain. He inserted his blessed hand deep into the pile and found that the grain underneath was damp. He asked: *"What is this, O merchant?"* The man replied: *"Rain fell on it, O Messenger of Allah!"* The Prophet ﷺ said:  
  *“Why did you not place the damp grain on top so people could see it? Whoever cheats us is not one of us!”* (Sahih Muslim 102).
* **Full Disclosure of Defects:** If you sell a bike, computer, or shoes that have a flaw or scratch, it is an **obligation (*Fard*)** to disclose that defect to the buyer before accepting payment. Concealing a defect makes your earned profit unlawful (*Suht*)!
* **Khiyar al-Majlis (The Contract Option):** In the Maliki school, based on 'Amal Ahl al-Madinah, once a buyer and seller agree upon the price and finalize the contract verbally or by exchange, the sale is binding and finalized, unless a defect (*'Ayb*) is subsequently discovered.

## 3. Digital Ethics in the Age of Algorithms and Social Media
The internet and social media platforms are modern extensions of our public and private lives. The angels record every keystroke, comment, share, and view!

| Digital Ethic Principle | Scriptural Reference | Practical Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Verification (*Tabayyanu*)** | Surah Al-Hujurat 49:6 | Never forward or believe viral news without investigating its authenticity first. |
| **2. Zero Cyberbullying** | Sahih al-Bukhari 10 | The Muslim is one from whose tongue and hand others are safe. Mockery online is a grave sin. |
| **3. Digital Modesty & Privacy** | Hadith in Tirmidhi 2501 | Never share screenshots of private messages, post without consent, or display private 'Awrah online. |
| **4. Intellectual Property** | IIFA Fatwas on Copyright | Pirating software, stealing courses, or cracking commercial digital products violates the owner's legal rights. |
| **5. Time Stewardship** | Surah Al-'Asr 103:1-3 | Guard your finite hours from endless algorithm scrolling (*Lahw*) to protect study, family, and prayer. |
