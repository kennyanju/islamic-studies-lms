# Level 1 Student Handout: Applied Fiqh & Everyday Life
**Age Group:** Ages 6–8 (Level 1 Track: Primary Stage 1)  
**Core Strands:** Islamic Table Manners | Halal & Pure Foods | Modesty & Cleanliness | Respect for Others' Property  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari* (Everyday Purity & Manners)  
**Riwayah & Surah Target:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 30: Surah Quraysh & Surah Al-Kawthar)

---

## 1. Table Manners of a Young Believer
Islam turns every meal into a time of joy, health, and worship! Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught a young boy named 'Umar ibn Abi Salamah three golden rules for eating:
> **يَا غُلاَمُ: سَمِّ اللهَ، وَكُلْ بِيَمِينِكَ، وَكُلْ مِمَّا يَلِيكَ**  
> *"O young boy! Mention the name of Allah (say Bismillah), eat with your right hand, and eat from what is directly in front of you!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari 5376, Sahih Muslim 2022).

| Golden Rule | Practical Action | Sunnah Etiquette |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Say Bismillah** | Mention Allah's Name before the first bite. | Starts the meal with blessing and mindfulness. |
| **2. Use Right Hand** | Eat and drink with your clean right hand. | Shows dignity, cleanliness, and obedience to the Sunnah. |
| **3. Eat From the Front** | Take from what is directly in front of you. | Avoids reaching across other people's plates or food. |

### When You Finish Your Meal:
Wipe your mouth, clear your plate, and say our happy thank you to Allah:  
> **الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَطْعَمَنَا وَسَقَانَا وَجَعَلَنَا مُسْلِمِينَ**  
> *"All praise belongs to Allah who fed us, gave us drink, and made us Muslims!"* (Sunan Abi Dawud 3850, Jami' at-Tirmidhi 3457).

## 2. Halal and Pure Foods: Fuel for Growing Champions
Allah created delicious fruits, crunchy vegetables, sweet dates, golden honey, and cool milk for us to enjoy!

* **What is Halal?** Halal means lawful, healthy, and permitted by Allah.
* **What is Haram?** Haram means forbidden by Allah because it harms our body or soul. We never eat pork, bacon, or anything containing alcohol or harmful chemicals.
* **Pure Cleanliness (Tayyib):** Always wash fresh apples and carrots before eating, and wash your hands with soap before sitting at the table.

---

## 3. Cleanliness, Pleasant Smell, and Modesty
Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was the cleanest and most dignified person who ever walked the earth! He loved:
* Putting on clean, pressed clothes.
* Brushing his teeth with the fragrant siwak twig.
* Wearing a drop of pleasant perfume (*'Itr*).
* Dressing with **modesty** (*Haya'*), ensuring that our private parts are always covered respectfully.

---

## 4. Respecting Others' Property (Amanah)
A young Muslim is completely trustworthy (*Amin*):
* If you borrow a toy, book, or crayon from a brother, sister, or classmate, ask with a smile: *"Please may I borrow this?"*
* Keep it safe and never break it!
* Return it promptly with a warm smile and say: *"JazakAllahu Khayran!"*
* If you find a dropped coin, jacket, or pencil on the playground, never keep it secretly; give it to your teacher or parent so the owner can find it!
