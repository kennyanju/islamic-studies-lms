# Level 5 Quiz: Applied Fiqh & Everyday Life

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. In the Maliki Madhhab, what is the legal status of a marriage contract (*Nikah*) conducted secretly where the two witnesses are sworn to confidentiality?
   (A) It is fully valid and encouraged for university students.
   (B) It is legally void (*Fasid*), because public proclamation (*I'lan*) is an essential condition in Maliki fiqh.
   (C) It requires a fine of one hundred gold dinars.
   (D) It becomes valid after forty days automatically.

2. What is the fundamental nature of the bridal gift (**Mahr**) in Islamic family law?
   (A) A purchase price paid to the bride's father for her custody.
   (B) An obligatory gift of wealth given exclusively and directly to the bride, remaining her absolute private property.
   (C) A joint savings account managed by the local mosque committee.
   (D) A government tax collected upon signing the marriage license.

3. Which legal mechanism allows an Islamic judge or Shari'ah council to dissolve a marriage if the husband commits abuse or fails to provide financial maintenance?
   (A) Talaq Raj'i.
   (B) Faskh (Judicial Dissolution).
   (C) Khul'.
   (D) Zihar.

4. What are the six explicit fractional shares (*Furud*) revealed in the Quran for Islamic inheritance?
   (A) 1/5, 1/10, 1/20, 1/30, 1/40, and 1/50.
   (B) 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 2/3, 1/3, and 1/6.
   (C) 1/3, 2/5, 3/7, 4/9, 5/11, and 6/13.
   (D) 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50%, and 60%.

5. Which five primary family members can NEVER be excluded from receiving inheritance under Islamic law?
   (A) Grandfather, Grandmother, Uncle, Aunt, and Cousin.
   (B) Father, Mother, Husband or Wife, Son, and Daughter.
   (C) Neighbor, Business Partner, Teacher, Friend, and Adopted Child.
   (D) Eldest Son, Eldest Daughter, Stepmother, Nephew, and Niece.

6. Why does a brother typically inherit double the share of his sister from their deceased parents' estate?
   (A) Because men are inherently superior in spiritual rank to women.
   (B) Because Islamic law imposes 100% of all household financial burdens on men, while the woman's share is completely free of any financial obligations.
   (C) Because women were not allowed to own bank accounts in early history.
   (D) Because the brother is required to donate his entire share to the mosque.

7. At how many days of embryonic development does ensoulment (*Nafkh al-Ruh*) take place according to authentic Prophetic tradition?
   (A) At 14 days.
   (B) At 40 days.
   (C) At 120 days (approx. 4 months).
   (D) At the exact moment of birth.

8. When is abortion legally permitted after the milestone of 120 days (ensoulment)?
   (A) If the parents discover the gender is not what they preferred.
   (B) Only if a certified medical panel confirms it is the sole means to save the established life of the mother.
   (C) If the family experiences financial inflation.
   (D) Under no circumstances whatsoever, even if the mother dies.

9. What is the consensus of the International Islamic Fiqh Academy (IIFA) regarding third-party surrogacy and donor sperm in IVF?
   (A) Permissible if the surrogate mother is paid an agreed salary.
   (B) Strictly prohibited, because it introduces foreign genetic lineage, violating the objective of preserving lineage (*Hifdh al-Nasl*).
   (C) Encouraged for couples living in Western countries.
   (D) Permitted only if the donor remains anonymous.

10. On what Quranic verse did major contemporary fatwa councils base the permissibility of deceased and living organ donation?
    (A) Surah Al-Baqarah (2:255 - Ayat al-Kursi).
    (B) Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:32: *"And whoever saves a life, it is as though he has saved all of humanity"*).
    (C) Surah Al-Ikhlas (112:1).
    (D) Surah Al-Kafirun (109:6).
