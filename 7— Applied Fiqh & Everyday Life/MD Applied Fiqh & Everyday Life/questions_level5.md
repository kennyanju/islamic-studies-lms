# Level 5 Quiz: Applied Fiqh & Everyday Life

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. In the Maliki Madhhab, what is the legal ruling regarding a marriage contract (Nikah) conducted in secret where the attendees are sworn to confidentiality (Nikah al-Sirr)?
   (A) It is legally invalid (Fasid), because public proclamation (I'lan) is an essential condition.
   (B) It is fully valid and recommended for university students.
   (C) It becomes valid automatically after the birth of the first child.
   (D) It requires only a financial penalty paid to the local judge.

2. What is the legal definition and ownership of the bridal gift (Mahr / Sadaq) in Islamic jurisprudence?
   (A) A joint family savings account co-managed by both sets of parents.
   (B) A purchase payment given to the bride's father for custody of his daughter.
   (C) An obligatory financial gift given exclusively and directly to the bride as her absolute personal property.
   (D) A government registration fee paid to the local Islamic court.

3. Which legal mechanism allows an Islamic judge or Shari'ah council to dissolve a marriage if the husband commits abuse or fails to provide essential financial maintenance?
   (A) Talaq Raj'i (Revocable repudiation).
   (B) Faskh (Judicial Dissolution).
   (C) Zihar (Pre-Islamic injurious comparison).
   (D) Li'an (Mutual sworn imprecation).

4. What are the six explicit fractional shares (Al-Furud) revealed in Surah An-Nisa (4:11-12) for Islamic inheritance?
   (A) 1/5, 1/10, 1/15, 1/20, 1/25, and 1/30.
   (B) 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50%, and 60%.
   (C) 1/3, 2/5, 3/7, 4/9, 5/11, and 6/13.
   (D) 1/2, 1/4, 1/8, 2/3, 1/3, and 1/6.

5. Which five primary family members can NEVER be completely excluded from receiving an inheritance share under Islamic law?
   (A) Father, Mother, Husband or Wife, Son, and Daughter.
   (B) Paternal Uncle, Maternal Aunt, Nephew, Niece, and Grandparent.
   (C) Eldest Son, Eldest Daughter, Stepmother, Cousin, and Milk Sibling (Rada'ah).
   (D) Business Partner, Neighbor, Teacher, Close Friend, and Adopted Ward.

6. What is the primary socio-economic rationale in Islamic law for why a brother often receives double the inheritance share of his sister?
   (A) Men possess higher spiritual and devotional standing in the sight of Allah.
   (B) Female heirs are legally prohibited from opening personal commercial investments.
   (C) Men carry complete legal liability for all household maintenance and dependent relatives, whereas women have zero financial maintenance obligations.
   (D) Brothers are required by law to surrender their entire inherited share to the state treasury.

7. At how many days of gestational development does ensoulment (Nafkh al-Ruh) take place according to authentic Prophetic tradition?
   (A) At 14 days following conception.
   (B) At 40 days following conception.
   (C) At the exact moment of physical birth.
   (D) At approximately 120 days (four months) of development.

8. Under what circumstance does consensus among major Islamic fiqh councils permit abortion after the milestone of 120 days (ensoulment)?
   (A) If the parents experience economic anxiety regarding education costs.
   (B) Only if a certified specialist medical panel confirms it is the sole means to save the established life of the mother.
   (C) If prenatal testing reveals that the infant will be of an unpreferred gender.
   (D) Under no circumstances whatsoever, even if the mother's life is definitely lost.

9. What is the unanimous resolution of the International Islamic Fiqh Academy regarding third-party surrogacy, donor sperm, or donor eggs in IVF?
   (A) Strictly prohibited, because introducing third-party genetic material destroys lineage preservation (Hifdh al-Nasl).
   (B) Lawful and encouraged so long as the donor is paid a fair commercial fee.
   (C) Permitted exclusively if the donor agrees to remain completely anonymous.
   (D) Permissible only for couples residing in Western jurisdictions.

10. On which Quranic verse did major contemporary fatwa councils base the ethical permissibility of altruistic organ donation?
    (A) Surah Al-Baqarah (2:275: "Allah has permitted trade and has forbidden usurious interest").
    (B) Surah Al-An'am (6:145: "Say: I do not find in what has been revealed to me anything forbidden to eat").
    (C) Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:32: "And whoever saves a life, it is as though he has saved all of humanity").
    (D) Surah An-Nisa' (4:58: "Indeed, Allah commands you to render trusts back to whom they are due").
