# Level 4 Quiz: Applied Fiqh & Everyday Life

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ characterize the virtue of modesty (Haya') in Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim?
   (A) As an ancient custom that will disappear in the modern world.
   (B) As a weakness that prevents people from achieving business success.
   (C) As a quality restricted only to young children.
   (D) As an attribute that does not bring anything except good.

2. In the Maliki school (Mukhtasar al-Akhdari and Matn Ibn 'Ashir), what is the definition of 'Awrah Mughallazah (severe nakedness) for a mature male?
   (A) From the navel down to the bottom of the feet.
   (B) The private parts (the front and back passages).
   (C) The chest, shoulders, and back.
   (D) The knees, shins, and ankles.

3. Which two luxury materials are strictly prohibited for Muslim men to wear, but permitted for women as adornment?
   (A) Pure silk garments and gold jewelry.
   (B) Soft wool garments and bronze pins.
   (C) Polished leather shoes and linen turbans.
   (D) Silver rings and embroidered cotton shirts.

4. When the Prophet ﷺ discovered damp grain concealed beneath dry grain in the market, what decisive standard did he state?
   (A) "Sell damp grain at double the price to travelers."
   (B) "Burn the grain with fire before sunset."
   (C) "Whoever cheats us is not one of us!"
   (D) "Feed the damaged grain to stray birds."

5. In Islamic commercial law, what is the legal obligation upon a seller who knows their item has a flaw or defect?
   (A) They may conceal the flaw if the buyer forgets to ask about it.
   (B) They are only required to disclose defects if the item costs more than one hundred dirhams.
   (C) They should sell the item in dim lighting to avoid awkward questions.
   (D) They are obligated to disclose the defect clearly to the prospective buyer prior to sale.

6. What vital communication ethic is commanded in Surah Al-Hujurat (49:6) regarding news and internet rumors?
   (A) Repost every viral story quickly to build an audience.
   (B) Verify and investigate reports thoroughly before believing or spreading them (Tabayyanu).
   (C) Forward unverified messages to family group chats for advice.
   (D) Accept any video clip as authentic if it has many likes.

7. What is the status of online harassment, trolling, and cyberbullying in Islamic ethics?
   (A) Permissible if conducted under an anonymous username.
   (B) Recommended as an entertaining way to practice debating.
   (C) A serious sin that violates human dignity and prophetic brotherhood.
   (D) Lawful as long as the victim does not know who wrote the comment.

8. What is the Islamic legal ruling on taking screenshots of private text conversations and broadcasting them online without consent?
   (A) A betrayal of trust (Khiyanat al-Amanah) and an unlawful violation of privacy.
   (B) A recommended practice for establishing social transparency.
   (C) Lawful so long as the conversation took place during daylight hours.
   (D) Obligatory whenever someone says something amusing.

9. How do contemporary Islamic jurisprudence councils evaluate intellectual property, software copyright, and digital piracy?
   (A) Digital code has no physical body, so downloading pirated software is always lawful.
   (B) Copyright only applies to religious manuscripts written by hand.
   (C) Piracy is encouraged as long as the user is a student under age eighteen.
   (D) Intellectual creations represent protected property rights, and pirating commercial software is a form of theft.

10. What is the spiritual danger of spending excessive, uncontrolled hours scrolling social media feeds (Lahw)?
    (A) It automatically doubles a student's memorization capacity.
    (B) It numbs the spiritual heart, causes neglect of prayer, and wastes irreplaceable life.
    (C) It replaces the requirement of fasting during Ramadan.
    (D) It is classified as an act of sacred contemplation.

## Part B: Fill in the Blanks

2. The basic 'Awrah for young men extends from the ______.

4. Intentionally deceiving a buyer or concealing flaws in a sale is termed ______.
5. The Quranic command in Surah Al-Hujurat to verify incoming reports is ______.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Haya' ─── Sacred modesty and self-respect in conduct and appearance
* 'Awrah ─── The physical parts of the body that must be covered in public and prayer
* Al-Ghash ─── Cheating, deceptive marketing, or concealing defects in trade
* Tabayyanu ─── The ethical duty to investigate and verify information before sharing
* Suht ─── Unlawful, tainted wealth acquired through deception or fraud

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. A friend purchases a used video game console, discovers the disc drive malfunctions intermittently, and plans to resell it online without mentioning the issue. Explain the Islamic legal and spiritual ruling to them.
2. How does the Quranic injunction of Tabayyanu (Surah Al-Hujurat 49:6) provide a blueprint for combating algorithmic disinformation, viral hoaxes, and fake news today?
3. Discuss how Islamic guidelines on dress liberate youth from consumerist body-shaming and peer-pressure fashion trends.
4. Formulate three practical rules a Muslim teenager can adopt to protect their time and mental health from social media addiction.

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. D - The Prophet ﷺ declared that Haya' brings only goodness and is a branch of faith (Sahih al-Bukhari 6117, Sahih Muslim 37).
2. B - In Maliki fiqh, 'Awrah Mughallazah for men is the front and back private parts, while thighs and buttocks are Mukhaffafah.
3. A - Silk and gold are prohibited for males, preserving masculine dignity and humility.
4. C - The Prophet ﷺ stated in Sahih Muslim 102: "Whoever cheats us is not one of us."
5. D - Full disclosure of known defects is mandatory; concealment generates tainted income (Suht).
6. B - Surah Al-Hujurat 49:6 mandates rigorous verification of news brought by unverified sources before taking action.
7. C - Cyberbullying inflicts psychological harm, violating prophetic brotherhood and dignity (Sahih al-Bukhari 10).
8. A - Breaching private communication without consent is classified as betrayal of trust (Amanah).
9. D - Major contemporary fatwa councils recognize intellectual property as protected wealth (Mal).
10. B - Excessive distraction destroys spiritual mindfulness and wastes irreplaceable life hours (Surah Al-'Asr 103:1-3).
