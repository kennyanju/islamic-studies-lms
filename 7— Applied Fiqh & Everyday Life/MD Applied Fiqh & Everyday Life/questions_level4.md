# Level 4 Quiz: Applied Fiqh & Everyday Life

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ characterize the virtue of modesty (Haya') in Sahih al-Bukhari?
   (A) As an ancient custom that will disappear in the modern world.
   (B) As an attribute that does not bring anything except good.
   (C) As a weakness that prevents people from achieving wealth.
   (D) As a quality restricted only to women.

2. What is the defined basic 'Awrah (area of modesty) for mature Muslim males?
   (A) From the ankles to the knees only.
   (B) From the navel to the knee.
   (C) From the shoulders to the wrists.
   (D) The entire upper torso.

3. Which two luxury materials are strictly prohibited for Muslim men to wear, but permitted for women?
   (A) Cotton and linen.
   (B) Pure silk and gold jewelry.
   (C) Leather and wool.
   (D) Silver rings and bronze buckles.

4. What did the Prophet ﷺ declare when he discovered damp grain hidden beneath dry grain in the market?
   (A) "Sell it at a higher price to foreigners."
   (B) "Whoever cheats us is not one of us!"
   (C) "Dry it with fire before the sunset prayer."
   (D) "Distribute it to the horses of the army."

5. In Islamic commercial law, what is the legal obligation upon a seller who knows their item has a defect?
   (A) They must conceal the defect and lower the price by one dollar.
   (B) They must explicitly disclose the defect to the prospective buyer prior to sale.
   (C) They must sell it only at night under dim light.
   (D) They are exempt from telling anyone if the buyer does not ask.

6. What vital principle of communication is commanded in Surah Al-Hujurat (49:6) regarding internet rumors?
   (A) Share every exciting message immediately to gain followers.
   (B) Verify and investigate news thoroughly before believing or spreading it (*Tabayyanu*).
   (C) Forward rumors to at least ten group chats for advice.
   (D) Block anyone who questions the truth of an online video.

7. What is the Islamic ethical status of participating in online harassment, cyberbullying, or trolling?
   (A) Permissible if done anonymously behind a pseudonym.
   (B) A serious sin that violates the honor, dignity, and psychological safety of a human being.
   (C) Recommended as a method of developing debate skills.
   (D) Allowed if the target belongs to a different sports team.

8. What is the Islamic ruling regarding taking screenshots of private text messages and publishing them publicly without permission?
   (A) Completely lawful under free speech rules.
   (B) A betrayal of trust (*Khiyanat al-Amanah*) and a violation of privacy rights.
   (C) Obligatory if the messages contain humorous jokes.
   (D) Required for maintaining internet friendship scores.

9. How does Islamic jurisprudence evaluate intellectual property, software copyright, and digital piracy?
   (A) Digital code has no physical body, so stealing it is completely permissible.
   (B) Intellectual creations represent lawful property rights; pirating commercial software is a form of theft.
   (C) Software copyright only applies to books written before the year 1800.
   (D) Downloading pirated media is encouraged for students.

10. What is the spiritual consequence of allowing social media algorithms to steal hours of daily time into mindless distraction (*Lahw*)?
    (A) It increases a person's intelligence scores.
    (B) It numbs the heart, neglects prayer obligations, and wastes the finite capital of human life.
    (C) It fulfills the prophetic recommendation of contemplation.
    (D) It replaces the obligation of paying annual Zakah.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Haya' ─── Sacred modesty and self-respect in conduct and appearance
* 'Awrah ─── The physical parts of the body that must be covered in public and prayer
* Al-Ghash ─── Cheating, deceptive marketing, or concealing defects in trade
* Tabayyanu ─── The ethical duty to investigate and verify information before sharing
* Suht ─── Unlawful, tainted wealth acquired through deception or fraud

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. A friend purchases a used video game console, discovers the disc drive malfunctions intermittently, and plans to resell it online without mentioning the issue. Explain the Islamic legal and spiritual ruling to them.
2. How does the Quranic injunction of *Tabayyanu* (Surah Al-Hujurat 49:6) provide a blueprint for combating algorithmic disinformation, viral hoaxes, and fake news today?
3. Discuss how Islamic guidelines on dress liberate youth from consumerist body-shaming and peer-pressure fashion trends.
4. Formulate three practical rules a Muslim teenager can adopt to protect their time and mental health from social media addiction.

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - The Prophet ﷺ declared that Haya' brings only goodness and is a branch of faith.
2. B - Navel to knee represents the foundational male 'Awrah across Sunni consensus.
3. B - Silk and gold are prohibited for males, preserving masculine dignity and humility.
4. B - "Man ghashana fa-laysa minna" establishes complete intolerance for commercial deception.
5. B - Full disclosure of known defects is mandatory; concealment generates tainted income (*Suht*).
6. B - Surah Al-Hujurat mandates rigorous verification of news brought by unverified sources.
7. B - Cyberbullying inflicts psychological harm, violating prophetic brotherhood and dignity.
8. B - Breaching private communication is classified as betrayal of trust (*Amanah*).
9. B - Majority contemporary fatwa councils recognize intellectual property as protected wealth (*Mal*).
10. B - Excessive distraction destroys spiritual mindfulness and wastes irreplaceable life hours.
