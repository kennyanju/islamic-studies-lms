# Level 3 Quiz: Applied Fiqh & Everyday Life

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. What foundational legal maxim governs food and drinks in Islamic jurisprudence?
   (A) All foods are forbidden unless listed in the Quran.
   (B) The default state of worldly things is permissibility (*Al-Aslu fil-Ashya'i al-Ibahah*).
   (C) Only green vegetables are permitted for believers.
   (D) Foods must be imported from the Middle East to be halal.

2. Which of the following items is explicitly classified as strictly unlawful (Haram) in the Quran?
   (A) Apples and grapes.
   (B) Swine flesh (pork), flowing blood, and carrion (dead animals).
   (C) Seafood harvested from oceans.
   (D) Olives and figs.

3. What vital anatomical vessels must be severed during valid Islamic slaughter (*Dhabh*)?
   (A) The tail and the ears.
   (B) The windpipe, the esophagus, and the two carotid/jugular neck veins.
   (C) The spinal cord in the center of the spine.
   (D) The hoofs of the feet.

4. What divine proclamation must be uttered at the moment of slaughtering an animal?
   (A) "Al-Hamdulillah!"
   (B) "Bismillahi, Allahu Akbar!"
   (C) "Astaghfirullah!"
   (D) "SubhanAllah!"

5. What plant-based gelling agent serves as an excellent, completely Halal alternative to animal gelatin?
   (A) Crushed seashells.
   (B) Agar-Agar or fruit Pectin.
   (C) Wheat flour mixed with honey.
   (D) Olive oil extract.

6. What did the Prophet ﷺ instruct young 'Umar ibn Abi Salamah when sitting down for a meal?
   (A) "Eat as fast as you can before others finish!"
   (B) "Mention the Name of Allah, eat with your right hand, and eat from what is directly in front of you!"
   (C) "Always drink while standing on one foot!"
   (D) "Criticize any food that lacks salt!"

7. If a person forgets to say *Bismillah* before starting their meal and remembers halfway through, what should they say?
   (A) They must stop eating and discard the rest of the plate.
   (B) "Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirahu" (In the Name of Allah, at its beginning and its end).
   (C) They must pay a fine to a local charity.
   (D) "Allahu Akbar" three times.

8. What was the noble habit of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ if he was served food he did not personally favor?
   (A) He loudly complained about the ingredients to the host.
   (B) He left it quietly without ever criticizing or disparaging it.
   (C) He threw the bowl onto the floor.
   (D) He commanded that the cook be dismissed.

9. Why does Islamic law require that the slaughtering blade be razor-sharp?
   (A) To demonstrate the wealth of the butcher.
   (B) To embody prophetic excellence (*Ihsan*) and ensure the animal experiences zero unnecessary pain.
   (C) To prepare the meat for export to foreign countries.
   (D) To comply with ancient Roman trade laws.

10. What is the status of seafood (fish, prawns, tuna) according to Surah Al-Ma'idah (5:96)?
    (A) Strictly prohibited unless caught on a Friday.
    (B) Lawful to catch and eat (*Uhilla lakum saydul-bahri*).
    (C) Permitted only if boiled in milk.
    (D) Prohibited for children under twelve years old.
