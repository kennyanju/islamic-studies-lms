# Level 3 Quiz: Applied Fiqh & Everyday Life

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. What foundational legal maxim governs food, drinks, and worldly objects in Islamic jurisprudence?
   (A) Everything is forbidden unless explicitly permitted in hadiths.
   (B) The default state of worldly things is permissibility (*Al-Aslu fil-Ashya'i al-Ibahah*).
   (C) Only raw grains and vegetables are permitted.
   (D) Permissibility depends exclusively on regional tribal culture.

2. Which category of Islamic legal rulings describes an action that is rewarded for doing it, but incurs no sin if omitted (e.g. saying Bismillah before eating)?
   (A) Wajib (Obligatory).
   (B) Haram (Prohibited).
   (C) Mandub / Mustahabb (Recommended).
   (D) Makruh (Disliked).

3. In Islamic law, what are the primary neck vessels that must be swiftly severed for valid slaughter of livestock?
   (A) The spinal nerves inside the backbone.
   (B) The cartilage of the lower ribs.
   (C) The muscles along the shoulder blades.
   (D) The windpipe (*Hulqum*) and the two carotid/jugular veins (*Wadajayn*).

4. What divine invocation should be uttered at the moment of ritual slaughter?
   (A) Bismillahi, Allahu Akbar.
   (B) Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi.
   (C) Astaghfirullah al-'Azim.
   (D) La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.

5. In the Maliki school, what is the legal ruling if a Muslim slaughterer forgets to pronounce Bismillah out of honest forgetfulness?
   (A) The meat becomes unlawful carrion (*Maytah*).
   (B) The butcher must pay a fine to charity.
   (C) The meat remains Halal and lawful to eat.
   (D) The meat must be boiled for forty minutes before consumption.

6. What did the Prophet ﷺ teach young 'Umar ibn Abi Salamah regarding dining etiquette (Sahih al-Bukhari 5376)?
   (A) "Begin from the center of the shared platter, eat with either hand, and finish before the elders."
   (B) "Mention the Name of Allah, eat with your right hand, and eat from what is directly in front of you."
   (C) "Wait until all guests have finished eating before taking your portion from the far side of the dish."
   (D) "Recite Surah Al-Ikhlas aloud before every bite and drink water only after completing the meal."

7. If a person forgets to say *Bismillah* before their first bite and remembers during the meal, what is the Sunnah invocation to recite?
   (A) "Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirahu" (In the Name of Allah, at its beginning and end).
   (B) "Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi" (Glory and praise be to Allah) before taking the next bite.
   (C) "Al-Hamdulillahi Rabbil-'Alamin" (Praise be to Allah, Lord of the worlds) three times aloud.
   (D) "Astaghfirullaha wa atubu ilayh" (I seek Allah's forgiveness and repent to Him) after the meal.

8. What was the noble habit of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ if he was served food he did not personally prefer (Sahih al-Bukhari 5409)?
   (A) He instructed the cook to prepare a replacement immediately.
   (B) He publicly pointed out the mistakes in the dish.
   (C) He complained about the seasoning to the host.
   (D) He left it quietly without ever criticizing or disparaging it.

9. What plant-based gelling substance serves as an excellent, completely Halal alternative to animal gelatin in supermarket sweets?
   (A) Powdered bone marrow.
   (B) Seaweed-derived Agar-Agar or fruit Pectin.
   (C) Animal tallow.
   (D) Wheat gluten mixed with lard.

10. What is the legal status of seafood (fish, prawns, squid) in the Maliki school of jurisprudence?
    (A) Disliked (*Makruh*) unless the creature has visible scales and swims near the surface of the water.
    (B) Prohibited (*Haram*) for everything except scaled fish unless it undergoes formal land-animal slaughter.
    (C) Lawful (*Halal*) to catch and consume without ritual slaughter (Muwatta Malik 43).
    (D) Permitted only for coastal travelers who cannot find lawful livestock meat during their journey.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Halal ─── Lawful and permitted by the Lawgiver
* Tayyib ─── Wholesome, pure, nutritious, and ethically prepared
* Al-Dhabh ─── Humane ritual slaughter invoking the Name of Allah
* Khinzir ─── Swine or pork, strictly forbidden for consumption
* Agar-Agar ─── A 100% plant-derived gelling substitute for animal gelatin

## Part D: Reflection & Application

2. Detail how being a "Supermarket Detective" empowers a Muslim youth to practice their faith with awareness in contemporary society.
3. Analyze the Prophet's dining guidance to 'Umar ibn Abi Salamah: How does eating with the right hand and taking from what is in front build social respect and hygiene?
4. Contrast the Islamic concept of *Tayyib* (wholesomeness) with modern industrial fast food: How does Islam encourage healthy and ethical nutrition?

## Answer Key
1. B - Maxim: All things are permissible by default unless explicitly forbidden by divine text.
2. C - Mandub / Mustahabb refers to actions rewarded for doing, but not punished if omitted.
3. D - Humane slaughter severs the windpipe and the two jugular veins swiftly.
4. A - Pronouncing Bismillah Allahu Akbar dedicates the taking of life to the Creator.
5. C - In Maliki fiqh, unintentional omission of Bismillah leaves the slaughter lawful (*Al-Risalah*).
6. B - The hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari establishes foundational table etiquette for youth.
7. A - Prophetic sunnah prescribes: "Bismillahi awwalahu wa akhirahu."
8. D - The Prophet ﷺ never found fault with food; he ate if he wished, or left it gently.
9. B - Agar-agar (red algae) and pectin (fruit peel) are completely plant-based halal thickeners.
10. C - Muwatta Malik 43 confirms all game from the sea is lawful without ritual slaughter.
