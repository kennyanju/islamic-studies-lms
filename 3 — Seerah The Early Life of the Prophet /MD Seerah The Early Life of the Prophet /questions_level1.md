# Level 1 Activity Sheet: Early Life of the Prophet ﷺ
**Age Group:** Ages 6-8 (Level 1 Track)  
**Focus:** Year of the Elephant, Baby Muhammad ﷺ, and Al-Amin  

---

## Part A: Multiple Choice

### 1. In which blessed city was Prophet Muhammad ﷺ born?
(A) Madinah
(B) Ta'if
(C) Makkah
(D) Jerusalem

### 2. Who was the loving nurse who cared for baby Muhammad ﷺ in the desert of Banu Sa'd?
(A) Halimah al-Sa'diyyah
(B) Umm Ayman
(C) Lady Fatimah
(D) Lady Asiyah

### 3. What happened when the ruler Abrahah led an army with elephants to attack the Ka'bah?
(A) The people of Makkah fought them with heavy weapons
(B) Sudden desert rain flooded the valley
(C) The army turned around peacefully to build a town
(D) Allah sent flocks of small birds (Ababil) that protected the Ka'bah

### 4. What title did the people of Makkah give young Muhammad ﷺ because he always spoke the truth and never broke a promise?
(A) Al-Hakim (The Judge)
(B) Al-Amin (The Trustworthy)
(C) Al-Batal (The Champion)
(D) Al-Khatib (The Speaker)

### 5. How can a young student follow the noble example of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ at home and at school?
(A) By boasting about personal belongings
(B) By ignoring friends who ask for assistance
(C) By speaking the truth, keeping promises, and greeting others with a smile
(D) By arguing whenever a game is lost

## Part D: Reflection

9. What is one honest action you can take today at home or at school to follow the example of young Muhammad ﷺ as Al-Amin?

### Reflection Rubric
* **Full Understanding:** Student mentions a clear, honest action (such as admitting a mistake, returning a borrowed pencil, or telling the truth) and connects it to being trustworthy like the Prophet ﷺ.
* **Basic Understanding:** Student mentions an honest behavior without explaining why it reflects being Al-Amin.

---

## Teacher Answer Key
1. C - Makkah is the blessed birthplace of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ.
2. A - Lady Halimah al-Sa'diyyah was the desert nurse who cared for him.
3. D - Surah Al-Fil relates how Allah sent flocks of birds (Ababil) to protect the Ka'bah.
4. B - Al-Amin means The Trustworthy, given because he was known for truthfulness.
5. C - Speaking the truth and being trustworthy embodies the Sunnah in daily life.
