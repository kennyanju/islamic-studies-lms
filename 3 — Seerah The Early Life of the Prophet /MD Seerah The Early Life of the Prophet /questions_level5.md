# Level 5 Quiz: Seerah: Early Life of the Prophet ﷺ

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How does the biographical preservation of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ differ fundamentally from that of founders of ancient classical religions?
   (A) It relies on anonymous folklore written down several centuries after his death.
   (B) It is documented through contemporary divine text (the Quran) and rigorous unbroken chains of verified eyewitness transmission (*Isnad*).
   (C) It was reconstructed purely from archaeological stone monuments in southern Yemen.
   (D) It was invented by Abbasid imperial decree in Baghdad.

2. In classical Islamic historiography, what is the methodological difference between Hadith compilation and historical *Maghazi* chronicles?
   (A) Historical Maghazi required stricter narrator verification than legal Hadith.
   (B) Hadith collections required strict chain validation for legal rulings and creed, whereas Maghazi permitted narrative continuity for non-legislative history.
   (C) Hadith compilations were written in Persian, while Maghazi were in Syriac.
   (D) Maghazi chronicles were restricted only to medical observations.

3. What is the unanimous theological consensus (*Ijma'*) regarding the Prophet's infallibility (*'Ismah*) in delivering revelation (*Al-Tabligh*)?
   (A) He was prone to seasonal forgetfulness in legal matters.
   (B) He was absolutely protected by Allah from any error, addition, omission, or deceit in delivering the message.
   (C) Infallibility began only after the migration to Madinah.
   (D) It applied only to the Arabic grammar of the Quran, not the legal rulings.

4. What geopolitical factor characterized the Arabian peninsula in the 6th century compared to the Byzantine and Sasanian empires?
   (A) It was heavily occupied and governed by Roman imperial legions.
   (B) It existed as a pristine geopolitical buffer zone, free from imperial bureaucracy, preserving linguistic eloquence and chivalric autonomy.
   (C) It was completely submerged under ocean waterways.
   (D) It possessed a unified central monarchy ruling from San'a.

5. Who among the pre-Islamic *Hunafa'* famously refused to worship idols, rejected meat slaughtered for false gods, and died before the open call of Islam?
   (A) Abu Jahl ibn Hisham.
   (B) Zayd ibn 'Amr ibn Nufayl.
   (C) Umayyah ibn Khalaf.
   (D) Al-Walid ibn al-Mughirah.

6. Why does the secular "epilepsy/hallucination" hypothesis fail to explain the historical reality of the Prophet's revelation?
   (A) Because people with epilepsy in antiquity were immediately exiled from Arabia.
   (B) Because neurological seizures produce cognitive degradation and memory collapse, whereas the Quran displays peerless linguistic structure and legal sophistication over 23 years.
   (C) Because early Arabians did not know what neurological illnesses were.
   (D) Because the Prophet ﷺ refused to speak Arabic in public gatherings.

7. How do Islamic historians refute the allegation that Prophet Muhammad ﷺ copied biblical scriptures from the monk Bahira?
   (A) Bahira was illiterate and unable to speak any Semitic languages.
   (B) The encounter occurred during a brief childhood caravan stop when the Prophet was around 9–12 years old, surrounded by relatives who witnessed the entire brief exchange.
   (C) Bahira converted to Zoroastrianism shortly after the caravan left.
   (D) The city of Busra had no Christian inhabitants.

8. What profound ethical deduction did Sayyidah Khadijah make to reassure the Prophet ﷺ after the harrowing descent of revelation in Cave Hira?
   (A) She told him that his wealth would buy him military protection from Quraysh.
   (B) She swore by Allah that He would never humiliate him because he maintained family ties, helped the poor, honored guests, and stood for justice.
   (C) She advised him to consult Roman physicians in Antioch.
   (D) She suggested that he hide the incident from all family members forever.

9. In Ibn Khaldun's *Muqaddimah*, how is the tribal solidarity (*'Asabiyyah*) of the Arabian desert analyzed in relation to the Prophetic mission?
   (A) As an evil force that prevented Islam from expanding outside Makkah.
   (B) As a powerful sociological vehicle that, once purified by divine religion, provided the necessary defense and mobility for truth to prevail.
   (C) As an economic banking cartel that funded early mosques.
   (D) As a poetic rhythm used exclusively in camel racing.

10. What does Qadi 'Iyad declare regarding fabricated narratives (such as the so-called "Satanic Verses" myth) in his critical study *Al-Shifa*?
    (A) He accepts them as authentic historical proof without question.
    (B) He decisively proves they are uncorroborated, baseless fabrications invented by early heretics, completely contradicting the Quran and sound Isnad.
    (C) He claims that angels permitted Satan to write certain verses of the Quran.
    (D) He recommends reciting them during solar eclipses.

## Part C: Matching Scholarly Terms
* 'Ilm al-Rijal ─── Biographical evaluation of narrator trustworthiness in Hadith chains
* 'Ismah ─── Absolute moral protection and infallibility of Prophets
* Hunafa' ─── Pre-Islamic monotheists who rejected idol worship before revelation
* Namus al-Akbar ─── The great angelic emissary (Jibril) identified by Waraqah ibn Nawfal
* Ibn Hisham ─── The 9th-century scholar who edited and preserved the canonical Sirah of Ibn Ishaq

## Part D: Senior Seminar Case Study & Reflection
1. Contrast the epistemological rigor of Islamic *Isnad* methodology with the historical-critical standards applied to ancient Greco-Roman or biblical texts.
2. A secular university lecturer claims: "Muhammad's revelations were simply the subconscious expression of 7th-century Arabian social reform." How does the internal textual evidence of the Quran dismantle this assertion?
3. Analyze Sayyidah Khadijah's immediate response to the first revelation: Why does her ethical deduction represent an unassailable proof of the Prophet's sincerity?
4. Using Qadi 'Iyad's *Al-Shifa*, formulate the theological boundaries of Prophetic *'Ismah* regarding temporal matters vs. legislative conveyance.

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - Islamic Seerah is grounded in contemporary text and unbroken, critically verified eyewitness transmission chains.
2. B - Classical scholarship rigorously distinguished between evidentiary standards for law/creed and historical narrative chronicles.
3. B - Complete consensus affirms absolute infallibility in conveying the divine message (*Tabligh*).
4. B - Arabia was an independent, uncolonized cultural matrix preserving raw linguistic purity and chivalric ethics.
5. B - Zayd ibn 'Amr lived and died as a pure monotheist following the Ibrahimic legacy before the advent of prophethood.
6. B - Intellectual coherence and sustained legislative mastery over 23 years preclude neurological pathology.
7. B - A brief childhood meeting in a public caravan cannot account for 23 years of transcendent, unique revelation.
8. B - Khadijah deduced that divine benevolence does not betray a life dedicated to moral virtue and charity.
9. B - Ibn Khaldun explains that religious truth requires social solidarity (*'Asabiyyah*) to take root and defend itself.
10. B - Qadi 'Iyad systematically deconstructed the myth, showing it lacked any continuous chain (*Isnad Sahih*) and violated theology.
