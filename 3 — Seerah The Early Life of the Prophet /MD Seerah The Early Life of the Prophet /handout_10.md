# Handout: The Early Seerah & The School of Madinah
## Student Guide for 10-Year-Olds

Welcome to your Seerah guide! In this book, we will take a journey back in time to explore the early life of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ, the dawn of Islam, and how the teachings of the Prophet ﷺ were carefully preserved in the blessed city of Madinah. 

Throughout this handout, you will see special flags:
*   **Creed Discovery (Aqidah):** Helps us understand who Allah is and how we think about His Messengers.
*   **Fiqh Connection:** Teaches us how we practice our faith, specifically through the teachings of **Imam Malik**.
*   **Language Hub:** Introduces beautiful Arabic words, their transliterations, and meanings.

---

## Chapter 1: Makkah Before Islam — The City, the Family, and the Boy
Many, many years ago, in a dry valley surrounded by dark, rocky mountains in Arabia, lay the city of Makkah. It was a place where it rarely rained, and the ground was sandy and dry. Yet, it was the most important city in all of Arabia! Why?

### The Holy Ka'bah
At the center of Makkah stood a simple, cube-shaped stone building called the **Ka'bah**. It was originally built by the Prophet Ibrahim (as) and his son Ismail (as) for the worship of **Allah** alone. 

But over time, the people forgot the teachings of Ibrahim (as). Instead of praying only to Allah, they brought statues of wood and stone, called idols, and placed them in and around the Ka'bah. By the time of our story, there were over 360 idols there! The people of Makkah would pray to these statues, feed them, and ask them for help. This is called **Shirk** (worshipping others besides Allah).

### The Tribe of Quraysh and Banu Hashim
Makkah was ruled by a powerful tribe called the **Quraysh**. Within this tribe was a very noble family called **Banu Hashim** (the Children of Hashim). They were known for their generosity, especially in providing fresh well water (from the well of **Zamzam**) and food to the pilgrims who visited Makkah.

The leader of this family was a wise, kind grandfather named **Abdul Muttalib**. He had a son named **Abdullah**, who married a noble woman named **Aminah**. Tragically, Abdullah passed away before their baby was born.

On the 12th of the month of **Rabi' al-Awwal**, Aminah gave birth to a beautiful baby boy. Abdul Muttalib held his grandson, walked around the Ka'bah to thank Allah, and named him **Muhammad**, which means "The Praised One."

### Growing Up in the Desert
When Muhammad ﷺ was a tiny baby, his mother sent him to live with a foster mother named **Halimah** in the quiet desert of **Banu Sa'd**. This was an ancient custom. The fresh, clean air of the desert made children strong, and they learned to speak the purest Arabic language away from the busy city.

While Muhammad ﷺ lived with Halimah's family, amazing blessings (**Barakah**) happened:
*   Their old, weak donkey suddenly ran faster than all others.
*   Their goats and camels produced abundant milk, even though there was a drought.
*   Their dry land turned green and beautiful.

### The Cleansing of the Heart
When Muhammad ﷺ was about four years old, a miraculous event took place. While he was playing, two angels in white robes appeared. They gently laid him down, opened his chest, and took out his heart. They removed a small black spot from his heart, which is the part where Satan could whisper bad thoughts. Then, they washed his heart with pure **Zamzam** water in a golden basin and placed it back. 

From that day on, Muhammad's ﷺ heart was perfectly pure. He never wanted to do anything bad or worship idols, even though everyone around him did!

### The Young Shepherd and Al-Amin
Muhammad ﷺ returned to Makkah. Sadly, by the age of eight, both his mother and his grandfather had passed away. He was taken in by his uncle, **Abu Talib**, who loved him like his own son.

Because his uncle was not rich, Muhammad ﷺ wanted to help. He became a shepherd, taking care of sheep in the hills of Makkah. This was the perfect training for a future Prophet! A shepherd must be patient, gentle, and protect his sheep from danger.

As Muhammad ﷺ grew, everyone in Makkah noticed his incredible character. He was polite, never spoke rudely, and always helped the poor. He was given two famous nicknames:
1.  **Al-Sadiq:** The Truthful One (he never told a single lie).
2.  **Al-Amin:** The Trustworthy One (people left their money and valuables with him because they knew he would never steal or lose them).

---
### 💡 Creed Discovery: Prophetic Character
> Under the **Ash'ari school of Creed**, we learn that Allah protects all of His Prophets from committing major sins, telling lies, or displaying bad manners. This is called **'Ismah** (Infallibility/Protection). Even before Muhammad ﷺ received revelation, Allah protected him from worshipping idols or acting dishonestly. This was to prepare him to be a perfect model for us!

---
### 🗣️ Language Hub
*   **Ka'bah (كَعْبَة):** The cube-shaped House of Allah in Makkah.
*   **Shirk (شِرْك):** Worshipping statues, people, or anything else alongside Allah.
*   **Barakah (بَرَكَة):** Divine blessings and increase in goodness.
*   **Al-Amin (الأَمِين):** The Trustworthy.
*   **Al-Sadiq (الصَّادِق):** The Truthful.

## Chapter 2: The First Revelation in the Cave of Hira
As Muhammad ﷺ grew older, he married a wonderful lady named **Khadijah** (ra). He managed her trading caravans with absolute honesty, and she was so impressed by his noble character that she asked to marry him. They lived a happy, peaceful life.

However, as Muhammad ﷺ approached the age of 40, his heart felt heavy when he saw the people of Makkah fighting, drinking, and bowing down to stone statues. He wanted to seek the truth.

### Jabal al-Nur & Jabal Hira
Muhammad ﷺ began taking hikes up a steep mountain just outside Makkah called **Jabal al-Nur** (The Mountain of Light). Near the top of the mountain was a small cave called the **Cave of Hira**. 

He would carry some bread and water and spend several days and nights alone in this quiet cave. He would look down at the Ka'bah in the distance and reflect on the Creator of the universe. This acts of quiet worship and reflection is called **Tahannuth**.

### The Arrival of Jibril (as)
One night during the blessed month of **Ramadan**, while Muhammad ﷺ was resting in the dark cave, a brilliant light filled the room. The Angel **Jibril** (Gabriel) appeared in front of him!

Jibril commanded the Prophet ﷺ: **"Iqra!"** (Read/Recite!).
Muhammad ﷺ was trembling with fear. He replied: **"Ma ana bi-qari'"** (I do not know how to read).

The Angel Jibril took hold of the Prophet ﷺ and squeezed him tightly, then released him and said again: **"Iqra!"**
Again, the Prophet ﷺ replied: **"Ma ana bi-qari'."**

Jibril squeezed him a second time and a third time, until the Prophet ﷺ was completely out of breath. After the third squeeze, Jibril recited the first-ever verses of the Qur'an:
> *"Recite in the name of your Lord who created! Created man from a clinging substance. Recite, and your Lord is the Most Generous. Who taught by the pen. Taught man that which he knew not."* (Surah al-'Alaq, 96:1-5)

Muhammad ﷺ repeated the words, and they were carved directly into his heart. Jibril then vanished into the night sky.

---
### 💡 Creed Discovery: The Speech of Allah
> What is the Qur'an? In the **Ash'ari creed**, we believe that the Qur'an is the **Speech of Allah** (*Kalam Allah*). Allah's Speech is an eternal attribute of Himself. It does not have physical letters or sounds like human speech. 
> The Arabic words that we read in the book (the Mus-haf) and hear with our ears are a created expression of Allah's eternal, uncreated Speech. This helps us understand that when we read the Qur'an, we are connecting with the divine message of Allah, who does not speak with a physical voice or throat like humans do.

---
### 🗣️ Language Hub
*   **Wahy (وَحْي):** Revelation—the way Allah sends messages to His Prophets.
*   **Tahannuth (تَحَنُّث):** Spiritual retreat and meditation in isolation.
*   **Iqra (اقْرَأْ):** Read or Recite.
*   **Kalam (كَلَام):** Speech.

## Chapter 3: The Comfort of Khadijah and the Early Believers
The Prophet ﷺ was terrified by what he had experienced. His heart was beating fast, and he ran all the way down the mountain, shivering. He burst into his house and cried to his wife, Khadijah: **"Zammiluni! Zammiluni!"** (Cover me! Cover me!).

Khadijah (ra) immediately wrapped him in a warm blanket, held his hand, and calmed him down. After he told her what happened, she smiled and gave him beautiful words of comfort:

> *"Never! By Allah, He will never let you down. You keep good relations with your family, you speak the truth, you carry the burdens of the weak, you feed the poor, you host your guests generously, and you stand up for justice."*

This shows us how much Khadijah (ra) loved and trusted him. She didn't think he was losing his mind; she knew he was too pure and good for Allah to allow any evil to harm him. She was the very first person to believe in his prophethood!

### Waraqah ibn Nawfal
To understand this amazing event better, Khadijah took the Prophet ﷺ to see her cousin, **Waraqah ibn Nawfal**. Waraqah was a very old, blind scholar who had studied the ancient Christian and Jewish scriptures.

When Waraqah heard the story of the Cave of Hira, his eyes filled with tears. He said:
*"This is the Namus (the Angel Jibril) who was sent to Musa (Moses)! I wish I were young and strong to help you when your people drive you out of Makkah!"*

The Prophet ﷺ was surprised. *"Will they drive me out?"* he asked.
Waraqah replied, *"Yes. No man has ever brought what you have brought except that his own people turned against him."*

### The Secret Dawah
For the next three years, the Prophet ﷺ was commanded by Allah to invite people to Islam quietly and secretly. This is called the phase of **Al-Da'wah al-Sirriyyah** (The Private Invitation).

He did not stand on mountains to shout the message yet. Instead, he went to those he trusted most. 
*   **Abu Bakr** (his best friend) accepted immediately.
*   **Ali ibn Abi Talib** (his cousin, who was only about 10 years old) accepted immediately.
*   **Zayd ibn Harithah** (his adopted son) accepted immediately.

These early Muslims would meet in secret at the house of a young companion named **Al-Arqam** (**Dar al-Arqam**). Inside this secret school, the Prophet ﷺ taught them how to pray, read the new verses of the Qur'an, and purify their characters. They would slip out into the quiet mountain valleys to pray where nobody could see them.

---
### 💡 Creed Discovery: The Intellect and Faith
> In our creed, we learn that the truth of the Prophet's ﷺ message is verified by his perfect character. When Khadijah (ra) and Waraqah (ra) heard what happened, they used their **sound intellect** (*'Aql*) to realize that a man who is honest, kind, and just could only be contacted by an Angel, not a devil. Islam encourages us to use our minds to recognize the truth!

---
### 🗣️ Language Hub
*   **Dawah (دَعْوَة):** Inviting people to learn about Islam and worship Allah.
*   **Dar al-Arqam (دَار الأَرْقَم):** The House of Al-Arqam, the first secret learning space for Muslims.
*   **Al-Da'wah al-Sirriyyah (الدَّعْوَة السِّرِّيَّة):** The private phase of calling people to Islam.

## Chapter 4: Kindness Under Hardship — The Journey to Ta'if
As the years passed, the Prophet ﷺ began preaching Islam openly. The leaders of Makkah became very angry. They loved their idols and did not want a religion that taught that all humans are equal under Allah.

They began torturing the poor and enslaved Muslims. They also boycotted the Prophet's ﷺ family, refusing to sell them food or water for three long years. During this time, the Muslims had to eat leaves and leather just to survive.

### The Year of Grief ('Am al-Huzn)
In the tenth year of prophethood, two major tragedies struck:
1.  **Abu Talib** (the Prophet's uncle who protected him from the Quraysh chiefs) passed away.
2.  **Khadijah** (his beloved wife who supported him) passed away.

This year became known as the **Year of Grief**. With no protector left in Makkah, the Quraysh chiefs increased their abuse.

### Walking to Ta'if
The Prophet ﷺ decided to seek help outside Makkah. He took his companion Zayd ibn Harithah and walked 60 miles up steep, rocky mountain roads to a city called **Ta'if**. He hoped the tribe of Thaqif who lived there would protect the Muslims and accept the message of peace.

But the leaders of Ta'if were cruel. When the Prophet ﷺ spoke to them, they laughed at him. They refused his message and ordered the street children and slaves to pelt him with rocks.

As the Prophet ﷺ and Zayd walked out of the city, they were chased by a mob throwing stones. Zayd was wounded in the head trying to shield the Prophet ﷺ, and the Prophet's ﷺ sandals were soaked with his own blood.

### The Orchard and the Prayer of Ta'if
Exhausted, bleeding, and heartbroken, the Prophet ﷺ rested under the shade of a grape arbor in an orchard belonging to two Mekkan brothers. There, a kind Christian slave named **Addas** gave him grapes and, after hearing the Prophet ﷺ speak, kissed his hands and accepted Islam.

In that orchard, the Prophet ﷺ made a beautiful prayer to Allah, complaining only of his own weakness and asking for Allah's pleasure, saying:
> *"If You are not angry with me, O Allah, then I do not care about any of this hardship."*

### The Choice of Mercy
Suddenly, a cloud shaded the Prophet ﷺ. He looked up and saw Angel Jibril (as), accompanied by the **Angel of the Mountains**.
The Angel of the Mountains called out:
*"O Muhammad! Allah has heard what your people said to you. If you wish, I will bring the two mountains surrounding Makkah together and crush them!"*

Imagine if someone hurt you, and you were given the power to destroy them. What would you do?
The Prophet ﷺ, who was sent as a **Rahmah** (Mercy) to all worlds, replied:
> *"No, I do not want that. Rather, I hope that Allah will bring from their children people who will worship Allah alone and not associate partners with Him."*

What a beautiful, patient heart! Years later, his prayer was answered, and the people of Ta'if became strong, devout Muslims.

---
### 💡 Creed Discovery: divine Decree (Qadar)
> In our creed, we believe that everything—both good and difficult—happens by the **Decree of Allah** (*Qadar*). The Prophet's ﷺ suffering in Ta'if was not because Allah abandoned him. It was a test to show his high status, teach us how to have patience (**Sabr**), and reveal his ultimate character of mercy. Allah is Wise, and His plans are always perfect.

---
### 🗣️ Language Hub
*   **Rahmah (رَحْمَة):** Mercy, compassion, and love.
*   **Sabr (صَبْر):** Patient perseverance and self-restraint.
*   **Qadar (قَدَر):** The divine decree of Allah.
*   **'Am al-Huzn (عَام الحُزْن):** The Year of Grief.

## Chapter 5: Madinah and the Legacy of Imam Malik
Eventually, Allah commanded the Prophet ﷺ and the Muslims to migrate from Makkah to the city of Yathrib, which was renamed **Madinah** (The City). 

In Madinah, the Prophet ﷺ built the first Islamic community. For ten years, the companions lived directly with the Prophet ﷺ, watching how he prayed, ate, smiled, resolved conflicts, and worshipped Allah.

### The Home of Knowledge
Even after the Prophet ﷺ passed away, Madinah remained the heart of Islamic knowledge. The children and grandchildren of the companions grew up doing the exact same actions they had seen the Prophet ﷺ do.

In this blessed atmosphere, a boy named **Malik ibn Anas** was born in the year 93 AH (about 80 years after the Prophet's ﷺ passing). 

Imam Malik loved knowledge. He studied under the greatest scholars of Madinah, who had learned directly from the Sahabah. He became so famous for his intelligence and memory that he was called the **Imam of Madinah**.

### Imam Malik's Love for the Prophet ﷺ
Imam Malik had a heart filled with immense love and respect for the Prophet ﷺ. 
*   **No Riding in Madinah:** He refused to ever ride a horse or camel in the streets of Madinah. He said: *"I feel too ashamed before Allah to ride an animal on the very ground where the body of the Prophet ﷺ is buried!"*
*   **Preparing for Hadith:** When people asked him about everyday rulings, he would answer easily. But if they asked him to narrate a **Hadith** (a saying of the Prophet ﷺ), he would perform a full Wudu (or Ghusl), wear his finest white clothes, apply beautiful perfume, and sit in a special, respectful posture. He would burn incense throughout the class because he believed the words of the Prophet ﷺ deserved the highest respect.

### The Muwatta'
Imam Malik compiled the very first major book of Hadith and Islamic law, called **Al-Muwatta'** (The Approved Path). It took him over 40 years to write and check it. 

---
### 💡 Fiqh Connection: The Practice of Madinah
> In the **Maliki school of Fiqh**, when we want to know the correct way to pray or act, we do not only look at a single, written text. Imam Malik taught that the **inherited, collective practice of the people of Madinah** (*'Amal Ahl al-Madinah*) is one of the strongest proofs of the Sunnah. 
> Why? Because if thousands of people in Madinah—the children of the Companions—all prayed a certain way (such as praying with their arms resting naturally at their sides, known as **Sadl**), it is like a living Hadith passed down by actions, which is much harder to distort than a single written text.

---
### 🗣️ Language Hub
*   **Sahabah (صَحَابَة):** The companions of the Prophet ﷺ.
*   **Hadith (حَدِيث):** The sayings, actions, and approvals of the Prophet ﷺ.
*   **'Amal Ahl al-Madinah (عَمَل أَهْل المَدِينَة):** The living, inherited practice of the people of Madinah.
*   **Sadl (سَدْل):** Draping the arms naturally at the sides during prayer (a key practice in the Maliki school).
