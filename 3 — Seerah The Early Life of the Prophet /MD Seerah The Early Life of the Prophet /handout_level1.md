## 🐘 1. The Miraculous Year of the Elephant ('Am al-Feel)
In the very year our beloved Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was born (570 CE), an arrogant ruler named Abraha marched toward Makkah with a massive army and a gigantic war elephant named Mahmud! Abraha wanted to destroy the sacred Ka'bah so people would visit his cathedral in Yemen instead.

The people of Makkah could not fight such a huge army. The Prophet's grandfather, **'Abd al-Muttalib**, stood holding the door of the Ka'bah and prayed to Allah:  
> *"O Allah! A man defends his home, so defend Your own House!"*

Allah answered his prayer with a spectacular miracle! When Abraha ordered the elephant to attack Makkah, the giant elephant knelt down on the sand and refused to move! But when they turned him towards Yemen or Syria, he ran happily.

Then, Allah sent flocks of tiny birds called **Ababil** flying through the skies! Each little bird held small clay pebbles in its beak and claws. They dropped the pebbles on the army, defeating Abraha completely without a single sword drawn by the people of Makkah! Allah revealed **Surah Al-Feel** to remind us that Allah always protects what is sacred:  
> **أَلَمْ تَرَ كَيْفَ فَعَلَ رَبُّكَ بِأَصْحَٰبِ ٱلْفِيلِ**  
> *"Have you not seen how your Lord dealt with the companions of the elephant?"*

## 🍼 2. The Blessed Baby in the Desert
A few months after the Year of the Elephant, on a sweet Monday morning in Rabi' al-Awwal, **Muhammad ﷺ** was born in Makkah. His father **'Abdullah** had passed away before his birth, so his loving mother **Aminah** cared for him.

In those days, noble Arab families sent their babies to grow up in the clean desert air so they would grow healthy, strong, and speak the purest Arabic. A kind woman named **Halimah al-Sa'diyyah** came to Makkah. Other women wanted rich babies, but Halimah's heart felt deep love for the sweet orphan baby.

The moment Halimah took baby Muhammad into her arms, miraculous blessings filled her home! Her weak camel began giving abundant fresh milk, the dry desert grass turned lush and green, and peace settled over her family. She loved him like her own son for four wonderful years.

---

## 🛡️ 3. The Orphan Boy Under Loving Care
When Muhammad ﷺ was six years old, his sweet mother Aminah fell ill and passed away. Although he had lost both father and mother, Allah placed love for him into every heart.

* **His Grandfather 'Abd al-Muttalib:** The great chieftain of Quraysh loved little Muhammad dearly. He had a special carpet near the Ka'bah where no one was allowed to sit—except little Muhammad, whom he sat on his lap with pride!
* **His Uncle Abu Talib:** When his grandfather passed away, his uncle Abu Talib took Muhammad into his home, loving him even more than his own sons.

As a young boy, Muhammad ﷺ helped his family by working as a **shepherd**, guiding sheep across the quiet hills of Makkah. Being a shepherd taught him patience, gentleness, courage, and reflection under the stars. The Prophet ﷺ later said:  
> *"Every prophet sent by Allah was once a shepherd of sheep!"*

---

## 💎 4. Al-Amin: The Trustworthy Youth Who Never Lied
Even before receiving revelation, Muhammad ﷺ was known across all of Arabia by the honorable title:  
> **الأَمِين** — **Al-Amin** (The Completely Trustworthy and Truthful!).

* He never told a lie, even in play or jokes.
* He never cheated anyone out of a single dirham.
* If anyone had precious gold, silver, or documents, they trusted Muhammad ﷺ to guard them!
* He never drank bad drinks, worshipped stone idols, or participated in foolish street arguments.

### The Little Believer's Honesty Challenge:
1. **Tell the truth always:** Even if you accidentally break a cup or make a mistake, tell Mum and Dad the truth immediately! Truthfulness brings light to your heart and Allah's blessings.
2. **Take care of what is borrowed:** If a friend lends you a pencil, toy, or book, treat it better than your own and return it with a smile and *"JazakAllahu Khayran!"*
