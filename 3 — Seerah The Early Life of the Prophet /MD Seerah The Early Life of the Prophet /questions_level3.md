# Level 3 Quiz: Seerah: Early Life of the Prophet ﷺ

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. Why did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ assemble the clans of Quraysh at Mount Safa?
   (A) To organize an annual commercial caravan to Damascus.
   (B) To openly proclaim the message of Islam in response to divine revelation.
   (C) To elect a new tribal chieftain for the city of Makkah.
   (D) To distribute the harvest of dates following a rainfall.

2. Before proclaiming the message of Tawhid atop Mount Safa, what testimony did the Prophet ﷺ elicit from Quraysh?
   (A) That they would immediately elect him as the absolute King of Arabia.
   (B) That he had never spoken an untruth in his entire life (Al-Amin).
   (C) That they were prepared to demolish all 360 idols that afternoon.
   (D) That they would surrender their merchant ships in the Red Sea.

3. Who holds the sacred honor of being the very first martyr (Shahid) in Islamic history?
   (A) Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah.
   (B) Sayyidah Sumayyah bint Khayyat.
   (C) Sayyidina Hamzah ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib.
   (D) Sayyidina Ja'far ibn Abi Talib.

4. What phrase did Sayyidina Bilal repeat while enduring torture under a massive boulder in the desert?
   (A) "Allahu Akbar!"
   (B) "Ahad! Ahad!" (Allah is One!).
   (C) "SubhanAllah!"
   (D) "Al-Hamdulillah!"

5. Why did the Prophet ﷺ select Abyssinia (Habashah) as the destination for the first Muslim migration?
   (A) Because it was governed by a righteous Christian king (The Negus) who oppressed no one.
   (B) Because it possessed massive gold mines that could fund a Muslim army.
   (C) Because Quraysh had signed a commercial alliance with its navy.
   (D) Because the climate was cooler than the desert heat of Makkah.

6. Which chapter of the Holy Quran did Sayyidina Ja'far ibn Abi Talib recite in the court of the Negus?
   (A) Surah Al-Baqarah.
   (B) Surah Maryam.
   (C) Surah Al-Fath.
   (D) Surah Yasin.

7. How long did the cruel socio-economic boycott of Banu Hashim in Shi'b Abi Talib last?
   (A) Three weeks.
   (B) Three months.
   (C) Three full years.
   (D) Ten years.

8. What miraculous event brought an end to the boycott parchment hanging inside the Ka'bah?
   (A) A bolt of lightning struck the Ka'bah roof.
   (B) Termites devoured the parchment, leaving only the words "Bismika Allahumma."
   (C) The document was stolen by a passing Roman trade delegation.
   (D) Heavy floodwaters washed the entire parchment out into the sea.

9. Why was the tenth year of Prophethood designated as the "Year of Sorrow" ('Am al-Huzn)?
   (A) The Muslims lost their entire savings in a catastrophic market collapse.
   (B) Both the Prophet's protective uncle Abu Talib and his beloved wife Sayyidah Khadijah passed away.
   (C) A severe earthquake destroyed half of the homes in Makkah.
   (D) The Prophet ﷺ was imprisoned inside Cave Hira for eight months.

10. What was the sublime response of the Prophet ﷺ when the Angel of the Mountains offered to crush the persecutors of Ta'if?
    (A) He celebrated and commanded the mountains to destroy them immediately.
    (B) He asked for time to think about it for three days.
    (C) He refused and prayed that Allah would bring forth righteous believers from their descendants.
    (D) He demanded that Ta'if pay a ransom of gold coins.

4. The just Christian king who granted sanctuary to the Muslim refugees in Abyssinia was the ______.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Mount Safa ─── The hill from which the Prophet ﷺ openly proclaimed Islam
* Sayyidah Sumayyah ─── The first martyr in Islamic history
* Sayyidina Ja'far ─── The eloquent spokesman who recited Surah Maryam to the Negus
* Shi'b Abi Talib ─── The barren mountain ravine where Muslims endured 3 years of boycott
* The Du'a of Ta'if ─── The prayer of humility and forgiveness uttered under the orchard grapevines

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. Analyze the Prophet's approach at Mount Safa: Why did he establish his reputation for truthfulness (*Al-Amin*) before delivering the divine warning?
2. What leadership lessons can contemporary youth extract from Sayyidina Ja'far's speech before the King of Abyssinia?
3. Contrast the vengeance of typical worldly leaders with the Prophet's reaction to the violence he suffered at Ta'if.
4. How did the three years of suffering in Shi'b Abi Talib forge the unshakeable character of the early Sahabah?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - The Prophet ﷺ gathered Quraysh at Safa following the divine command to warn his closest kin.
2. B - Quraysh unanimously testified to his unblemished truthfulness before hearing the message.
3. B - Sayyidah Sumayyah was executed by Abu Jahl, earning the title of the first martyr in Islam.
4. B - "Ahad! Ahad!" demonstrated Bilal's unshakeable monotheistic conviction under torture.
5. A - The Negus was renowned for absolute justice, protecting any refugee who sought sanctuary.
6. B - Surah Maryam highlighted the profound monotheistic reverence Islam holds for Jesus and his virgin mother.
7. C - The quarantine and famine in Shi'b Abi Talib lasted for three agonizing years.
8. B - Termites ate the parchment except the divine name, exposing the injustice of Quraysh.
9. B - The passing of Abu Talib and Sayyidah Khadijah stripped away physical protection and emotional comfort.
10. C - The Prophet's response at Ta'if is the ultimate historical testament to his universal mercy (*Rahmah*).
