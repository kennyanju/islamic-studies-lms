# Level 3 Quiz: Seerah: Early Life of the Prophet ﷺ
**Age Group:** Ages 10-12 (Level 3 Track)  
**Focus:** Mount Safa, Early Persecution, Abyssinia, Boycott, and Ta'if  

---

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. Why did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ assemble the clans of Quraysh at Mount Safa?
(A) To organize an annual commercial caravan to Damascus
(B) To openly proclaim the message of Islam in response to divine revelation
(C) To elect a new tribal chieftain for the city of Makkah
(D) To distribute the harvest of dates following a rainfall

### 2. Before proclaiming the message of Tawhid atop Mount Safa, what testimony did the Prophet ﷺ elicit from Quraysh?
(A) That they would immediately elect him as the king of Arabia
(B) That they were prepared to demolish all 360 idols that afternoon
(C) That they would surrender their merchant ships in the Red Sea
(D) That he had never spoken an untruth in his entire life (Al-Amin)

### 3. Who holds the sacred honor of being the very first martyr (Shahid) in Islamic history?
(A) Sayyidah Sumayyah bint Khayyat (RA)
(B) Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah (RA)
(C) Sayyidina Hamzah ibn 'Abd al-Muttalib (RA)
(D) Sayyidina Ja'far ibn Abi Talib (RA)

### 4. What phrase did Sayyidina Bilal (RA) repeat while enduring severe torture under a massive boulder in the desert sun?
(A) "Allahu Akbar!"
(B) "Al-Hamdulillah!"
(C) "Ahad! Ahad!" (Allah is One! One!)
(D) "SubhanAllah!"

### 5. Why did the Prophet ﷺ select Abyssinia (Habashah) as the destination for the first Muslim migration?
(A) Because it possessed massive gold mines that could fund the early community
(B) Because it was governed by a righteous Christian king (The Negus) who oppressed no one
(C) Because Quraysh had signed a commercial alliance with its leaders
(D) Because the mountain climate was cooler than the desert heat of Makkah

### 6. Which chapter of the Holy Quran did Sayyidina Ja'far ibn Abi Talib (RA) recite in the royal court of the Negus?
(A) Surah Maryam (Chapter 19)
(B) Surah Al-Baqarah (Chapter 2)
(C) Surah Yasin (Chapter 36)
(D) Surah Al-Fath (Chapter 48)

### 7. How long did the cruel socio-economic boycott of Banu Hashim in the ravine of Shi'b Abi Talib last?
(A) Three weeks
(B) Three months
(C) Six months
(D) Three full years

### 8. What remarkable event brought an end to the boycott parchment hanging inside the Ka'bah?
(A) A sudden desert flood washed the parchment out into the valley
(B) The document was removed by a visiting Persian trade delegation
(C) Termites devoured the parchment except the divine phrase "Bismika Allahumma"
(D) A bolt of lightning struck the sanctuary roof

### 9. Why was the tenth year of prophethood designated as the "Year of Sorrow" ('Am al-Huzn)?
(A) The Muslims experienced a severe economic drought across the Hijaz
(B) Both the Prophet's protective uncle Abu Talib and his beloved wife Sayyidah Khadijah (RA) passed away
(C) A severe earthquake damaged dwellings in Makkah
(D) The early Muslims were expelled permanently from the Arabian peninsula

### 10. What was the noble response of the Prophet ﷺ when the Angel of the Mountains offered to crush the hostile persecutors of Ta'if?
(A) He refused retribution and prayed that Allah bring forth righteous believers from their descendants
(B) He asked for time to deliberate with his companions for three days
(C) He commanded the angel to overturn the two mountains upon them immediately
(D) He demanded that the leaders of Ta'if pay a heavy ransom in gold

## Part D: Reflection & Application

11. Analyze the Prophet's approach at Mount Safa: Why did he establish his verified reputation for truthfulness (*Al-Amin*) before delivering the divine warning?
12. What leadership and communication lessons can young Muslims extract from Sayyidina Ja'far's speech before the Negus of Abyssinia?
13. Contrast the typical vengeance of worldly rulers with the Prophet's reaction to the hostility he encountered at Ta'if.
14. How did enduring three years of hardship in Shi'b Abi Talib prepare the early Companions for the responsibilities of community building in Madinah?

### Reflection Rubric
* **Full Understanding:** Student provides structured, reflective responses citing specific Seerah details (such as the value of moral integrity before preaching, Ja'far's respectful diplomacy, the Prophet's prayer for the descendants of Ta'if, and perseverance under boycott).
* **Basic Understanding:** Student provides brief answers without referencing Seerah context or practical moral applications.

## Teacher Answer Key
1. B - The Prophet ﷺ assembled Quraysh at Safa following the divine command to warn his close kin (Surah Ash-Shu'ara 26:214).
2. D - Quraysh testified that he was Al-Amin who never spoke an untruth.
3. A - Sayyidah Sumayyah bint Khayyat (RA) was martyred by Abu Jahl, becoming the first martyr in Islam.
4. C - "Ahad! Ahad!" expressed Bilal's firm monotheistic conviction under torture.
5. B - The Negus was known for justice, offering protection to all oppressed refugees.
6. A - Surah Maryam demonstrated the Quran's profound honor of Prophet 'Isa and Lady Maryam.
7. D - The quarantine and famine in Shi'b Abi Talib lasted three full years.
8. C - Termites ate the parchment except the divine name, as recorded in Sirat Ibn Hisham.
9. B - The passing of Abu Talib and Sayyidah Khadijah (RA) stripped away external protection and emotional solace.
10. A - The Prophet's response at Ta'if demonstrates his role as a mercy to all creation (Sahih al-Bukhari 3231).
