# Level 3 Student Handout: Seerah: Early Life of the Prophet ﷺ
**Age Group:** Ages 10–12 (Level 3 Track - Years 5 & 6)  
**Topics:** The Open Call at Safa, The Persecution of Believers, The Boycott of Shi'b Abi Talib, Abyssinia, The Year of Sorrow, and The Valley of Ta'if  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah* of Ibn Hisham & *Al-Shifa* of Qadi 'Iyad  
**Riwayah & Tajweed:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 30: Surah At-Takwir and 'Abasa; Qalqalah Rules)

## 1. Standing for Truth: The Call from Mount Safa

For three years after the initial revelation in Cave Hira, the message of Islam was shared quietly with trusted companions and family members. Then, Allah revealed the decisive command:  
*“And warn your closest kinfolk.”* (Surah Ash-Shu'ara 26:214).

Prophet Muhammad ﷺ ascended Mount Safa in the center of Makkah and called out the traditional alarm: *"Ya Sabaha!"* (A cry alerting the tribe to an impending dawn attack). The clans of Quraysh gathered in haste.

Standing atop the rocks, the Prophet ﷺ asked them:  
> *"If I were to tell you that armed horsemen were in the valley behind this mountain preparing to attack you, would you believe me?"*  
> Without hesitation, they replied: *"Yes! We have never known you to speak anything but the truth (Al-Amin)."*  
> He ﷺ then proclaimed: *"Then know that I am a warner sent to you before a severe punishment! Declare: La ilaha illallah!"*

His uncle, Abu Lahab, sneered in fury: *"May you perish for the rest of the day! Is it for this you have gathered us?"* In response, Allah revealed Surah Al-Masad, condemning the arrogance of Abu Lahab.

## 2. Steadfastness Under Fire: The First Martyrs of Islam

As the message spread, Quraysh initiated a systematic campaign of torture against vulnerable believers who had no tribal protectors:

* **Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah:** An enslaved Ethiopian youth whose master, Umayyah ibn Khalaf, dragged him into the blazing desert sun, placing a massive red-hot boulder upon his chest. Through the agonizing pain, Bilal uttered only one triumphant word: **"Ahad! Ahad!"** (Allah is One! Allah is One!). He was purchased and freed by Sayyidina Abu Bakr.
* **The Family of Yasir:** Yasir, his wife Sumayyah, and their son 'Ammar were tied and tortured under the Makkan sun. The Prophet ﷺ passed by them with tears of compassion, comforting them:  
  *"Patience, O family of Yasir! For indeed your promised appointment is Paradise!"*  
  Sayyidah Sumayyah became the **first martyr in Islamic history**, refusing to renounce Tawhid even when executed by Abu Jahl.

## 3. The Migration to Abyssinia (Habashah)

Seeing his companions brutalized, the Prophet ﷺ instructed a group to seek asylum in the Christian kingdom of Aksum (Abyssinia):  
*"Go to the land of Abyssinia, for therein is a Christian King (The Negus / Al-Najashi) in whose realm no one is wronged."*

Quraysh dispatched diplomats loaded with leather gifts to bribe the King and extradite the refugees. In the royal court, Sayyidina Ja'far ibn Abi Talib stood tall and delivered a timeless defense:
> *"O King! We were a people steeped in ignorance—worshipping idols, eating carrion, committing vices, and severing family ties—until Allah sent to us a Messenger whose lineage, truthfulness, and honesty we know. He called us to worship Allah alone, speak truth, fulfill trusts, and protect neighbors."*

When Ja'far recited the opening verses of **Surah Maryam** describing the miraculous birth of Prophet 'Isa (Jesus), the Negus wept until his beard was drenched, declaring:  
*"Indeed, this message and what Jesus brought radiate from the exact same lantern of light! Go, you are safe in my land!"*

## 4. The Boycott of Shi'b Abi Talib (3 Years of Famine)

Infuriated by the growth of Islam, Quraysh drafted a ruthless social and economic boycott against the entire clan of Banu Hashim. They hung the parchment inside the Ka'bah, declaring:
* No one could trade with them, marry their women, sell them food, or speak to them.
* For three long years, the Prophet ﷺ, his family, and companions were quarantined in the barren ravine of Shi'b Abi Talib. They survived on boiled tree leaves and dried leather skins while children cried from hunger.
* The miracle: Termites ate through the boycott parchment inside the Ka'bah, devouring every word of injustice and leaving only the sacred phrase: **"Bismika Allahumma"** (In Your Name, O Allah).

## 5. The Year of Sorrow (*'Am al-Huzn*) and The Mercy of Ta'if

In the tenth year of Prophethood, two towering pillars of support passed away:
1. **Abu Talib:** The Prophet's protective uncle who shielded him from Quraysh assassinations.
2. **Sayyidah Khadijah (RA):** His beloved wife, confidante, first believer, and emotional sanctuary.

With protection stripped away, the Prophet ﷺ walked 50 miles over rugged mountains to the city of **Ta'if**, hoping the tribe of Thaqif would accept Islam. Instead, their leaders mocked him and unleashed street mobs who threw stones, cutting the Prophet's feet until blood soaked his sandals.

Taking refuge in a shaded orchard, the Prophet ﷺ raised his hands in one of history's most poignant supplications:
> *"O Allah! To You alone I complain of my weakness, my lack of resources, and my insignificance before people. O Most Merciful of those who show mercy! You are the Lord of the oppressed, and You are my Lord... If You are not angry with me, I do not care!"*

Angel Jibril and the Angel of the Mountains appeared, offering to crush the city of Ta'if between the two towering peaks of Makkah (*Al-Akhshabayn*). The Beloved Prophet ﷺ refused, saying:  
> *"No! Rather, I hope that Allah will bring forth from their loins people who will worship Allah alone without associating any partners with Him!"*
