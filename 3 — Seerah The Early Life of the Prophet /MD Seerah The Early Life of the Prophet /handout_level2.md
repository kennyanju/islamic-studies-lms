## 🤝 1. Hilf al-Fudul: The League of the Virtuous
When Muhammad ﷺ was around twenty years old, an unjust merchant from Quraysh bought valuable goods from a visiting trader from Yemen, but refused to pay him! The foreign trader climbed the hill of Abu Qubays in Makkah, crying out for justice.

Several noble clans gathered in the home of 'Abdullah ibn Jud'an and signed a historic treaty called **Hilf al-Fudul (The League of the Virtuous)**. They swore an oath before Allah:  
> *"Whenever anyone in Makkah—whether a citizen or a foreigner—is wronged, we will stand united on their side until their full rights are returned!"*

Young Muhammad ﷺ participated in this pact. Decades later as the Messenger of Allah, he remembered it fondly and said:  
> *"I witnessed in the house of Ibn Jud'an a treaty more beloved to me than red camels! If I were invited to it today in Islam, I would eagerly respond!"*

This teaches every Muslim student that defending justice, protecting the weak, and stopping bullying are fundamental Islamic duties everywhere!

## 💍 2. Business Integrity & Marriage to Sayyidah Khadijah (RA)
In Makkah lived a noble, wealthy, and intelligent businesswoman named **Sayyidah Khadijah bint Khuwaylid**. Hearing of Muhammad's immaculate honesty, she hired him to lead her trading caravan to the markets of Sham (Syria).

She sent her trusted assistant **Maysarah** along to observe his character. Maysarah returned with glowing reports:
* Muhammad ﷺ was completely honest in weighing, pricing, and accounting for every penny.
* He never raised his voice, used deceptive sales talk, or broke an agreement.
* Maysarah noticed miraculous blessings: wherever Muhammad traveled, clouds shaded him from the scorching midday sun!

Impressed by his transcendent noble character, Khadijah proposed marriage to him through her friend Nafisah. Muhammad ﷺ accepted, and they entered a blessed, loyal marriage that lasted twenty-five happy years. Sayyidah Khadijah was his greatest emotional support, confidante, and partner.

## 🕋 3. The Wisdom of Placing the Black Stone
When Muhammad ﷺ was 35 years old, a sudden flood damaged the ancient walls of the Ka'bah. The tribes of Quraysh cooperated to rebuild it with clean, halal money. But when they reached the sacred corner to place the **Black Stone (Al-Hajar al-Aswad)**, a furious quarrel broke out!

Every tribe wanted the supreme honor of placing the stone; swords were drawn and war was on the brink! An elder suggested: *"Let the first person who enters through the courtyard gate decide between you."*

```
       ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │             THE WISDOM OF THE PROPHET'S CLOAK          │
       └───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
              ┌────────────────────┼────────────────────┐
              ▼                    ▼                    ▼
       1. The Chieftains    2. The Prophet's     3. United Triumph
       Swords drawn; on     Cloak Spread         All chiefs lift
       the brink of civil   Black stone placed   together; Muhammad ﷺ
       war over honor.      in the center!       places it in Ka'bah!
```

Suddenly, Muhammad ﷺ walked through the gate! The chiefs smiled with immense relief and cried:  
> **هَذَا الأَمِينُ، رَضِينَا بِهِ!**  
> *"This is Al-Amin (The Trustworthy)! We accept his judgment!"*

With profound diplomatic genius, Muhammad ﷺ removed his outer cloak and laid it flat on the ground. He placed the Black Stone in the center and called the leader of every single clan to take hold of an edge of the cloak. Together, all the chieftains lifted the stone simultaneously to the wall! Then, Muhammad ﷺ lifted it with his own hands and placed it into its socket. Conflict was averted, honor was shared, and peace was preserved!

## 🏔️ 4. Cave Hira & The First Revelation (Iqra'!)
As he approached forty years old, Muhammad ﷺ felt an intense longing for quiet reflection away from the idolatry and materialism of Makkah. He would walk to **Mount Nur** and climb up to **Cave Hira**, carrying dry dates and water, spending nights in contemplation of the Creator.

One night in the blessed month of Ramadan (610 CE), the Archangel **Jibril** descended with blinding divine light! Jibril embraced him with overwhelming force and commanded:  
> **ٱقْرَأْ** — *"Read!"*  
> Muhammad ﷺ replied with humility: *"I cannot read!"*

After embracing him three times, Jibril conveyed the very first words of the Holy Qur'an:  
> **ٱقْرَأْ بِٱسْمِ رَبِّكَ ٱلَّذِى خَلَقَ • خَلَقَ ٱلْإِنسَٰنَ مِنْ عَلَقٍ • ٱقْرَأْ وَرَبُّكَ ٱلْأَكْرَمُ • ٱلَّذِى عَلَّمَ بِٱلْقَلَمِ**  
> *"Read in the name of your Lord who created! Created man from a clinging clot! Read, and your Lord is the Most Generous, who taught by the pen!"* (Surah Al-'Alaq 96:1–4).

Trembling with awe, the Prophet ﷺ rushed home to his wife, crying: *"Zammiluni! Zammiluni!"* (Wrap me up! Wrap me up!). Sayyidah Khadijah wrapped him in a blanket and uttered her immortal words of faith:  
> *"Never! By Allah, Allah will never disgrace you! For you maintain ties of kinship, bear the burdens of the weak, help the poor, honor guests, and stand up for those afflicted by justice!"*
