## 🔑 Answer Key & Marking Guide (For Teacher/Parent)

### Part 1: Multiple Choice
1.  **b) Banu Hashim** (The noble clan of the Quraysh).
2.  **c) The Truthful** (*Al-Amin* means the Trustworthy).
3.  **c) To build strong bodies, learn pure Arabic, and escape city diseases**.
4.  **b) *'Ismah*** (Infallibility/Protection from sins. *Wahy* means revelation, *Barakah* means blessings, *Fitrah* means natural state).
5.  **b) Jabal al-Nur and Cave of Hira** (Jabal al-Nur is the mountain; Hira is the cave).
6.  **c) *Iqra*** (Meaning: Read or Recite).
7.  **b) Waraqah ibn Nawfal** (He recognized the Angel Jibril as the same Angel sent to Prophet Musa).
8.  **b) Dar al-Arqam** (The House of Al-Arqam).
9.  **c) He made a prayer for their descendants to worship Allah alone** (Demonstrating his attribute of Mercy).
10. **b) Al-Muwatta'** (The book compiled by Imam Malik over 40 years).

### Part 2: True or False
11. **False.** (They believed Allah was the Supreme Creator, but they practiced *Shirk* by worshipping idols as go-betweens).
12. **True.** (This is the Ash'ari creed definition of *Kalam Allah*).
13. **False.** (Ali ibn Abi Talib (ra) was the first child; Zayd (ra) was the first freed slave).
14. **True.** (This is a unique methodology of the Maliki school, representing the living Sunnah).
15. **False.** (Imam Malik never rode a mount in Madinah out of respect for the soil where the Prophet ﷺ is buried).

### Part 3: Short Answer & Fill in the Blank
16. **Fitrah** (فِطْرَة).
17. **Khadijah bint Khuwaylid (ra)** (first female/first believer) and **Abu Bakr al-Siddiq (ra)** (first free male).
18. **Answer:** Because the Prophet ﷺ lost his two main pillars of worldly support (his uncle Abu Talib, who provided political protection, and his wife Khadijah, who provided emotional support).
19. **Answer:** *Sadl* is praying with the arms resting naturally at the sides. It is a signature practice of the **Maliki school of Fiqh**.
20. **Answer:** Because the people of Madinah were the children and grandchildren of the Sahabah. Their daily actions were passed down directly from the Prophet ﷺ, representing a continuous living Sunnah.
