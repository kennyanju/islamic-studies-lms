# Level 4 Quiz: Seerah: Early Life of the Prophet ﷺ
**Age Group:** Ages 12-14 (Level 4 Track)  
**Focus:** Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj, Pledges of 'Aqabah, Sublime Shama'il, and Prophetic Love

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions

### 1. What is the orthodox Sunni belief regarding the nature of Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj?
(A) It was a symbolic dream that occurred while the Prophet ﷺ was resting in Madinah
(B) It was an allegorical vision witnessed solely by the angelic realm
(C) It was a miraculous journey occurring in both physical body and soul (*bil-Jasad wal-Ruh*) while fully awake
(D) It was a terrestrial journey that concluded at the borders of the Sinai peninsula

### 2. What monumental event took place at Masjid al-Aqsa in Jerusalem during the Night Journey?
(A) The Prophet ﷺ concluded a formal treaty with the regional Roman governor
(B) The Prophet ﷺ led all prior Prophets and Messengers in congregational prayer
(C) The Prophet ﷺ translated the Psalms of David into the Arabic language
(D) The Prophet ﷺ supervised the building of an overlook tower

### 3. At what celestial boundary did the Prophet ﷺ receive the direct divine ordination of the Five Daily Prayers?
(A) At the Well of Zamzam within the sacred sanctuary of Makkah
(B) On the summit of Mount Sinai in the presence of Harun (AS)
(C) Inside the quiet sanctuary of Cave Hira
(D) Beyond the Seventh Heaven at *Sidrat al-Muntaha* (The Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary)

### 4. Why was Sayyidina Abu Bakr bestowed with the permanent honorific title "Al-Siddiq"?
(A) Because he immediately affirmed the absolute truth of the Night Journey without hesitation
(B) Because he was the wealthiest merchant among the clans of Quraysh
(C) Because he led the trading caravan safely across the Syrian desert
(D) Because he financed the reconstruction of the municipal wells of Makkah

### 5. Who was appointed by the Prophet ﷺ as the first diplomatic ambassador and teacher to Yathrib (Madinah)?
(A) Sayyidina Khalid ibn al-Walid (RA)
(B) Sayyidina 'Amr ibn al-'As (RA)
(C) Sayyidina Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (RA)
(D) Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah (RA)

### 6. What was the central commitment undertaken by the Yathrib delegates at the Second Pledge of 'Aqabah?
(A) To remain in Yathrib while sending annual financial tribute to support the believers staying in Makkah
(B) To negotiate a commercial trade truce with the leaders of Ta'if on behalf of the Quraysh clans
(C) To relocate the Muslims of Yathrib to the Christian kingdom of Abyssinia under the Negus's protection
(D) To protect and shelter the Prophet ﷺ and his companions just as they protected their own families

### 7. Which celebrated 12th-century Maliki jurist of Ceuta authored the masterwork *Al-Shifa bi-Ta'rif Huquq al-Mustafa*?
(A) Al-Qadi 'Iyad ibn Musa al-Yahsubi
(B) Imam Ibn Rushd al-Hafid
(C) Imam 'Abd al-Wahid Ibn 'Ashir
(D) Imam Shihab al-Din al-Qarafi

### 8. How did classical Companions describe the physical gait and posture of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ?
(A) He walked sluggishly with his gaze tilted toward the sky
(B) He walked purposefully and with dignity, leaning forward naturally as if descending a gentle slope
(C) He walked while leaning on attendants on both sides
(D) He ran continuously through the streets of Makkah

### 9. What was the physical appearance of the "Seal of Prophethood" (*Khatam an-Nubuwwah*) between the Prophet's shoulder blades?
(A) A golden inscription carved in classical Syriac script
(B) A silver talisman resting on a cord of woven silk
(C) An incision produced by surgical instruments in early childhood
(D) A raised, radiant birthmark roughly the size of a pigeon's egg surrounded by fine hairs

### 10. According to the authentic hadith of Sayyidina 'Umar in Sahih al-Bukhari (6632), when is a believer's faith complete?
(A) When they donate half of their business capital to public endowments
(B) When they undertake an annual journey across the Arabian peninsula on foot
(C) When the Messenger of Allah ﷺ becomes dearer to them than all creation, including their own self
(D) When they memorize forty thousand prophetic traditions without written notes

## Part C: Matching Pairs
* Al-Isra' ─── The night journey from Makkah to Bayt al-Maqdis in Jerusalem
* Al-Mi'raj ─── The bodily ascension through the heavens to Sidrat al-Muntaha
* Bay'at al-'Aqabah ─── The solemn covenants of protection and allegiance made by Yathrib delegates
* Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr ─── The first ambassador who taught the Quran to the households of Madinah
* Al-Shama'il ─── The branch of Seerah studying the physical appearance, demeanor, and character of the Prophet ﷺ

## Part D: Reflection & Application

1. Why was it spiritually significant that the Prophet ﷺ led all prior Prophets in congregational prayer at Masjid al-Aqsa during the Night Journey?
2. Analyze the diplomatic methodology of Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr in Yathrib: How did moral character and Quranic recitation succeed in transforming a city where military force had failed?
3. Examine Sayyidina 'Umar's progression in understanding prophetic love in Sahih al-Bukhari 6632: Why must rational, intentional love of the Prophet ﷺ precede mere emotional sentiment?
4. How does studying the detailed *Shama'il* of the Prophet ﷺ provide modern teenagers with a durable moral standard against transient pop-culture trends?

---

## Teacher Answer Key
1. C - Sunni orthodoxy affirms that the Isra' and Mi'raj occurred in both body and soul while fully awake.
2. B - Leading all prior Prophets cemented Islam as the fulfillment of divine revelation.
3. D - The Five Daily Prayers were gifted directly at Sidrat al-Muntaha above the Seven Heavens.
4. A - Abu Bakr affirmed the journey without hesitation, earning the title Al-Siddiq.
5. C - Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr was appointed as the ambassador to Yathrib.
6. D - The Second Pledge of 'Aqabah established a mutual defense pact offering sanctuary to the Muslims.
7. A - Al-Qadi 'Iyad of Ceuta authored the classical compendium *Al-Shifa*.
8. B - Narrations in Tirmidhi's *Shama'il* describe his forward-leaning, purposeful gait.
9. D - The Seal of Prophethood was a raised birthmark between his shoulder blades.
10. C - Sahih al-Bukhari 6632 establishes that faith is perfected when the Prophet ﷺ is dearer than oneself.
