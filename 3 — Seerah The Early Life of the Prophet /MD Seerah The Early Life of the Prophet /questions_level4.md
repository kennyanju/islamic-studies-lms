# Level 4 Quiz: Seerah: Early Life of the Prophet ﷺ

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. What is the orthodox Sunni belief regarding the nature of Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj?
   (A) It was a symbolic dream that occurred while the Prophet ﷺ was sleeping in Madinah.
   (B) It occurred in both physical body and soul (*bil-Jasad wal-Ruh*) while fully awake.
   (C) It was an allegorical vision that only the angels witnessed.
   (D) It was a physical journey that stopped at the borders of the Sinai peninsula.

2. What miraculous event took place at Masjid al-Aqsa in Jerusalem during the Night Journey?
   (A) The Prophet ﷺ signed a peace treaty with the Roman governor.
   (B) The Prophet ﷺ led all the previous Prophets and Messengers in congregational prayer.
   (C) The Prophet ﷺ constructed the wooden pulpit of Jerusalem.
   (D) The Prophet ﷺ translated the Psalms of David into Arabic.

3. At what celestial landmark did the Prophet ﷺ receive the direct divine gift of the Five Daily Prayers?
   (A) At the Well of Zamzam in Makkah.
   (B) Beyond the Seventh Heaven at *Sidrat al-Muntaha* (The Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary).
   (C) On the summit of Mount Sinai.
   (D) Inside the Cave of Thawr.

4. Why was Sayyidina Abu Bakr bestowed with the permanent honorific title "Al-Siddiq"?
   (A) Because he was the wealthiest merchant in the Quraysh tribe.
   (B) Because he immediately affirmed the truth of the Night Journey without hesitation.
   (C) Because he defeated thirty champions in single combat at Uhud.
   (D) Because he financed the construction of the Ka'bah walls.

5. Who was chosen by the Prophet ﷺ as the first diplomatic ambassador of Islam to Yathrib (Madinah)?
   (A) Sayyidina Khalid ibn al-Walid.
   (B) Sayyidina Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr.
   (C) Sayyidina 'Amr ibn al-'As.
   (D) Sayyidina Bilal ibn Rabah.

6. What was the central commitment made by the Yathrib delegates at the Second Pledge of 'Aqabah?
   (A) To send fifty horses to Makkah every lunar month.
   (B) To protect and shelter the Prophet ﷺ as they would protect their own families.
   (C) To declare war against the Persian Sasanian empire immediately.
   (D) To cease fishing in the Red Sea.

7. Which 12th-century Maliki jurist of Ceuta authored the masterwork *Al-Shifa bi-Ta'rif Huquq al-Mustafa*?
   (A) Imam Ibn Rushd.
   (B) Al-Qadi 'Iyad ibn Musa.
   (C) Imam Ibn 'Ashir.
   (D) Imam al-Qarafi.

8. How did classical companions describe the physical gait of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ?
   (A) He walked sluggishly with his head tilted backward.
   (B) He walked purposefully and with dignity, leaning forward as if descending a gentle slope.
   (C) He ran continuously through the streets of Makkah.
   (D) He walked while leaning on two attendants at all times.

9. What was the physical appearance of the "Seal of Prophethood" located between the Prophet's shoulder blades?
   (A) A golden tattoo written in classical Syriac script.
   (B) A raised, radiant birthmark roughly the size of a pigeon's egg surrounded by fine hairs.
   (C) A silver pendant hanging from a silk cord.
   (D) An incision made by surgical tools in childhood.

10. According to the authentic hadith of Sayyidina 'Umar in Sahih al-Bukhari, when is a believer's faith considered complete?
    (A) When they donate half of their business inventory to charity.
    (B) When the Prophet ﷺ becomes dearer to them than all creation, including their own soul.
    (C) When they travel on foot across the Arabian peninsula.
    (D) When they memorize forty thousand prophetic traditions.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Al-Isra' ─── The night journey from Makkah to Bayt al-Maqdis (Jerusalem)
* Al-Mi'raj ─── The bodily ascension through the heavens to Sidrat al-Muntaha
* Bay'at al-'Aqabah ─── The sacred pledges made by the people of Yathrib to protect the Prophet ﷺ
* Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr ─── The first ambassador who taught the Quran to the people of Madinah
* Al-Shama'il ─── The study of the physical appearance, manners, and daily life of the Prophet ﷺ

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. Why was it crucial that the Prophet ﷺ led all the previous Prophets in prayer at Masjid al-Aqsa during the Night Journey?
2. Analyze the character and pedagogical strategy of Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr in Yathrib: How did he succeed in transforming an entire city without weapons or wealth?
3. Discuss the psychological and theological impact of the dialogue between the Prophet ﷺ and Sayyidina 'Umar regarding loving the Prophet above one's own soul.
4. How does studying the physical and moral portrait (*Shama'il*) of the Prophet ﷺ protect Muslim youth from taking pop culture celebrities as moral role models?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - Mainstream Sunni consensus confirms that Isra' and Mi'raj occurred in waking reality in both body and soul.
2. B - Leading all prophets in prayer established the universality and finality of the Prophet's message.
3. B - Salah was gifted directly by Allah at Sidrat al-Muntaha, demonstrating its supreme status among pillars.
4. B - Abu Bakr earned the title Al-Siddiq by immediately verifying the Prophet's words without skepticism.
5. B - Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr was appointed as the teacher and ambassador to Yathrib.
6. B - The Second Pledge was a covenant of mutual defense, sanctuary, and loyalty.
7. B - Al-Qadi 'Iyad of Ceuta (Morocco) authored the renowned *Al-Shifa*.
8. B - Narrations in Tirmidhi's *Shama'il* describe his dignified, purposeful, forward-leaning gait.
9. B - The seal was a raised mark between his shoulders confirming prophetic office.
10. B - True faith requires loving the Messenger ﷺ above wealth, family, and one's own ego.
