## 1. Historiography: Methodology of Classical Seerah Sources

At the capstone level, students analyze how the life of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was documented. Unlike ancient mythological founders whose lives were recorded centuries later by anonymous scribes, the biography of the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ is the **most thoroughly documented life of any religious figure in human history**.

### The Hierarchy of Seerah Literature
1. **The Noble Quran:** The contemporary primary divine witness, recording psychological states, battles, setbacks, and divine rebukes (e.g. Surah 'Abasa, Al-Duha, Al-Ahzab).
2. **The Sahih Hadith Compilations (Bukhari, Muslim, Muwatta Malik):** Eyewitness accounts transmitted through unbroken chains of verified narrators (*Isnad*), evaluated under the world's most rigorous biographical critique (*'Ilm al-Rijal*).
3. **The Early Maghazi and Sirah Compilers:**
   * 'Urwah ibn al-Zubayr (d. 94 AH) and Aban ibn 'Uthman (d. 105 AH).
   * Muhammad ibn Muslim ibn Shihab al-Zuhri (d. 124 AH): The master who systematized Seerah narratives.
   * Muhammad ibn Ishaq (d. 151 AH) and 'Abd al-Malik Ibn Hisham (d. 218 AH): The canonical narrative framework *Al-Sirah al-Nabawiyyah*.

## 2. Prophetic Infallibility (*'Ismah*): Dogmatic Boundaries

In *Al-Shifa*, Qadi 'Iyad dedicates extensive chapters to the theological consensus regarding the moral protection of the Prophet ﷺ:

1. **Before Revelation:**  
   The Prophet ﷺ was protected from worshipping idols, drinking alcohol, attending pagan festivities, or engaging in dishonorable conduct throughout his 40 years prior to prophethood. He was known unanimously as *Al-Sadiq al-Amin*.
2. **In Conveying Divine Revelation (*Al-Tabligh*):**  
   Complete and absolute consensus (*Ijma'*): It is rationally and textually impossible for a Messenger to commit error, forgetfulness, or deceit in conveying Allah's message.
3. **Major and Minor Sins:**  
   Protected from all major sins (*Kaba'ir*) and disgraceful minor sins (*Sagha'ir al-Khasisah*). Sunni theologians hold that minor oversights (*Zallat*) occasionally mentioned in the Quran were elevated divine pedagogies to teach the Ummah legal rulings and humility.

### The Preservation of the *Hunafa'* (The Pre-Islamic Monotheists)
Even during the peak of pagan Jahiliyyah, a small group of seekers rejected idolatry, refused to eat meat slaughtered for false gods, and sought the pure religion of Ibrahim (*Hanifiyyah*):
* **Zayd ibn 'Amr ibn Nufayl:** Who stood by the Ka'bah and declared: *"O Allah, if I knew how You loved to be worshipped, I would worship You, but I do not know!"* The Prophet ﷺ testified that Zayd will be resurrected on the Day of Judgment as a solitary righteous nation (*Ummah Wahdah*).
* **Waraqah ibn Nawfal:** The learned Christian scholar who immediately verified the first revelation of Cave Hira, confirming that the angel Jibril was the *Namus al-Akbar* who visited Prophet Musa.

## 4. Deconstructing Orientalist Revisionism

In university humanities courses, students frequently encounter 19th- and 20th-century secular orientalist theories regarding the origins of Islam:

1. **The "Epilepsy / Hallucination" Hypothesis:**  
   *Claim:* Early European orientalists asserted that prophetic revelations were neurological seizures or hallucinations.  
   *Refutation:* Neurological seizures cause cognitive degradation, loss of memory, incoherent rambling, and motor dysfunction. The Quran, by contrast, is a pinnacle of sublime linguistic architecture, intricate legal legislation, historical consistency, and profound philosophical depth revealed consistently over 23 years! A person undergoing neurological decline cannot establish an empire and author an unshakeable civilization.
2. **The "Borrowing from Monks" Hypothesis:**  
   *Claim:* That Muhammad ﷺ copied the Quran from Christian monks like Bahira or Waraqah.  
   *Refutation:* The meeting with Bahira occurred when the Prophet was approximately 9–12 years old on a brief trade journey lasting a few hours in a public caravan! Waraqah passed away shortly after the first revelation. Furthermore, the Quran explicitly corrects the theological dogmas of both Christianity (rejecting the Trinity, original sin, and the crucifixion of Jesus) and rabbinic Judaism, demonstrating complete independent divine revelation.
