## 1. Comfort After Catastrophe: Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj

Following the heartbreak of the Year of Sorrow and the brutal rejection at Ta'if, Allah granted His Beloved Messenger ﷺ the supreme miraculous journey of human history: **Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj** (The Night Journey and Heavenly Ascension).

```
       ┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │             THE MIRACULOUS NIGHT JOURNEY               │
       │                   (Al-Isra' wal-Mi'raj)                │
       └───────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────┘
              ┌────────────────────┴────────────────────┐
              ▼                                         ▼
        1. Al-Isra' (Earthly)                    2. Al-Mi'raj (Heavenly)
     From the Sacred Mosque in Makkah          Ascension through the 7
     to Bayt al-Maqdis (Jerusalem) on          heavens; meeting Prophets;
     the celestial mount Al-Buraq.             reaching Sidrat al-Muntaha;
     Leads all Prophets in prayer.             gifting of the 5 daily prayers.
```

### Key Realities of the Night Journey:
1. **Physical and Spiritual Reality:** Mainstream Sunni orthodoxy affirms that the Isra' and Mi'raj occurred in **both body and soul (*bil-Jasad wal-Ruh*)** while awake, not as a mere dream.
2. **Imamate Over All Prophets:** At Masjid al-Aqsa, the Prophet ﷺ led all the Messengers of history (Adam, Ibrahim, Musa, 'Isa) in prayer, cementing Islam as the fulfillment and capstone of all prophetic missions.
3. **The Gifting of Salah:** Above the Seventh Heaven, at *Sidrat al-Muntaha* (The Lote Tree of the Utmost Boundary), Allah gifted the Ummah with the **Five Daily Prayers**. Initially ordained as 50, they were reduced through the counsel of Musa (AS) to 5 in practice, yet retaining the divine reward of 50!
4. **Abu Bakr al-Siddiq:** When Quraysh ridiculed the journey the next morning, Abu Bakr famously stated: *"If he said it, then it is the truth! I believe him in news from the heavens descending day and night, so why would I doubt this?"* For this certitude, he was titled **Al-Siddiq** (The Verifier of Truth).

## 2. Diplomatic Breakthrough: The Pledges of 'Aqabah (*Bay'at al-'Aqabah*)

During the Hajj pilgrimage season, the Prophet ﷺ met six seekers from the northern oasis city of **Yathrib** (later Madinah). Recognizing his noble character, they embraced Islam and returned home to spread the faith.

* **The First Pledge of 'Aqabah (12th Year of Prophethood):** Twelve delegates pledged to abandon idolatry, theft, slander, and infanticide.
* **Mus'ab ibn 'Umayr (The First Ambassador):** The Prophet ﷺ sent the young nobleman Mus'ab to Yathrib. Through gentleness, Quranic recitation, and brilliant character, Mus'ab brought almost every household in Yathrib into Islam within a single year!
* **The Second Pledge of 'Aqabah (13th Year):** Seventy-three men and two women secretly met the Prophet ﷺ at night, pledging to protect him as they would protect their own families, opening the path for the Great Hijrah.

## 3. Shama'il: The Sublime Person & Character of the Prophet ﷺ

In his celebrated masterwork *Al-Shifa*, the Andalusian Maliki jurist **Al-Qadi 'Iyad** (d. 544 AH) gathered authentic narrations describing the physical appearance and noble temperament of the Messenger of Allah ﷺ:

### A. Physical Description (from Sayyidina 'Ali and Hind ibn Abi Halah):
* **Complexion:** Luminous, radiant fair skin with a healthy rosiness (*Azhar al-Lawn*).
* **Face:** Glowing with majesty like the full moon on a cloudless night.
* **Eyes:** Large, deep, piercing black eyes with long eyelashes and natural kohl beauty.
* **Hair:** Jet black, slightly wavy, resting between his earlobes and shoulders.
* **Gait:** Walked with purpose and dignity, leaning forward as if descending a gentle slope, never swaggering or strutting with arrogance.
* **The Seal of Prophethood (*Khatam an-Nubuwwah*):** A raised, radiant birthmark the size of a pigeon's egg located between his shoulder blades.

### B. Moral Temperament:
* He was the most generous of people, the most truthful in speech, the most lenient in temperament, and the most noble in companionship.
* He never retaliated for a personal insult; he only showed anger if the sacred boundaries of Allah were violated.
* He mended his own sandals, patched his own tunic, milked his goat, served his family at home, and sat among the poor as an equal.

## 4. The Obligation of Loving the Prophet ﷺ Above Oneself

In Sahih al-Bukhari, Sayyidina 'Umar ibn al-Khattab said to the Prophet ﷺ:  
> *"O Messenger of Allah! You are dearer to me than everything except my own soul."*  
> The Prophet ﷺ replied: *"No, 'Umar, until I am dearer to you than your own soul!"*  
> 'Umar paused, reflected deeply on how the Prophet ﷺ had saved him from darkness to eternal light, and said: *"By Allah, now you are dearer to me than my own soul!"*  
> The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Now, O 'Umar, your faith is complete!"*
