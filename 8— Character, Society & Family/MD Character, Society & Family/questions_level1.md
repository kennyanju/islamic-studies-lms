# Level 1 Quiz: Character, Society & Family

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How should a young Muslim respond when their mother or father calls them?
   (A) Wait until they call three times before answering.
   (B) Complain and ask why someone else cannot go instead.
   (C) Answer immediately and cheerfully with "Yes Mum" or "Yes Dad!"
   (D) Pretend not to hear and keep playing games.

2. What did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ teach about smiling at someone?
   (A) It is only required on Eid morning.
   (B) It should only be done for people we already know well.
   (C) It is something only teachers and parents should do.
   (D) It is an act of charity (Sadaqah) that brings joy.

3. How does Islam teach us to treat animals and pets?
   (A) With gentleness, kindness, and providing food and clean water.
   (B) Leaving them alone without water or shelter.
   (C) Only caring for animals that have expensive fur.
   (D) Scaring them when they try to rest in the shade.

4. What special Quranic prayer (du'a) from Surah Al-Isra' do we recite for our parents?
   (A) "My Lord, grant me many toys and candies."
   (B) "My Lord, have mercy upon them, just as they raised me when I was small."
   (C) "My Lord, make every day a holiday."
   (D) "My Lord, forgive only my older relatives."

5. What is the beautiful greeting of peace that Prophet Muhammad ﷺ told us to spread?
   (A) Subhan Allah.
   (B) Astaghfirullah.
   (C) As-Salamu 'Alaykum.
   (D) La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah.

## Part B: Fill in the Blanks
Vocabulary Bank: Charity, Mercy, Parents
1. Smiling at your brother or sister is an act of ______.
2. We must always be kind, loving, and respectful to our ______.
3. We pray that Allah showers His ______ upon our mothers and fathers.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Smiling ─── An act of charity (Sadaqah) that brings joy
* Parents ─── Blessed family members we must honor and obey in good
* Gentle Care ─── Treating animals, birds, and nature with compassion

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. What is one kind action you will do today to make your mother or father smile?
2. Why did Prophet Muhammad ﷺ tell the companion to return the baby chicks to their mother bird?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. C - Responding promptly and cheerfully honors parents with excellence (Surah Al-Isra' 17:23).
2. D - The Prophet ﷺ taught that smiling in the face of your brother is charity (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1956).
3. A - Islam commands kindness and feeding animals, as in the story of the thirsty dog (Sahih al-Bukhari 2363).
4. B - Surah Al-Isra' 17:24 teaches the du'a: "Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira."
5. C - Spreading Salam spreads love and peace among believers (Sahih Muslim 54).

### Fill in the Blanks Key
1. Charity - Smiling is Sadaqah.
2. Parents - Honorable family members.
3. Mercy - Divine blessing prayed for parents.

### Matching Key
* Smiling connects with An act of charity (Sadaqah) that brings joy.
* Parents connects with Blessed family members we must honor and obey in good.
* Gentle Care connects with Treating animals, birds, and nature with compassion.

### Reflection Marking Rubric
- Question 1: Awards full points for naming a specific, realistic act of kindness at home (tidying room, saying thank you, listening promptly).
- Question 2: Awards full points for explaining that the Prophet ﷺ felt compassion for the mother bird's sadness and commanded mercy for all living creatures.
