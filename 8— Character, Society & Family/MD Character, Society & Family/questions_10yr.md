### Part B: Scenario-Based Questions (5 Questions)
*Read the scenario and write or choose the correct response based on the curriculum.*

11. **Yunus wants to play a voluntary online game tournament that takes all weekend, but his parents ask him to stay home and help look after his younger siblings. He thinks, "This is voluntary, so I can just go anyway." What is the Maliki ruling on this?**
    *   *Answer:* Yunus is incorrect. In Maliki fiqh, parental permission is required for voluntary travel or voluntary undertakings that affect the parents. Serving his parents and showing them **Birr al-Walidayn** (righteous treatment) is a strict obligation (*Wajib*), which takes priority over voluntary activities.

12. **Layla is in a WhatsApp group chat with her classmates. Two girls start sharing private messages from another classmate to make fun of her. Layla doesn't write anything bad, but she keeps reading the messages. How can Layla use Sadd al-Dhara'i' here?**
    *   *Answer:* Layla should apply **Sadd al-Dhara'i'** (blocking the path to harm) by leaving the group chat. Even though she isn't typing bad comments, staying in the chat makes her a silent witness to gossiping (*Gheebah*) and violates the duty of guarding honor (**Hifdh al-'Ird**). Leaving the chat blocks the path to participating in the sin.

13. **Zayd is building a treehouse in his backyard. He wants to build a large window that points directly into his neighbor's bedroom window. Can his neighbor complain, and what is the Maliki legal view?**
    *   *Answer:* Yes, the neighbor can complain. In Maliki law, there is a rule of **Da'f al-Darar** (prevention of harm). A property owner is legally blocked from using their property in a way that causes direct harm or violates the privacy of their neighbor. Zayd would be forced to block or move the window.

14. **Amina is extremely close friends with Salma. They share everything and are best friends. Salma is involved in a small dispute at school, and the principal asks Amina to give witness testimony. In a Maliki court of law, would Amina's testimony in favor of Salma be accepted?**
    *   *Answer:* No. In the Maliki school, testimony in favor of an intimate close friend (**Shahadat al-Sadiq li-Sadiqihi**) is rejected because the legal system recognizes that the bond of friendship is so strong that it can create bias, making it hard to be objective.

15. **Hamzah drops his tablet and cracks the screen. He gets very angry, screams, and throws his toy across the room. His older brother tells him to think about Qadar (Divine Decree). How does this help?**
    *   *Answer:* Believing in **Qadar** means knowing that the tablet cracking was already decreed by Allah as a test. Knowing that everything is in Allah's hands helps Hamzah accept the situation, remain patient (*Sabr*), and immediately cool down his anger (*Kadhm al-Ghaydh*).

### Part C: Short Answer & Matching (5 Questions)
*Answer the questions briefly or match the terms.*

16. **Match the Arabic term to its correct definition:**
    *   1. *Tawadu'*  --> ______ (Answer: Humility/Knowing blessings are from Allah)
    *   2. *'Adl*      --> ______ (Answer: Justice/Putting things in their proper place)
    *   3. *'Ird*      --> ______ (Answer: Personal honor and dignity)
    *   4. *Nafaqah*   --> ______ (Answer: Legal obligation to financially maintain poor parents)

17. **What did the Prophet (pbuh) mean when he said a good friend is like a perfume seller (Misk)?**
    *   *Answer:* He meant that good company (*Suhbah Salihah*) naturally influences you in a positive way. Even if they don't teach you directly, just being around them makes your character and actions better.

18. **Why does Allah forbid saying even "Uff" (أُفّ) to parents?**
    *   *Answer:* Because "Uff" is the smallest expression of irritation or annoyance. By forbidding even this tiny sound, Allah is teaching us that we must treat our parents with the utmost gentleness and respect, avoiding all forms of frustration toward them.

19. **What is the difference between 'Adl (justice) and Qist (equity)?**
    *   *Answer:* **'Adl** is giving everyone their exact rights under the law. **Qist** is fairness that takes into account the specific needs and situations of different people (like giving different sized shoes to people of different ages).

20. **Give one example of Maslahah Mursalah (Public Interest) that we follow in modern schools or cities.**
    *   *Answer:* School fire drills or wearing seatbelts in a car. These are modern rules that aren't mentioned in the Quran directly, but they protect human life and safety, which is a major goal of Islamic law.
