# Level 2 Student Handout: Character, Society & Family
**Age Group:** Ages 8-10 (Level 2 Track: Primary Stage 2)  
**Core Strands:** Filial Piety (Birr al-Walidayn) | Family Ties (Silat al-Rahim) | Rights of Neighbors & Teachers | Controlling Anger & Guarding the Tongue  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Matn al-'Ashmawiyyah* & Imam an-Nawawi's *40 Hadith* (Hadiths 13, 15, 16)  
**Riwayah & Surah Target:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 30: Surah Al-Humazah & Surah Al-Ma'un)

## 1. Birr al-Walidayn: Excellence to Parents
A man came to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and asked: *"O Messenger of Allah! Who among the people is most deserving of my good companionship?"*  
The Prophet ﷺ answered:  
> **أُمُّكَ** (*"Your mother!"*)  
> The man asked: *"Then who?"*  
> The Prophet ﷺ repeated: **أُمُّكَ** (*"Your mother!"*)  
> The man asked a third time: *"Then who?"*  
> The Prophet ﷺ repeated again: **أُمُّكَ** (*"Your mother!"*)  
> The man asked a fourth time: *"Then who?"*  
> The Prophet ﷺ replied: **أَبُوكَ** (*"Then your father!"*)  
> (Sahih al-Bukhari 5971, Sahih Muslim 2548).

The mother carries the child through nine months of pregnancy, endures the pain of childbirth, feeds her baby, and stays awake nights nursing them when sick. Islam gives the mother three times the honor, while also strictly obligating deep respect, support, and obedience to the father.

| Sequence of Honor | Recipient | Significance in Islamic Tradition |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First Mention** | Your Mother | Enduring pregnancy and physical exhaustion |
| **Second Mention** | Your Mother | Enduring the labor and pain of delivery |
| **Third Mention** | Your Mother | Sleepless nights of nursing and early care |
| **Fourth Mention** | Then Your Father | Striving to protect, provide, and guide the family |

### Safeguarding Boundary:
We honor and obey parents in all good deeds. However, the Prophet ﷺ set an absolute boundary: *"There is no obedience to a creature in disobedience to the Creator"* (Sahih Muslim 1840). If anyone ever asks you to commit a sin, harm someone, or keep a harmful secret, you must refuse and immediately inform a trusted teacher or counselor.

## 2. Silat al-Rahim: Upholding Family Ties
Allah created families to be tied together by love, mercy, and mutual support:
* **Who are your Rahim?** Grandparents, aunts (*Khala* / *'Ammah*), uncles (*Khal* / *'Amm*), and cousins.
* **The Blessing of Family Ties:** The Prophet ﷺ promised:  
  > *"Whoever loves that their wealth be expanded and their lifespan be prolonged, let them maintain their family ties!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari 2067, Sahih Muslim 2557).
* Even if a relative annoys you, be the bigger person: call them on Eid, exchange gifts, and forgive quickly.

## 3. The Rights of Neighbors and Teachers
* **The Neighbor's Sacred Status:** The Prophet ﷺ said:  
  > *"Angel Jibril kept advising me about the rights of the neighbor so intensely that I thought he was about to give neighbors a share of inheritance!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari 6014, Sahih Muslim 2624).  
  * Classical scholars noted that the neighborly circle extends to forty houses in every direction!
  * Never make loud noise late at night that disturbs neighbors.
  * Share warm food and treats when cooking special meals.
  * Help elderly neighbors carry groceries or shovel pathways.
* **Honoring Your Teachers:** Scholars and teachers carry the inheritance of the Prophets. Listen attentively in class, do your schoolwork diligently, never speak rudely, and make du'a for your teachers every single day.

## 4. Controlling Anger and Guarding the Tongue
A man came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: *"Advise me!"* The Prophet ﷺ replied:  
> **لاَ تَغْضَبْ** (*"Do not become angry!"*)  
The man repeated his request several times, and the Prophet ﷺ answered every time: *"Do not become angry!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari 6116).

### The Prophetic Remedy for Anger:
1. **Seek Refuge in Allah:** Say immediately: *“A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim!”*
2. **Remain Silent:** Close your mouth until your anger cools, so you do not say hurtful words.
3. **Change Your Posture:** If you are standing, sit down; if sitting, lie down (Sunan Abi Dawud 4782).
4. **Make Cool Wudu:** Anger is like a fire; cool water extinguishes fire.

### Guarding the Tongue: Two Deadly Sins
Prophet Muhammad ﷺ warned against two specific sins of the tongue:
* **Backbiting (*Ghibah*):** Talking about an absent person in a way they would dislike, even if what you say is true! The Prophet ﷺ defined it in Sahih Muslim 2589. If it is false, it is slander (*Buhtan*).
* **Tale-Bearing (*Namimah*):** Carrying gossip between people to cause fights or break up friendships. The Prophet ﷺ warned: *"The tale-bearer will not enter Paradise"* (Sahih al-Bukhari 216, Sahih Muslim 105).
