## 🏡 1. Birr al-Walidayn: Excellence to Parents
A man came to Prophet Muhammad ﷺ and asked: *"O Messenger of Allah! Who among the people is most deserving of my good companionship?"*  
The Prophet ﷺ answered:  
> **أُمُّكَ** — *"Your mother!"*  
> The man asked: *"Then who?"*  
> The Prophet ﷺ repeated: **أُمُّكَ** — *"Your mother!"*  
> The man asked a third time: *"Then who?"*  
> The Prophet ﷺ repeated again: **أُمُّكَ** — *"Your mother!"*  
> The man asked a fourth time: *"Then who?"*  
> The Prophet ﷺ replied: **أَبُوكَ** — *"Then your father!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari).

The mother carries the child through nine months of exhaustion, endures the pain of childbirth, feeds her baby, and stays awake nights nursing them when sick. Islam gives the mother **three times the honor**, while also strictly obligating deep respect and obedience to the father!

## 🌳 2. Silat al-Rahim: Upholding Family Ties
Allah created families to be tied together by love, mercy, and mutual support:
* **Who are your Rahim?** Grandparents, aunts (*Khala* / *'Ammah*), uncles (*Khal* / *'Amm*), and cousins.
* **The Blessing of Family Ties:** The Prophet ﷺ promised:  
  > *"Whoever loves that their wealth be expanded and their lifespan be prolonged, let them maintain their family ties!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari).
* Even if an aunt or cousin says something that annoys you, be the bigger person: call them on Eid, give gifts, and forgive quickly.

## 🏘️ 3. The Rights of Neighbors & Teachers
* **The Neighbor's Sacred Status:** The Prophet ﷺ said:  
  > *"Angel Jibril kept advising me about the rights of the neighbor so intensely that I thought he was about to give neighbors a share of inheritance!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari).  
  * Never play loud music or bounce balls against neighbor's walls late at night.
  * Share warm food and treats when cooking special meals.
  * Help elderly neighbors carry heavy grocery bags.
* **Honoring Your Teachers:** Scholars and teachers carry the inheritance of the Prophets. Listen attentively in class, do your homework diligently, never speak rudely, and make du'a for your teachers every single day!

## 🛡️ 4. Controlling Anger & Guarding the Tongue
A man came to the Prophet ﷺ and said: *"Advise me!"* The Prophet ﷺ replied:  
> **لاَ تَغْضَبْ** — *"Do not become angry!"* The man repeated his request several times, and the Prophet ﷺ answered every time: *"Do not become angry!"* (Hadith 16 of An-Nawawi).

### The Prophetic First-Aid Kit for Anger:
1. **Say the Refuge Du'a:** Say immediately: *“A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim!”*
2. **Remain Silent:** When angry, we say words we will deeply regret later. Close your mouth until your heart calms down!
3. **Change Your Posture:** If you are standing, sit down; if sitting, lie down.
4. **Make Cool Wudu:** Anger is like a fire ignited by Shaytan; cool water extinguishes fire!
