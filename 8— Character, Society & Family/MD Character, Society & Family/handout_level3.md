## 1. The Heart of Faith: Sincerity (*Ikhlas*)

Welcome to Level 3 Character and Ethics! In Islam, outward good deeds mean nothing if the inner intention is corrupt. The foundational Hadith of the entire religion states:  
*“Actions are judged only by intentions, and every person will have only what they intended.”* (Hadith 1 of Imam an-Nawawi).

## 2. Guarding the Tongue: Three Destructive Sins of Speech

The Prophet ﷺ said:  
*“Whoever can guarantee for me what is between his two jawbones (the tongue) and what is between his two legs, I guarantee for him Paradise!”* (Sahih al-Bukhari).

At ages 10–12, schoolyard gossip and text chats can easily lead into three poisonous sins of the tongue:

1. **Al-Ghibah (الْغِيبَة) — Backbiting:**  
   The Prophet ﷺ defined it: *"Mentioning your brother in a way he would dislike."* Someone asked: *"What if what I said is true about him?"* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *"If it is true, you have backbitten him; and if it is false, you have slandered him (*Bahattahu*)!"*  
   *The Quranic Warning:* Surah Al-Hujurat compares backbiting to eating the dead flesh of your own brother!
2. **Al-Namimah (النَّمِيمَة) — Tale-Bearing:**  
   Carrying words between two friends to cause enmity (e.g. *"Did you know Sarah said she hates your shoes?"*). The Prophet ﷺ said: *"The tale-bearer will not enter Paradise!"*
3. **Al-Buhtan (الْبُهْتَان) — Slander / False Accusation:**  
   Fabricating lies about an innocent person to ruin their reputation.

## 3. Filial Piety: *Birr al-Walidayn* (Honoring Parents)

Allah pairs worship of Him with kindness to parents:  
*“And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and that you be kind to parents. Whether one or both of them reach old age with you, say not to them a word of disrespect (not even 'Uff!'), nor shout at them, but address them with noble speech.”* (Surah Al-Isra' 17:23).

### Practical Ways to Practice *Birr* Every Day:
1. **Never say "Uff!":** Avoid rolling eyes, sighing in frustration, or slamming doors when asked to clean up or study.
2. **Listen Attentively:** When your mother or father speaks, put down your phone and make respectful eye contact.
3. **Make the Quranic Du'a Daily:**  
   `رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا`  
   *“My Lord, have mercy upon them both as they brought me up when I was small!”* (17:24).

## 4. The Sacred Rights of Neighbors

The Prophet ﷺ said:  
*“Jibril kept advising me about good treatment of the neighbor until I thought he would make him an heir!”* (Sahih al-Bukhari).

* **Who is a Neighbor?** Classical scholars defined the neighborhood as extending **forty houses in every direction**!
* **Universal Kindness:** Whether your neighbor is a Muslim, Christian, Jewish, or of no faith, you must protect their peace, share delicious food, avoid loud noise late at night, and help them during blizzards or illness.
