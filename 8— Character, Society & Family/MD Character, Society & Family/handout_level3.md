# Level 3 Student Handout: Character, Society & Family
**Age Group:** Ages 10-12 (Level 3 Track: Years 5 & 6)  
**Topics:** Sincerity (Ikhlas) vs. Ostentation (Riya'), Guarding the Tongue (Ghibah & Namimah), Filial Piety (Birr al-Walidayn), and The Rights of Neighbors  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Al-Arba'in al-Nawawiyyah* (Hadiths 11 to 20) & *Al-Adab al-Mufrad* of Imam al-Bukhari  
**Riwayah & Tajweed:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 30: Surah Al-A'la and Al-Layl)

---

## 1. The Heart of Faith: Sincerity (*Ikhlas*)

Welcome to Level 3 Character and Ethics! In Islam, outward good deeds carry no spiritual weight if the inner intention is corrupt. The foundational Hadith of the entire religion states:  
*“Actions are judged only by intentions, and every person will have only what they intended.”* (Sahih al-Bukhari 1, Sahih Muslim 1907).

| Spiritual Dimension | Al-Ikhlas (Sincerity) | Al-Riya' (Ostentation / Showing Off) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Definition** | Performing good deeds solely for the pleasure of Allah. | Performing good deeds to impress people or win praise. |
| **Motive** | Seeking divine reward and pleasing the Creator. | Craving social approval, attention, or internet likes. |
| **Spiritual Status** | The foundation of all accepted worship. | Classified as the "Hidden Shirk" (*Al-Shirk al-Khafi*). |
| **Outcome** | Enduring reward and spiritual peace in Paradise. | The deed is rejected and earns spiritual punishment. |

## 2. Guarding the Tongue: Three Destructive Sins of Speech

The Prophet ﷺ said:  
*“Whoever can guarantee for me what is between his two jawbones (the tongue) and what is between his two legs, I guarantee for him Paradise!”* (Sahih al-Bukhari 6474).

At ages 10-12, schoolyard banter and group messaging can easily slip into three poisonous sins of the tongue:

1. **Al-Ghibah (الْغِيبَة), Backbiting:**  
   The Prophet ﷺ defined it: *"Mentioning your brother in a way he would dislike."* Someone asked: *"What if what I said is true about him?"* The Prophet ﷺ replied: *"If it is true, you have backbitten him; and if it is false, you have slandered him (*Bahattahu*)!"* (Sahih Muslim 2589).  
   *The Quranic Warning:* Surah Al-Hujurat (49:12) compares backbiting to eating the dead flesh of your own brother.
2. **Al-Namimah (النَّمِيمَة), Tale-Bearing:**  
   Carrying words between friends to cause hostility and broken friendships. The Prophet ﷺ warned: *"The tale-bearer will not enter Paradise!"* (Sahih al-Bukhari 216, Sahih Muslim 105).
3. **Al-Buhtan (الْبُهْتَان), Slander and False Accusation:**  
   Inventing false claims about an innocent person to harm their reputation.

## 3. Filial Piety: *Birr al-Walidayn* (Honoring Parents)

Allah pairs worship of Him with supreme excellence to parents:  
*“And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and that you be kind to parents. Whether one or both of them reach old age with you, say not to them a word of disrespect (not even 'Uff!'), nor shout at them, but address them with noble speech.”* (Surah Al-Isra' 17:23).

### Practical Ways to Practice *Birr* Every Day:
1. **Never say "Uff!":** Avoid rolling eyes, sighing in frustration, or slamming doors when asked to clean up or study.
2. **Listen Attentively:** When your mother or father speaks, put down devices and make respectful eye contact.
3. **Make the Quranic Du'a Daily:**  
   `رَّبِّ ارْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِي صَغِيرًا`  
   *“My Lord, have mercy upon them both as they brought me up when I was small!”* (Surah Al-Isra' 17:24).
4. **The Sacred Safeguard:** While honoring parents is a supreme duty, obedience is bounded by goodness. The Prophet ﷺ stated: *“There is no obedience to anyone in disobedience to Allah”* (Sahih al-Bukhari 7257, Sahih Muslim 1840). If anyone pressures you to harm someone, lie, or keep unsafe secrets, you must refuse and inform a trusted teacher or counselor.

## 4. The Sacred Rights of Neighbors and Fellow Muslims

The Prophet ﷺ said:  
*“Jibril kept advising me about good treatment of the neighbor until I thought he would make him an heir!”* (Sahih al-Bukhari 6014, Sahih Muslim 2624).

* **Who is a Neighbor?** Classical Islamic scholars defined the neighborhood as extending **forty houses in every direction**.
* **Universal Kindness:** Whether your neighbor is a Muslim or of another faith, you must protect their peace, share food, avoid loud noise late at night, and assist them during illness or emergencies.
* **The Six Rights of a Muslim:** The Prophet ﷺ taught: *"The rights of a Muslim upon another Muslim are six: When you meet him, greet him with Salam; when he invites you, accept his invitation; when he seeks your advice, advise him sincerely; when he sneezes and praises Allah, reply 'Yarhamukallah'; when he is ill, visit him; and when he passes away, attend his funeral"* (Sahih Muslim 2162).
