# Level 4 Student Handout: Character, Society & Family
**Age Group:** Ages 12-14 (Level 4 Track: Adolescent & Puberty / Years 7 & 8)  
**Topics:** Islamic Brotherhood (Ukhuwwah), Overcoming Anger, Enjoining Good & Social Justice, and The Adab of Knowledge  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Al-Arba'in al-Nawawiyyah* (Hadiths 21 to 35) & *Riyad al-Salihin*  
**Riwayah & Tajweed:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 29: Surah Al-Qalam and Al-Haqqah)

---

## 1. Spiritual Brotherhood: *Al-Ukhuwwah al-Islamiyyah*

As you navigate early adolescence, social cliques, peer pressure, and tribal loyalties often dominate school life. Islam shatters superficial cliques by establishing **Ukhuwwah** (spiritual brotherhood and sisterhood) rooted purely in love for Allah.

The Prophet ﷺ said in Hadith 13 of Imam an-Nawawi:  
*“None of you truly believes until he loves for his brother what he loves for himself!”* (Sahih al-Bukhari 13, Sahih Muslim 45).

### The Six Rights of a Fellow Muslim (Sahih Muslim 2162):

| Right | Occasion | Practical Etiquette |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Greeting (*Salam*)** | When meeting | Initiate Salam with a warm smile and clear voice. |
| **2. Invitation (*Ijabah*)** | When invited | Attend celebratory or community meals to build love. |
| **3. Sincere Advice (*Nasihah*)** | When consulted | Offer honest, constructive, and compassionate counsel in private. |
| **4. Tashmit** | When sneezing | Say *"Yarhamukallah"* when they sneeze and say *"Alhamdulillah"*. |
| **5. Visiting the Sick** | During illness | Visit, comfort them, make du'a for healing, and offer practical help. |
| **6. Funeral (*Jana'iz*)** | At passing | Attend the funeral prayer and burial to honor their soul. |

* **The Three-Day Limit on Boycotts:** The Prophet ﷺ strictly forbade Muslims from boycotting or refusing to speak to a brother for **more than three days** (Sahih al-Bukhari 6077, Sahih Muslim 2560). The superior of the two is the one who initiates the greeting of Salam first!

## 2. Emotional Mastery: The Prophetic Cure for Anger

A man once came to the Messenger of Allah ﷺ and asked: *"Advise me!"* The Prophet ﷺ repeated three times:  
**“Do not become angry!” (*La Taghdab*)** (Sahih al-Bukhari 6116).

Islam does not demand that you never feel irritation; rather, it commands you to **master your emotional reactions** rather than becoming a slave to blind rage:
* The Prophet ﷺ asked: *"Who do you consider the strong wrestler among you?"* The companions replied: *"The one who cannot be thrown to the ground by men!"* The Prophet ﷺ corrected them:  
  *“The strong person is not the wrestler; rather, the truly strong person is the one who controls his own soul during anger!”* (Sahih al-Bukhari 6114, Sahih Muslim 2609).

### The Prophetic Step-by-Step Protocol for Anger:
1. **Seek Refuge:** Say immediately: *A'udhu billahi minash-shaytanir-rajim*.
2. **Change Physical Posture:** If you are standing, sit down; if you are sitting, lie down (Sunan Abi Dawud 4782). Lowering your physical posture cools adrenaline.
3. **Perform Wudu:** Water extinguishes the heat of demonic rage.
4. **Remain Silent:** Never send an angry text message, post a comment, or speak in rage. Wait until the storm clears.

## 3. Social Justice and Enjoining Good: Hadith 34

The Prophet ﷺ commanded:  
*“Whoever among you sees an evil, let him change it with his hand; if he cannot, then with his tongue; if he cannot, then with his heart, and that is the weakest of faith.”* (Sahih Muslim 49).

| Tier of Intervention | Level of Authority | Practical Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. By the Hand (*Bil-Yad*)** | Legitimate lawful jurisdiction | Parents in the home, teachers in the classroom, or lawful civic authorities in society. Unlawful vigilantism is strictly forbidden. |
| **2. By the Tongue (*Bil-Lisan*)** | Spoken and written advocacy | Speaking up for a bullied classmate, explaining truth respectfully, offering gentle private advice (*Nasihah*), or reporting abuse. |
| **3. By the Heart (*Bil-Qalb*)** | Inner spiritual rejection | When physical intervention and speech would cause greater harm, detesting the wrong in the heart and refusing to participate. |

### The Sacred Safeguarding Standard:
Legitimate authority is bounded by divine law. The Prophet ﷺ declared: *“There is no obedience to a created being in disobedience to the Creator”* (Sahih Muslim 1840). If anyone in authority orders something harmful, illegal, or abusive, one must not comply, and should immediately seek help from trusted counselors or child protection advocates.

## 4. The Sacred Adab of Seeking Knowledge (*Adab Talab al-'Ilm*)

When Imam Malik was a boy, his mother dressed him in scholarly clothes and advised him:  
*“Go to Rabi'ah and learn from his Adab (manners and reverence) before you learn his knowledge!”* (Reported by Qadi 'Iyad in *Tartib al-Madarik*).

In his classical manual *Tadhkirat al-Sami'*, Qadi Ibn Jama'ah outlines essential student etiquette:
* **Humility Before the Teacher:** Listen attentively even if you have heard the lesson before. Never speak over your instructor.
* **Preparedness:** Bring your notebook, pen, and texts ready; sit with upright, alert posture.
* **The Sincerity of Inquiry:** Ask questions to understand and practice, never to show off intelligence or humiliate a classmate.
