# Level 3 Quiz: Character, Society & Family

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How does Prophet Muhammad ﷺ define the ultimate criterion by which human deeds are judged in Hadith 1 of Nawawi?
   (A) By how fast they are completed.
   (B) By the inner intentions (*Niyyat*) of the doer.
   (C) By how much money was spent on tools.
   (D) By the number of spectators watching.

2. What is "Al-Riya'" (Ostentation), and why did the Prophet ﷺ describe it as the "Hidden Shirk"?
   (A) It is forgetting to wash your hands before eating.
   (B) It is performing acts of worship to seek human admiration, social praise, or digital popularity.
   (C) It is speaking Arabic with a foreign accent.
   (D) It is wearing clothes that are too colorful.

3. How did the Prophet ﷺ precisely define "Al-Ghibah" (Backbiting) to the companions?
   (A) Speaking untruths about ancient historical kings.
   (B) Mentioning your brother or sister in a way they would dislike, even if it is completely true.
   (C) Singing songs in the desert.
   (D) Criticizing the weather on a rainy afternoon.

4. To what horrific imagery does Surah Al-Hujurat compare the sin of backbiting?
   (A) Drowning in an ocean of ice.
   (B) Eating the dead flesh of one's own deceased brother.
   (C) Being struck by falling meteors.
   (D) Losing all one's teeth in a nightmare.

5. What is the definition of "Al-Namimah" (Tale-Bearing)?
   (A) Carrying speech between people with the malicious intent of causing enmity and conflict.
   (B) Reading aloud from an encyclopedia.
   (C) Translating poetry from Persian into Arabic.
   (D) Teaching children how to ride horses.

6. What is the smallest sound of frustration or annoyance that Allah strictly forbids saying to parents in Surah Al-Isra' (17:23)?
   (A) The word "No!"
   (B) Even saying "Uff!" (or sighing with irritation/rolling eyes).
   (C) The word "Wait!"
   (D) Asking for five more minutes of sleep.

7. How did classical scholars describe the territorial boundary of a neighborhood in Islamic adab?
   (A) The two houses directly sharing a fence.
   (B) Forty houses in all four directions.
   (C) Only the houses located on the same street.
   (D) Any house that has a green front door.

8. According to the authentic hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari, what did Angel Jibril repeatedly advise the Prophet ﷺ regarding neighbors?
   (A) To borrow their gardening tools without asking.
   (B) To treat them with such extraordinary kindness that the Prophet thought neighbors would be granted a share of inheritance.
   (C) To invite them to the mosque only once a year.
   (D) To build stone walls between their properties.

9. What is the proper Islamic reaction if you hear friends gossiping or backbiting about an absent classmate at school?
   (A) Join in and add an even funnier insult.
   (B) Defend the absent classmate's honor gently, change the subject, or walk away from the sinful gathering.
   (C) Secretly record the conversation on your phone and post it online.
   (D) Laugh loudly to fit in with the peer group.

10. What beautiful du'a for parents is taught directly in Surah Al-Isra' (17:24)?
    (A) "O Allah, give my parents palaces of gold!"
    (B) "My Lord, have mercy upon them both as they brought me up when I was small!"
    (C) "O Allah, make my parents travel the world!"
    (D) "O Allah, forgive my parents only if they are pleased with my exam scores!"

## Part B: Fill in the Blanks

1. Performing acts of devotion to seek social applause or praise is called ______.
2. Mentioning someone in their absence with words they would dislike is ______.
3. The smallest syllable of irritation prohibited when addressing parents is ______.
4. The Islamic virtue of honoring and showing supreme kindness to parents is ______.
5. In classical Islamic adab, the neighborhood extends ______ in every direction.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Ikhlas ─── Purifying one's intention solely for the pleasure of Allah
* Riya' ─── Showing off good deeds to win human approval and popularity
* Ghibah ─── Backbiting by mentioning an absent person's faults
* Namimah ─── Carrying words between people to stir up hatred and division
* Birr al-Walidayn ─── Supreme dutifulness, gentleness, and honor shown to parents

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. A student studies hard and recites Quran beautifully at a public assembly, but later catches themselves thinking: "I hope everyone thinks I am the most religious kid in school!" How should they spiritually cure this thought of *Riya'*?
2. Distinguish clearly between *Ghibah* (backbiting), *Namimah* (tale-bearing), and *Buhtan* (slander). Give an example of each.
3. Why did Allah pair filial piety (*Birr al-Walidayn*) with Tawhid in multiple verses across the Quran?
4. How can a Muslim family put the Prophetic sunnah of neighborly kindness into practice with non-Muslim neighbors in a Western suburb?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - Hadith 1 of Nawawi confirms that deeds are evaluated entirely by their underlying intentions.
2. B - Riya' is the minor shirk where human approval is prioritized over or paired with divine pleasure.
3. B - Ghibah is speaking about someone behind their back in a manner that would cause them displeasure.
4. B - Surah Al-Hujurat 49:12 uses the vivid analogy of eating the flesh of one's dead brother.
5. A - Namimah is maliciously transmitting gossip to destroy friendships and social cohesion.
6. B - Surah Al-Isra' 17:23 forbids even saying "Uff", representing the minimal audible sigh of impatience.
7. B - Prophetic traditions define the neighborhood radius as forty houses in all compass directions.
8. B - Jibril's constant emphasis led the Prophet to anticipate that neighbors would inherit estate shares.
9. B - Believers are commanded to defend an absent brother's honor or withdraw from gatherings of sin.
10. B - Surah Al-Isra' 17:24 reveals: "Rabbi irhamhuma kama rabbayani saghira."
