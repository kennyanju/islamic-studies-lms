# Level 3 Quiz: Character, Society & Family

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How does Prophet Muhammad ﷺ define the ultimate criterion by which human deeds are judged in Hadith 1 of Nawawi?
   (A) By how fast they are completed.
   (B) By the number of spectators observing the action.
   (C) By the inner intentions (Niyyat) of the doer.
   (D) By the monetary cost of the resources used.

2. What is "Al-Riya'" (Ostentation), and why did the Prophet ﷺ describe it as the "Hidden Shirk"?
   (A) Forgetting to wash one's hands prior to eating.
   (B) Speaking a language with an unfamiliar regional accent.
   (C) Wearing brightly colored clothes during celebrations.
   (D) Performing acts of worship to seek human admiration, social praise, or digital popularity.

3. How did the Prophet ﷺ precisely define "Al-Ghibah" (Backbiting) in Sahih Muslim?
   (A) Mentioning your brother or sister in their absence in a way they would dislike, even if true.
   (B) Speaking untruths about historical leaders from ancient civilizations.
   (C) Reciting poetry aloud in an open field.
   (D) Offering constructive advice directly to a companion in private.

4. To what vivid imagery does Surah Al-Hujurat (49:12) compare the sin of backbiting?
   (A) Drowning in an icy lake in the middle of winter.
   (B) Eating the dead flesh of one's own deceased brother.
   (C) Falling into a deep pit without any ladder.
   (D) Breaking a glass cup against a stone wall.

5. What is the definition and legal status of "Al-Namimah" (Tale-Bearing) in Islamic ethics?
   (A) Memorizing genealogies of early Arabian tribes.
   (B) Reading historical news articles to school students.
   (C) Carrying words between people with the malicious intent of causing enmity and conflict.
   (D) Translating scholarly texts between different languages.

6. What is the smallest sound of frustration or disrespect that Allah strictly forbids saying to parents in Surah Al-Isra' (17:23)?
   (A) Even saying "Uff!" (or sighing with irritation and rolling eyes).
   (B) Asking for clarification about a homework question.
   (C) Inquiring about what time dinner will be served.
   (D) Requesting permission to read a book before bed.

7. How did classical scholars describe the territorial boundary of a neighborhood in Islamic adab?
   (A) The two houses directly sharing a fence on the left and right.
   (B) Any house visible from the highest hill in the city.
   (C) Only the houses located along the primary market street.
   (D) Forty houses in all four compass directions.

8. According to the authentic hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari, what did Angel Jibril repeatedly advise the Prophet ﷺ regarding neighbors?
   (A) To borrow their household tools without returning them.
   (B) To treat them with such extraordinary kindness that the Prophet thought neighbors would be granted an inheritance share.
   (C) To invite them to meals only once every ten years.
   (D) To build high walls between adjacent properties to avoid interaction.

9. What is the proper Islamic conduct if you hear friends gossiping or backbiting about an absent classmate?
   (A) Secretly record the conversation and share it on social media.
   (B) Join in and add another embarrassing story about the classmate.
   (C) Defend the absent classmate's honor gently, change the subject, or leave the gathering.
   (D) Laugh loudly to avoid feeling left out of the social group.

10. What beautiful du'a for parents is taught directly in Surah Al-Isra' (17:24)?
    (A) "My Lord, grant my parents unlimited riches and gold."
    (B) "My Lord, make my parents famous across all nations."
    (C) "My Lord, forgive my parents only if they purchase what I desire."
    (D) "My Lord, have mercy upon them both as they brought me up when I was small!"

## Part C: Matching Terms
* Ikhlas ─── Purifying one's intention solely for the pleasure of Allah
* Riya' ─── Showing off good deeds to win human approval and popularity
* Ghibah ─── Backbiting by mentioning an absent person's faults
* Namimah ─── Carrying words between people to stir up hatred and division
* Birr al-Walidayn ─── Supreme dutifulness, gentleness, and honor shown to parents

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. A student studies hard and recites Quran beautifully at a public assembly, but later catches themselves thinking: "I hope everyone thinks I am the most religious kid in school!" How should they spiritually cure this thought of Riya'?
2. Distinguish clearly between Ghibah (backbiting), Namimah (tale-bearing), and Buhtan (slander). Give an example of each.
3. Why did Allah pair filial piety (Birr al-Walidayn) with Tawhid in multiple verses across the Quran?
4. How can a Muslim family put the Prophetic sunnah of neighborly kindness into practice with non-Muslim neighbors in a Western suburb?

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. C - Hadith 1 of Nawawi confirms that deeds are evaluated entirely by their underlying intentions (Sahih al-Bukhari 1, Sahih Muslim 1907).
2. D - Riya' is the minor shirk where human approval is prioritized over or paired with divine pleasure.
3. A - Ghibah is speaking about someone behind their back in a manner that would cause them displeasure (Sahih Muslim 2589).
4. B - Surah Al-Hujurat 49:12 uses the vivid analogy of eating the flesh of one's dead brother.
5. C - Namimah is maliciously transmitting gossip to destroy friendships and social cohesion (Sahih al-Bukhari 216).
6. A - Surah Al-Isra' 17:23 forbids even saying "Uff", representing the minimal audible sigh of impatience.
7. D - Classical scholars defined the neighborhood radius as forty houses in all compass directions.
8. B - Jibril's constant emphasis led the Prophet ﷺ to anticipate that neighbors would inherit estate shares (Sahih al-Bukhari 6014).
9. C - Believers are commanded to defend an absent brother's honor or withdraw from gatherings of sin.
10. D - Surah Al-Isra' 17:24 reveals: "Rabbi irhamhuma kama rabbayani saghira."
