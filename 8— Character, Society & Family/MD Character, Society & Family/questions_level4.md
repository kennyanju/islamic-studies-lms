# Level 4 Quiz: Character, Society & Family

## Part A: Multiple Choice Questions
1. How does Prophet Muhammad ﷺ define the perfection of faith in Hadith 13 of Imam an-Nawawi?
   (A) By memorizing one hundred thousand poems.
   (B) Loving for your brother what you love for yourself.
   (C) Refusing to speak to non-Muslims.
   (D) Building the tallest minaret in your city.

2. What is the maximum time limit allowed for two Muslims to boycott or refuse to speak to one another following a dispute?
   (A) Twenty-four hours.
   (B) Three days.
   (C) One lunar year.
   (D) Forever if the argument was about money.

3. According to the authentic hadith, who is the superior person when two Muslims reconcile after a disagreement?
   (A) The one who pays the other person twenty dirhams.
   (B) The one who initiates the greeting of Salam first.
   (C) The one who remains silent and walks away.
   (D) The one who hires a lawyer.

4. What advice did the Prophet ﷺ repeat three times to the man who asked for life guidance in Hadith 16 of Nawawi?
   (A) "Do not become angry! (La Taghdab)."
   (B) "Travel to Rome!"
   (C) "Stop eating wheat bread!"
   (D) "Avoid riding horses at night!"

5. How did the Prophet ﷺ redefine "the truly strong person" (Al-Shadid) in Sahih al-Bukhari?
   (A) The wrestler who throws thirty men to the ground in single combat.
   (B) The one who controls his own soul and temper during moments of anger.
   (C) The general who commands the largest army of archers.
   (D) The merchant who owns the most camels in Arabia.

6. What is the very first physical action recommended in the Sunnah when you feel intense anger rising?
   (A) Shouting into a pillow.
   (B) Seeking refuge with Allah from Satan (*Isti'adhah*), sitting down if standing, and performing wudu.
   (C) Breaking a clay pot on the floor.
   (D) Running around the block three times.

7. In Hadith 34 of Nawawi, what are the three graduated tiers of changing an observed wrong or injustice?
   (A) By money, by voting, and by boycott.
   (B) By the hand (lawful authority), by the tongue (counsel/truth), and by the heart (inner rejection).
   (C) By war, by treaties, and by exile.
   (D) By fire, by water, and by earth.

8. When is changing a wrong "with the hand" legally permitted in Islamic society?
   (A) By any random person on the street who feels offended.
   (B) By those holding legitimate legal or parental jurisdiction (e.g. parents at home, authorities in law).
   (C) Only on Friday afternoons.
   (D) By teenage vigilantes on social media.

9. What golden rule did Imam Malik emphasize regarding the acquisition of sacred knowledge?
   (A) "Learn to read Greek before learning Hadith."
   (B) "Learn Adab (manners and reverence) before you learn knowledge!"
   (C) "Knowledge is only for those who own large libraries."
   (D) "Debate with scholars to make yourself famous."

10. What is the correct student etiquette if a teacher explains a concept you already know well?
    (A) Interrupt the teacher immediately and announce that you already know it.
    (B) Listen attentively with humility and respect, recognizing that reviewing knowledge is a blessing.
    (C) Look at your mobile phone and whisper to your neighbor.
    (D) Walk out of the classroom.

## Part B: Fill in the Blanks

1. The Prophetic advice repeated three times regarding emotional self-control is ______.
2. Boycotting a fellow Muslim following a dispute is forbidden beyond ______.
3. Loving for your brother what you love for yourself is the core message of Nawawi's ______.
4. The truly strong person who controls his soul during anger is titled ______.
5. Imam Malik taught that a seeker of knowledge must master ______ before learning facts.

## Part C: Matching Terms
* La Taghdab ─── The repeated prophetic counsel to master emotional anger
* Al-Shadid ─── The truly strong person who restrains his temper when provoked
* Ukhuwwah ─── Spiritual brotherhood and sisterhood rooted in love for Allah
* Hadith 34 ─── Changing injustice through hand, tongue, or heart
* Adab al-Talib ─── The sacred etiquette and humility of the student before teachers

## Part D: Reflection & Application
1. A classmate at school insults you in front of other students, tempting you to react with a physical fight or screaming insults. Walk through how you apply the prophetic anger protocol in that exact moment.
2. Analyze the three tiers of Hadith 34: Why did the Prophet ﷺ designate changing evil "in the heart" as the minimum standard of faith?
3. What is the danger of pursuing academic or Islamic knowledge without first mastering personal *Adab* (manners and humility)?
4. Explain how the Prophetic principle of loving for your brother what you love for yourself transforms peer competition into mutual cooperation.

## Answer Key & Marking Guide
1. B - Hadith 13 establishes empathy and selflessness as the benchmark of mature faith.
2. B - Transgressing the 3-day limit on anger boycotts is strictly forbidden in prophetic Hadith.
3. B - The one who initiates the greeting of Salam breaks pride and earns greater reward.
4. A - The Prophet ﷺ repeated "La Taghdab" to emphasize emotional discipline as life's anchor.
5. B - Spiritual emotional self-control requires far greater strength than mere physical wrestling.
6. B - Seeking refuge, lowering physical posture, and cooling anger with wudu are proven Sunnah methods.
7. B - Changing by hand, tongue, and heart represents the comprehensive framework of enjoining good.
8. B - Direct physical intervention requires lawful authority to prevent chaotic vigilantism.
9. B - Imam Malik insisted that character and reverence must precede theoretical learning.
10. B - Classical Adab requires sitting with the attentiveness of someone hearing the lesson for the first time.
