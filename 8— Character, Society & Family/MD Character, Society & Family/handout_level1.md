# Level 1 Student Handout: Character, Society & Family
**Age Group:** Ages 6-8 (Level 1 Track: Primary Stage 1)  
**Core Strands:** Excellence to Parents (Birr al-Walidayn) | Spreading Salam | Smiling is Charity | Gentleness to Animals  
**Classical Text Spine:** *Mukhtasar al-Akhdari* (Spiritual Adab) & Nawawi Selections  
**Riwayah & Surah Target:** Hafs 'an 'Asim (Juz' 30: Surah Al-Ma'un & Surah Al-'Asr)

---

## 1. Parents: Our Greatest Blessing
Who stayed awake when you had a high fever? Who packed your lunchbox with loving care? Who works hard every day to provide your warm home and food?

Allah commands in the Holy Quran to treat parents with supreme love and gentleness:  
> **وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ أَلَّا تَعْبُدُوٓا۟ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُ وَبِٱلْوَٰلِدَيْنِ إِحْسَٰنًا**  
> *"And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and treat your parents with absolute excellence!"* (Surah Al-Isra' 17:23).

### How to Make Mum & Dad Smile Every Day:
1. **Listen the first time:** When Mum asks you to tidy your toys or wash your hands, respond happily with *"Yes, Mum!"* without sighing or saying "Uff!".
2. **Show gratitude:** Walk up to Dad and say: *"I love you, Dad! Thank you for working so hard for our family!"*
3. **Make the Quranic Du'a for them:**  
   > **رَّبِّ ٱرْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِى صَغِيرًا**  
   > *"Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira!"*  
   > (My Lord, have mercy upon my parents, just as they raised me when I was small!).
4. **Safety and honesty:** We obey parents in all good things. But remember: if anyone ever tells you to keep a bad secret or asks you to do something wrong, always tell a trusted adult, because we never obey anyone in disobedience to Allah.

## 2. Spreading Salam: The Greeting of Peace
Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught us the best words to say when meeting anyone:  
> **أَفْشُوا السَّلاَمَ بَيْنَكُمْ**  
> *"Spread peace (Salam) among yourselves!"* (Sahih Muslim 54).

* When you enter your home, say: **“As-Salamu 'Alaykum!”** (Peace be upon you!). The angels enter with you and bring blessings to your home.
* When someone greets you, reply with even more love: **“Wa 'Alaykum As-Salam wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh!”** (And upon you be peace, the mercy of Allah, and His blessings!).

## 3. Smiling is Charity (Sadaqah)
Did you know that you do not need gold coins to give charity? Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was always smiling warmly at children!
* The Prophet ﷺ said: *"Your smile in the face of your brother is charity for you"* (Jami' at-Tirmidhi 1956).
* A cheerful smile brings joy to others.
* When a new classmate looks nervous or shy, give them a warm smile and invite them to play with you.

## 4. The Merciful Heart: Kindness to Animals and Nature
Islam is a religion of mercy to every living thing:
* **The Thirsty Dog:** Prophet Muhammad ﷺ told the story of a traveler in the desert who was very thirsty. After drinking from a well, he saw a dog panting and licking moist sand from thirst. The kind traveler climbed down the well, filled his leather shoe with water, carried it up in his teeth, and gave it to the dog. Allah appreciated his mercy and forgave his sins (Sahih al-Bukhari 2363, Sahih Muslim 2244).
* **The Mother Bird:** Once, a companion took two chicks from a nest. The mother bird flew around flapping her wings in grief. The Prophet ﷺ said with compassion: *"Who has hurt this bird by taking her children? Return her chicks to her immediately!"* (Sunan Abi Dawud 2675).
* **Caring for Nature:** Never break branches off trees for fun, never harm tiny creatures, and never let the water tap run wastefully.
