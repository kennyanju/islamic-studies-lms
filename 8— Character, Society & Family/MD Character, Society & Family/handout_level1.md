**Core Strands:** Excellence to Parents (Birr al-Walidayn) | Spreading Salam | Smiling is Charity | Gentleness to Animals

## 💖 1. Mum and Dad: Our Greatest Treasures!
Who stayed awake all night when you had a high fever? Who packed your favorite lunchbox with loving care? Who works hard every day to provide your warm home and toys?

Allah commands in the Holy Qur'an to treat parents with supreme love and gentleness:  
> **وَقَضَىٰ رَبُّكَ أَلَّا تَعْبُدُوٓا۟ إِلَّآ إِيَّاهُ وَبِٱلْوَٰلِدَيْنِ إِحْسَٰنًا**  
> *"And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him, and treat your parents with absolute excellence!"* (Surah Al-Isra' 17:23).

### How to Make Mum & Dad Smile Every Day:
1. **Listen the first time:** When Mum asks you to tidy your toys or wash your hands, jump up happily with *"Yes, Mum!"* without sighing or saying "Uff!".
2. **Give surprise hugs:** Walk up to Dad and say: *"I love you, Dad! Thank you for working so hard for us!"*
3. **Make the Quranic Du'a for them:**  
   > **رَّبِّ ٱرْحَمْهُمَا كَمَا رَبَّيَانِى صَغِيرًا**  
   > *"Rabbir-hamhuma kama rabbayani saghira!"*  
   > (My Lord, have mercy upon my parents, just as they raised me when I was small!).

## 🕊️ 2. The Secret Password of Peace: As-Salamu 'Alaykum!
Prophet Muhammad ﷺ taught us the best words to say when meeting anyone:  
> **أَفْشُوا السَّلاَمَ بَيْنَكُمْ**  
> *"Spread peace (Salam) among yourselves!"* (Sahih Muslim).

* When you enter your home, say: **“As-Salamu 'Alaykum!”** (Peace be upon you!). The angels enter with you and bring blessings to your home.
* When someone greets you, reply with even more love: **“Wa 'Alaykum As-Salam wa Rahmatullahi wa Barakatuh!”** (And upon you be peace, the mercy of Allah, and His blessings!).

---

## 😊 3. Smiling is Charity (Sadaqah)
Did you know that you don't need gold coins to give charity? Prophet Muhammad ﷺ was always smiling warmly at children!
* A cheerful smile melts sadness away.
* When a new classmate looks nervous or shy, give them a big, warm smile and invite them to play with you on the swings!

## 🐱 4. The Merciful Heart: Animals and Trees
Islam is a religion of mercy to every living thing:
* **The Thirsty Dog:** Prophet Muhammad ﷺ told the story of a traveler in the desert who was very thirsty. After drinking from a well, he saw a poor dog panting and licking moist sand from intense thirst. The kind man climbed down the well, filled his leather shoe with water, carried it up in his teeth, and gave it to the dog. Allah loved his mercy so much that He forgave all his sins!
* **The Tiny Mother Bird:** Once, a companion took two tiny chicks from a nest. The mother bird flew around flapping her wings in grief. The Prophet ﷺ said with compassion: *"Who has hurt this bird by taking her children? Return her chicks to her immediately!"*
* **Caring for Nature:** Never break branches off trees for fun, never step on tiny ants, and never let the water tap run wastefully!
